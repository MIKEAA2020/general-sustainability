# STYLE DIRECTIVE v2 — Formal register for all manuscript-carrier files (2026-09-17)

Extends (does not replace) `STYLE_DIRECTIVE_GEMINI_TONE_20260917.md`. Owner
edict 2026-09-17: manuscript files are formal journal articles. The Gemini
accessible prose mechanics stand, but the following classes of language are
banned from manuscript-carrier files (they may exist only in audit/change-log
documents):

1. Diary/navigation/meta: version banners, "humanized/frozen/merged" notes,
   "moved here at the audits' request", "carried verbatim at typesetting",
   transplant/assembly instructions, "(status notes)", "carried without
   expansion".
2. References to older or superseded manuscript versions, and to any named
   review/audit loop, UNLESS genuinely pedagogical/methodological — and never
   naming the superseded version.
3. Phantom/naive/strawman constructions (imagined-objection framing,
   "one context warning…", defensive-as-first-line structures).
4. Editorial/self-praise/self-commentary and chat-register informality:
   "honestly", "plainly", "honest halves", "coin flip", "as a gift",
   "bank account", "wearing a gauge", "rails", "not buried",
   "not re-litigated", "a cleaner sentence than hedging".
5. Project-management jargon inside manuscripts: frozen/ledger/firewall/W1/W2/
   CL-… claim IDs, audit paths, "rejected material", checkpoint/runner notes,
   MD-5 checks, push scripts.
6. Over-hedging and double qualifiers; internal dialogue ("the paper discloses"
   is borderline — prefer stated facts about the data/design).
7. Migration-note constructions: withhold nothing that belongs to the paper's
   own scientific content; but file-mechanics (which v-file a table comes
   from) belong to the change-log layer, never the prose.

Provenance/reject-material records live in the CHANGELOG files next to the
audits. Manuscript files start at the title and end at the conclusions;
rolling-origin conventions, rule-version comparisons, and scope cautions are
written as scientific content or omitted.
