A general theory is not a larger packet, a longer U-list, or a more emphatic scope sentence. It is a closed language plus existence, classification, and transfer theorems that cover every object the language names. The present work is a typed obstruction calculus. Elevation is a change of theorem class, not a change of branding.

---

## 1. What the phrase would have to mean

Fix the intended class \(\mathcal{C}\) of systems the theory claims to govern: finite-dimensional and history-valued plants, sampled and hybrid executions, robust / epistemic / chance / institutional judgments, generation-indexed specifications, conservation moieties, and noncompensatory constraint registries.

A **general theory of viability-based sustainability judgments on \(\mathcal{C}\)** would consist of four things, all proved, none rhetorical.

1. **Language completeness.** Every judgment the programme utters is a sentence in a frozen typed language, and every sentence true in all of \(\mathcal{C}\) is derivable from the axioms. R09 is a claim inventory. Completeness is a metatheorem.
2. **Existence, not verification.** For each named object — regulation map, certificate family \(V\), continuation kernel, assume–guarantee depth, generation policy — there is a theorem that produces it from checkable data, or a theorem that says the data are insufficient. R02, R07.Thm2, and R05.Cor3 are still mostly the second kind, or the first kind only after the object is assumed.
3. **Classification, not independence.** Part M shows that delay, substitution, aggregation, diagnostics, bifurcation, and information are not axiom-level laws. A theory says *which extra structure decides the truth value*. Opposite examples are the start of a classification, not the classification.
4. **Transfer onto systems that exist.** At least one domain module is admitted by a completed five-map certificate with numerical \((m,L,C,r,\varepsilon)\), a nonempty \(V\) or an explicit emptiness proof, and a confinement certificate. Until then the theory governs typed diagrams, not groundwater, phosphorus, or fisheries.

Even after (1)–(4), the honest name is still not “a general theory of sustainability.” Sustainability is a normative and empirical subject. The honest elevated name is:

> a general mathematical theory of viability-based sustainability judgments.

“General” then means: complete for the typed judgment family on \(\mathcal{C}\). It never means: predicts the biosphere, decides substitution ethics, or replaces model-class analysis.

## 2. What is already in hand (do not redo)

These are assets. They are not the missing theory.

- Typed ledger with the TCS-1.1 guards (solution concept, branch quantifier, chance support, confinement, erosion triple, non-strategic \(I\), layered physical/epistemic registry).
- Lipschitz-envelope strong invariance and prox-regular erosion with an explicit budget.
- Obstruction theorems: endpoint-only tests, aggregate-as-kernel, stock-to-rate without a rate band, separate local \(u_i\), untyped physical/epistemic inclusions, continuous crossing of disjoint specifications.
- Independent-control product theorem; joint-regulation-map theorem; tubular assume–guarantee with an \(M\)-matrix budget on the convexified inclusion.
- Continuum-moment non-closure; projectability as the exact-closure criterion.
- Closed-loop *verification* lemma; diagnostic trichotomy; admission *checklist*; generation-path *type*; hierarchy converses; U/M honesty split.

That is a conditional calculus plus a register of things that must not be claimed. It is the scaffolding of a theory, not the theory.

## 3. The elevation bar (necessary conditions)

Refuse the phrase until every item below is a numbered theorem with quantifiers, regularity, solution concept, and a proof that does not cite an unseen file.

### A. Language completeness (makes R09 a theorem)

**A1. Representation.** Every sustainability judgment in the intended informal class is equivalent to a viability (or chance-viability, or institutional-viability) statement on a typed product
\[
Z = X_{\mathrm{phys}}\times H_{\mathrm{harm}}\times B_{\mathrm{info}}\times U_{\mathrm{held}}\times G_{\mathrm{gen}}.
\]
Necessity and sufficiency, with a counter-model for each omitted block.

**A2. Completeness of U.** A sentence in the frozen language is true in every instantiation if and only if it follows from U1–U5 plus the typed inference rules (quantifier monotonicity, mapping contracts, empty-solution guard). New candidates cannot be added by narrative; they join U by derivation or M by witness.

Without A1–A2, “this list is complete” remains editorial.

### B. Existence theorems (replace verification lemmas)

**B1. Constructive institutional viability.**  
Data: observation \(O\), conservative or exact filter \(\Phi\), non-strategic \(I,\mathrm{DeP}\), compact \(U,D\), prox-regular \(K\), Lipschitz plant.  
Conclusion: either an explicitly constructed nonempty downward-closed \(V\) satisfying (REG) and a locally Lipschitz selector \(\pi\), or a checkable emptiness certificate (common-action obstruction, delayed information, empty joint \(R\)).  
Quantifiers: \(\exists\pi\;\forall x_0\in B_0\;\forall\) observations \(\forall u^{\mathrm{real}}\in I\;\forall\mathrm{DeP}\;\forall d(\cdot)\).  
Infinite horizon only with a confinement certificate and joint Hausdorff-usc.

R02.Thm1 becomes the *checking* half of B1. B1 is the missing *production* half.

**B2. Selector theorems, not caveats.**  
Michael / implicit-function / strongly convex QP conditions under which \(R_K\), the (REG) witness map, the predecessor map, and the generation witness map admit locally Lipschitz selections. Measurable selection stays an existence remark, never an implementation claim.

**B3. Intergenerational existence.**  
R07.Thm2 stays a characterization. Add: (i) aligned universal or existential reset preimage, used consistently; (ii) jump-margin analogue of erosion; (iii) non-Zeno / non-blocking; (iv) a theorem that produces nonempty \(W^{(G)}\) from data, not from the hypothesis that they are nonempty; (v) the repaired Lem4 hypotheses written into the infinite-generation statement.

### C. Classification theorems (make Part M a theory)

Independence witnesses stay. Each must be completed by a necessary-and-sufficient extra-structure theorem.

| Mechanism | Required classification theorem |
|---|---|
| **C1 Delay** | On linear RFDEs: the characteristic crossing locus (M1’s root locus, completed for all \(\tau>\pi/2\)) is necessary and sufficient for delay-induced instability. On nonlinear history systems: a Halanay / history-space small-gain condition that is necessary on a stated class, not only sufficient. |
| **C2 Substitution** | Linear case: Farkas is already the classification — write it as such, with the cone and the alternative. Nonlinear case: a constraint-qualification theorem saying when a local substitution pathway exists, and a fibre obstruction when it does not. No exchange rate unless \(\Omega\) declares a common numeraire *and* the qualification holds. |
| **C3 Aggregation** | Exact closure \(\iff\) projectability (already). Finite Markovian closure \(\iff\) fibres of \(P\) are separated by a finite statistic of stated type. Explicit positive cases: finite \(N\), exponential families, invariant manifolds (identical patches). Explicit negative case: continuum logistic fields (R06.Thm3, scope-locked). Approximate closure \(\iff\) defect \(\varepsilon\) plus a nonempty erosion budget. |
| **C4 Diagnostics** | A diagnostic is a certificate iff it is an adversarial-exit functional or a margin-with-budget functional on a prox-regular set (R03.Thm1), *and* the converse: every sound nonviability (resp. viability) certificate is equivalent to one of those two forms on the stated class. Rate-band necessary and sufficient for stock-to-rate soundness, with an explicit factor. |
| **C5 Bifurcation** | Kernel changes under a parameter iff the continuation of the viability boundary meets a fold/transversality condition (normal to \(K\) at the contact point is not orthogonal to the unfolding). M5’s uncoupled fold and the parameter-free emptying \(\dot x=1\) on \([0,1]\) become the two extremal cases of one theorem. |
| **C6 Information / implementation** | Refinement enlarges viability iff the lift satisfies R08.Prop1’s hypotheses. Implementation enlargement shrinks \(\forall u^{\mathrm{real}}\) safety and enlarges \(\exists u^{\mathrm{real}}\) safety, with no further cases. Delayed information is inert iff it arrives after every adversarial-exit time. |

After C1–C6, Part M is a classification calculus. Until then it is a register.

### D. Composition beyond the tubular product

**D1. Nonlinear assume–guarantee.**  
Contract amplitudes \(\delta_{ij}(r)\) not necessarily linear. A monotone operator \(\Phi\) on depth vectors whose attractive fixed point, if it lies in the tube, certifies \(Q^r\). State the contraction metric. No insertion of nonlinear maps into a numerical matrix without a comparison system.

**D2. Shared control plus gain.**  
Merge the joint regulation map \(R(x)\ne\emptyset\) with D1. Local nonemptiness of projections remains neither necessary nor sufficient. Produce one theorem whose hypotheses mention both \(R\) and \(\Phi\), and keep the shared-actuator witness as the sharpness example.

**D3. Relaxation exactness.**  
When \(U\) or \(F\) is nonconvex: either a theorem that Filippov/Young-measure solutions of the convexification stay in \(K\) iff original solutions do, or a standing restriction of every composition theorem to the convexified inclusion, written in the theorem statement.

**D4. Variable-event hybrid composition.**  
Review-synchronised events are not enough. Event functions, reset maps sending eroded sets into eroded sets, a jump-margin inequality, and a non-Zeno certificate. This is a separate theorem. The continuous product argument does not imply it.

### E. Uncertainty classes that the language already names

**E1. Stochastic viability.**  
Pathwise / chance-\(p\) kernels under an explicit law and filtration. Robust \(\Rightarrow\) chance-1 iff \(\operatorname{supp}(\mathrm{law})\subseteq W\) (already the guard). Converses classified. Second-order / viscosity inequalities; the deterministic proximal inequality is stated *not* to imply them. Stochastic filter exactness, or a conservative-filter soundness theorem with a precise incompleteness witness.

**E2. Output feedback.**  
Observer-invariance or estimation-error barrier: a set \(K\times E\) in plant-observer coordinates whose projection is the certified plant set. R02’s sampled conservative filter is one discrete case, not the theorem.

**E3. Partial observation with common-action calculus.**  
A necessary and sufficient condition for nonempty intersection of the fibrewise safe-control sets, in terms of the observation partition. R02.Prop4 is the obstruction; E3 is the classification.

### F. What makes it about sustainability rather than abstract control

Without at least two of the following, the theory is general control-and-viability with a sustainability vocabulary.

**F1. Conservation–viability coupling.**  
A theorem: if \(L\) is a declared moiety and a barrier is built from \(L^\top x\) (or from a vector of moieties) plus a remainder that the controls can dominate, then the kernel is nonempty, empty, or equal to a stated face. This turns U1 from an identity into a production rule for kernels.

**F2. Obligation dynamics as a hard constraint, typed apart from welfare.**  
Accumulate / cap / forgive are not comments. Theorems: finite support horizon under mandatory increment (R07.Cor6, kept); existence under cap; the forgive reset as an Operator II map that must send \(K_g\) into \(K_{g+1}\). Discounting inequalities stay in a separate judgment type (A001 14.3). No ethical aggregation inside the kernel.

**F3. Open-system throughput.**  
A theorem relating declared boundary fluxes to kernel emptiness (too little inflow, too much obligatory outflow). This is the sustainability-specific half of F1.

**F4. One closed empirical admission.**  
All blocking items on one module (groundwater or phosphorus) actually closed; maps (1)–(5) exhibited; \((m,L,C,r,\varepsilon)\) numerical; (REG) exhibited or refuted; confinement stated. Classification tables that cite unaudited source files do not count.

### G. External closure

**G1.** Literature identification against robust DP, viability kernels, ISS / small-gain, moment closure, hybrid invariance, stochastic viability. Novelty fields become citations or withdrawals.

**G2.** Status monotonicity enforced in print: no paper cites R02 as an existence theorem, R05 as nonlinear small-gain, R06 as finite-\(N\) non-closure, R07 as hybrid existence, or R09 as a completed general theory.

Until G1–G2, even a completed theorem set is not a theory the literature can use.

## 4. Minimum set that would justify the *cautious* phrase

If the goal is the narrow honest sentence — *a general mathematical theory of viability-based sustainability judgments on a declared class \(\mathcal{C}\)* — the minimum closure is:

1. **A1 + A2** (language completeness).  
2. **B1 + B2** (constructive closed loop and selectors).  
3. **D1 + D2** (nonlinear A–G with joint feasibility), or a published theorem that \(\mathcal{C}\) excludes shared and nonconvex controls.  
4. **C3 + C4 + C6** (the three classifications the programme already almost has).  
5. **F1 + F4** (conservation produces kernels; one real module admitted).  
6. **G1** (external identification).

That is six theorem-clusters, not nine more result records. C1, C2, C5, D4, E1–E3, F2–F3, and B3 can remain as classified open problems *inside* the theory, provided they are stated as classification targets and not as slogans.

Without any one of 1–6 the cautious phrase is still false:

- drop A1–A2: the language is a ledger;  
- drop B1–B2: governance is unchecked;  
- drop D1–D2: composition is a product theorem;  
- drop C3–C4–C6: mechanisms are a register;  
- drop F1+F4: it is not about sustainability systems;  
- drop G1: it is not a theory anyone else can locate.

## 5. What would remain forever model-class dependent

Even a completed theory does not absorb the following into the axioms. The classification theorems *say so*.

- Whether delay stabilizes or destabilizes.  
- Whether a substitution pathway exists.  
- Whether a given coarse map closes.  
- Whether a diagnostic is a certificate.  
- Whether a fold moves a kernel.  
- Whether more observation or more implementation branches help.  
- Numerical values of \(m,L,C,r,\varepsilon,\rho(\Gamma)\).  
- Ethical choice of \(\tau^h\in\{\mathrm{accumulate},\mathrm{cap},\mathrm{forgive}\}\).  
- Any stochastic law whose support is not declared.

A general theory classifies these. It does not decide them.

---

## 6. What must still never be claimed

After elevation, these remain forbidden. They are false, not “not yet proved.”

- Endpoint-only or aggregate transformation tests as sound (R01).  
- Metric erosion on arbitrary closed sets.  
- Pointwise local \(u_i\Rightarrow\) joint invariance.  
- Viability \(\Rightarrow\) strong invariance.  
- Finite-horizon \(\Rightarrow\) infinite-horizon without confinement and joint usc.  
- Finite-\(N\) moment non-closure as a universal quadratic-field law.  
- Smooth crossing of disjoint specifications.  
- Literal inclusions between physical and epistemic kernels.  
- Deterministic proximal inequalities as stochastic-viability theorems.  
- Nonlinear gains inserted into a matrix without a comparison system.  
- Any universal delay, substitution, aggregation, diagnostic, or bifurcation law.

Elevation does not repeal the obstruction calculus. It sits on top of it.

## 7. Dependency-ordered programme

**Wave 1 — make the language a theory.**  
A1, A2, G2 (status discipline in print), TCS-1.1 freeze. No new applications.

**Wave 2 — existence.**  
B2 first (selectors unlock everything). Then B1 (constructive \(V\)). Then B3 with aligned reset quantifiers. Parallel: F1 (conservation–viability), written so it feeds B1.

**Wave 3 — classification of what you already obstruct.**  
C3, C4, C6 (projectability / diagnostics / information). These are near the page. Then C1 (finish the RFDE crossing) and C2 (write Farkas as a classification, then one nonlinear theorem).

**Wave 4 — composition.**  
D3 (convexification stated, not footnoted). D1. D2. D4 last; it is a hybrid paper of its own.

**Wave 5 — named uncertainty.**  
E3 (common-action classification) before E2 (observer invariance) before E1 (stochastic). Chance support stays a guard until E1 exists.

**Wave 6 — contact with a system.**  
F4 on one module. Only after Waves 1–3. F2–F3 as needed by that module’s obligation and flux data.

**Wave 7 — literature.**  
G1 continuously from Wave 1; freeze novelty claims only after Wave 4.

Do not start Wave 6 to “show generality.” An unaudited domain table is how the present packet overclaimed.

## 8. If the bar is not met

Publish what you have under an accurate name:

- Paper 1: architecture, admission checklist, boxed impossibilities (R01, shared-control witness, R07.Thm4).  
- Paper 2: invariance/erosion lemma, R05 restricted A–G, R03 trichotomy, R08 register, M1/M5 witnesses.  
- Monograph: U/M scope chapter, explicitly *not* a general theory.  
- Open programme: D1–D4, B1, E1–E3, F4.

That is a respectable, refereeable contribution to viability-based sustainability *analysis*. It is already more than most programmes in this area have. Calling it a general theory is the only part that is not serious.

---

## 9. One-sentence specification

The work becomes a general mathematical theory of viability-based sustainability judgments when, and only when: the language is complete for its sentences; every named object is produced or refuted by a theorem; every Part-M mechanism is classified by extra structure, not merely refuted by a pair; composition includes nonlinear contracts and joint feasibility or officially excludes them; conservation produces kernels; one real module is admitted with numbers; and the literature can find the theorems. Until those are written, keep the name you have earned — a typed viability calculus with obstruction theorems — and do not spend another page defending a title.
