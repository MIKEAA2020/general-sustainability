### Where the DOIs are found (the how-to, for any future reference round)

1. **Crossref** — the registration agency for all 23 of the above. Two doors:
   the human search UI `https://search.crossref.org/` (paste the article
   title; the DOI appears in each hit), and the REST API
   `https://api.crossref.org/works?query.bibliographic=<title+authors>` (what
   this round used; returns full metadata: DOI, title, authors, volume, pages,
   year — machine-checkable against the manuscript's entry).
2. **doi.org** — resolves any DOI string to the publisher's page
   (`https://doi.org/10.1038/287017a0` → the Nature page). If you have a DOI
   and want to verify it, this is the door.
3. **The publisher page itself** — Nature, Springer, Elsevier/ScienceDirect,
   Wiley, ACM, INFORMS each print the DOI on the article page (usually under
   the title or in the "Cite this article" block).
4. **Google Scholar** — the "Cite ▸" dialog shows the DOI in the BibTeX entry.
5. **JSTOR** (for the two `10.2307/…` retro DOIs) — the item page
   (e.g. `jstor.org/stable/1881734` for Ezekiel 1938) displays
   "https://doi.org/10.2307/1881734" in its citation block.
6. **Where they already sit in the repo**: the 7 pinned DOIs are **in the
   manuscript** — the References block of
