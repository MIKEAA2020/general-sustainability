**Method.** (1) Corpus = the agent's version chains in the rewrites folder (9 papers, 21 pairs; the unversioned originals and the P3 "reconstructed" files are rebuilds of user-saved copies and are outside the corpus; the corpus is listed below). (2) Each pair was diffed at sentence level (table rows kept as atomic units; ~3,900 units across the corpus) with `difflib` (autojunk off); a unit present in an earlier version and absent from the latest is a candidate. (3) Candidates were machine-classified by trigram content-survival against the latest version, then every GONE/PARTIAL item was read by hand against the latest text; a structural pass compared the numbered results (Theorem/Proposition/Lemma/Corollary/Example) version by version.
