## What the six additional routes actually yielded

1. **Dependence/correlation sensitivity.** For two *hypothetical Gaussian* error channels with known marginal scales `σ₁,σ₂`, mean `μ` and correlation `ρ`, their 10th-percentile *sum* is `q₁₀(ρ)=μ−z₀.₉√(σ₁²+σ₂²+2ρσ₁σ₂)`. A catch `C` has nonnegative **local LRP budget** precisely if `g(LRP)+q₁₀(ρ)−C≥0`; when `σ₁σ₂>0`, the algebraic flip point is `ρ*=[((g(LRP)−C+μ)/z₀.₉)²−σ₁²−σ₂²]/(2σ₁σ₂)`. Using an *illustrative allocation of a single measured residual SD* (`μ=−10.9`, `σ₁=σ₂=114.9/√2` kt), this gives a zero-catch flip at **ρ≈0.204**, and a 5-kt-catch flip at **ρ≈0.130**. At ρ=0 the hypothetical Gaussian q10 is **−158.15 kt**, already quite different from the empirical q10 **−80.87 kt**. These numerical thresholds **are not estimated channel correlations or calibrated future bounds**: neither the component scales, Gaussian tails nor independence are established. Correlation alone, without a joint distribution or tail bounds, cannot give a quantile or a worst-case defect. [Equations and full grid](content_audit/revision_pair_cod_2026-10-02/MODEL_CLASS_EXTENDED.json).

2. **ACI and an EnbPI-style second method.** On the Edwards 1934–1990 fixed affine fit (56 transitions) and annual 1991–2023 retrospective errors (33 transitions), a rolling-score Gibbs–Candès-style ACI update at predeclared `γ=.005,.01,.05,.1` covered **30/33, 30/33, 31/33, 31/33** respectively; the last two required **8 and 18 infinite-width predictions**. With `γ=.005`, **22/31** rolling three-year windows had no miss, a retrospective diagnostic rather than a stated 95% simultaneous guarantee. A 300-model, five-year-block bootstrap OOB ensemble with an online rolling score pool (**EnbPI-style**, not a claim to reproduce every theorem/implementation detail of EnbPI) covered **31/33** individual years, with **25/31** three-year windows containing no miss and median finite radius **16.02 ft**; 2019 was missed by the small-γ ACI and the EnbPI-style method. These intervals score *realized* annual recharge and pumping covariates, which are unavailable for ex-ante control without forecasts. The training fit's in-sample residuals also initialize ACI, and neither method has a demonstrated 95% pathwise/physical guarantee. Gibbs and Candès' arbitrary-shift theorem concerns **long-run realized coverage frequency**, not finite-time conditional or simultaneous coverage [3]. [Code](content_audit/revision_pair_cod_2026-10-02/edwards_adaptive_and_epsilon.py) · [results](content_audit/revision_pair_cod_2026-10-02/EDW_ADAPTIVE_AND_EPSILON.json). No ACI radius was inserted as a *sure* `ε` in the paper's robust recursion.

3. **Moment / partial-identification exercise.** The one displayed source-year least-squares moment `E[X_K(ΔS+C−rX_K)]=0`, `X_K=S(1−S/K)`, identifies **a curve** `r(K)=E[X_K(ΔS+C)]/E[X_K²]`, not both parameters; measurement error can invalidate the moment. On the archived K grid, the **conditional 95% profile-F likelihood-cut** (not assumption-free partial identification) admits `K=1500…50,000` kt, fixed-q10 `C*≈67.90…95.19` kt, or **≈62.87…96.49 kt** if q10 is *recomputed at each profiled fit*. This narrow-looking functional profile is conditional on the map, likelihood/tolerance cut and convention; it cannot erase the negative bootstrap tail. [Computation](content_audit/revision_pair_cod_2026-10-02/model_class_extended.py).

4. **Failure boundary and region intersection.** With *fixed registered q10* `−80.86978` kt and `K>LRP`, the exact local boundary is `r_crit(K;C)=(C+80.86978)/[884.6(1−884.6/K)]`; the LRP budget fails for `r<r_crit`. A best-fitting zero-budget model is `K=5000`, `r≈0.11107`, SSE/SSEmin **1.24560**; for 5-kt BAU it is `r≈0.11794`, ratio **1.21951**. Both are within an *approximate conditional two-parameter Gaussian F 95%* ratio cut **1.31303** and a Wilks-LR approximate cut **1.28357**, including the expansive `K≥2LRP` domain. These are **not validated 95% coverage regions**: the nonlinear boundary-pinned fit and time-series/measurement errors violate easy textbook calibration. If q10 is *refit from the same residuals at every candidate*, the gridded minimum local `C*` under the F cut is **+14.38 kt**, rather than negative. This is a real floor-construction sensitivity, but the refitted floor is not an independently validated process shock. [Parameter-space figure](content_audit/revision_pair_cod_2026-10-02/COD_FAILING_SET.png) · [data and assumptions](content_audit/revision_pair_cod_2026-10-02/MODEL_CLASS_EXTENDED.json).

5. **Bayesian posterior, with its proper name.** Under a **stated but unvalidated** independent zero-mean Gaussian increment likelihood, Jeffreys variance prior `p(σ²)∝1/σ²`, and uniform `r∈[.001,2]`, `K∈[max(training SSB)+10,5000]`, a 700×2100 grid gives posterior mass **≈1.96%** on fixed-floor `C*≤0`. Its **95% highest-posterior-density *credible* region** *includes* a failing model (minimum `C*≈−1.89` kt). A log-uniform `r,K` prior on the same box moves negative-budget mass to **≈6.15%** and the HPD minimum to **≈−80.81** kt. If the residual q10 is recomputed on every model, the minimum across the uniform-prior HPD cells instead is **≈+23.23 kt**. Posterior credible regions are not frequentist confidence sets; all these outputs inherit their prior, likelihood and floor assumptions and do **not** prove that the true dynamics lie in the region. The parameter-space figure makes the fixed-floor failure intersection visible.

6. **Fitted-map bound versus imposed defect.** [Figure](content_audit/revision_pair_cod_2026-10-02/EDW_EPSILON_CURVE.png) and [CSV](content_audit/revision_pair_cod_2026-10-02/EDW_EPSILON_CURVE.csv) rerun the direct feedback interval recursion at the **618-ft safe threshold, UC-min recharge**. The largest assumed uniform additive `ε` with a nonempty **three-year** kernel is approximately **16.01 ft** BAU, **16.82** flat-90%, **16.71** Stage I, and **17.23** cascade (floating-point bisection). The analogous four-year edges are approximately **9.91, 10.71, 10.94, 11.60 ft**. The training maximum `15.41 ft` sits below the three-year limits but above each four-year limit; the observed out-of-sample maximum `21.81 ft` exceeds every listed three-year limit. This identifies *conditional model tolerance*, not a calibrated future defect or real-world protection probability. It also does not imply that the true error is bounded by any plotted `ε`.

## Scoping the 2J3KL error-budget literature

The 2025 DFO/CSAS research assessment documents a stratified trawl-survey index, separate acoustic/sentinel/tagging data, and an integrated xteNCAM with observation equations and process errors [1]. But the trawl index is **not a direct estimate of stock size**; catchability, survey-area coverage and the biomass estimate require a model [1]. It explicitly says the **magnitude and temporal variation of biases in total catch reporting have not been quantified** for Northern cod; its historical catch multipliers are stakeholder-bounded *assumptions*—for example lower bound 1.1× reported catch in all four periods, and upper bound 1.5× in 1978–91 or 2× in 1992–2005—not independent measurement-error standard deviations [1]. The same assessment's tag-based 2023 recreational catch estimate is **2.2±0.63 kt**, but its coverage and assumptions do not supply an independent annual catch-error budget for the 1983–2007 Schaefer fit [1]. The 2019 DFO advice independently flags uncertain catch bounds and gaps in recreational landing estimates [2].

**Scoping verdict:** relevant separate *data streams and modelled variance components exist*, so more work could build a new joint state-space analysis; this search did **not** find ready-to-insert, independent, source-year- and stock-matched additive kt bounds for catch, NCAM SSB observation error, process variation and Schaefer discrepancy. These data streams are combined in NCAM, so calling their errors independent in the Schaefer residual would be an inference requiring further justification. The 2025 report concerns a later assessment specification; its multipliers cannot simply be pasted into the registered 1983–2007 residual model. This is a scoped finding, **not a proof that no such estimates exist anywhere**.

## Submission/release boundary

Paper09 v38 reports its **conditional fragility** as a contribution, without importing illustrative correlation thresholds, approximate posterior coverage, or retrospective ACI intervals as validated protections. Paper03's pair theorem still needs external mathematical scrutiny; posting a preprint is a separate decision and has **not** been done. No DOI or portal record was created. A valid robust-within-class physical claim would additionally require a justifiable class containing the physical plant, a non-circular defect/error budget, and a nonvacuous all-model recursion.
