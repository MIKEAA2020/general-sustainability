**Coverage boundary:** A static import-graph check of the preserved sources (`evidence/aggregate_import_inventory.txt`) finds 57 of the 60 `Formalizations/` modules imported transitively by the default target. Three historical files are outside that closure: `EBC_ExactBelief.lean`, `P1_AssessmentSeparation.lean`, `P1_AssessmentSeparation_v4.lean`. Their bytes are in the archive, but this run did not compile or footprint-check them individually. The active aggregate already fails the strict axiom gate, independently of this additional coverage limit.

The 10 distinct axiom names all have the `Formalizations.P3.*._native.native_decide.ax_*` form. They arise from **four** source uses: one in `P3_Binomial.lean` (`choose_six_three`) and three in `P3_SupportValue.lean` (the `wmBelief` nonnegativity proof, `rat_half_ne_zero`, `wmBelief_total`). They propagate into derived P3 support/value theorems. They are **not** textual `axiom` declarations or `sorryAx`, and they are not erased by a green build or by the 21 clean named checks. Whether/how to replace the native-evaluation proof steps needs a separate source-specific proof repair and full re-audit. A global acceptance rule was kept report-only first, so the finding cannot be silently suppressed by whitelisting this mechanism.

The 21 named declarations are representative of manuscript-linked formalizations, not proof that every prose claim is formalized. In particular the computational-certification manuscript's unformalized claims remain unformalized. No conclusion about the corrected paper prose follows solely from the build.
