## What changed in substance

1. **From a report of counts to an article organised around theorems.** v4 listed fourteen results in the order the computations were run. v5 is organised around six theorems that the computations now *test*: the monotonicity theorem along the observation, protocol and horizon axes (Thm 3.1), the closed forms of the no-repeat kernels (Thm 4.3), statewise factoring of decentralised unanimity (Thm 5.2), one-step adequacy as a graph property with its Bell-number count (Thm 6.2), dynamic = one-step adequacy for the whole viable set (Thm 6.3) and the monitoring-design census (Thm 6.4). Every count of v4 is kept, but each is now an instance, a strictness witness or a boundary of one of these statements.
2. **The audit frame is made explicit (new Section 2).** A fibrewise belief game with three independent axes — observation map, protocol (free / shared ledger / private register / optimistic ledger) and horizon — replaces v4's four named "policy classes". The kernel is the greatest fixed point of one operator Φ; down-closure and finite stabilisation are proved once (Lemma 2.5) instead of being observed per class.
3. **The census is extended from the 36 pairs to all 511 beliefs** (Theorem 4.1, Table 1; the full universe census is new). The four shared-ledger kernels are 111, 143, 119 and 255; v4's pair counts 24/26/25/28 are their size-2 slices (checked). New: the join of the two middle classes misses the full kernel by exactly 104 beliefs with four minimal members; the maximal beliefs of every kernel (SI Table S2.3).
4. **Closed forms** (Theorem 4.3, new). The no-repeat protocol with two instruments is alternation (Lemma 4.2); the shared-ledger kernel depends on the observation only through the first step and the private-register kernel is a phase-coherence condition. Both closed forms are checked against the game engine on all 511 beliefs (checks A3–A4). Corollary 4.4 explains three v4 facts that were previously bare numbers: the ledger institutional kernel is the blind kernel (111), the private institutional kernel equals the aggregate kernel setwise, and the optimistic ledger regains 12|31 and 13|21.
5. **The register convention is now a stated axis, not a footnote.** v4 reported the fixed-thread conventions (25, 26, 26, 28) as alternatives; v5 proves why they are artefacts (they quantify over instruments issued instead of a fixed thread) and moves them to SI §S2. The idle instrument's irrelevance (check K12) is stated once.
6. **Stationary policies** (Proposition 4.5, new content). The 32 memoryless aggregate policies sustain nothing, the 32 768 one-period-memory policies sustain exactly the aggregate kernel, and so do the 32 alternation policies; the 512 memoryless full-observation policies realise the full kernel. The 2^27 full-information memory enumeration is not attempted and is said so.
7. **Decentralised unanimity** (Section 5). The statewise-factoring theorem is new (v4 computed the orbits directly); the factored and the direct computations agree on all 511 beliefs (check D2). The 30-belief universe is now explained as 2·15 (subsets of the two maximal beliefs); the authority census (Proposition 5.3(c)) keeps v4's 12/4/4/12 and 0 counts.
8. **Monitoring design** (Section 6). The incompatibility graph K_{2,2} and the Bell-number count 151+372+372+799 = 1 694 are new; the census of all 4 140 partitions (16 coarsest designs, 74 distinct kernels, 18 sizes, minimum 111 for exactly the 52 partitions merging the four critical states, no adequate design under the shared ledger, maximum 119) replaces v4's qualitative statements. The four-state target of v4 (15 partitions, 7 adequate, two coarsest designs, Helly 2/3) is kept as Proposition 6.1 with the inclusion–exclusion count made explicit.
9. **Hidden regimes and review timing** (Section 7). v4's 93-cell grid is retained; the worst-damage recursion is now stated as a max-plus product (Proposition 7.3), the alternation damage closed form ⌈T/2⌉+⌊T/2⌋/2 is proved and checked, and the three boundary cells where the strict form of the timing identity fails are explained (Corollary 7.4), together with the 21-cell failure of the floored form.
10. **Continuous fibre** (Section 8). Crossover 27/5, margin law (Y−27/5)/10 and radius 3/50 are kept; the distinction between the dual-weighted deficiency (3/50) and the raw cap-sum deficiency (3/25) — conflated in v4 — is now explicit. The two-floor saddle (−1/10 at u = 1/2), the nonconvex gap (−1 vs 0), the feasible contrast (+1/10 at u = 3/10) and the Helly-tight triple are recomputed with the primal in the correct orientation (max_u min_j).
11. **Design rules** (Section 9) rewritten as seven rules, each pointing to the theorem or count that supports it.
12. **Verification** (Section 10, SI §S10). New exact core `work/ws_core.py` (bitmask kernels, closed forms, policy enumerations, law pairs, partition census, max-plus recursion, exact rationals) replaces the v17/v4 scripts; `run_all.py` writes `results.json` (69 s) from which every table and figure is generated; the verifier evaluates 64 named checks (v4: 14 results re-stated, no independent implementations) and compares the numbers quoted in both PDFs. Lean: the three axes of Theorem 3.1 cite the repository modules `WS_WorkedSystems` / `WS_WorkedSystems_v2` (no new Lean is claimed).
