### 05: the substantive constructive suggestion, corrected

- **M/D:** The proposed exact **drift-feasibility LP** is worth investigating for *some finite starting slack*: if the finite set of blind actions has rational per-cell drift vectors, an indefinitely surviving blind sequence gives a limiting frequency vector `p` with each required cell's mean drift nonnegative (Cesàro subsequence). Conversely, a **rational** feasible `p` yields a finite periodic action word; choose sufficiently large initial stock to cover its finite first-cycle dip, and nonnegative cycle totals prevent later loss. Because rational linear inequalities have a rational feasible point when nonempty, this gives an exact equivalence **for existence from some finite starting stock** under the stated deterministic constant-drift assumptions. It does **not** immediately give an initial-grid `z₀` threshold, settle observation-feedback, or validate a claim that all 10,752 m=6 maximal clique cases have been computed.
