### Reject or narrow—the new reviewers also disagree with the source or with one another

1. **“Supplement pp. 8–9 consist of repeated `1`s; recourse proof is missing.” False.** Both pages render continuous, legible prose and mathematics; extracted **762 and 649 words** respectively. Supplement p. 8 completes the oracle-recourse support-function argument and p. 9 begins Farkas/ladder discussions. The repeated characters arose in a reviewer extraction, **not** in the deposited PDF. The actual recourse *hypotheses* and computability still warrant J4.
2. **“Replace `π/2` with `κ/2`,” “no upper bounds in A.2,” “hat S needs to replace dot S,” “V_safe/Y_safe clash,” “Velivor/Sentag/\Game”** are false **for actual v67 source/PDF**. A.2 already has `κ/2` and `[C_i/2,\bar S_i]`; both documents use `\mathcal Y_{safe}`; `\hat S` is the *biased reading* and `\dot S` the *state derivative*. Do not introduce a new error by implementing OCR artifacts. Defining finite `\bar S_i` and scope is still useful.
3. **“Closed graph + compact values gives USC, and LSC of D implies LSC of D_ε.”** Neither assertion is unconditional as stated. Closed graph requires local boundedness to give the usual USC conclusion; more importantly, LSC **does not pass through an arbitrary weak-inequality slice**. Elementary counterexample: `D(z)=[−1,1]` constant and `g(z,d)=−1+zd`. Then `inf_d g(z,d)≤−1` for all `z`, but `D₁(0)=[−1,1]` and `D₁(z>0)=[−1,0]`, so the point `d=1` cannot be approximated from positive `z`. This can be embedded as `q(s,z)=s`, `f_s=−1+zd`. **This refutes the audit's proposed inheritance step**, not the possibility of a different valid solution-existence proof for the theorem.
4. **“Helly gives *at most* `m+1` and therefore main Proposition 4's exactly `m+1` is false.”** The conventional sharp wording is “at most `m+1` **distinct** states.” The printed main statement does not require `x_i` to be distinct; a shorter witness can be **padded by repetitions**, so the exactly-`m+1` display is not a counterexample to the theorem. Improve wording for clarity, but do not class it with false proofs.
5. **“Ladder emptiness implications inverted.” False; fixed-duration policy conclusion qualified.** For `A_tube⊆R^B_V⊆U^B`, emptiness of **U** does imply emptiness of **R** and **tube**. The *last* arrow to viability needs held-class typing; supplement proof's **constant disturbance** `d∈D(x₀)` need not remain admissible when `D(x)` varies. A blanket suggestion that emptiness for all `Δ>0` proves nonviability for every unrestricted **time-varying** policy also needs its own quantified trajectory argument. See J2.
6. **“LP heading truncated to `3.3)`,” “Table 1 unreadable,” “figures S1–S3 collide arithmetically with main Figures 1–3.”** The heading on rendered **p. 14** is complete, just not theorem-numbered; the table on **p. 12** is visibly legible (small fractions). Supplement has its own Figure 1/Table 1 and S1–S3; overlapping **bare numbers between two PDFs** can confuse citations, but the PDFs do not have an internal floating-number corruption. Adding consistent supplementary prefixes is a good editorial choice; not a mathematical bug.
7. **Barrier-certificate inference in S2 p. 13 needs a stronger qualification than the reviewers propose.** A standard mathematical barrier *may depend on the physical state* even when the **implemented controller** is output-based; it need not be constant on an observation fibre. If the discussion deliberately restricts to **observation-only barrier functions**, name that subclass and distinguish a failed search within it from nonexistence of state-dependent barrier proofs. Likewise, estimation-space “common controls” and “no loss of value” need primary-source checking; a survey citation alone does not verify a particular no-loss formulation.
8. **A.2 LaSalle criticism is partly right, partly wrong.** It is valid to seek the **largest invariant subset** of `{Wdot=0}` instead of writing an impossible limit `p*` followed by an informal expansion. It is **not** correct to say that a nonempty ω-limit set was actually shown to equal `{p*}` as a realizable orbit—the argument is *by contradiction*. Compactness is encoded by a box with upper symbols, but their finiteness and control-class scope should be explicit. The conclusion is conditional on the declared compact/autonomous model, not refuted by the contradictory intermediate supposition.
9. **Do not auto-renumber hypotheses or drop duplicated theorem statements.** The independent *environment numbers* are confusing, but `(H2.3)/(H5.2)` refer to the *same hypotheses* in both texts and should stay cross-identifiable. The auxiliary duplicated results should be **aligned or removed with cross-reference validation**, not silently deleted if main text still points to them. S1/S2/S3/S4 page-map changes after a reorganization require a new compile and review.
