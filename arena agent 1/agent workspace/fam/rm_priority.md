# Priority Roadmap Toward a General Theory of Sustainability

## Governing principle

Prioritize work that increases unification, formal coherence, nontrivial explanatory power, and empirical contact. Choose the optimal number of articles and lean toward the minimum when the case is genuinely doubtful. Do not spend equal effort repairing every historical draft. Where a later source supplies a stronger canonical result, preserve the earlier source but integrate only its unique content. Numbered companion files, reading order, and technical volume do not by themselves justify separate papers.

## New A018–A025 coordinated packet

The submitted Paper-I–VIII sequence is one applied intellectual package, not eight publications. Corrected A018 now incorporates the finite-donor, protective-channel, first-passage, and validation-status bridges; corrected stage, spatial, yield-gap, and interval material is assigned to one technical/computational supplement. This package should replace or merge the existing A012 publication identity rather than add to the assured count. A spatial companion remains only a conditional possibility if corrected A023 acquires a genuinely independent PDE/RFDE theorem and audience. A025 is a technical validation note within the supplement, not an independent paper.

## Priority 3 — Develop Operator II as a genuine transformation theory

### Objective

Move beyond a placeholder architecture graph to a formal theory of architecture-changing sustainability.

### Why third

Transformation is one of the project’s most distinctive claims and the main element not already supplied by classical viability theory.

### Required work

- define the hybrid architecture state space and meta-policy class;
- formalize cross-architecture translation of stocks, obligations, beliefs, identities, and liabilities;
- define admissible transition relaxations and non-relaxable constraints;
- connect emergency recovery inside an architecture to transformation between architectures;
- characterize transformability, impossibility within a registered architecture class, and option loss;
- construct at least one complete toy theorem/example.

### Deliverable

A restricted but nontrivial transformation theorem or exact worked model, not only a definition.

## Priority 5 — Ground the architecture in one complete domain case

### Objective

Take one domain from boundary definition through data, model comparison, viability/recovery analysis, and prospective falsification.

### Why fifth

One complete application is more valuable than several uncalibrated module templates. It tests whether the architecture produces new diagnoses or decisions.

### Domain sequence

- **Groundwater is the primary end-to-end test**, conditional on a named basin passing the boundary, data, threshold, institutional-record, implementable-policy, and held-out-validation readiness gate.
- **Phosphorus/catchment is the secondary accounting/composition specialization**, emphasizing material accounting, trade displacement, multi-scale interfaces, substitution, and receiving-water safety.

### Contingency rule

If no groundwater basin passes the readiness gate, phosphorus may proceed first operationally. It must then be reported as the cleaner accounting/composition test rather than as a complete test of the architecture’s epistemic and institutional layers. Do not develop both simultaneously unless resources justify it.

### Required work

- apply Article 007’s corrected module-admission standard;
- resolve the selected domain’s critical verification queue;
- preregister H0/H1/H2 comparator models;
- estimate uncertainty and interface flows;
- compute or approximate a kernel, capture basin, obstruction, or decision margin;
- compare with simpler models.

### New bounded case evidence from A014–A016

A014 supplies a conditional Northern-cod scalar-model discrimination result and a useful crash/non-recovery split. A016 proposes a linked community/distributive series. Neither is presently a complete end-to-end architecture test: A014 requires autonomy, causal-status, uncertainty, and reproducibility corrections; A016 requires population matching, a 2J3KL geographic crosswalk, current/historical poverty-vintage discipline, and an archived data pipeline. Their corrected material should strengthen A011 or a flagship case box without displacing the groundwater-first decision.

### Deliverable

One auditable end-to-end case capable of surprising or falsifying the architecture.

## Priority 6 — Rebuild institutional and distributive operationalization

### Objective

Develop valid measurable institutional and distributional modules without reusing the flawed scalar indices in Articles 008–009.

### Why later

These dimensions are essential, but the current drafts require redesign rather than incremental repair. Building them before the formal spine and one empirical case would risk premature metric construction.

### Institutional direction

Use a vector of observation, latency, authority, implementation, enforcement, mandate load, compliance, learning, and time-to-exit margins. Treat monetary intensity only as a possible covariate.

### Distributive direction

Use typed normalized component margins, exact nonsmooth conjunction or conservative smooth inner certificates, validated indicators, correct pooled distributions, and explicit intergenerational transitions.

### Deliverable

Validated modules or a distinct empirical/methods paper only if independent results merit publication.
