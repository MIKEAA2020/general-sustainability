v37: README v13 + audit v37 - prop:bands closed, prop:ladder scoped

prop:bands is closed in all four clauses. The last open piece was the
"nothing larger" half at z0 >= 1.1; cor_hamming_m4 is stated at z0 = 1
(it bounds accumulated drift FROM ZERO) and does not transfer to a
higher start, so the pair-sum bound was re-derived with z0 as a
parameter.

  EBC_Classification_v2: pairIpSum_lower_of_survive_z0 (2(1-z0) of
  combined drift, so 5*((1-z0)+(1-z0)+L) <= pairIpSum), and
  pairIpSum_upper_far (pairIpSum <= L*4 at h >= 2, m = 4).
  EBC_Classification_v3: far_pair_horizon_bound -- |us| <= 10*(z0 - 1).

At z0 = 1 that forces L = 0, which is how cor_hamming_m4 goes through;
at z0 = 1.1 it gives L <= 1, so a distance-2 pair survives at most one
step and cannot appear in any set surviving at ALL horizons - the
classification's actual quantifier. For larger z0 the horizon is merely
capped, which is equally fatal to survival at every horizon.

The counts 16 and 32 stay with the verifier, by directive.

prop:ladder is analysed and scoped but NOT started. Its proof has three
inputs: the antichain value formula (P3 slot, proved -
P3_FreezeValue.VfamAdm_prune_eq), heredity of survivability (proved here
as survivesSet_mono), and prop:bands (proved). With those, a 2^j-cell
subcube has value 1/2^j at the edge and 2/2^j above it, which is the
ladder. Instance-level and left to the verifier: the ten numbers, the 16
cells, the 2^j subcube sizes, and the claim that supports after 0..4
probes are subcubes with j = 4,3,2,1,0 - the paper says the script
recomputes all ten values by exhaustive enumeration.

The remaining work is a cross-paper bridge rather than a new idea: the
P3 slot and the EBC slot use different notions of survivable set (P3's
class-restricted SfamAdm family versus EBC's drift-and-floor
survivesTo), and a probe needs modelling as a restriction of the index
set. That bridge is the work; the EBC mathematics is done.
