## Findings (2, both minor; proposed for a v72 fix round)

**F1 — supp's selector-principle proof header points at the wrong main-text section.**
Supp source line 321 (paginated supp p6): "**Complete proof of the selector principle (main text, Section 8).**" The proof that follows proves Proposition 1's clauses (i) admissibility, (ii) safety, (iii) recursive viability, plus the induction over the four response correspondences — i.e. the basic selector principle, **stated as Proposition 1 at the end of main Section 2.4** (source line 544; paginated main p7, immediately before the §3 heading), which is also where the main's own textual pointer sends the reader (main p3: "a single selector principle (Proposition 1, Section 2.4)"). Main Section 8 ("The Selector Principle, Dual Certificates, and Monitoring Adequacy") *records the typed consolidation* (Γfin/Γhold, scope of witnesses, exact and open cases, monitoring adequacy) and never restates or proves the basic principle. All five sibling proof/discussion headers in the supp point at the **statement's** location — "(main article, Section 3.2)", "(main text, Section 3; Proposition 3 of the main article)", "(main text, Section 3.7)", "(Section 3.3)", "(main article, Section 7)", "(main text, Section 7)" — so this header is the single inconsistent pointer (stable since at least v63; longevity is no defense, cf. the v71 "Proposition 4" fix). *Proposed v72 fix:* "(main text, Section 2.4; Proposition 1 of the main article)" — matching the sparse-witness header's dual-pointer style.

**F2 — Proposition 1 is the unique proof-bearing statement with no locatable proof in the main.**
The main's proof-localization census (all 8 theorems, 11 propositions, 1 corollary): 19 of 20 carry an in-place proof or sketch; **Proposition 1 — the organizing claim of the calculus — has neither**, and no main→supp pointer supplies its location (verified: no "Supplementary" mention near it, and the Organization's supp-collection clauses cover only "the complete proofs of the Section 3 certificates", which does not include §2.4's closing statement; the supp's S1 does carry its complete proof). The Organization's blanket sentence "Brief sketches and short proofs of the main-text statements are given in place" is therefore inaccurate for exactly this one statement, and a main-text reader who wants the calculus's organizing claim proved has no pointer. *Proposed v72 fix:* one sentence after Proposition 1, e.g. "The complete proof, with the induction over the four response correspondences, is collected in the Supplementary Material (S1)." (mirrors the main's existing pointer style; F1's header fix lands on the supp side of the same seam).
