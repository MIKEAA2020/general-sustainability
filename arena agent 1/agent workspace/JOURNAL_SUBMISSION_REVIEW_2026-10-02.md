## Paper 08: claim boundary

The composite abstract now states the continuous-feedback characteristic-spectrum result, the exact sampled-review monodromy comparison, and their empirical limits together. The two originally authored part abstracts have been retained as substantive section lead-ins, rather than silently removed. Part I and Part II share one abstract and one keywords list. The conclusion distinguishes continuous response lag $\tau$ from review interval $T_r$ and does not transfer a threshold or policy prescription between them.

**What is established, at the declared tier:** algebraic characteristic-equation analysis, interval enclosures of the specified local imaginary-root/spectral crossings, a protective-channel small-gain stability result, and exact sample-and-hold linear monodromy calculations for the stated model and parameters. A parameter-sensitive illustrative review crossing near 6.5 years and numerical screens are reported without universal-policy or general empirical-cycle claims. The local operator contrast does not use periodic-orbit-fold existence as a premise.

**What is not established:** the Krawczyk enclosures are for finite-collocation Moore–Spence turning candidates. They are not an off-grid residual bound or a validated lift to folds of the continuous delay differential equation. Finite-collocation nondegeneracy and plotted Floquet/basin transitions do not repair that gap. The continuous-DDE fold result therefore remains open; neither a global continuous-delay five-regime theorem nor a rigorously certified nonlinear Hopf bifurcation is inferred from the local spectral enclosures. Proving the continuous fold would strengthen global topology, not the narrower operator comparison. Since the fold is not load-bearing for that principal claim, no speculative time-boxed proof has been substituted for the missing continuum estimates.

The delay supplement's S7 is retained as a thematic evidence-tier synopsis. It is **not** described as an exhaustive statement-by-statement inventory. The separate sampled-review supplement retains its own S1 inventory. These distinct roles are made explicit rather than conflated.

**Reviewer-facing recommendation:** Submit, if desired, with the present *local spectral/operator-comparison* framing and conditional numerical-topology language; do **not** sell the five-regime portrait as a proved continuous-DDE fold result. A future continuous fold theorem needs rigorous off-grid residual control, a continuous-delay lift and the relevant infinite-dimensional nondegeneracy argument. The author should make the final journal-specific choices and verify the model's scientific assumptions.
