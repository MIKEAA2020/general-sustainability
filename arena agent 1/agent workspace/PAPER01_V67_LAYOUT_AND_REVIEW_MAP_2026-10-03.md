### Implement or resolve **before scientific sign-off** — modified where necessary

1. **Priority M1 — adverse selection / solution existence / original-versus-relaxed system.** Audit §1.2 and §1.5 remain live. Article §2.1–2.2 **p. 4**, §3.2 Theorem **2** and Proposition **2**, **pp. 7–8**, §3.5 Theorem **5** and Remark **4**, **pp. 10–11**, and supplement S1 **pp. 1–4, 18–19** need a theorem-by-theorem proof and hypothesis check. Closed graph alone does not supply an adverse branch near a given disturbance. Lower semicontinuity of `D` does not, without more, give a closed-loop solution for the *negative-drift subcorrespondence*. Nonviability in a convexified **adversary** inclusion does not immediately prove nonviability in the original game; any approximation route requires its own theorem, regularity, policy/trajectory topology and strict-margin argument. Prefer explicit existence of an original-system adverse solution or a separately proved relaxation-transfer result; do **not** claim one follows merely from LSC or Marchaud labels. Reconcile §3.5's “unconditional” with §6.5(v)'s “heuristic” limitation. Also review the current Theorem 5 phrase “strict violation ... by every time strictly larger than q0/epsilon” alongside its `inf{t:q<0}≤q0/epsilon`: these are different statements and should not be compressed to strict violation *at* the threshold.
2. **Priority M2 — tangent cones, selector principle, beliefs and hierarchy.** Audit §§1.4–1.5 and §6 notes: define `A_tube(B,Δ)` with `a∈U^B(B)` before claiming the ladder inclusion; qualifying gradients/active-constraint formula at corners needs constraint qualification or a true tangent cone; a measurable boundary selector alone is not a blanket robust invariance theorem. Assess §2.1 hierarchy's state-feedback `RViab` versus observation-history/nonanticipative policy logic and §2.3's unsupported equivalence of record-based and set-based policies. Clarify Theorem 2's dwell-time sentence: absent dwell time, the *held-action* tube statement does not automatically replace instantaneous tangency. §3.2 Proposition **2**'s uniform margin should either deliver a **uniform quantitative** conclusion with a proved horizon or be dropped; fixed-action negativity alone already gives the qualitative ladder. The audit's criticism of a weak-inequality `Δ↓0` limiting claim also requires review, not a cosmetic edit. Resolve in main article **pp. 4–8, 16–17** and supplement **pp. 1–3, 6, 17–19**.
3. **Priority M3 — two-phase revelation and LP/dual calculations.** Audit §§1.7–1.8, §6 and structural item 7: Proposition **9**, §7 **p. 17**, uses full-information `RViab` after “exact branch revelation”; if within-window disturbances leave the physical state uncertain, reveal the *state*, exclude such disturbances, or replace the terminal condition with a post-reveal **belief** kernel and prove it. Its (ii) already implies (i); simplify only after verifying model conventions. Theorem **4**, §3.3 **p. 9**, still writes `max_w F E_j A^ℓ w`; derive rowwise `F A_j^ℓ E_j w` using the stated dynamics and audit indexing. The discrete nonconvex hold-class gap should be described as a policy-class/relaxation comparison, not advertised as LP failure. Proposition **6** §3.7 **pp. 11–12**, including supplement's support-function proof **pp. 7–11**, needs branch/record equivalence and convolution-kernel sign verification (the sign can matter for asymmetric `U`).
4. **Priority M4 — edge conditions and examples.** Remark **4**, §3.5 **p. 10**, currently defines `α` on `(0,a]` yet states a **strict exit** integral from zero; finite arrival at zero does not alone yield a strict negative value. Provide continuation/negative drift through `q=0`, or limit the conclusion to contact. Remark **1**, §3.2 **p. 7**, incorrectly says normalization itself *maximizes* the robust tolerance; the ratio is scale invariant and a best multiplier **direction** requires optimization. Verified approximate multipliers must actually be nonnegative. Recheck the §3.4 example assumptions on `U`, branch sizes, indexing and epsilon **pp. 9–10**, and Remark **5**, §4.2 **p. 14**, for admissible `g(S+b)`/`u` range; avoid treating an uncorrected biased policy as an intrinsic information impossibility. These are worth a small targeted correction **after** checking the proof dependencies.
5. **Priority M5 — independent formal and empirical scope audit.** §3.9 Lean mapping **pp. 12–13** is a selected discrete declaration map, not a formalization of Theorems 3/7 or Table 4. Rebuild pinned Lean if making formal claims; compare declaration statements to the claimed paper theorems; retain generated-axiom disclosure. Repeat Table 3/Figure 3 and Table 4 checks under their exact policy classes and verify bibliography against **primary sources**. The auditor's arithmetic endorsements in §7 are valuable *targets to reproduce*, not a substitute for review. No DOI may be fabricated.
