## 03: the all-control pair proof is now closed at the stated horizon

For a pair of hidden initial branches at the paper's fixed horizon **T=6/5**, give its two *critical position facets* weights `(3/8,5/8)` for pairs `{1,2}` and `{1,3}`, or `(1/2,1/2)` for `{2,3}`. The pooled normal is respectively `(0,1/2)`, `(0,−1/2)`, or `(−3/5,0)`; its support over the six-vertex control hexagon is **t=2/5** or **t=3/5**. At a common facet time `s*=1+(1−t)τ≤T`, integration of the pre-information common control and separately optimized post-information controls gives the exact two-label dual margin

`Γ_pair(τ)=−7/50+(1−t)τ−t(1−t)τ²/2`.

A *positive* margin excludes **every measurable admissible control**, not merely a held or selected braking schedule. Its first roots are `(15−√183)/6≈0.24537512` for `{1,2}`/`{1,3}` and `(10−√58)/6≈0.39737115` for `{2,3}`. At rational delays `1/4` and `2/5` the respective dual margins are exactly **1/400** and **1/1250**, with `s*≤6/5`. Root-neighbourhood positivity plus information-delay monotonicity covers **all** later delays, even where that particular `s*` would exceed the fixed horizon. At the roots the paper's common-blind then individual-braking schedule attains the critical position bound 2. Its other two position facets are **≤18/25<2 throughout** from `n_i·n_j<0` and `h_U(n_i)=1`; each velocity is affine within each control leg, and endpoint evaluation bounds components by 1 (by 4/5 on `{2,3}`), below 6/5. Earlier disclosure can be ignored until the root. Thus the pair thresholds are necessary **and** sufficient for the specified model/horizon, rather than heuristic schedule ceilings.
