## A robust-over-a-set test and why it does not restore protection

[Reproducible finite-set diagnostic](content_audit/revision_pair_cod_2026-10-02/conformal_and_ensemble_diagnostic.py) · [outputs](content_audit/revision_pair_cod_2026-10-02/ROBUST_SCOPE_DIAGNOSTIC.json). Holding the registered q10 residual floor fixed across the **2,000 archived joint `(r,K)` bootstrap refits**, **387** have `C*≤0`; among the **1,457** with `K≥2LRP`, **83** still have `C*≤0`. One expansive-regime sample has `r=0.001`, `K≈4999.82` and `C*≈−80.14 kt`. Thus an LRP self-viability claim with a uniform *positive* harvest budget fails even across that restricted finite ensemble. These are **counterexamples to uniform positivity over that particular finite list**, not a calibrated model confidence region, estimates of the fraction of plausible *physical* worlds, or a proof about all possible policies/horizons. A genuinely justified `M` needs a defensible confidence/identification construction and an independently justified disturbance class; bootstrap draws alone do not supply either.

For Edwards, I also ran a **held-out split-conformal diagnostic**, fitting on 1934–1990, calibrating absolute one-step residuals on 1991–2010 (`n=20`), and evaluating 2011–2023 (`n=13`). Its conventional two-sided 95% split-conformal rank is 20/20, so the finite threshold equals the calibration maximum **21.8106 ft**; all 13 later errors happen to fall below it (largest **19.0468 ft**). But exchangeability of future errors with the calibration residuals is *not established under time-series shift*, and this says nothing about a sure trajectory bound. Even under exchangeability, the elementary Bonferroni route to a **95% three-step** joint guarantee asks for each-step miscoverage ≤`0.05/3`; with only 20 calibration samples its conformal rank is 21/20, hence the finite-sample threshold is **infinite**. Adaptive conformal or EnbPI would require their own dependence/shift assumptions and validated empirical evaluation; neither automatically gives a distribution-free uniform error bound under arbitrary distribution shift. I have **not** substituted this one-step diagnostic into the feedback theorem or asserted empirical protection.

**Scope decisions:** catch error, survey error, process variation and model discrepancy must not be labelled independent without source-specific evidence and a justified combination rule. No such external error budget was built here. Profile functional ranges are conditional on the fitted form and declared likelihood cut, not nonparametric biological identification. Causal policy effects and field reinspection schedules remain outside both manuscripts' warranted claims. For a *nonvacuous* robust-within-class result, justify the class first, distinguish marginal coverage from pathwise safety, and run an all-model recursion on that class; a fractional pass rate through a bootstrap list is only a sensitivity summary.
