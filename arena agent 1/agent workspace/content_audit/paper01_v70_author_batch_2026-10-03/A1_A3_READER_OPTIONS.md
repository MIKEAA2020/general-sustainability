## A1 · Records versus belief selectors

**Location:** article §2.3, selector Proposition 1 in §2.5, and §8; supplementary auxiliary selector near the end of S2. The declared general policy uses the observation record `h_t`, while the proposition writes `a=π(B)` for a cell `B` and transfers a one-review correspondence across reviews.

**Option R (retain general record policies):** Type the action as `a_t=π_t(h_t)∈U^B(B(h_t))` for *each reachable record* `h_t`; give `B(h_t)` as the set of initial/current states compatible with that entire record and the declared model, admissible disturbances, and previous common controls. Define the held-review successor `Post(h_t,a,y)` as a successor **record and cell** (with all original-system continuations/non-vacuity specified). The successor condition must quantify over every admissible next reading and every compatible realization; the continuation may use the extended record. Reformulate the one-review necessity and any claimed sufficiency at histories, and separately prove an implementable nonanticipative policy can be selected at *all* reachable histories (including measurability). Do not infer an infinite-horizon continuous viability result from the finite sampled recursion. Reviewer check: does an action feasible separately at identical cells reached by different records have a common measurable policy continuation, or is record information essential?

**Option B (restrict to belief policies):** Explicitly change the selector proposition and its supplement analogue to the subclass `π_t(h_t)=\barπ_t(B(h_t))`, relative to a specified measurable belief-state space and update. State its conclusions only for that subclass. If equality with the original record-policy kernel is desired, prove separately: (1) controlled belief evolution is a sufficient statistic for safety under the matched observation/disturbance convention; (2) usable measurable selectors and strategy gluing exist on the reachable belief space; (3) posterior and solution nonemptiness/continuation; and (4) the exact review/finite/continuous bridge used. If any step fails, preserve only subclass-relative claims and drop equality to the unrestricted kernel. Reviewer check: which topology/σ-algebra and controlled-update measurability hypotheses actually hold for the continuous examples?

A separate switching-class certificate would need to quantify over all admissible common, nonanticipative control signals on the blind interval and provide an original-system adverse path for each; this is not supplied here.” Retain `A_tube ⊆ R_V^B ⊆ U^B` only under H2.3 and the matched hold class, without a converse. **Solution-convention alternatives, choose and prove one:** (E) require for each compatible start and allowed held command nonempty original-system Carathéodory solutions defined through the review, with `D(x(t))`-admissible disturbance and all paths quantified for safety; or (F) permit finite maximal/noncontinuable paths but classify noncontinuation **explicitly** as failure (and specify endpoint/explosion, control admissibility, observation record, and whether escape is counted). Reviewer check: can (E) actually be established from the manuscript's state-dependent correspondences, or must existence be an explicit additional assumption? Do not use closed graph, convexification, or ambient lower semicontinuity as an unproved shortcut.
