\begin{abstract}

The viable-selector framework reads every obstruction certificate as the
emptiness of a response correspondence at an information state; its closing
section previewed the stochastic counterpart, in which the information state
is a distribution and viability is the value-one level of a belief-state
safety recursion. This paper develops that theory. The belief-state safety
value is the stochastic selector, and its finite-horizon values are
piecewise linear with exactly representable witnesses: over rational input
data, the backward recursion propagates rational alpha-vectors, so all
values are exact rationals computable without floating point. The model is
stated on the augmented state space with total transition rows, an
absorbing unsafe state carrying its own observation, and masked witnesses.
At the deterministic limit the recursion is replaced by a distinct robust
operator --- a survived-mass formula over blind action sequences --- which
recovers the robust verdicts of the companion papers and implies their
deficit bounds. Obstruction certificates are read systematically as deficit
lower bounds: the chance-constrained common-action bound, the degenerate
minimal-mass bound, and monotonicity. On the audited delayed-regime class
the theory is carried exactly: the class-restricted alpha-vector recursion
reproduces the closed-form values on all forty-eight audited cells, the
certificate partition coincides with the value boundaries, the attaining
witness and the value's jump loci trace the certificate boundary exactly,
the deficit is constant on the certified region, and the class declaration
is quantified --- the declared hold class and the unrestricted sequential
blind class coincide at the one-step horizon and differ exactly on the
timing cells, where open-loop time-variation within the blind window lifts
the value to one. All computations run in exact rational arithmetic.

\end{abstract}

\subsection{1. Introduction}\label{introduction}

The companion papers develop obstruction certificates for viability under
incomplete observation --- finite witnesses that no observation-based policy
is safe --- and unify them as emptiness of a response correspondence at an
information state (Abaee, 2026a,b,c). The unifying paper closes with the
stochastic counterpart, left there as an outlook: replace the
set-valued information state by a distribution, the correspondence's
nonemptiness by the value-one level of a safety recursion, and the
certificate by a lower bound on the deficit from unity. This paper
delivers that theory, seeded by the worked instance already carried in the
calculus paper's probabilistic section and its coverage audit. This second
edition incorporates the dispositions of a joint audit of the first:
a policy-class parameter made explicit throughout the value theory, the
model's boundary cases completed, the selector's attaining set separated
from its value-one level, and the audit's own computations absorbed as
verified results (Section 6 lists the dispositions' effects; the joint
audit record accompanies the paper).

Four results. First, the \emph{exact representation}: class-restricted
belief-state safety values are piecewise linear in the belief at every
finite horizon, and over rational data the witnessing alpha-vectors are
rational and propagated exactly by the backward recursion (Section~3,
Propositions~\ref{prop:pl} and~\ref{prop:degen}) --- no floating point
enters the value theory, in keeping with the companions' verification
discipline. Second, the \emph{degeneration}: at the deterministic limit
the stochastic operator is replaced by its robust counterpart, which
collapses to a survived-mass formula over blind action sequences and
recovers the companion papers' robust verdicts and their deficit bound
(Proposition~\ref{prop:degen}). Third, the \emph{certificate reading}:
obstruction certificates are deficit lower bounds, systematically
(Proposition~\ref{prop:deficit}). Fourth, the \emph{agreement theorem} on
the audited classes: on the delayed hidden-regime grid the
class-restricted alpha-recursion, the closed form, the certificate
partition, and the witness-family boundaries coincide cell by cell, with a
constant deficit on the certified region and a quantified class
declaration (Section~5, Theorem~\ref{thm:agree}). Everything is verified
in exact rational arithmetic (Section~6).

\subsection{2. The stochastic selector}\label{model}

\emph{The augmented state space.} Let the physical safe set be
\(\mathcal{V}\) and let the state space of the model be
\(X^{\perp} = \mathcal{V} \cup \{\bot\}\), where \(\bot\) is an absorbing
unsafe state. For \(x \in \mathcal{V}\) the transition row splits between
\(\mathcal{V}\) and \(\bot\),
\[
\textstyle\sum_{x' \in \mathcal{V}} T(x' \mid x, a) \le 1,\\
T(\bot \mid x, a) = 1 - \sum_{x' \in \mathcal{V}} T(x' \mid x, a),
\]
and the bottom row is \(T(\bot \mid \bot, a) = 1\): every row of \(T\)
sums to one and \(\bot\) is absorbing, so exit from \(\mathcal{V}\) is
absorption and no trajectory re-enters \(\mathcal{V}\) from outside it.
The observation model is completed on all of \(X^{\perp}\): there is a
distinguished observation \(y_{\bot}\) with
\(g(y_{\bot} \mid \bot, a, \bot) = 1\) (and \(g(y \mid \cdot, \cdot, \bot)
= 0\) for \(y \ne y_{\bot}\)), while \(g(\cdot \mid x, a, x')\) on
\(\mathcal{V}\) is arbitrary non-negative with the usual normalization
per \((x, a)\). A belief is a distribution \(b\) over \(X^{\perp}\),
updated by Bayes' rule \(\tau(b, a, y)\), with
\(\mathbb{P}(y \mid b, a) = \sum_{x, x'} b(x)\, T(x' \mid x, a)\,
g(y \mid x, a, x')\); because every row sums to one and every
\((x, a, x')\) emits exactly one observation with probability one, the
laws \(\mathbb{P}(y \mid b, a)\) sum to one over \(y\) at every belief —
no sub-probability bookkeeping is needed. The \emph{safety value} at
horizon \(k\), for a declared policy class \(\Pi\), is the maximal
class-survival probability
\[
V^{\Pi}_{k}(b) = \max_{\pi \in \Pi}\; \mathbb{P}_{\pi}\bigl( x_{t} \in
\mathcal{V} \text{ for } t = 0, \dots, k \;\big|\; b \bigr),
\]
the horizon counting the current state. The unrestricted class
\(\Pi = \Pi_{\mathrm{seq}}\) is the class of all sequential policies.

\emph{The selector and its levels.} In the selector framework this is the
distributional counterpart of the recursive correspondence: the
information state is \(b\), a response is a class-admissible policy
segment. Two objects must be distinguished. The \emph{attaining set} at
\(b\),
\[
\Gamma^{\mathrm{s}}_{k}(b) = \Bigl\{ a \in A : \textstyle\sum_{y}
\mathbb{P}(y \mid b, a)\, V^{\Pi}_{k-1}\bigl(\tau(b, a, y)\bigr)
= V^{\Pi}_{k}(b) \Bigr\},
\]
collects the actions attaining the value; for finite \(A\) it is nonempty
at every belief and horizon — nonemptiness is automatic and carries no
viability content. The \emph{value-one level},
\[
S^{\Pi}_{k}(b) = \Bigl\{ a \in A : \textstyle\sum_{y} \mathbb{P}(y \mid
b, a)\, V^{\Pi}_{k-1}\bigl(\tau(b, a, y)\bigr) = 1 \Bigr\},
\]
is the selector's viable-response set, and its nonemptiness corresponds
exactly to \(V^{\Pi}_{k}(b) = 1\). Obstruction certificates correspond to
deficits \(1 - V^{\Pi}_{k}(b) > 0\). The first edition conflated the two
nonemptiness statements; the dispositions repair the definition, and the
distinction is verified instance by instance (Section~6).

\begin{theorem}[belief-state value recursion]\label{thm:recursion}
\(V^{\Pi}_{0}(b) = b(\mathcal{V})\) (the horizon counts the current
state), and for \(k \ge 0\),
\[
V^{\Pi}_{k+1}(b) = \max_{a \in A(\Pi)}\; \sum_{y \in Y} \mathbb{P}(y \mid
b, a)\; V^{\Pi}_{k}\bigl(\tau(b, a, y)\bigr),
\]
where \(A(\Pi)\) is the class-admissible action set at the current stage
for Markov classes \(\Pi\). The recursion is exact on every finite
horizon; over the unrestricted class it is the Smallwood--Sondik
belief-state value iteration specialized to the absorbing safety reward
(Smallwood and Sondik, 1973).
\end{theorem}

The proof is standard value iteration for the belief-state Markov decision
process, with the absorbing state making \(V^{\Pi}_{k}\) the \(k\)-step
class-survival probability. For non-Markov classes (the hold and
open-loop classes of Section~5) the class constraint couples stages; the
audited computations there use the equivalent segment form of
Proposition~\ref{prop:pl} below.

\subsection{3. Exact piecewise-linear values}\label{exact}

\begin{proposition}[rational alpha-vectors, class-restricted]\label{prop:pl}
Fix a declared class \(\Pi\). At every horizon \(k\),
\(V^{\Pi}_{k}\) is piecewise linear and convex:
\(V^{\Pi}_{k}(b) = \max_{\alpha \in \Gamma^{\Pi}_{k}} \alpha^{\top} b\)
for a finite witness set \(\Gamma^{\Pi}_{k}\) on
\(\mathbb{Q}^{|\mathcal{V}|+1}\) (coordinates indexed by
\(\mathcal{V} \cup \{\bot\}\)), constructed by the masked backup
\[
\alpha^{a,\,\gamma_{\cdot}}(x) =
\sum_{x'} T(x' \mid x, a) \sum_{y} g(y \mid x, a, x')\, \gamma_{y}(x')\\
\gamma_{y} \in \Gamma^{\Pi}_{k},
\]
with \(\Gamma^{\Pi}_{0} = \{ \mathbf{1}_{\mathcal{V}} \}\) and the
boundary condition \(\alpha(\bot) = 0\) applied at every backup (the
backup acts on \(\mathcal{V}\) only; the \(\bot\)-coordinate of every
witness is anchored at zero and remains zero under the backup) and \(\Gamma^{\Pi}_{k+1} = \{ \alpha^{a, \gamma_{\cdot}}
: a \in A(\Pi),\ \gamma_{\cdot} \text{ a selection} \}\), duplicates
removed by exact equality. For a blind open-loop window the equivalent
segment form applies: each admissible action segment
\((u_{0}, \dots, u_{m}) \in \Pi\) contributes the witness
\(\alpha^{\mathrm{seg}} = (\mathbf{1}[\text{the segment saves branch } x])_{x}\)
and \(V^{\Pi}_{k}(b) = \max_{\mathrm{seg}} \alpha^{\mathrm{seg}\top} b\).
Over rational \(T\), \(g\), and priors, every \(\alpha\) is an exact
rational vector, every \(V^{\Pi}_{k}(b)\) at a rational belief is an exact
rational, and the attaining witness is part of the computation. The
worst-case growth is \(|\Gamma^{\Pi}_{k+1}| \le |A|\,|\Gamma^{\Pi}_{k}|^{|Y|}\);
deduplication and pruned selections are the practical controls, and the
exponential law is the honest complexity statement for the general class.
\end{proposition}

\emph{Proof sketch.} The PL structure is Smallwood--Sondik's; the masking
factor anchors \(\alpha(\bot) = 0\) (survival requires the current state
in \(\mathcal{V}\), and \(\bot\) is absorbing, so no backup can revive a
lost branch). The point here is the exact-arithmetic discipline: the
backup maps rational vectors to rational vectors by finite sums and
products, selections are finite, and equality tests for deduplication are
exact on rationals. The segment form is the same statement with the
class constraint folded into the enumeration of segments.
\hfill\(\square\)

\begin{remark}[the two operators]\label{rem:operators}
The expectation over the disturbance law and the adversarial reading of
its support are \emph{different Bellman operators}. Proposition
\ref{prop:degen} states the deterministic degeneration as a change of
operator: when the kernels are deterministic the stochastic expectation
is replaced by the robust (adversarial-support) reading, and the
resulting value is the robust value of the companion papers. The
degeneration is a theorem about the robust operator; it is not a claim
about the stochastic plant, whose expectation and robust readings differ
in general (Section~7).
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[deterministic-limit degeneration]\label{prop:degen}
Let the kernels be deterministic, \(x^{+} = F(x, a, d)\), and let the
disturbance be read adversarially within its support — the robust operator
of Remark~\ref{rem:operators}. Let the window be blind with declared
sequential class \(\Pi_{B}\). Then the degenerated value is the
\emph{survived-mass formula}
\[
V_{k}(b) = \max_{(u_{0},\dots) \in \Pi_{B}}\;
\sum_{x \in \mathrm{supp}\, b} b(x)\,
\mathbf{1}\bigl[ x \text{ survives } (u_{0}, \dots) \bigr],
\]
a maximum of rational linear functions of \(b\) (the surviving indicators
are the degenerate alpha-vectors). Consequently: if the support \(B\)
carries a jointly surviving blind sequence, \(V_{k}(b) = 1\); and if every
declared sequence loses some branch, then \(V_{k}(b) \le 1 - \min_{x \in
B} b(x)\) --- the robust common-action verdicts and the minimal-mass
deficit bound of the companion papers, recovered as exact values of the
robust operator.
\end{proposition}

\emph{Proof sketch.} With deterministic kernels the posterior support
follows the set-valued recursion, survival from a branch under a fixed
sequence is a deterministic event, and prior mass sums over surviving
branches; maximizing over declared sequences gives the formula, from
which both consequences are immediate (a lost branch carries at least the
minimal mass). \hfill\(\square\)

\subsection{4. Certificates as deficit bounds}\label{deficit}

Write \(\Delta_{k}(b) = 1 - V_{k}(b)\) for the deficit. For the
statement below, \(W_{k}\) denotes the \emph{k-step robust kernel in
belief space}: the family of beliefs whose support admits a jointly
surviving declared-class sequence (a subset of the belief simplex, not of
the state space).

\begin{proposition}[deficit lower bounds]\label{prop:deficit}
Each companion certificate is a computable lower bound on the deficit:
(i) \emph{chance-constrained common action:} if the one-step violation
probabilities satisfy \(\sum_{x} b(x)\, p(x, a) \ge \delta\) for every
\(a \in A\), then \(\Delta_{k}(b) \ge \delta\) for every \(k \ge 1\);
(ii) \emph{degenerate minimal mass:} at the deterministic limit, if
\(b \notin W_{k}\) (the support admits no jointly surviving declared
sequence), then \(\Delta_{k}(b) \ge \min_{x \in \mathrm{supp}\, b} b(x)\);
(iii) \emph{monotonicity:} \(V_{k+1}(b) \le V_{k}(b)\), so the deficit is
nondecreasing in the horizon. For finite horizons the exact deficit
\(1 - \max_{\alpha \in \Gamma_{k}} \alpha^{\top} b\) is computable, and
the bounds are witnesses of it, not surrogates.
\end{proposition}

\emph{Proof sketch.} (i) is the chance-constrained obstruction of the
companion paper: every action loses probability at least \(\delta\) at the
first step and survival probabilities never increase. (ii) is
Proposition~\ref{prop:degen}'s second consequence. (iii) holds since
\(V_{k+1} = \max_a \mathbb{E}[V_{k} \circ \tau] \le \max_a \mathbb{E}
[V_{k-1} \circ \tau] = V_{k}\) by induction.
\hfill\(\square\)

\subsection{5. Agreement on the audited classes}\label{agreement}

The delayed hidden-regime instance of the calculus paper --- regimes
\(\theta \in \{-1, +1\}\), blind window \(T_{\mathrm{obs}}\), declared hold
class \(\{u \equiv \pm 1\}\), prior \((\tfrac12, \tfrac12)\) from
\(z_{0}\) --- is the agreement test bed.

\begin{theorem}[certificate--value agreement]\label{thm:agree}
On the audited grid (\(z_{0} \in \{1.0, \dots, 2.5\}\),
\(T_{\mathrm{obs}} \in \{1,2,3\}\); 48 cells), over the declared hold
class: (a) the class-restricted alpha-vector recursion of
Proposition~\ref{prop:pl} returns exactly the closed form
\begin{gather*}
V_{k}(b_{0}) = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(\mathbf{1}[z_{0} - k \ge 1] +
\mathbf{1}[z_{0} + k \ge 1]\bigr) \quad (k \le T_{\mathrm{obs}}),\\
V_{k}(b_{0}) = \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(1 + \mathbf{1}[z_{0} \ge 1 +
T_{\mathrm{obs}}]\bigr) \quad (k > T_{\mathrm{obs}});
\end{gather*}
on the audited grid the second indicator of the first line is vacuous
(\(z_{0} + k \ge 2\)); it is retained for branch symmetry.
(b) the certificate partition coincides with the value boundaries:
``viable'' iff \(V_{k} \equiv 1\); common-action-certified iff
\(V_{1}(b_{0}) < 1\); timing-certified iff \(V_{1}(b_{0}) = 1\) but
\(V_{T_{\mathrm{obs}}+1}(b_{0}) < 1\);
(c) at the one-step horizon the attaining witness family changes exactly
at \(z_{0} = 2\): below it two distinct witnesses attain
\(\tfrac12\); at and above it, the merged unit witness attains \(1\) —
and at horizon \(k \le T_{\mathrm{obs}}\) the family changes exactly at
\(z_{0} = 1 + k\). Read as a function of the continuous parameter
\(z_{0}\), the hold-class value has jump discontinuities exactly at
\(z_{0} = 1 + \min(k, T_{\mathrm{obs}})\) (a jump of
\(\tfrac12\), from \(\tfrac12\) to \(1\)); on the audited grid these loci
are the certificate edges. (Kinks in the strict sense belong to the
piecewise-linear value as a function of the belief, not of \(z_{0}\).)
(d) the deficit equals \(\tfrac12\) on every nonviable cell --- the
minimal-mass bound attained with the minimal prior; and
(e) the class declaration is quantified: the declared hold class and the
unrestricted sequential blind class coincide at the one-step horizon; for
\(k \ge 2\) they differ exactly on the timing cells
\(\{2 \le z_{0} < 1 + T_{\mathrm{obs}}\}\), where the unrestricted class
attains \(1\) --- a single action already saves both branches at
\(z_{0} \ge 2\) (the down-branch sits at \(z_{0} - 1 \ge 1\)), and the
alternating open-loop sequence \(u, -u, u, \dots\) keeps both branches
within \([z_{0}-1, z_{0}+1]\) at every horizon. The timing cells are
exactly the cells where open-loop time-variation within the blind window
lifts the declared-class value.
\end{theorem}

\emph{Proof sketch.} The two-regime blind POMDP has, at each window
horizon, exactly two alpha-vectors \(\alpha^{u} = (\mathbf{1}[z_{0} - u t
\ge 1\ \forall t \le \cdot],\ \mathbf{1}[z_{0} + u t \ge 1\ \forall t \le
\cdot])\), \(u = \pm 1\); evaluating \(\max_{u} \alpha^{u} b_{0}\) at the
uniform prior gives (a), from which (b) and (d) read off against the
companion paper's certificate conditions; (c) compares the attaining
witness family across the boundaries \(z_{0} = 2\) and \(z_{0} = 1 + k\),
and the jump loci follow from the closed form's indicators. For (e), the
one-step coincidence is immediate (both classes choose one action), the
alternating sequence's branch positions are computed exactly (they
oscillate between \(z_{0} \pm 1\) and \(z_{0}\), minimum \(z_{0} - 1\)),
and brute force over all sequences gives the matching upper bound. The
complete computations are machine-verified in exact rational arithmetic.
\hfill\(\square\)

\begin{remark}[the timing obstruction is class-scoped]\label{rem:scope}
The calculus paper's timing certificates (\(\sigma^{*}(B_{0}) = z_{0} -
1\), \(T_{\mathrm{obs}} < \sigma^{*}\)) are statements about the declared
hold-until-review class --- the institutional reading of a sampled-review
regulator, which is how that paper's blind-window control classes are
defined. Theorem~\ref{thm:agree}(e) quantifies the contrast: within the
declared class the timing cells are nonviable with deficit exactly
\(\tfrac12\); under the unrestricted sequential blind class the same
cells attain value \(1\) by oscillation, and no other cell differs. The
timing obstruction therefore certifies exactly what it claims —
nonviability \emph{within the declared class} --- and no statement of the
companion papers is altered by this reading. The word ``adaptive'' is
avoided for blind windows: while the window is blind, no observation
updates the belief, and the only distinction available is
hold-constant versus time-varying open-loop control.
\end{remark}

\subsection{6. Verified re-derivations}\label{verified}

\texttt{paper2\_stochastic\_selector\_v2\_verify.py} (standard library;
exact rational arithmetic; deterministic) re-derives every claim in
fifteen check families, all passing:

\begin{itemize}\tightlist
\item \textbf{S1} two-floor instance: the backup produces exactly
\(\Gamma_{1} = \{(1,0), (0,1)\}\) on the physical coordinates,
\(V_{k}(b_{0}) = \tfrac12\) for all probed horizons, the chance bound
\(\delta = \tfrac12\) attained;
\item \textbf{S2} piecewise linearity confirmed at eleven simplex beliefs
against the direct value \(\max(b_{1}, b_{2})\);
\item \textbf{S3} the degenerated (robust) value equals brute-force
evaluation, and at \((z_{0}, T) = (\tfrac32, 2)\) every blind sequence
loses a branch, so the deficit is at least the minimal mass;
\item \textbf{S4} the recursion matches the closed form on all 48 cells
and horizons \(k \le 4\);
\item \textbf{S5} the partition coincidence holds cell by cell;
\item \textbf{S6} the witness-family change sits exactly at \(z_{0} = 2\)
at the one-step horizon and at \(z_{0} = 1 + k\) at horizon \(k \le
T_{\mathrm{obs}}\) (in particular \emph{not} at \(z_{0} = 2\) for
\(k = 2\));
\item \textbf{S7} the deficit is exactly \(\tfrac12\) on all 42 nonviable
cells;
\item \textbf{S8} the unrestricted-class table and the sharpened class
declaration (coincidence at \(k = 1\); difference exactly on the timing
cells for \(k \ge 2\)), by brute force over all sequences;
\item \textbf{S9} rationality: all 384 campaign alpha-vectors exact
rationals (here all in \(\{0,1\}\), maximal denominator \(1\));
\item \textbf{S10} deficit monotonicity at every probed belief of both
instances;
\item \textbf{S11} the oscillation identity: branch positions under the
alternating sequence, minimum exactly \(z_{0} - 1\), never exiting for
\(z_{0} \ge 2\) at every horizon to 24;
\item \textbf{S12} model completeness: transition rows sum to one,
\(\bot\) is absorbing with observation \(y_{\bot}\), the observation laws
sum to one, and witness masking is preserved;
\item \textbf{S13} the selector split: attaining sets nonempty everywhere
probed; value-one level sets nonempty exactly where \(V_{k}(b) = 1\);
\item \textbf{S14} dimension consistency: the two-floor witnesses pad to
\((1,0,0), (0,1,0)\) on \(\mathcal{V} \cup \{\bot\}\) — the first edition
suppressed the zero \(\bot\)-coordinate in displays, now stated;
\item \textbf{S15} the jump loci \(z_{0} = 1 + \min(k, T_{\mathrm{obs}})\)
with one-sided values \(\tfrac12\), \(1\) exactly.
\end{itemize}

On complexity: the worst-case witness growth is exponential
(\(|\Gamma_{k+1}| \le |A|\,|\Gamma_{k}|^{|Y|}\)); the audited instances
stay two-witness precisely because the blind window emits a single
observation (\(|Y| = 1\)), so each backup enumerates \(|A|\) candidates
and deduplicates by exact equality — 384 vectors across the campaign,
all \(\{0,1\}\)-valued. No pruning beyond deduplication was needed on the
audited classes.

\subsection{7. Delimitations and outlook}\label{scope}

The semantics here is stochastic expectation: the value is a survival
\emph{probability}, and the robust quantifiers of the companion papers
are recovered only at the deterministic degeneration, which is a change
of operator (Remark~\ref{rem:operators}); the stochastic and robust
readings of a genuinely stochastic plant differ, and this paper does not
conflate them. The timing layer's statements, here and in the companion
papers, are class-scoped: the closed forms are hold-class
(sampled-review) values, and the unrestricted sequential class is
quantified separately in Theorem~\ref{thm:agree}(e) and
Remark~\ref{rem:scope}. The alpha-set growth is exponential in the worst
case; the agreement theorem's classes are two-witness instances precisely
because the audited systems are. General information structures beyond
history partitions (control-dependent observations, active sensing),
continuous-time exit-time value functions, and the five-layer successor
architecture are the next items of the programme; the deficit-bound
reading of Proposition~\ref{prop:deficit} is the interface through which
this theory attaches to the certificate families already established.
