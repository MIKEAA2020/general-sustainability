grok:
**This paper develops a continuous-to-finite computational pipeline for obstruction certificates of linear systems under exogenous (mode) partial observation.** An outer moment relaxation of measurable information-adapted controls is paired with an inner finite adversarial discretization (stored labels/scenarios), producing a finite LP whose value \(\rho_{\mathcal{H},G}\) sandwiches the continuous-time minimax safety violation \(J_{\mathcal{I}}(T)\). Dual-feasible solutions are policy-independent finite certificates; a strictly positive margin is an obstruction certificate covering every measurable adapted policy. Completeness holds along refinements whose meshes and stored-scenario/enclosure widths vanish (though positivity is not monotone). Witness cardinality is governed by information–time rank of the safety-row matrix (at most \(r+1\) labels), not input dimension; a scalar-input construction saturates this with arbitrarily many modes. The pipeline is demonstrated end-to-end on a three-branch delayed-observation double-integrator instance (\(\tau^*=7/50\), exact shared-authority hierarchy, HiGHS agreeing with rationals to \(\sim 4.2\times 10^{-17}\)). A finite-side library `viacert` packages exact recursion and fibre-partition checks. Computations use exact rational arithmetic; the solver is only confirmation.

The core identities (sandwich (8), atomic dual representation (9), \(\Gamma(\tau)=\tau-7/50\), \(\rho(h)=3/50-Th/4\) on aligned meshes, pair/triple delay thresholds, Farkas witnesses, residual certificate (11)) are internally consistent with the stated kernels, weights \(\lambda=(3/8,5/16,5/16)\), \(\sum\lambda_j n_j=0\), Table 1, and the verification claims. No catastrophic arithmetic or logical contradiction appears in the extracted text. Defects are overwhelmingly organizational, presentational, and copy-paste, plus a few wording/scope issues.

### Wording and conceptual issues
- The sandwich \(\rho_{\mathcal{H},G}\le J_{\mathcal{I}}(T)\le\rho+\bar{e}+\delta_\beta+L h_t\) is correctly a certified lower bound on the violation (hence upper bound on safety) plus certified inflation. Related-work item 1 usefully distinguishes it from classical viability-kernel under/over-approximations and from moment-SOS exactness certificates. The phrasing “the two-sided structure is not new, and this paper does not claim it is” is accurate but abrupt.
- “Worst-case” vs. scenario literature is handled well: the inner approximation is adversarial (finite stored labels/scenarios), the bound holds for every measurable information-adapted policy, and there is no confidence parameter. The parenthetical that \(\epsilon,\delta_\beta,L h_t\) are deterministic inflation certificates (not violation probabilities) and the flag on \(\beta\)-collision are helpful.
- Solver status is stated clearly (“numerical confirmation… never proof objects”; primal-dual witnesses in exact arithmetic close the gap). Table 1 and the \(4.2\times 10^{-17}\) claim are consistent with that division of labour.
- Dual-feasibility as a universal certificate (Remark 3, Theorem 4(ii), Corollary) is correctly policy-independent: the finite list of labels and weights does not depend on the policy; a violating realization may. The “prescriptive” reading (shared-authority ladder, conflict-interval decomposition (12), redesign levers) is instance-specific and the paper itself warns that algebraic dilation plots (\(\Gamma_a(\tau)=\tau-7/50-(a-1)/2\)) are not optimized thresholds (Proposition 8 counter-example at \(a=2\), \(\tau=16/25\)).
- Rank vs. input dimension (Proposition 5) is the distinctive claim. The scalar-input, \(q+1\)-mode construction (interval-wise affine dynamics, terminal bounds producing a Helly-style contradiction only when all modes are present) is coherent; subsets of \(q\) modes remain viable. Helly’s theorem is correctly scoped as inapplicable to a common blind control function. The “negative half… most likely to surprise” sentence is informal.
- Completeness (Theorem 4(iii)): positivity is not itself monotone under refinement, and detection requires the stored-scenario widths \(\delta_{\mathrm{init}},\delta_{\mathrm{num}}\) to vanish together with the mesh. This is stated; the dyadic schedule and \(\gamma/2\) margin example are clear.
- Oracle-recourse and belief-cell extensions (Proposition 6, (13)) are labelled sound but incomplete; the paper does not claim they restore the exact theorem. That is honest.

## A. Wrong, contradicted or unsupported statements

1. **§17 contradicts §12.**
   - §17 says the measure-dual companion reaches "the same conclusion … witness size is set by control dimension k with a tight k+1 bound".
   - Prop. 5 and the Helly remark say the opposite: input dimension does not bound witnesses for blind control functions.
   - The two results are about different objects (a static common action versus a control function). "Same conclusion, one moral" is wrong.

2. **Pair thresholds are claimed as obstructions but only half-proved.**
   - The abstract says "the pairs [are obstructed] past ≈0.245 and ≈0.397".
   - Prop. 7 only computes where the shared-braking-then-brake policy's peak reaches 2. That gives viability below the thresholds and says nothing about nonviability above them.
   - No pair dual certificate is given.
   - Nonviability beyond the thresholds also needs optimality over all time-varying blind controls and all post-revelation controls.
   - I believe the constant control is optimal. Peak_j is convex and decreasing in the deceleration profile, and the frontier slope argument closes it. The paper does not show this.
   - "Exactly the optimal shared-braking programs" is true only inside the shared-braking family.
   - Fix: compute the pair LPs and exhibit rational dual certificates, or downgrade to "viable up to".

3. **Prop. 8 retracts what §5 and §14 still assert.**
   - §14 says "each redesign lever carries an exact rate" and §5(4) says the certificate separates "what buys tolerance from what buys nothing".
   - Prop. 8 says the a > 1 expression Γ_a is only an algebraic plot, not a valid certificate, and that the a = 2 equality claim is false.
   - Fig. 2 still plots the a > 1 "zeros" as thresholds. The caption hedges, but the figure invites misreading.
   - Reword §5(4) and §14, or drop Fig. 2.

4. **"Unrestricted level ρ" and the leftover max{0,·}.**
   - §10 refers to "the max{0,·} in the table's law", but no such max appears in any displayed law or in Table 1. This is a stale sentence.
   - "ρ(h) = 3/50 − Th/4 for h < 1/5 and vanish at h = 1/5" is redundant, because the formula already gives 0 there.
   - ρ is not truly unrestricted with exact per-label ē. The t = 0 velocity row for mode 1 is 1 − 6/5 = −1/5 independent of the controls, so ρ ≥ −1/5.
   - The unequal-block value −1/5 is therefore exactly this floor. That coincidence should be said, since it is a floor and not a measure of safety slack.

5. **§9: "held policy is continuously safe exactly when its moments satisfy the unrelaxed rows".**
   - This holds only on the label grid G. Between grid times it needs the L·h_t term.
   - Even with ē = 0 the equivalence is false as written.

6. **Prop. 7 proof, off-critical drift.**
   - It says the transverse drift "only decreases the noncritical coordinates".
   - For the {1,2} blind control u = (−2/5, −4/5), the transverse velocity is −4/25 on both branches. With n₃ = (−3/5, −4/5), the n₃ᵀp coordinate of branch 1 and of branch 2 increases.
   - The margin is large, so the conclusion survives, but the stated reason is wrong.

7. **J_I(T) = τ − 7/50 and "τ > 7/50 ⇒ B₀ ∉ K_I" need T ≥ τ + 1.**
   - With the fixed T = 6/5 this means τ ≤ 1/5.
   - The "unit rate" claim and Fig. 2's τ-axis (to 0.7) are outside the regime where the label at t* = τ + 1 exists.

8. **§4 versus Theorem 4(iii) and §11.**
   - §4 says the paper "deliberately does not use" interval or validated methods.
   - Semidecidability and the general rows (X_θ, ∫ of matrix exponentials, TV, L bounds) require certified enclosures.
   - Exact rational arithmetic works here because the double integrator is nilpotent. The abstract's "finite, attainable, independently checkable" pipeline is demonstrated only in that setting.

9. **Related-work mischaracterisations.**
   - §1 attributes "the estimation-tube program" to Veliov (1993). That paper gives sufficient output-feedback conditions.
   - §2 says moment–SOS values "converge monotonically". Here convergence is explicitly non-monotone (Thm 4(iii)).
   - "Moment relaxation" is a stretch for a block-mean projection.
   - "The direct ancestor" is overstated.
   - I could not confirm the Saint-Pierre "open constraint sets" statement. Please check it.

10. **§16: "Theorem 4 provides a justified continuous-to-finite bridge" for exact finite safety games.**
    - Theorem 4 gives one-sided certificates and a sandwich. It does not give a sound abstraction in the sense used for finite games.

## B. Proof gaps

1. **Thm 4(ii), soundness of the finite dual as a continuous certificate, is stated, not proved.**
   - Needed: Σ_j|J|ϕ_U(ḡ_j) − Σλē ≤ ∫ϕ_U(g).
   - By subadditivity, ϕ_U(ḡ) − ϕ_U(g) ≤ h_U(g − ḡ) ≤ Σλ h_U(k − k̄).
   - ē is defined via h_U(k̄ − k), so the two signs must agree. That holds only for centrally symmetric U, or if ē is defined for both signs. The hexagon is symmetric; Thm 4 is stated for general polytopes.
   - Corollary (a)'s second inequality is attributed to "the weighted-average bound of the proof". It is simply the definition of J as a minimum over policies.

2. **Thm 4(v), the r + 1 bound.**
   - The argument is that a basic optimal solution of the λ-LP, with Σλ_aA_a = w and Σλ = 1, has at most r + 1 support points. This is correct but never written down. Give it.
   - Contrary to "any prescribed margin strictly below ρ", the basic solution retains ρ itself.
   - r is the rank of the discretised matrix and grows with the mesh. "Saturating r + 1" in Prop. 5 holds only for the aligned unit-block mesh.
   - For r_I (span of lifted kernels), generic LTI rows have the cutoff 1{σ≤t}, which makes the span infinite-dimensional. The theorem is then vacuous in the continuous setting.
   - "Governed by information–time rank" is therefore a statement about the discretisation. Define an intrinsic rank, or soften.

3. **Prop. 5's example is degenerate.**
   - Each x_j is forced to u ≡ 1 on I_j, so the obstruction is a covering counting argument.
   - Say so, or give a non-tight example. Also fix "q + 1 witnesses are necessary" to "q + 1 labels".

4. **Proof of (iv).**
   - It is deferred to the supplement ("complete version") yet claimed "in full".
   - Step counts conflict: "Steps 1–6" versus "complete seven-step … Step 5b".
   - Sion needs semicontinuity hypotheses that are only gestured at.
   - The "Arzelà–Ascoli consumes" phrasing is not a proof step.

5. **Prop. 6 (belief cells) has no mathematical content beyond "sound relaxation".**
   - The rest is hedging prose.
   - The counterexample uses sup_C β where the worst case is inf_C β. It is a deliberately loose enclosure, not the true label, and this is not stated.

6. **Prop. 2 repeats its proof verbatim inside the statement.** It also forward-references "(6)".

7. **Positivity is "not monotone under refinement" (abstract, intro).** No example is given. The instance shows monotone increase.

## E. Suggested improvements

1. **Two-sided decisions.**
   - The upper side already yields viability certificates: if ρ + ē + δβ + Lh_t < 0, the held policy is safe.
