**This paper develops the measure-theoretic dual of the common-action obstruction:** for control-affine dynamics with compact convex \(U\), the common safe-action set is empty iff an adversarial probability measure \(\mu^*\) on the active boundary-disturbance bundle \(\mathcal{K}(B)\) makes the expected inward drift strictly negative against every control (\(\max_u\Phi(u,\mu^*)\le-\varepsilon<0\)), with a finitely supported certificate of at most \(k+1\) atoms (\(k=\dim U\)). Eight (or six) exact-rational check families and a Lean 4 module are claimed.

## A. Wrong, unsupported or self-contradictory statements

1. **Prop. 1's proof does not give k+1.**
   - The proof pools the rows into (w, c̄) = Σλᵢ(vᵢ, cᵢ). That is a point in ℝ^{k+1}, so "Carathéodory in ℝ^k" would give k+2 atoms, not k+1.
   - "A basic solution of k+1 active constraints" is not meaningful for the λ-system. For polytopal U that system has one constraint per vertex of U, not k+1. For general compact convex U it has infinitely many.
   - The bound is true, but the correct route is Helly.
     - The sets Cᵢ = {u∈U : ψᵢ(u) ≥ 0} are compact convex subsets of ℝ^k with empty intersection.
     - Hence some ≤ k+1 of them have empty intersection (Helly for compact families).
     - Finite minimax duality on that subfamily gives the ≤ k+1 atoms. The "finite cover" step then becomes unnecessary.
   - The dimension can be lowered to dim aff U, or to the rank of the span of the vᵢ. "Control dimension" should say which.
   - The related-work claim that the k+1 bound "is not recoverable" from classical results is therefore overstated. It is Helly plus LP duality. The same count appears as the support-constraint bound in the scenario approach (Calafiore–Campi), which paper 3 cites and this paper omits.

2. **Tightness is asserted inconsistently and is easier than claimed.**
   - Prop. 1 says "no general-dimensional tightness example is asserted". The Conclusion says the bound "is tight — it is attained, not merely approached", with no restriction to k ≤ 2.
   - A general-k example takes one line: rows {uᵢ ≥ 1}, i ≤ k, and {Σuᵢ ≤ k−1}. Any k of them intersect, and all k+1 do not.
   - The k = 2 check "on the rational grid by the record" is unnecessary, since summing the rows gives −1. A grid check cannot prove emptiness.
   - Realizability is never shown. The rows must arise as (x, j, d) drifts of a real control-affine system with a safe set, and the example only supplies abstract rows.

3. **The convexity "boundary" is not located, and the failure is one-directional.**
   - Prop. 3 is a single counterexample. It does not locate any boundary.
   - Soundness survives without convexity: maxᵤΦ(u,µ*) < 0 still implies emptiness. Only completeness fails.
   - In fact the dual always certifies emptiness over conv U. In Prop. 3, conv U = [0,1] has the common safe action u = 1/2.
   - State this precisely.
   - Drop "exact location of the convexity boundary" (Abstract, Related work, Conclusion). Also drop the claim that this makes convexity "load-bearing in the strongest possible sense, simultaneously making kernels convex and measure duality sound". The convexity in the discriminating-kernel result concerns the dynamics and the kernel, not U in this sense. This is rhetorical conflation.

4. **"Recovers the ℓ₁-normalized Farkas pair exactly" is only true when U is unconstrained.**
   - The measure certificate uses h_U, so U's bounds are absorbed into the max over u. A Farkas pair for the full polyhedral system carries multipliers on U's facets.
   - The two-floor example works only because Σλᵢvᵢ = 0.
   - The paper's own two-stock certificate µ* = (½,½) has pooled slope (−½,−½) ≠ 0. It relies on the demand floor u₁+u₂ ≥ 2 inside U.
   - A statement of the general recovery is missing: a theorem relating Farkas multipliers on rows plus U-facets to µ*, with the normalization defined.
   - "Rational data" is irrelevant to the recovery.

5. **Isaacs "recovery" is not defined and is partly backwards.**
   - "Isaacs drift" is never defined.
   - In the example, min_d maxᵤψ = 1 ≠ maxᵤ min_d ψ = 0. The Isaacs (saddle) condition fails for pure disturbances. Mixing restores it.
   - The example is also not an obstruction, since maxᵤ min ψ = 0 ≥ 0.
   - The Conclusion says the measure form is "the common generalisation from which both drop out". That conflicts with the Related-work statement that the Farkas recovery "is a consistency check, not the generalisation itself". The Conclusion also says "Neither is a consistency check". The two passages directly contradict each other.

6. **Certificate size is conflated with certification complexity.**
   - The Conclusion says "the complexity of certifying nonviability is governed by how many independent controls the agent has".
   - The bound limits the size of the witness, not the cost of finding it. Locating the atoms searches K(B), which depends on state and disturbance structure.
   - Paper 3 explicitly disclaims this reading.
   - The claim that this is "the same finding in different languages" as paper 3 is also muddled. Paper 3's point is that input dimension does not bound witnesses for blind control functions. That is consistent here only because a static common action has a single time–information block, so r ≤ m. Say this instead.

7. **The two-stock benchmark does not satisfy Theorem 1's hypotheses.**

   - U = {u ≥ 0, u₁+u₂ ≥ 2} is unbounded, but Theorem 1 requires compact U. A truncation (uᵢ ≤ 2) is harmless, since larger u only lowers drift, but it must be said.

## B. The dynamic part: the theory and the instance do not match

2. **Add an exact statement of the non-convex case.** Show that soundness always holds, that the measure certificate characterizes conv U, and give a necessary-and-sufficient reading of when the gap is zero.
3. **State the polyhedral recovery as a theorem with U's facets.** Define the normalization and demonstrate it on the two-stock example.
4. **Define R_V, the Isaacs reading and the bundle.** Say that a pure-disturbance saddle fails and that mixing restores it.
5. **Make the benchmark self-contained.** Give the equations, a compact truncation of U, the (x,j,d) atoms, the explicit viability threshold ΔX₁ ≤ 7/5, and the fibre-invariance caveat.
6. **Repair the dynamic theory.**
   - Put s in Ω.
   - Let the last cell be a general partition.
   - Allow arbitrary consistent µ*.
   - Define the robust value over a coupling class, state weak duality, and give the product versus comonotone example.
   - Resolve the contradiction with Conjecture 1(i).
7. **Link the static and dynamic parts**, or drop "unifies". The link would be: Γ_h is the integrated common-action certificate, relate it to Theorem 1's drift condition, and fix the sign convention.
8. **Add a verification section.** It should list all eight families, the instances used, and where the counts (243/171, 19683, 8192) come from. State which parts are exact instance checks and which are proofs.
9. **Test beyond toy instances.** Random polytopal and control-affine instances with k up to 5, counting the atoms the minimal certificate needs, would show how often k+1 is reached and make the scaling claim empirical.
10. **Trim.** Shorten the abstract, collapse Theorem 3 into Theorem 4, drop the Lean boilerplate, and delete the "retired candidate" narrative in "Three obstructions".
