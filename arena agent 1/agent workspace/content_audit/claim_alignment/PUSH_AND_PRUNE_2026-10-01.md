## Further budget reduction, with byte-level reclonability check

The 36-MB local `tools/tectonic` binary was **not** deleted merely because a download URL existed. First it was wrapped into the 14-MB `tools/tectonic-0.15.0-x86_64-static.tar.gz`, extracted in scratch space and confirmed byte-identical. Both archive and [`tools/RESTORE_TECTONIC.md`](../../tools/RESTORE_TECTONIC.md) were pushed as separate commit `fbb1a7703ceb0cb943a82d85390c45befa9316f7` on `e2-v3-source-year`. The archive was then **downloaded back through Git's blob API** (blob `b63a6d84ceb074c26512109bc6b18aa578064e4f`), decoded, extracted and compared byte-for-byte to the working binary (SHA-256 `4df19452c202c5bef9f7c7e4a01a3f2b9d5199f0a1f73b70b4fe1bffbc9837f6`). Only then was the local extracted binary removed. The verified archive **remains locally and remotely**; the deletion is an additional row in `PRUNE_MANIFEST.tsv` (123 total rows). `compile_drafts.py` now validates both archive and binary checksums and unpacks the compiler temporarily if the executable is absent; a full 12-item compile from this fallback passed after deletion. After recording the successful summary, the reproduced isolated compile outputs and network-download TeX cache were cleared, leaving `compile/results.tsv` and `compile/all_compiles.log` plus the packaged PDFs/assets. The cache is generated and excluded from workspace snapshots. No live paper, reviewed head, evidence source, publication asset or Lean source still needed by the count scanner was removed. Snapshot working tree is roughly **72 MB** after that step.
