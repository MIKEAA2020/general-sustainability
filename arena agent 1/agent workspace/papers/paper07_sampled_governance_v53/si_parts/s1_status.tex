\paragraph{Change in the interpretation of the \(\Tr\) test.} The recovered test is executable and its output has been regenerated. It reports both (a) no identifiable window-approach gradient because the only assigned values are 0.5 and 1.0 years while the sampled windows are 4 and 8 years, and (b) reproducible two-group associations in the opposite direction from a simple approach prediction. Those statements are not contradictory. The first is an identification limit; the second describes a proxy-group contrast. Neither supports an institutional causal interpretation. The Peru/Chile clustering makes regional ecology, including ENSO-related quasi-periodicity, a plausible confound, but the present test does not establish that ENSO caused the contrast.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.} The executable ranked-test script, per-stock output table, and reproduction log are included in the source bundle. The local rerun reproduces the logged values to the displayed precision. The inference reported here follows the current executable record and its two-proxy limitation.
