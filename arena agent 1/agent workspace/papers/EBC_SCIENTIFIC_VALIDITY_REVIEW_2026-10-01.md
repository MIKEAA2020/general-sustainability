## 3. Hypothesis-checked findings

### A. **False numerical ceiling already in the v13 source and live v16** — `17^4→at most 545` *per level*

`scale-prop:pbvi`, source v13's corresponding proposition, and both abstracts describe the four-step 4-cube alpha-set as deduplicating to **at most 545 distinct outcome vectors per level** on `z0∈{1.0,1.1,…,2.5}`. With **the paper's own 17 actions**, 16 hidden branches, deterministic drift `−1/2+⟨u,θ⟩/5`, blind length-4 sequences and floor `z≥1` checked after every step, exhaustive enumeration at `z0=2.2` (`L=12` tenths above the floor) yields **857** distinct binary survival vectors, not ≤545. A separate scalar exact-integer enumeration confirmed the NumPy result. At `z0=2.5` (`L=15`), the published **545** is correct; it is not the maximum over levels. Full 16-level counts are in `alpha_run.log`. **Classification:** false-as-stated universal numerical bound, not merely an unformalized interpretation. The 84 alpha-versus-trajectory *value equalities at their seven beliefs/three sampled levels* are not refuted by this count.

### B. **False numerical ceiling newly added in live v16** — `33^4→at most 11,145` *per level*

Under the same blind policy/floor/horizon rules, with the 5-cube's **33 actions**, 32 cells and drift tenths `5−4h` for sign actions (hold `−5`), exhaustive 16-level enumeration gives **15,929** distinct survival masks at `z0=2.3` (`L=13`), and **14,649** at `z0=2.2` (`L=12`). The printed **11,145** is correct at the final level `z0=2.5` but **not** an upper bound. Run `python3 content_audit/scientific_validity/ebc/alpha_check.py`. No EBC Lean theorem or published v13 script contains this added four-step ceiling. **Classification:** false-as-stated live numeric claim, directly refuted under every declared instance hypothesis; retain correct endpoint counts without generalizing them.

### C. New five-cube “proportionally more” deduction is **false**, independently of which ceiling is used

The text says raw length-4 sequences grow `14.2×` but deduplicated masks grow `20.4×`, “so deduplication buys proportionally **more**, not less.” A larger *distinct-output growth factor* than input growth factor means **less** compression per raw sequence, not more. Using the printed endpoint counts, `17^4/545≈153.25` raw sequences per 4-cube mask, versus `33^4/11145≈106.41` per 5-cube mask. Using the actual grid maxima makes it about `83,521/857≈97.46` versus `1,185,921/15,929≈74.45`, still **less**. **Classification:** false comparative inference in unformalized live prose; distinct from the valid antichain-storage scaling calculation (which counts another object).

### D. The source and live four-cube glance table has a **false formula**

The table states “matched `+3/10`; `k` mismatches: `−1/2−2k/5`; hold `−1/2`.” From the declared drift and four coordinates the correct sign-action drift is **`+3/10−2k/5`**, so at `k=0` the table's second formula gives `−1/2`, contradicting the matched entry and the verifier's actually correct drift table. This is a table/copy defect, not a counterexample to `lem:pairsum` or the four-cube viability classification.

### E. Five-cube maximal-set classification is *substantially supported*, but the claimed script/proof provenance overreaches

Independent enumeration finds the manuscript's **192** maximal pair-graph cliques: **32** six-vertex balls, **80** tetrahedra (six pairwise distances 2), **80** square faces (distances `1,1,1,1,2,2`). All **912** smaller cliques lie in balls. For every sign action a tetrahedron's four-branch drift sum is ≤`−4` tenths (hold: `−20`), hence no finite starting slack sustains it — a true **uncapped** impossibility. Each ball survives from the floor under its center action. For a square, every first action gives at least one branch drift ≤`−3`, so `L<3` fails immediately; alternating opposite corners has ≥`−3` transient dip and **`+2`** tenths net for *each* branch per two steps, so `L≥3` survives indefinitely. These direct facts confirm the printed 32/80 orbit classification and **`3·32+13·112=1,552`** antichain count on the stated 16 levels; do not call this true classification false because the published coverage is missing.

Nevertheless, the live “every number is regenerated by one deterministic [standard-library] script” is **not a statement the source v13 verifier supports**: that script predates this section and does not compute the 80-square orbit, the 1,552 count or 5-cube four-step masks. Workspace `p5/ebc_solver.py` is a NumPy/networkx-dependent **module**, not the advertised source-matched deposited one-script verification record; its capped-state transition rejects overflow instead of saturating, so cap stability at 16/40 is not itself a proof about unbounded stock. The elementary uncapped orbit reasoning above closes the specific classification gap without relying on that capped solver. **Classification:** proof/verification-provenance overstatement, not a finding against the classification itself.
