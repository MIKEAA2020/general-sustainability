Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript **“Certifying threshold management in one dimension: closed-loop robust kernels for the Northern cod limit reference point and the Edwards Aquifer index well”** for publication in your journal.

The paper develops an analytic framework for a policy question often approached only through simulation: whether a threshold can be maintained for every disturbance in a declared model class. For strictly increasing one-dimensional maps, we characterize the finite- and infinite-horizon closed-loop viability kernels and derive the constructive and vacuity margins for a surplus-production stock and the attractor criterion for an affine aquifer model. We then apply the results to Northern cod and the Edwards Aquifer under protocols frozen before the numerical evaluation.

The substantive contribution is not simply a second set of simulations. An exact backward recursion computes the robust kernel under bounded model defect and makes clear when the registered Lipschitz-inflation certificate is conservative, when it is exact, and when staged or zero-pumping controls create boundary-only exceptions. The cod application shows catch-sensitive exact horizons across three residual-floor classes; the Edwards application distinguishes a physically securable 618-ft threshold from an institutional 660-ft trigger that no admissible rule can certify under the declared floors. Stochastic and bootstrap evidence is reported alongside the robust results, not as a substitute for them.

The article is accompanied by an 18-page Supplementary Information document with full data panels, kernel tables, protocols and verification records. The source package contains the locked inputs, archived campaign artefacts, independent re-derivation core, table/figure generators and a verifier. The independent core passes 926 comparisons with the registered campaign outputs, and the final full verifier run passes 77/77 checks, including text checks against the compiled article and supplement.

The manuscript is single-authored. The author reports no conflicts of interest and no external funding. Generative-tool assistance in drafting and code preparation is disclosed in the manuscript's Declarations; all quantitative claims are reproducible from the distributed source package.

Thank you for your consideration.
