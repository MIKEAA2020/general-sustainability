## 2. Independent manuscript validity and reproducibility

The deposited v17 verification script (Git blob `df79a331d59f612057b525b1df744b46c6941ae0`) was rerun with its exact seven Git-blob-verified lineage scripts, the source TeX, and five referenced vector figures in a local staging directory: **55/55 top-level checks passed and 57/57 chained checks passed**. The figure/data generator (`758289d65f192bc94b4e9bee914c04955033d83a`) also passed **6/6**. This confirms the specific enumerations and their *implemented policy semantics*, but is not a validation of every sentence: its text layer checks that selected strings are present, not that incompatible sentences agree. The independent exact-arithmetic `content_audit/scientific_validity/ws/witnesses.py` reproduces the counterchecks below, including an independent enumeration of all 32 aggregate and 512 full-information stationary policies.

### A. False endpoint obstruction in `prop:bench` — substantive theorem wording

Upstream lines 947–972 (live derivative same proposition): assume `Y≥2` and both caps nonnegative. `Y=27/5` satisfies those assumptions, with `(cap₁,cap₂)=(29/25,21/25)`, sum `2`, and admissible input `u=(29/25,21/25)`. The proposition correctly says feasibility holds **iff** `Y≤27/5`, then incorrectly says the dual measure **certifies obstruction for `Y≥27/5`**; at equality its displayed margin is `0`, not a strict infeasibility certificate. The introduction and table correctly call the endpoint *feasible, boundary-critical*. **Repair:** certify obstruction only for `Y>27/5`; at equality retain the zero dual bound as a boundary identity, not an obstruction. This is not covered by WS Lean. The script's `P2` checks the correct feasibility and sign but does not reject the inclusive obstruction sentence.

### B. `prop:helly` sharpness proof is false as a dimension-uniform example

Upstream lines 1061–1083 claim `m+1` is tight for all dimensions and give `{u_i≥1 (i=1,…,m), Σu_i≤1}` as the general infeasible example. For **`m=1`**, this is `{u≥1,u≤1}`, feasible at `u=1`. The upper-bound theorem from finite Helly is sound; the exhibited sharp example is valid at `m=2` (the exact case `P5` tests) but not for all `m≥1`. **Repair:** use `Σu_i≤m−1` in the dimension-uniform construction; omit any one of the `m+1` constraints to get a feasible point, but all together demand `m≤m−1`. The proof's appeal to compact convex intersections is also unnecessarily stronger than the finite Helly theorem and the proposition's merely convex-domain hypothesis. No WS Lean Helly theorem exists.

### C. Verified stationary result contradicts verification-method prose

Upstream `prop:stationary`, lines 254–276, and deposited `P17` agree: the union of all 32 aggregate stationary policies' safe-pair sets is **empty**, despite a 26-pair aggregate kernel attained by time-varying policies; the 512 full-information stationary policies' union equals its 28-pair kernel. Yet upstream verification methods lines 1219–1224 (live v2 around 1312–1318) say the 32 and 512 stationary-policy enumerations **“reproduce the kernels exactly (memoryless determinacy on the instance)”**. That is false for the aggregate class. `P18` independently computes the 26-pair kernel by the horizon fixpoint, so the issue is prose conflating two distinct tests, not a failed enumeration. **Repair:** say `P17` shows the *contrast* (empty aggregate union, exact full-information union); `P18` reproduces all four kernel sets by fixpoint. Restrict memoryless sufficiency to the full-information case.

### D. Figure timing caption contradicts the 93-cell grid and scripts

Upstream Figure `fig:timing` lines 771–778 (retained in derivative v1 and v2) says strict `Tobs<τ*` fails at **exactly one** cell `(2,1)`, and strict `Tobs<σ*` fails on **all six viable cells**. On its own published grid `z0∈{1.0,…,4.0}` by tenths, `Tobs∈{1,2,3}`, there are **33 viable cells**; strict-real fails on **three** boundary cells `(2,1),(3,2),(4,3)`; strict-floored loses **21** viable cells. Both main text lines 630–648 and deposited `M6/M6b` already say 3/21. **Repair caption, not table or proven inclusive identity.** This is exactly a prose-versus-own-computation failure not caught by string-presence checks.

### E. Two further statement/scope discrepancies

- Upstream §design rules lines 1188–1192 and derivative v2 call the sum-index fibres ones that **“each mix states of conflicting [safe] verdict.”** In the stated `X={0,1,2,3}²` with state safety `z₁,z₂≥1`, sum fibres `4`, `5`, and `6` are *entirely safe*; sum `3` mixes safe and unsafe states. Narrow to **some** fibres. Distinguish state-safety from kernel viability; a `26`-versus-`28` policy-kernel count does not prove every observation cell crosses the **state-safety** boundary.
- Upstream `prop:regime` line 697 says the three-regime DP equals path enumeration **“all horizons to six”**; deposited `P21` uses `range(1,6)` (horizons **one through five**). The recurrence may well be valid more generally, but the advertised six-horizon computational coverage is not reproduced by that specific test. Either extend and rerun the test through six or narrow the computational claim. The timing proof's phrase “first violation occurring at `z0`” (line 668) also confuses a stock level with time: for `z(t)=z0−t` and a closed floor at `1`, the infimum of *strict violation times* is `z0−1`, with actual violation immediately after; the boundary itself is safe.

### F. New derivative prose conflicts with the proved observation axis

The literature paragraph added **only in the derivative v2** around lines 180–184 says *“the answer is not monotone in the observation structure”*. That directly opposes `prop:master` (ii) and the genuine Lean `Wk_mono_obs`, **when policy and register semantics are held fixed and observation is refined**. It may intend to describe the incomparable *mixed policy/observation* middle cells; if so, say exactly that rather than asserting nonmonotonicity in the observation axis. The upstream v17 and derivative v1 do not contain this paragraph. This is an important new-section-boundary inconsistency, not an inherited upstream proof failure.
