## One substantive missing companion: computational certification

The reviewed `paper03_computational_certification_v16.tex` refers to supplementary Steps 1–6, Step 5b's label-continuity argument, and the verification record. Before this repair, **no computational-certification supplementary file** existed in `papers/` (checked filenames and proof text). Source-year `paper2_computational_certification_supplementary_v6.tex` contains 23,017 bytes: S1 complete proofs (robust rows, value/nonviability, continuous-to-finite Steps 1–6 including the exact atomic dual and label continuity) and S2 the exact-witness/solver verification record. Its four-page PDF is 101,233 bytes. Only 4.23% of the supplement's normalized eight-word windows occur in the live *main article*—appropriately, since these are companion-only proofs, not omitted main-text paragraphs.

The older files are **not interchangeable**: source v4/v5 are named `paper2_computational_certification_v4_supplementary.tex` and `_v5_supplementary.tex`; v6 is `paper2_computational_certification_supplementary_v6.tex`. The v5→v6 diff corrects the time-grid upper bound, explicitly includes the widths of stored-scenario approximations in completeness, and replaces an inapplicable sup-norm label-continuity assumption for step kernels with an L¹ bound and its modulus. Main source v17→v20 retains the mathematical bridge and adds/updates references, a mechanization-scope paragraph and code pointer; its theorem still refers to these supplementary steps. The reviewed main's own proof explicitly uses the L¹ version. Thus **v6**, not a superseded proof, is the traceable companion.

**Repair:** restored the source-year v6 TeX and matching PDF byte-for-byte as `paper03_computational_certification_v16_supplementary.tex` and `.pdf`; added a short association paragraph in the reviewed main (with the exact historical source identity recorded here), without changing its theorem, proof, abstract, data, or results. SHA-256 of restored TeX: `d72a5fb2f5046aaa44d91c414ebcdee0591ba1b563f1c774bab76b5b39293a0a`; PDF: `4a84f8494f8d61d7b900164bbca055d0d53cef502455ca303018b2c1ec0d968f`. The supplement's September 26 historical title-page date is preserved, not silently updated to the reviewed article date. This restoration verifies **presence and provenance**; S2's historical 9/9 and 51/51 claims were not independently rerun in this review, and TeX compilation was not available here.
