## 2. New mathematical content

* **Proposition 3.2 (the value of observation).** (i) `W^bl_k ⊆ W^fb_k`, strict in general even under the hypotheses of the support identity; (ii) if every singleton of the support is blind-survivable then `V^seq_k(b) − V^ol_k(b) ≤ 1 − max_x b(x)`; (iii) the bound is attained for every support size `n ≥ 2` by an explicit family `F_n` (2n safe states, n+1 actions) with `V^seq_2 = 1`, `V^ol_2 = max b`, value of observation `1 − 1/n` at the uniform prior. v15 only noted the inclusion. The feedback/blind distinction is thereby a result, which matters because the Lean layer refuted an earlier blind reading of the support theorem.
* **Theorem 3.3 (antichain) — attainment.** The Sperner bound `C(n, ⌊n/2⌋)` is attained (n = 4: six maximal survivable sets); v15 stated the bound only.
* **Theorem 5.2 / Corollary 5.3 / Corollary 5.5.** The class lattice is stated with both gaps as explicit identities on disjoint cells (eqs. (10)–(11)); the class scope of the timing obstruction, a remark in v15, is a corollary with proof (the two classes differ exactly on `{2 ≤ z0 < 1 + min(k, T_obs)}`, 36 combinations on the certified grid); the certified grid corollary adds the deficit profile (0 before the falling branch's exit, ½ after) and the kernel/witness counts.
* **Theorem 6.2 (exact threshold of a hold–probe–learn schedule).** The exact threshold `1 + max_θ max{0, −T d(u0,θ), −T d(u0,θ) − d(up,θ)}` is now the theorem; the additive law `1 + T d0 + e_p` is its corollary under a common worst-case regime. **Example 6.3** gives two aligned instances (`21/10 + T/10`, `41/20 + 3T/20`) and a **misaligned instance** whose exact threshold is `1 + max(T, 1)` while the additive formula gives `2 + T` (at `T = 1`: 2 versus 3). **Remark 6.4** reads the delayed instance of §5 as a schedule with `d0 = 1`, `e_p = 0`.
* **Proposition 7.3 (probe-count bound)** now tabulates the exact wrong-majority probabilities `1/10, 7/250, 107/12500, 341/125000, 22273/25000000` (n = 1, 3, 5, 7, 9) against `(3/5)^n`, and the SI records the empirical decay rate (≈ 0.59 per reading between n = 1 and n = 9) against the bound's `0.6`. **Remark 7.4** fixes the noiseless limit.
* **Proposition 8.1 (contamination)** carries the explicit two-stage counterexample to affine interpolation (`V_2^{1/2} = 1/16` against the affine mix `1/8`; `V_k^ρ = ((1−ρ)/2)^k`) and the correct convexity statement (the value is convex, not concave: midpoint ½ against vertex average 1). **Proposition 8.2 (prior contamination on the delayed class)** is new: `V = (1−ρ) max(w, 1−w)` on the band `1 ≤ z0 < 1 + ℓ`.
* **Examples 4.2–4.3.** The two-floor instance (chance certificate `δ = ½` attained; `V_k = max(b0, b1)` for all `k ≥ 1`) and the asymmetric audit (`w = 3/10`: value `7/10`, deficit `3/10 = min mass`) are examples with proofs rather than campaign records.

## 3. Statements corrected or sharpened relative to v15

* The support identity is stated with the zero-mass convention in the statement itself (unnormalised step mass; outcomes of zero probability carry empty support), matching the Lean declaration `support_identity`.
* The belief-state recursion is a proposition (standard; Åström, Smallwood–Sondik), not a theorem.
* The "certificate–value agreement theorem" of v15 was a restatement of the closed forms on the grid; it is now Corollary 5.5 of Theorem 5.1.
* The sensor-instance claim is stated once and correctly: the reading-own map loses `ε` at every prior but the **optimised** deficit is `min{ε, b1, b2}`; at `(99/100, 1/100)` the optimal map is constant and the deficit `1/100` is below `ε`.
* The contamination operator's conventions (continuation at nominally null outcomes; rectangular stress operator, not a coherent perturbed joint law) are in SI S6 rather than inside the proposition statement.
* The paragraph cross-checking the minimax companion's interpolation record has been removed: it could not be regenerated from this paper's scripts.
* The claims about the Lean layer are stated with their exact scope (support identity under deterministic kernels, freeze count for the specified families, rationality/convexity of α-vectors; nine `native_decide` axiom names declared); the Sperner bound, the closed forms as such, Theorem 6.2, the noisy-probe formulas and the probe-count bound are enumerated, not formalised.
