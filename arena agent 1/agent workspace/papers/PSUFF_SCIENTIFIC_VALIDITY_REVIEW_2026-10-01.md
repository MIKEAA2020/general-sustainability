## 3. Finding classification with all printed hypotheses checked

### A. `prop:pathdeficit`'s optimized **sensor** loss is **false for every prior** (fixed-policy identity remains true)

The proposition correctly writes `Δ₁(b)=Σ_x b(x)p_x` for an *optimizing* per-reading action map, but its two-state example then claims `Δ₁(b)=ε` **“for every prior”**, saying **“no map does better.”** Under its own two safe states `x₁,x₂`, action `a_i` safe only at `x_i`, normalized sensor error `ε=1/20`, and arbitrary deterministic reading-to-action maps, take prior `b=(99/100,1/100)`. Ignore the reading and always play `a₁`. This admissible deterministic per-reading map loses only state `x₂`, so its deficit is **`1/100 < ε`**; enumerating all four maps confirms the optimum is `1/100`. The reading-own map indeed loses `ε` for *all* priors, but is optimal only where neither prior mass is smaller than `ε`; for `ε<1/2` the optimized loss is `min(ε,b(x₁),b(x₂))`. The manuscript's **symmetric** `b=(1/2,1/2),ε=1/20` counterexample to a universal min-mass bound remains valid. The master test sets `sensor_V1(w,eps)=1−eps` by **definition**, rather than maximizing over maps, explaining its green result. Lean's `path_deficit` is fixed-action and unaffected. **Classification:** false quantified optimized-example claim, not a false fixed-policy weighted-path identity.

### B. `prop:dr` (ii) **stagewise affine interpolation** is **false as stated**

Use exactly the paper's finite absorbing model: one safe state `s`, absorbing `⊥`, **one** action, `T(s|s)=T(⊥|s)=1/2`, `T(⊥|⊥)=1`, observation `y_s` after safe transition and distinguished `y_⊥` after exit (both observations have **positive nominal probability** from `s`), initial belief `δ_s`, horizon `k=2`, contamination radius `ρ=1/2`. Every row and observation law is normalized and rational, and the bottom cannot revive. Applying the **proposition's own recurrence** gives `V_k^ρ(δ_s)=[(1−ρ)/2]^k`: hence **`V_2^{1/2}=1/16`**, whereas `(1−ρ)V_2^0+ρ V_2^1=(1/2)(1/4)+(1/2)(0)=1/8`. One-stage identity (i) remains correct. The failure is multiplication of stagewise contaminated survival factors, not an action-selection artifact. No Lean theorem states (ii). **Classification:** false general proposition under all its model hypotheses; its separate prior-contamination formula on the held two-branch example is a narrower setup, not proof of stagewise linearity.

### C. `prop:dr` (iii) **concavity** is false even at `ρ=0` and contradicts `prop:pl`

The proposition asserts `V_k^ρ` **concave** and piecewise linear in belief for every `ρ∈[0,1]`. The same manuscript's two-floor one-step example has `V_1^0(b)=max(b(x₁),b(x₂))`. It equals `1` at both pure beliefs and `1/2` at their midpoint, violating the concavity inequality `V(mid)≥[V(e₁)+V(e₂)]/2=1`. It is **convex** piecewise linear, as `prop:pl` states and `Formalizations.P3.VAdm_convex` proves for the nominal case. Do not globally replace “concave” by “convex” for all `ρ` without separately establishing the robust Bellman operator; this witness refutes the asserted universal concavity only. **Classification:** false as stated and internally contradictory, unformalized as concavity.

### D. `prop:additivelaw` **sum of independent worst drifts** is not an iff

The stated assumptions permit `Θ={−1,+1}`, fixed hold action with `(c,h)=(-1/2,+1/2)`, fixed probe action with `(c,h)=(-1/2,−1/2)`, and post-learning action with `(c,h)=(+1,0)` (so `μ=1>0`). Take one unit hold interval `T=1`, a unit probe step, stock floor `1`. Worst hold loss `d₀=1` occurs at `θ=−1`; worst probe excursion `e_p=1` occurs at `θ=+1`. The proposition calls `z₀≥1+Td₀+e_p=3` **necessary**. But at **`z₀=2`** the exact stock paths through hold/probe are `θ=−1: (2,1,1)` and `θ=+1: (2,2,1)`; the learned action then rises forever on both. All policy, finite-parameter, additive-drift, pinned-probe and positive-post-learn hypotheses are satisfied. The stated right-hand side is a sufficient conservative charge here, not necessary: the two maxima cannot be simultaneously realized by one fixed hidden parameter. The proof's claim that “some θ realizes” *both* maxima is the invalid step. Neither the reported first/second instance, where special aligned/constant drifts may make the sum exact, nor Lean formalizes this general proposition. **Classification:** false-as-stated general iff, specific calibrated deadlines not refuted.

### E. Smaller domain/notation item (not a refutation of the repaired support theorem)

`thm:support` prints a posterior-support equality **“for every action and observation”**, while Bayes' posterior `b⁺(·|a,y)` is not defined by ordinary normalization if `P(y|b,a)=0`. Its actual proof uses the *unnormalized* observation-weighted successor (as `Formalizations.P3.support_identity` does), where the zero observation has empty support. Require positive-probability observations or explicitly name this zero-mass convention. The value-one iff uses only observations with positive probability and survives this qualification. **Classification:** under-specified display domain, distinct from the older blind/feedback mismatch already fixed in the current manuscript.
