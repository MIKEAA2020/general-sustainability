\section{Edwards replays, closed loop, finite floors and floor-class supply}\label{si:edw-replays}
Table~\ref{tab:si-edw-replays} collects the 1951--1956 replays from the observed 1950 head, the closed-loop end and minimum heads for 1934--1990 and 1990--2023, the finite-duration floors (the smallest head from which $n$ consecutive 1956 years are absorbed) and the floor-class supply (57 years of recharge pinned at the 1956 floor from the 1934 head). The observed 1956 annual mean is $623.15\ft$ (daily minimum $612.51\ft$); the model with the actual 1950s pumping reaches $631.2\ft$, a bias of $8.1\ft$ in the unsafe direction and within the declared defect.
