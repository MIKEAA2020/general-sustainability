$x_t$, $u_t$, $w_t$ & state, control (catch $C_t$ or pumping $P_t$), driver (residual $e_t$ or recharge $R_t$) \\
$F(x,u,w)$ & registered one-step map; $\underline F(x,u)=F(x,u,\underline w)$ its worst-floor map \\
$\safe=[\LRP,\bar x]$ & safe set: states at or above the threshold $\LRP$ and at or below the domain ceiling $\bar x$ (10\,000 kt; 710 ft) \\

$s_-$ & smallest fixed point of the worst-floor map in the safe set \\
$\Tempty$ & first horizon at which a kernel is empty (``first empty''); the last non-empty horizon is $\Tempty-1$ \\
$\varepsilon$ & declared uniform defect: largest absolute fit residual ($328.97\kt$; $15.41\ft$); out-of-sample defect $46.7\kt$; $21.81\ft$ \\
$r_T$, $r_\infty$ & Lipschitz accumulation $\varepsilon\sum_{k<T}L^k$ with $L=F'(\LRP)$ (cod) or $L=a$ (Edwards); $r_\infty=\varepsilon/(1-a)$ \\
$W^{\varepsilon}_T$ & exact bounded-defect kernel (Theorem~\mref{thm:exact}) \\
$\Tcert$ & certified horizon: last $T$ with $W^{\varepsilon}_T\neq\emptyset$ (exact) or with the Lipschitz certificate non-empty \\
