\section{Locked data panels and residuals}\label{si:data}
Tables~\ref{tab:si-cod-data} and~\ref{tab:si-edw-data} print the two panels exactly as read by the core, with the one-step residuals of the registered maps. The cod panel is the NCAM M-shift spawning stock biomass of the 2016 assessment (Table~A2, 1983--2015) with the Schijns et al.\ (2021) annual landings; the fit window is 1983--2007 (24 transitions) and the audit window 2008--2015 (8 transitions). The Edwards panel is the J-17 annual mean elevation, the USGS total recharge and the Edwards Aquifer Authority's total well pumping, 1934--2023; the fit window is 1935--1990 (56 transitions) and the audit window 1991--2023 (33 transitions). The 2024 reassessment series (xteNCAM, 1954--2024) is used only for the recent-window checks of Section~\ref{si:ident} and is not pooled with the 2016 series.
