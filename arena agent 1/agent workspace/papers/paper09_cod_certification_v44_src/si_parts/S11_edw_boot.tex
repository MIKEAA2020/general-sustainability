\section{Edwards residual bootstrap of the attractors}\label{si:edw-boot}
Table~\ref{tab:si-edw-boot} gives the archived residual bootstrap of the 1934--1990 fit ($B=5000$, seed 20260905) and the independent re-run in the core (seed 20261015): the bootstrap mean, standard deviation and 90\% interval of each rule's worst-floor attractor and the probability that it clears 618 ft. No replicate is non-contractive, $\gamma<0$ in every replicate, and the flat-90\% minus BAU gap is positive in every replicate (minimum $0.5\ft$, 90\% interval $[1.9,5.1]\ft$). The ratios $\beta/(1-a)$ and $\gamma/(1-a)$ have bootstrap coefficients of variation of $0.25$ and $0.30$; the ordering of the rules depends only on the sign of $\gamma$, the level of the attractors on the ratios.
