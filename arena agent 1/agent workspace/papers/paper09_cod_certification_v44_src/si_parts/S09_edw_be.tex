\section{Edwards bounded-defect audit}\label{si:edw-be}
Table~\ref{tab:si-edw-be} gives the exact bounded-defect kernel boundaries at 618 ft under the worst floor (Theorem~\mref{thm:exact}) for the declared defect ($15.41\ft$, fit window) and the out-of-sample defect ($21.81\ft$), and the supremum uniform defect that each horizon can carry. For the constant-pumping rules (affine closed-loop maps) the exact boundaries coincide with the Lipschitz-certified boundaries of Section~\ref{si:edw-kernels} wherever the recursion binds (Proposition~\mref{prop:lip}(ii)). The departures are listed in the table: for zero pumping at $T=1,2$ the certificate's inflation of the initial state binds instead of the recursion; for the Stage-I rule ($T=3$) and the cascade ($T=2,3$) the closed-loop map is piecewise affine and the exact kernel is larger by $2$--$7$ ft. No departure changes a horizon. At $T=3$ the supremum defects are $16.01$ ft for BAU, $16.71$ ft for Stage I, $17.23$ ft for the cascade, $17.62$ ft for flat 80\% and $24.04$ ft for zero pumping; at $T=4$ zero pumping alone retains a nonempty kernel, up to $17.93$ ft. The $21.81$-ft out-of-sample defect leaves only zero pumping certified through $T=3$, and even that rule fails at $T=4$.
