\section{Cod identification: profile in $K$, recent windows, alternative forms}\label{si:ident}
Table~\ref{tab:si-profile} profiles the one-step least-squares fit over the carrying capacity $K$ with $r$ in closed form at each $K$, and reports the two profile cuts (one-parameter $F$ cut $1.1955$ and two-parameter cut $1.313$ at the 95\% level with $n=24$), the implied sets of $K$ and the ranges of $g(\LRP)$, $F'(\LRP)$ and $\Cstar$ over them. Table~\ref{tab:si-windows} refits the map on post-moratorium windows of the 2016 series and on windows of the 2024 reassessment series with its own reference point ($276\kt$); the archived recent-window campaign stopped its optimiser at the box edge $K=5000$ for 1995--2015, where the unconstrained minimiser is $K=4316.6$ ($\Cstar=165.7$ against $171.4\kt$ at the edge), and both are reported. Table~\ref{tab:si-forms} gives the Fox and Allee alternatives. The observation-error sensitivity of the archived identification campaign (a share $\lambda$ of the residual variance attributed to observation error, which shrinks the floors) is reported in the notes to Table~\ref{tab:si-boot}: the vacuous worst-floor class disappears for $\lambda\ge0.2$, and at $\lambda=0.5$ the maximal robust flat catch is $112.1\kt$.
