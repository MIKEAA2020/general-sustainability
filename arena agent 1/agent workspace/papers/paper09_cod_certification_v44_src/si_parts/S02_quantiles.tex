\section{Cod floor classes under alternative quantile conventions}\label{si:quantiles}
The three floor classes are the minimum, the 5th and the 10th percentile of the 24 fit residuals (linear interpolation, the registered convention). Table~\ref{tab:si-quantiles} recomputes the two percentile floors under five conventions and shows the implied constants. The q05 floor is the one sensitive to the convention: with $n=24$ the 5th percentile falls between the two collapse residuals ($-329.0$ and $-323.5\kt$) and the third-smallest ($-82.6\kt$), so interpolating conventions place it anywhere in that gap and the ``lower'' convention returns the second-smallest residual itself. The q10 floor is stable to within $26\kt$ under all conventions except Weibull. None of the main-text verdicts under the worst and q10 floors depends on the convention; the q05 column of Table~\mref{tab:cod-kernels} does, and is reported for that reason.
