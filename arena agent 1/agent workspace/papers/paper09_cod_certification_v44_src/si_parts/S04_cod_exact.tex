\section{Cod exact bounded-defect kernels}\label{si:cod-exact}
Table~\ref{tab:si-cod-exact} gives the lower boundaries of the exact bounded-defect kernel of Theorem~\mref{thm:exact} with the declared defect $\varepsilon=329.0\kt$ on top of each floor class, at horizons $T=1,\dots,20$, together with the last non-empty horizon and the last non-empty horizon of the Lipschitz certificate for comparison. Under the worst floor the recursion is $W_{t+1}=\{S\ge\LRP:\ F(S)-|e_{\min}|-\varepsilon\ \ge\ b_t\}$ for a flat rule, so the $T=1$ boundary solves $F(S)-5=\LRP+2|e_{\min}|$ for BAU; the boundaries grow because the map is expansive on the kernel and the kernel becomes empty when the boundary passes the $10\,000$-kt ceiling. The Lipschitz certificate (Proposition~\mref{prop:lip}) inflates the threshold by $r_T=\varepsilon\,(F'(\LRP)^T-1)/(F'(\LRP)-1)$ instead and is empty two to twenty horizons earlier, because it pays for the worst excursion at every step rather than for the excursions the clamped state can make.
