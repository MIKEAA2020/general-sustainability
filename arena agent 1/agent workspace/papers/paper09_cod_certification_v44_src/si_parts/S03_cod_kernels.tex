\section{Cod nominal kernels: full horizon grid and rule supplies}\label{si:cod-kernels}
Table~\ref{tab:si-cod-kernels} gives the lower boundary of the nominal closed-loop kernel of the safe set $[\LRP,10\,000]$ kt for every rule of the registered family, every floor class and the horizons $T\in\{1,2,3,5,8,10,15,20,\infty\}$, computed by the exact backward recursion of Theorem~\mref{thm:kernel} on the worst-floor map. The Stage-I rule, the cascade and the flat 60-kt cap have identical rows under every floor (Proposition~\mref{prop:stage}), because the kernels live in the safe set, where all three take 60 kt; the two graded rules differ from them only through their higher stages above $1.25\,\LRP$.

\paragraph{Supply on the observed states.} On the 1983--2007 states the mean catch prescribed by the rules is $5.0\kt$ for BAU, $10.0\kt$ for the Stage-I rule (cut active in 20 of 24 years, $83\%$), $16.3\kt$ for the cascade, and $60\kt$ for the flat 60-kt cap. The staged rules therefore fail clause~(b) of the retention rule against the flat cap they match in protection: they are strictly less productive than it on the record, not more, because the record lies below the LRP in 83\% of years (20 of 24), where the Stage-I rule takes nothing and the cascade takes 30, 5 or 0 kt.
