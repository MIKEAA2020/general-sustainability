\section{Alpha-set tables and values at probed beliefs}\label{S:alpha}

Tables~\ref{tab:S6a4}--\ref{tab:S6a5} give $|\calA_k(L)|$ for $k=1,\dots,4$ and $L=0,\dots,15$ at $m=4$ ($17^k$ words) and $m=5$ ($33^k$ words), with the number of masks that are blind-survivable sets in parentheses; the $k=4$ columns reproduce the counts of the earlier scripts (\texttt{fivecube\_alpha\_check}). Tables~\ref{tab:S6v4}--\ref{tab:S6v5} give, for seven beliefs, the four finite-horizon values $\mathcal V^{(1)}_L/\mathcal V^{(2)}_L/\mathcal V^{(3)}_L/\mathcal V^{(4)}_L$ followed by the indefinite value $\mathcal V_L$. The beliefs: uniform; a far pair (mass $\tfr12$ on a cell and on a cell at distance $m-2$); an antipodal pair; a radius-one ball (mass $\tfr12$ spread uniformly) plus its antipode (mass $\tfr12$); three random beliefs with integer weights in $\{1,\dots,20\}$ drawn with \texttt{random.Random(7)} (the first three of the five computed). The equality $\mathcal V^{(4)}_L=\mathcal V_L$ holds for all beliefs at $L\le3$ at $m=4$ and at $L\le4$ at $m=5$ in these tables and fails at the next level, consistent with main Proposition~\mref{prop:pbvi}: at $m=5$ no non-survivable set outlives $2\lfloor(L-1)/2\rfloor$ steps, which is below $4$ for $L\le4$.
