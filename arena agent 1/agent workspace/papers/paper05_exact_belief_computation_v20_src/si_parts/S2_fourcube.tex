\subsection{The four-cube}
$G_4$ is the four-cube graph; its $32$ maximal cliques are the edges. The maximal survivable sets are the $32$ adjacent pairs ($V=3$, $\Lstar=1$ certified: the saturated game at cap $6$ has the antichain $\{(0,1),(1,0)\}$, and $(0,0)$ is lost in the first step). Table~\ref{tab:S2max} is the certificate record; the census is in Table~S4.4 together with the other cubes. The adjacent pair $\{a,b\}$ from $L=1$ under the alternating word $a,b,a,b,\dots$ has the slack trajectories $1,4,3,6,5,\dots$ and $1,0,3,2,5,\dots$; the matched action gives $+3$, the mismatched $-1$.
