## Scientific development

1. **The mathematical frame is stated as a research contribution.** The main text develops closed-form threshold kernels for strictly increasing one-dimensional maps and states the conditions under which the viable set is the whole safe set, a half-line beginning at the smallest fixed point, or empty after a finite horizon. The cod corollary makes the constructive and vacuity margins explicit; the Edwards corollary gives the worst-floor attractor and finite-horizon boundary.
2. **Model defect is treated by an exact recursion, not only by threshold inflation.** The exact bounded-defect kernel is computed alongside the registered Lipschitz certificate. For cod, where the map is expansive at the LRP but its slope falls away from it, the exact horizons are catch-sensitive and materially longer. The article distinguishes boundary equality for affine maps when the backward recursion binds from the finite exceptions caused by the inflated initial-state constraint and by piecewise-affine staged rules.
3. **Cod horizons are reported by floor and policy.** Exact horizons (worst/q05/q10) are 12/13/27 years for BAU, 12/13/28 for zero catch, 10/11/20 for 60 kt, 9/10/16 for 120 kt, 8/9/14 for 180 kt, and 8/8/12 for 240 kt. The registered Lipschitz certificate gives 5–7 years across the policy/floor grid. The prose and abstract now distinguish the 8–13-year worst/q05 range from the 12–28-year q10 range rather than implying that 13 years is a global maximum.
4. **The Edwards horizon exception is made explicit.** At 618 ft, positive-pumping rules have a three-year horizon under the declared defect and two under the audited out-of-sample defect; zero pumping retains four and three years, respectively. The text no longer says that every rule falls to two years. The exact-versus-Lipschitz departures at zero pumping for T=1,2 and at the staged-rule boundaries are documented without implying that any departure adds a horizon.
5. **Uncertainty and supply are presented as complementary evidence.** The paper distinguishes robust certification from Monte Carlo survival and bootstrap uncertainty, and relates the threshold margin to the supply retained by the staged rules. The independent core reruns the stochastic and bootstrap layers with a second seed and compares its re-derivation with the registered results.
6. **Identification claims are made numerically auditable.** The exact minimizer of the 1995–2015 cod fit is reported at K=4316.6 kt (C*=165.7 kt), separately from the archived K=5000-kt box-edge result (C*=171.4 kt). For the proportional family, exact fixed points are distinguished from finite-horizon proxies (1092.0/1751.8 kt versus 1074.8/1568.4 kt in the archived structural campaign). λ is consistently defined as the share of residual variance attributed to observation error.
7. **Out-of-sample conventions are stated consistently.** The cod audit is 2008–2015. The Edwards panel has 33 out-of-sample transitions, and closed-loop replay begins from H_1990. The staged cod policies are compared at the LRP, where Stage I and cascade equal flat 60 kt; their departures below the LRP are not presented as extra LRP protection. `first empty` is explicitly the first empty T, one more than the last nonempty horizon.
8. **The registered protocols are reproducible.** Both are dated 2026-08-26 and printed in SI Section S13. A separate core independently re-derives the results and passes 926 archive comparisons; the full 77-check verifier regenerates all tables and figures and checks headline claims in both compiled PDFs.
