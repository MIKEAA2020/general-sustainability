\section{Mathematical statements, proofs, and the review-map operator}\label{si:math}

\subsection{Forward invariance}

Let the flow on a review interval be
\[
\dot N=rN(1-N/K)-qE(t)N,\qquad
\dot Z=\{\Phi(qE(t)N-S(N))-Z\}/\tau_m,
\]
where \(r,K,q,\tau_m>0\), \(\Phi\geq0\), and \(E(t)\in[0,E_{\max}]\). At each review, the effort command is projected onto \([0,E_{\max}]\). Between reviews effort is either held at an admissible endpoint or interpolated convexly between consecutive admissible commands.

\begin{proposition}[Forward invariance of the sampled state space]\label{prop:invariance}
If \(N(0)\geq0\), \(Z(0)\geq0\), and \(E_0\in[0,E_{\max}]\), then every solution that exists remains in \(N\geq0\), \(Z\geq0\), and \(E\in[0,E_{\max}]\).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The effort statement follows from projection and convexity. For a fixed admissible effort path, the stock equation is locally Lipschitz and has the factorised form \(\dot N=N\{r(1-N/K)-qE(t)\}\). If \(N(t_0)>0\), then
\[
N(t)=N(t_0)\exp\!\left(\int_{t_0}^{t}[r(1-N(s)/K)-qE(s)]\,ds\right)>0;
\]
if \(N(t_0)=0\), uniqueness gives the zero solution. For the signal, variation of constants gives
\[
Z(t)=e^{-(t-t_0)/\tau_m}Z(t_0)+\frac{1}{\tau_m}\int_{t_0}^{t}e^{-(t-s)/\tau_m}\Phi(qE(s)N(s)-S(N(s)))\,ds\geq0.
\]
These facts hold interval by interval. Projection places each next command in the same effort interval, completing the induction. The logistic field also gives \(\dot N\leq0\) at \(N=K\), so on a finite interval \(N(t)\leq\max\{N(t_0),K\}\). No lower bound strictly above zero, persistence, viability, or stability follows from this proposition.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Finite-horizon rapid-review consistency}

\begin{lemma}[Finite-horizon rapid-review consistency]\label{lem:rapid-review}
Let \(\dot u=G(u)\), \(u=(N,Z,E)\), be the no-queue continuous system, with the effort component evolving under the same local controller law. Suppose \(G\) is continuously differentiable with bounded derivative on a compact neighbourhood of the trajectories over a fixed horizon \([0,T]\); assessments are contemporaneous and exact; projection is inactive on that neighbourhood; and no deployment queue, stochastic jump, or unmodelled reset is added. Let \(\Psi_{\Tr}\) be either the exponential frozen-assessment or forward-Euler flow-then-update map. Then, uniformly on the compact set,
\[
\Psi_{\Tr}(u)=u+\Tr G(u)+O(\Tr^2),
\]
and, for a common initial state, the sampled trajectory differs from the continuous trajectory by at most \(C_T\Tr\) at review times with \(n\Tr\leq T\). This is a fixed finite-horizon statement; it does not imply that any fixed positive \(\Tr\) has the same stability classification as the continuous system.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Taylor expansion of the held ecological flow and endpoint effort update gives the displayed one-step expansion. The exact flow of \(G\) has the same first-order expansion, so the local defect is \(O(\Tr^2)\). If \(L\) bounds the Lipschitz constant of \(G\), the global error \(\epsilon_n=u_n-u(n\Tr)\) satisfies
\[
\|\epsilon_{n+1}\|\leq(1+L\Tr)\|\epsilon_n\|+C\Tr^2.
\]
Discrete Gronwall gives \(\max_{n\Tr\leq T}\|\epsilon_n\|\leq C_T\Tr\), establishing the claimed uniform convergence at review times. No infinite-horizon convergence or finite-interval stability result follows.
\end{proof}

Let \(\widehat z_{n+1}=\widehat Z_{n+1}-Z^*\) be the endpoint assessment deviation after the ecological state has flowed for one review interval with effort held at \(E_n\). The nonlinear exponential update integrates \(d\mathcal E/ds=F_B(\mathcal E,\widehat Z_{n+1})\) for \(0\leq s\leq\Tr\), holding that assessment fixed. Linearizing this controller flow at the interior equilibrium, with \(C_E=\partial_EF_B(E^*,Z^*)\) and \(C_Z=\partial_ZF_B(E^*,Z^*)\), gives
\[
e_{n+1}^{\rm exp}=e^{C_E\Tr}e_n+C_Z\frac{e^{C_E\Tr}-1}{C_E}\widehat z_{n+1},
\]
with limiting coefficient \(C_Z\Tr\) when \(C_E=0\). The corresponding forward-Euler update is \(e_{n+1}^{\rm Euler}=e_n+\Tr(C_Ee_n+C_Z\widehat z_{n+1})\). Their difference is second order as \(\Tr\to0\), but can be material at finite \(\Tr\). In the monodromy, this post-flow command update is composed with the held ecological flow, which supplies the dependence of \(\widehat z_{n+1}\) on the pre-review state.

\subsection{Local stability and crossing conventions}

Let \(X^*=(N^*,Z^*,E^*)\) be an interior fixed point of the pre-review map \(X^-_{n+1}=\mathcal P_{\Tr}(X^-_n)\). The local multipliers are the eigenvalues of \(D\mathcal P_{\Tr}(X^*)\). Local asymptotic stability requires all multipliers to have modulus below one. A crossing is counted when a computed multiplier reaches the unit circle along the scanned interval branch. A complex-pair crossing, a real \(+1\) crossing, and a real \(-1\) crossing are distinguished; the values in this paper are numerical crossings refined by bisection and high-precision evaluation.

The baseline values at \(\Tr=1\) year are \(\rho=1.0003506565\) for exponential frozen-assessment mobilising, 1.0005452356 for forward-Euler mobilising, 0.9838095183 for exponential frozen-assessment protective, and 0.9837962340 for forward-Euler protective. The exponential-update mobilising crossing is a complex pair with eigenvalue approximately \(0.98464047\pm0.17459425i\), modulus one, and angle 0.175494 radians. Forward-Euler mobilising crosses as a complex pair at 47.535982 years and at a real multiplier \(-1\) at 79.142688 years. Forward-Euler protective crosses at a real multiplier \(-1\) at 2.306354 years. The exponential protective update has no crossing on the tested \([0.2,200]\) interval and has maximum sampled radius 0.996740. The forward-Euler crossings are properties of that distinct command update, not numerical estimates of the exponential-update boundary.
