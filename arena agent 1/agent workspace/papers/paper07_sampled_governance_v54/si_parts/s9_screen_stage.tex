\section{Screen sensitivity and stage-map reconstruction}\label{si:screen-stage}

\subsection{42-stock baseline and eleven-variant sensitivity battery}

The baseline cohort contains 42 of 58 forage-fish series with at least 20 non-missing annual SSB observations. SSB is linearly detrended; normalized Lomb--Scargle power is integrated in bands A (2.5--5 years) and B (5--9 years). Each stock-specific AR(1) null uses 200 simulations and seed 7; the empirical tail probability is \((k+1)/201\). Benjamini--Hochberg correction is applied to all 84 stock-by-band cells within each specification. In the baseline run, the minimum raw p-value is 0.0448 (PANCHNCHSP, band B) and the adjusted rejection count is zero.

The HP and first-difference variants have minimum attainable nominal p-values at 0.0100 at the retained simulation resolution; none of their cells survives BH correction. The result is a stable adjusted-zero decision under the specified battery, not a universal absence claim. The variant family includes distinct nulls and preprocessing, so it should not be interpreted as eleven independent replications.

An exploratory alternative-band check also returns zero BH-significant cells for a broad 2--10-year band and cadence bands 1--3, 3--8, and 8--20 years. An effort-proxy band of 8--80 years is unresolvable for every stock because no eligible series spans three times the upper period. These alternative-band outputs are screening checks, not preregistered tests of the institution-specific mechanism. The full per-stock SSB tables, q-values, null thresholds, and scripts are in the source bundle.

\subsection{Pre-registered two-stage reconstruction}

The original stage-map generating code, solver settings, and initial histories were not found in the repository. To avoid inventing a reconstruction as though it were the original object, a plan was frozen on 2026-09-01 before simulation. It specifies a new annual two-stage Beverton--Holt delayed-recruitment map with adults \(A\), juveniles \(J\), a recruitment lag, filtered signal \(Z\), and held effort \(E\). Ecological parameter values are in Table~\ref{tab:stage-parameters}; the exact pre-registration, code, data tables, and logs are retained. No parameter was fitted to archived response windows.

The trajectory rule uses 2,000 review steps and the last 500 as the tail. Persistent oscillation is relative tail SD of adult abundance \(\geq2\%\), weak response is 0.1--2\%, and convergence is below 0.1\%. A separate finite integer grid records the local multiplier spectral radius. These two records are not interchangeable: a finite simulated trajectory classification and a local multiplier scan answer different questions, and the discrete stage map is not the three-state logistic continuous-flow map.

The mismatch is reported without retuning. The archived window remains provisional, and a reconstruction mismatch does not adjudicate the legacy numbers because their generating object is unavailable. Full trajectory samples are \texttt{\detokenize{results/p5_stage_trajectories.csv}}; equilibrium and multiplier records are in the adjacent \texttt{\detokenize{results/}} files.

The local multiplier record at primary \(q=0.001\) has annual-review radii 0.8948 (anchovy), 0.9231 (sprat), 0.9560 (cod), and 0.9939 (slow stock). On the integer \(\Tr=1\)--50 grid, the extractive multiplier crosses above unity between years 33 and 34 for anchovy and sprat, and between 34 and 35 for cod; the slow-stock multiplier remains below unity throughout. The protective channel remains stable on the integer grid. These linear records are distinct from the finite-horizon trajectory classifier: at \(\Tr=10\), the slow-stock trajectory is labelled persistent oscillation despite a stable local multiplier, leaving bistability, a non-decayed transient, or threshold sensitivity unresolved.

At the declared sensitivity \(q=0.1\), the extractive annual multipliers exceed one for all four classes (\(\rho(1)=2.229,2.128,1.904,1.292\), respectively). On the integer grid, anchovy is below one at 19--35 years, sprat at 23--36, cod at 32--41, and slow stock remains above one; the protective channel is stable throughout. Crossings are bracketed by integer intervals, not refined to a continuous-time boundary. A declared steepness sensitivity \(h=0.6,0.9\) is retained in \texttt{\detokenize{p5_stage_h_sensitivity.csv}}; it is a local stability diagnostic, not the trajectory-classification criterion.

Under 30\% multiplicative assessment error, anchovy and sprat remain converged at their tested legacy-window cells (maximum relative tail SD 0.00086). Cod is converged at \(\Tr=1,5\) and weak at 10,20 years (maximum relative tail SD 0.00249). The slow-stock cells are converged at 5 years, weak at 10 and 20, and exceed the 2\% persistence threshold at 30--50 years (0.02126--0.02645). Because the trajectory threshold is not noise-adjusted, these long-horizon labels may reflect noise-driven variance rather than a persistent deterministic oscillation. These checks remain specific to the reconstructed plant and do not validate the archived windows.
