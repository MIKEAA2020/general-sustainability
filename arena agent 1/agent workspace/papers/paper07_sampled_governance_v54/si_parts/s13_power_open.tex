\section{Power record and unreproduced items}\label{si:power-open}

\subsection{Injected-signal power: 17 archived white-noise cells}

The committed driver specifies a sampled-governance operating model, generates annual effort, adds multiplicative observation error \(E_t^{\rm obs}=E_t\exp(\varepsilon_t)\), and tests target-band periodogram power against a per-series AR(1) red-noise null. The null uses 120 simulations and seed 7. Each power cell has 50 trial seeds 0--49. White-noise draws use \(\varepsilon_t\sim N(0,\sigma^2)\), with \(\sigma\in\{0.1,0.3\}\). The analyzed record spans \(H\) years after burn-in; the cell-specific integrator horizon is retained in the CSV.

The model cells are: sprat class \((r,g,\Tr)=(0.8,2,7)\) with target band 30--120 years; anchovy class \((1.6,1,3)\) with band 8--20 years; and a cod-labelled false-positive class \((0.3,5,5)\) with band 8--20 years. The archived 17-cell design is not a complete Cartesian grid: sprat \(H=400,\sigma=0.1\) is not among the specified cells. Fifteen cell outputs are present in the recovered run log; the two cod \(H=400\) values were recovered from a follow-up JSON. The complete driver previously stopped after 15/17 cells, so the consolidated table is an assembly of archived outputs, not a claim of a successful full driver rerun.

The sprat signal is detected in 50/50 trials at \(H=100\) and 200 when \(\sigma=0.1\); at \(\sigma=0.3\), power rises from 12/50 to 35/50 across those horizons, and is 50/50 in the single retained \(H=400\) cell. Anchovy power ranges 0/50--5/50 (0.00--0.10), and the cod-labelled false-positive rate ranges 1/50--4/50 (0.02--0.08). These conditional proportions are based on 50 trials and the declared model/null pairing. They do not certify adequate power for empirical fisheries or a specific stock. The sprat-class high detection at low noise can be driven by a growth transient in a broad 30--120-year band, not necessarily a stable cycle; power falls substantially at higher noise and shorter records. The empirical screen's null therefore cannot be interpreted as a strong mechanism test for every ecological class.

\subsection{Northern-cod constrained-mortality formulation: not reproduced}

The constrained-\(M\) formulation in earlier project notes proposes a mortality allocation that could distinguish fishing from other losses. In v53, its underlying table, uncertainty structure, and complete executable calculation were not recovered. The DFO assessment table used here reports SSB and natural mortality estimates, but does not by itself identify the causes of mortality. Consequently, v53 does not report numerical constrained-\(M\) estimates, attributed mortality shares, or a causal seal-versus-fishing decomposition. Those values must not be reconstructed from the plotted series alone. The open item is to recover the original input table, document the model definition and priors/constraints, and reproduce the calculation against alternative assessment formulations before drawing a mortality-allocation conclusion.

\subsection{Prospective work is not completed work}

The five research designs and their estimands, identification checks, and falsification rules are specified in S7. The source bundle also retains model-comparison, governance-event, human-in-the-loop, and MSE proposals. None has an executed data analysis, registered protocol identifier, field intervention, or effect estimate in v53. The 17-cell conditional power table can inform a future simulation plan; it is not itself a completed prospective field test.
