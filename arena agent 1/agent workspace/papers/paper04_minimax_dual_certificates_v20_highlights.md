# Highlights — paper04 v20

* **A certificate, not a verdict.** For control-affine plants with a compact convex action set, an information atom has no common first-order safe action **if and only if** a probability measure on its active boundary–shock bundle makes the pooled constraint row strictly negative on the whole action set; the best margin of such a certificate equals minus the lower value of the atom's game (Theorem 3.1). The certificate is portable: one support-function evaluation checks it, and it is sound for arbitrary (also finite) action sets.
* **Sparsity by Helly, tight in every dimension.** A certificate needs at most `min(dim aff U, rank) + 1` atoms — Helly's theorem, not Carathéodory's `k + 2` — and for every `k` a plant attains the bound (Theorem 3.3); random rows show how rarely the bound is met in practice (Remark 3.4). For polyhedral action sets the certificate is a Farkas pair whose facet multipliers name the constraint that carries the obstruction (Theorem 3.6).
* **The convexity boundary, exactly.** Without convexity a certificate exists exactly when the convexified atom is obstructed, so the gap between `U` and `conv U` is the only source of incompleteness; the parity instance has lower value −1, convexified value 0 and no certificate (Proposition 3.5; machine-checked).
* **Three tests and a benchmark law.** Physical, epistemic and institutional obstructions are tests with overlapping membership, read off the certificate's support and facet multipliers (Proposition 4.1). On a two-stock harvesting model with an aggregate observation and a demand floor `D`, the fibre margin is `(27 − 5Y)/50` with the unique certificate `(1/2, 1/2)`, and the critical aggregate stock obeys `Y*(D) = min(77/5 − 5D, 69/5)`: one unit of demand floor costs five units of stock, and the two branches are an epistemic–institutional and a physical obstruction (Propositions 5.1–5.2).
* **Finite review envelopes with action-dependent observation.** The node recursion is the supremum over adapted policy trees, with tower and martingale identities and refinement monotonicity; committed prefixes inherit node values; posterior-only evaluation is not an envelope (Theorem 6.2, Corollary 6.3, Example 6.4). Over a class of couplings with fixed marginals the robust value obeys a certified weak duality whose worst coupling can be an interior point of the class (Proposition 6.5, Example 6.6); on a two-window instance every one of 171 adapted trees is defeated with probability at least 3/8 at distance 4.
* **One integrated line for a companion threshold.** The integrated certificate for finite-horizon trajectories with adapted controls reproduces the review threshold `τ* = 7/50` of the companion instance and fixes the sign convention `h_U(−·)` (Proposition 6.7).
* **Reproducible to the last digit.** 66 exact-rational checks regenerate every value, witness, count and table; floating-point solvers only propose, never decide; the soundness direction, the parity gap and the benchmark algebra are machine-checked in Lean 4 with their scope declared.
