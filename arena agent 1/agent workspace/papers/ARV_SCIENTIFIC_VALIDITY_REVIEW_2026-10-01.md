## 3. Findings — witnesses checked against the printed domains

### A. `prop:profile`: “15 ... all-growth at the annual level” confuses net growth with growth in **each** year

The reference window has seven printed strictly positive readings (1983–1989), six transitions, and exactly `6+5+4+3+2+1=21` contiguous subwindows. The manuscript says **“Fifteen of the twenty-one subwindows are all-growth at the annual level.”** The verifier computes `ssb[end] > ssb[start]`: this counts **15 net-positive endpoint subwindows**, *not* subwindows on which all constituent annual transitions grow. Enumerating all 21 from the locked Table A2 values finds only **5** with every step growing. For example 1984→1987 has net increase `863.23→940.75` but contains the decline `902.94→836.00` from 1985→1986. The horizon-by-horizon **minimum endpoint ratios**, the six below-unity endpoint subwindows, and the statement that every subwindow of length at least four grows *in net* remain supported. This is an upstream-v9 **and live-v2** prose/verification-predicate mismatch, not a Lean failure. Re-run `python3 content_audit/scientific_validity/arv/witnesses.py`.

### B. `prop:recovery`: the percentage range silently drops the first stated year

The proposition describes removals for **2015–2021** as 4.4–12.9 kt per year and says they are **“between 2.3% and 3.0% of the same vintage's stock reading per year.”** The script calculates that percentage range on **2016–2021 only** (`range(2016,2022)`). In 2015 the same RAM database vintage used for recovery gives `SSB=277` kt and official removals `4,436` t: `4,436/277,000 = 1109/69250 ≈ 1.60%`, **below 2.3%**. Using input-vintage `298.65` kt instead gives ≈1.49%, also below it. The 2015–2021 catch *tonnage* range and the 2016–2021 biomass-share range remain supported separately; their joined 2015–2021 percentage claim is not. This mismatch is present in upstream v9 and live v2, despite the green verifier.

### C. `prop:discrimination`: the mean does not place “roughly half” below **by construction**

The paper's **particular** seven readings have three below the stated `884.6` kt reference point; this checked instance is correct. The same proposition generalizes that **a threshold fixed at a window's own mean places roughly half that window's readings below it “by construction”** and has “no headroom above it.” The proof shows only that a nonconstant window's mean lies between its minimum and maximum. A positive seven-reading window `(1,10,10,10,10,10,10)` has mean `61/7` and **one** of seven readings below it, disproving the purported count consequence under the proposition's finite, positive-reading setup. An outlier can also leave arbitrarily large headroom above the mean. The printed three-of-seven and 6.4% maximum deviation are *data-specific* facts, not generic mean-rule theorems. This is a false general explanatory sentence (upstream and live), not a refutation of the example's threshold classification.

### D. Live-only related work extrapolates beyond the algebra and survey checks

The new live section correctly distinguishes historical causation from its arithmetic bracket, but also phrases its question as whether a step **“was ... a contraction even had removals been zero”** and says the raw survey proves **“the contraction is accordingly robust to the choice of series.”** `lem:bracket` conditions on a **realized** reading pair, removals row and one of two accounting equations. It proves a subunitary inferred non-fishing factor **at that observed pair**, not that biological dynamics and stocks under a no-harvest intervention would remain unchanged; an actual zero-catch counterfactual needs invariance assumptions absent from Lean and script. `prop:survey` does show an independently tabulated **net survey-index decline** over 1989–1994/1991–1992, but no survey-based catch allocation, biomass conversion or `g` bracket. Thus **presence of the net decline** is checked across those two series, while **harvest-free certification** is only shown on assessment SSB under its accounting assumptions. This is an unsupported scope/causal extension introduced by the merge's new related-work prose, not evidence that the 2016 Table A2 ratios or published survey-index ratios are wrong. The source paper itself explicitly calls the bracket “accounting, not biology” and disclaims catch-path counterfactuals in `prop:moratorium` and §Delimitations; retain that boundary.
