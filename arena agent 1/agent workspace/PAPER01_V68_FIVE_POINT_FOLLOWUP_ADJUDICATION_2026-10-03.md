# Paper01 proposed v68 — adjudication of the five-point follow-up

**2026-10-03 · Review-only.** This assesses the latest proposed J1, constraint-qualification, posterior-exactness, recourse-P4 and sensor-(R3) claims against the [joint upgrade adjudication](PAPER01_V68_MAIN_AND_SUPPLEMENT_UPGRADE_JOINT_ADJUDICATION_2026-10-03.md) and the unchanged, pinned [v67 article](paper%202%20family/01_obstruction/paper01_obstruction_calculus_v67.tex) (SHA-256 `4f5cc909e9035319e7056d9d9b8d64f60285d9a68bcf801cf5b8154aff9fad39`) and [supplement](paper%202%20family/01_obstruction/paper01_obstruction_calculus_v67_supplementary.tex) (`99819f72de9c85570ab2daa2f8610ca5c1e8fb703d0a5be8c593b08f2022ce62`). The [small exact checks](content_audit/paper01_v68_five_point_followup_2026-10-03/verify_counterexamples.py) and [log](content_audit/paper01_v68_five_point_followup_2026-10-03/VERIFICATION.log) are counterexamples/source checks, **not** verification of continuous-time existence theorems or an independent referee. No manuscript or supplement was edited.

**Overall:** The follow-up corrects the *direction* of the active-gradient/tangent inclusion; acknowledges that relaxation only gives slack; and supplies useful counterexamples to compact posterior and sensor semicontinuity claims. But **J1.1 is not proved under its written hypotheses**; “posteriors compact in the convex case,” “no measurability across histories,” and “P4(b)/(c) proved” are still overstatements. The proposed Lemma T needs a stronger, uniform joint approximation condition. These are not merely citation-cleanup items.

## 1. Adjudicated statuses

| Item | Verdict | Exact reason and surviving statement |
|---|---|---|
| **J1.0 (one adverse pair refutes a committed sampled-window action)** | **Proved under a declared sampled/open-loop class**, provided the policy commits before *new* disturbance choices and robust viability quantifies **all existing admissible pairs**. A single compatible original-system exiting pair suffices; nature need not be feedback and multiple solutions are harmless. | This does **not** produce the pair, ensure non-vacuous solutions, defeat a disturbance-reactive controller, or automatically preserve a common observation record if information arrives inside the window. Distinguish fixed held commands from chosen measurable open-loop segments. The v67 kernel definition ~447–461 says `∃π∀` admissible disturbances and distinguishes priors from post-observation cells; spell out the solution convention. |
| **J1.1 (closed graph/compact convex D + bounded f makes Ψ usc and yields an adverse path)** | **False as a claimed proof; conclusion conditional.** Even for a singleton compact convex `D(x)` with closed graph and continuous disturbance-affine `f`, the velocity map Ψ need not have a closed graph when `D` is not **locally bounded**. Example: `D(x)={1/x}` if `x>0`, `{0}` if `x≤0`; `q(x)=x`, `f(x,d)=−2+xd`, `ε=1`, `S=[−1,1]`. Every thresholded value is nonempty, `f` is bounded by 2 on the *admissible graph*, and `D` has closed graph, but `Ψ(x)={−1}` for `x>0`, `Ψ(0)={−2}`; Ψ is not usc. The [log](content_audit/paper01_v68_five_point_followup_2026-10-03/VERIFICATION.log) verifies the finite identities. This counterexample **refutes the application of the cited inclusion theorem, not the existence of a path in this particular system**. | Add local uniform boundedness of `D` (or directly upper semicontinuous, compact, convex, locally integrably bounded **velocity** correspondence), nonempty thresholded values, joint measurability in time for measurable **admissible** `u`, viable state-domain/finite-time continuation, and measurable recovery of `d(t)`. Check the exact existence theorem's hypotheses rather than claiming Lusin alone proves them. Jarník–Kurzweil's 1977 paper exists [2](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/A:1006578722084), but the exact theorem as invoked was **not verified**; a published existence abstract explicitly lists integrable boundedness, time measurability, upper semicontinuity in state and nonempty compact convex values [1](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/abs/10.1137/0329014?journalCode=sjcodc). Continue to prefer an explicit original-system path hypothesis until an outside expert checks the structural lemma. |

Approximation of a relaxed trajectory by admissible original solutions on a specified finite horizon requires its own regularity/compactness conditions. | With a uniform `q`-error `<κ` and a **strict positive timing margin**, the approximant can witness exit before a later deadline; it does **not** satisfy exact H5.2 or pointwise `q̇≤−ε`. For history-dependent controls, a nearby state path may change observations and hence `u`; preserve the blind common-record assumption or prove strategy-stable approximation. |
| **J1.3 (open-loop, continuous state feedback, clairvoyant)** | **Conditional with distinct information orders.** Continuous state feedback can be inserted into a *corrected* usc/convex-inclusion theorem when the closed loop remains well posed; an open-loop theorem for a fixed `u(t)` alone does not prove it. The proposed **uniform constant** `d*∈D` with `sup_u g(x,u,d*)≤−ε` on the strip is a strong **sufficient** condition against any controller that sees `d`, **provided compatible closed-loop solutions exist**. It is **not necessary**: a state-dependent adverse selection can suffice under other well-posedness conditions. Sion's pointwise minimax swap requires its own compact-convex/affinity/continuity hypotheses and does not produce one constant `d*` across states. | Replace “defeats even clairvoyant controls” by a policy-class statement, not merely “(i) and (ii) under H5.2” if H5.2 was proved only for exogenous open-loop controls. Specify the disturbance-reactive controller's timing and establish original-system solutions; otherwise refrain from that claim. |
| **CQ: `T_V(x)⊆L(x)` and MFCQ** | **Correct for the Bouligand contingent cone and a finite `C¹` inequality representation**, with `V={q_j≥0}` locally: any feasible tangent direction satisfies every active linearized inequality. The supplied cusp `V={0≤x₂≤x₁³}` at `(0,0)` has `L={v₂=0}`, while `T={v₂=0,v₁≥0}`; `(-1,0)` separates them. Under an appropriate MFCQ at the relevant point, equality `T=L` is a standard regularity conclusion. | Split **necessary** active-gradient tangency from **sufficient** robust invariance. At a common admissible action `a`, `a∉R_V^act(x)` means an active `j` and `d∈D(x)` have *strict negative derivative*; if a compatible realized trajectory retains this strict drift briefly, it exits. If `a∉U(x)` the failure is **admissibility**, not a negative-gradient witness. Thus `∩R^act=∅` can give a conservative obstruction without MFCQ **only with adverse-path existence**. In contrast, using `R^act` as *the* safe-control correspondence or invoking Nagumo sufficiency needs a tangent-cone equivalence plus measurable, viable closed-loop solutions. MFCQ alone does not manufacture the latter. Affinity in `a` and convex `U(x)` are convenient **sufficient**, not necessary, conditions for Helly's convex intersections; closed subsets of a common compact `U` help pass from finite to infinite beliefs. |
| **Lemma E (exact belief recursion by concatenating paths)** | **Proved under genuinely rectangular, exact semantics.** For sampled, memoryless `O_step`, no hidden persistent parameter, all permitted original paths extend/concatenate at review times, and no cross-review history constraint, restriction and gluing establish equality of sets of compatible successor **states** by induction. | This does not make posteriors *compact* or yield a Borel policy. Define `Post` using precisely the same action/observation histories as the kernel; ensure existence rather than treating an empty path set as safe. Initial priors split into `P∩O₀⁻¹(y)` and a branch-only reveal leaves a belief, not a physical state. |
| **Compactness of reachable sets/posteriors** | **Noncompactness example correct; claimed dichotomy false.** With `ẋ=d∈{±1}`, `ż=x²`, start `(0,0)`, triangular switching with `N` cycles returns `x(1)=0` and yields `z(1)=1/(12N²)>0`; `(0,0)` lies in the closure but is unattainable because `∫₀¹x²dt=0` would force `x≡0`, contradicting `|ẋ|=1` a.e. | **Implementability and Borel policy selection** | **“No measurability across histories is needed” is false without a stated policy convention.** Along one fixed sampled history, a chosen constant/`L∞` segment is time-measurable; this does not make an arbitrary belief→action choice Borel or satisfy a manuscript that requires measurable policy maps. | For a Borel selector from `C(B)`, first prove a standard-Borel belief domain, nonempty **closed** values, an actually measurable multifunction (a closed graph plus compact *ambient* action space and appropriate Polish domains can suffice), closed/Borel viable target `K`, and measurability of the posterior update. | **P4(b): ordinary attainment “proved off singular arcs”** | **Conditional, not proved here.** A Pontryagin minimum condition is **necessary** for an appropriate relaxed optimum, not a global existence theorem for an ordinary optimum. Even if a pointwise Hamiltonian minimizer is unique a.e. and an extreme point lies in the compact image of `Θ`, one must prove a globally optimal relaxed trajectory exists, that the **full Γₕ objective** admits PMP in the augmented branch/recourse model, and that the minimizer yields a measurable original scenario preserving the same path/value and information order. | Treat “unique optimal relaxed Hamiltonian minimizer a.e. + measurable representability + full objective continuity” as a **possible sufficient route to be proved**, not as a verified P4 closure. |

## 2. Prioritized corrected path, without conflating policy classes

1. **Information and state types:** Specify `Π_hold(Δ)`, open-loop segment policies, continuous state feedback and disturbance-reactive strategies **separately**. For each, name the timing of information and the class of original admissible pairs. “One adverse path defeats every policy” means **for each policy there exists a compatible path**, not necessarily one scenario shared across policies. A *common* `d*` in (H5.1ᵘ) is a stronger sufficient witness, subject to closed-loop existence.
2. **J1 proof options:** Either keep the explicit original-system adverse-solution hypothesis with `∀u∃(x,d)` and strict deadline margin, or prove a corrected inclusion lemma: local uniform boundedness/usc of the **velocity** map, measurability for the chosen control class, nonempty convex compact thresholded velocities, growth/continuation and a measurable original-disturbance lift. Nonconvex relaxation is at best a separately proved finite-time **κ-slack** route preserving the policy's information record. **No “J1 closed” label** until those checks are complete.
3. **CQ and belief implementation:** Add MFCQ (or a correct tangent-cone representation) only where the active-gradient formula is used as a **sufficiency/equality** criterion, and retain realized strict-negative-drift obstructions independently. Prove exact posterior gluing, *then* separately prove reachable/posterior compactness and policy-selection measurability for any Hausdorff/Borel theorem. Do not replace exact beliefs by their closure in a nonviability proof.
4. **P4 and sensor:** Work with the actual article Proposition 6 expression (weighted branch-specific variation-of-constants kernels plus blind and post-reveal support terms), not a standalone terminal linear cost. Test the actual selected sensor's possible readings, tie conventions and posterior map against R3 with sequences of beliefs/actions. Until then, P4 and sensor regularity remain **open model-specific tasks**; the hypothetical tolerance example does not settle the manuscript's chosen sensor.
5. **Release gate:** The frozen v65 remains challenged. v66/v67 are unrefereed drafts; no v68 files were created here. The user may authorize a new version after the policy model and proof obligations are fixed. Outside mathematical review has **not** happened; internal counterexamples, literature titles and scripts are not it. An explicitly unrefereed preprint is a separate author decision with status disclosure, not a conclusion of this adjudication.

**Bottom line:** The new correction to `T_V⊆L` is worth carrying forward. The existence-theorem application, compact posterior inference and P4 “proved” labels are **not**. The strongest immediate route is to state honest original-system path and exact sampled-policy hypotheses, use the typed finite predecessor, and leave ambitious structural and numerical closures as separate, reviewable theorems.
