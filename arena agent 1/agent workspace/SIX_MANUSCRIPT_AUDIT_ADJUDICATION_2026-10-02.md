## 02 — probabilistic sufficiency v14

**Confirmed, high priority.** The introduction/related work's nominal recursion says “minimises over the disturbance” and “maximal worst-case probability” (~115, ~185–200), whereas the operative nominal recursion takes an expectation; isolate the separately defined robust operator. The declared-hold proposition (~578–602) omits `z₀<1` and claims the value-one threshold `1+T_obs` even for `k<T_obs`: the later parametric theorem (~604–617) correctly uses `z₀≥1+min(k,T_obs)` and a zero branch below 1. This is a direct same-paper contradiction. The deadline figure (~1285–1295) calls the free-probe and probe-free thresholds upper/lower brackets although both lie **below** the pinned threshold (at T=4: 1.4, 2.1, 2.5). Its 5→1 grid kernel size is a staircase, not a continuous linear kernel-size law. The abstract (~88–99) calls a **three**-probe majority error `7/250` a “second probe” and an “exact probe-count bound” despite printing an inequality: for independent errors `ε=1/10`, fair-tie two probes leave error `1/10`; three give `7/250` and require two extra probe steps. Prop. 19's read-then-act timing needs a separate pre-action sensor stage (the main Bellman equation is act-then-observe). The robust `D_ρ` operator (~1405–1433) needs posteriors at zero-nominal-probability outcomes or restriction of the contamination support; state whether nominal posterior plus changed observation law is a deliberately rectangular adversarial observation model, **not** an automatically coherent Bayesian joint model.

**Qualified:** The antichain indicator representation applies to deterministic, class-scoped survivability—not all stochastic POMDP α-vectors. The freeze statement (~864–882) bounds the *number of strict drops*, not the iteration index of the last drop; for a nonstationary declared class one cannot infer a computable stopping time from finiteness alone. Also “every certified table” contains horizons before revelation; only its columns at/after the effective window freeze. The bound `Δ≥min_x b(x)` follows when **every** candidate survivor set misses *some* positive-mass state; equality needs a realizable survivor set missing a suitable minimum-mass state, not merely `|Sᶜ|=1` for an arbitrary set. Correct the reference to “348 distinct α-vectors” to “348 after per-cell deduplication”; across the two-state grid there are only three vector *types*. Explicitly declare hold, blind sequential/open loop, adaptive post-revelation, and known-θ classes. Count and figure aesthetics are lower priority than these operator and theorem conditions.

**Already fixed here:** the positive-mass support qualifier and the counterexample to multistage affine contamination. **Do not infer:** that an unrestricted robustness result follows from the hold-class value tables. **Disposition:** major revision.

## 03 — continuous-to-finite computational certificates v18

**Confirmed, high priority.** The abstract and pair passage (~58–65, ~1070–1185) treat pair-delay roots ~0.245/~0.397 as *obstruction thresholds*. The displayed braking constructions prove viability **up to** those roots; without an all-admissible-controls optimum proof or pair dual certificates, they do not establish nonviability beyond. At fixed horizon `T=6/5`, the exact triple identity `J_I(T)=τ−7/50` requires `T≥τ+1`, hence `τ≤1/5`; do not plot/infer it unqualified through `τ≈0.7`. In the pair proof (~1075–1080), the transverse velocity need not decrease every *off-critical* facet; verify those facets individually, rather than supplying the false monotonicity rationale. The statement that a held control is **continuously** safe iff its unrelaxed block rows are satisfied (~480–505) drops the between-grid-time `L h_t` condition. Static common-action `k+1` (paper 04) is not the same result as blind time-block rank `r+1` here; the §17 conflation should be removed. Discretized `r=rank A` is mesh-dependent and can grow; finite continuous lifted-kernel rank is a separate additional assumption, not a general small-dimensional bound.

**Qualified/rejected:** The supplement's one-sided charge is sound in the printed direction; do not change it to a symmetric bound just to satisfy the objection. The instance has a symmetric hexagon but the algebraic sign argument above also handles nonsymmetric `U` if the stated exact `h_U` charge is used. Its advertised general semidecision depends on certified coefficient/time/label enclosures; demonstrated rational arithmetic on a nilpotent double-integrator does not by itself implement general LTI integration. A positive relaxed dual remains a valid obstruction when its premises hold; a solver's agreement to many digits is not a second proof. Explicitly separate a policy upper bound (`ρ+e+δ+Lh_t<0`), positive dual lower bound (`ρ>0`) and inconclusive middle band. **Open:** derive or compute the pair impossibility certificates and independent between-time bounds; do not claim them now. **Disposition:** major revision, with pair-threshold wording gated on new certificates.

## 04 — minimax dual certificates v18

**Confirmed, high priority.** Replace Prop. 1's `k+1` proof (~156–185) with compactness/Helly in `aff U`, then finite minimax; retain a clear **instantaneous tangency** definition and the viability/tangent-cone qualification (a zero first derivative can still exit at second order). Prop. 3's nonconvex example illustrates **loss of completeness**, not loss of certificate **soundness**: a negative averaged row still rules out every `u∈U`; the convexified feasible action can explain why no negative dual exists when the nonconvex set has no common action. The unbounded benchmark control set `U={u≥0:u₁+u₂≥2}` (~264) violates compactness in Theorem 1; cap it explicitly (e.g. `u_i≤2` if proved harmless). Its `Y≤27/5` is a common **first-order fibre-action** test, not global robust viability of an evolving fibre; state the plant and a valid invariance/tangency argument before promoting the latter. `R_V(x)` is referenced but never defined in this source.

**Dynamic theorem/example mismatch:** the general setup (~580–640) takes `Ω=D₁×…×D_K` and makes `P_K` singletons, granting the last action access to `d_K`. In the two-window instance (~480–555), the controller sees `z₁` before `d₂`, and the hidden branch `s` is outside Ω. The announced “K=2 case” therefore is not covered as stated. An independent eight-path rational enumeration gives first-action best values `1/2,0,1/2` for `u₁=-2,0,2`: the **numeric value survives**. But there are 81, **9**, 81 reachable recourse policies for those first actions: **171** in total (243 if using four redundant slots for every first action), not “all 3⁴=81 policy trees.” The example's recourse argmax need not be unique. To repair the theorem: enlarge Ω to include `s`, use review partitions before each action, allow a non-singleton final review cell, and use a single *consistent* joint prior, not necessarily a product. Then restate the finite Bellman recursion and distinguish a fixed-prior Bayesian envelope from a robust `inf_μ sup_π` certificate. “Negative expected weighted facets implies positive-measure physical violation” requires that the functional actually be a **weighted facet margin**. Do not claim the timing certificate is an application of the static contact-set theorem without a linking lemma.

**Qualified:** a generic `k+1` affine incompatibility example shows sharpness of Helly, but does not automatically construct a realizable controlled system. A sparse-witness bound does not give an algorithmic search-complexity bound. The polyhedral Farkas interpretation must include multipliers of `U`'s own facets unless their pooled control slope cancels. **Disposition:** major revision; the finite dynamic statement cannot currently serve as its instance's justification.

## 05 — exact blind belief computation v18

**Confirmed, high priority.** Distinguish **possible subsets counted by formula** from actually evaluated masks, enumerated cliques, and stored maximal survivor sets. For the four-cube 16-level tally, `16·2^16/496≈2114`; for the five-cube `16·2^32/1552≈4.43×10^7`. These are *representation ratios to a hypothetical entire power set*, not measured exhaustive subset-evaluation costs or a runtime benchmark. A closer four-cube downset baseline above the edge is `1+16+32=49` sets for 32 maximal pairs, ratio `49/32≈1.53`. The five-cube downset upper count `32·64+80·16=3328` counts overlaps, so it is **only an upper bound**, not an observed workload. The statement “four orders of magnitude” describes improvement in the *power-set ratio across m=4→5*, **not** a four-order improvement in measured algorithmic performance (and is not asserted as m=4's own absolute ratio in this v18). The word “conservative” is backwards if including the empty subset increases a claimed compression ratio. Four-cube pairwise-Hamming necessity does **not** imply pair survivability at every initial level: at the floor edge the pairs fail. Tetrahedral impossibility in the five-cube is indeed proved by an aggregate-drift argument; “not by averaging” (~413–415) is contradicted by the proof.

**Proof/representation scope:** the text invokes a safety game in §0.9 and §0.11 while also denying that a fixed-point computation is used (~600–630); either exhibit the game and state whether it was run, or restrict to the proved static/periodic classification. The four-cube deadline necessity should use the action-independent **mean drift `−1/2`** across all hidden cells, not only failure of the displayed hold schedule; at `T=4` the required `z₀≥3` lies outside the 1.0–2.5 initial grid. The probe ladder's `2/2^j` needs the `j=0` saturation at 1; the final probe need not “double” a value already one. Do not conflate finite-horizon 4-step α-mask counts with *indefinite* blind-survivability antichains. The separate five-cube scripts returned 0 and gave the advertised 32 balls, 80+80 four-clique orbits and finite-mask maxima; their printed historical v13 `FALSE ceiling` notices are **not findings against v18**. **Open:** no timing/memory benchmarking or proof for an unrestricted observation-feedback extension. **Disposition:** major framing/definition correction, retain the exact scoped combinatorial classification where its proof applies.

## 09 — cod/Edwards intervention certification v34

**Confirmed, submission-blocking.** The 6/6/7-year cod horizon is a *ceiling-relative, method-dependent* certificate (Def. 2.7 / Prop. 2.4), not a “shelf life” of a certificate for the observed stock: the rounded-coefficient **certified** q10 T=1 lower boundary is ~1096.6 kt, above every fit-window observed SSB (max 940.75). At the registered fit (`r≈0.2369,K=5000,LRP=884.6,ε=328.9725`), replay from the 10,000-kt ceiling with nominal q10/worst floor reproduces published 7/6 years using the global `a_max=F′(LRP)` radius; iterating the explicitly different, direct per-step worst map `F−ε` from that ceiling gives **27/12** years. This is a **conditional ablation of the method**, not a replacement theorem or an observed-stock certificate: the meaning of ε and its overlap with the floor must be re-specified before choosing a competing robustness model. The paper itself says its residual floor and defect declaration are the same measured quantity in two roles (~337–345). Drop “set by expansion rate alone,” universal reinspection-cadence advice and “cannot be bought,” or limit each to the particular conversion and high hypothetical initial states. Constant-catch algebra at `K*` does not prove no reactive rule can jointly improve protection and supply on *other state distributions*. The 10th-percentile bound `C*=91.59` is conditional on the fit and percentile estimator, not a universal policy limit.

**Corrected current-version arithmetic and semantics:** Prop. 2.3's `φ=0.60`, q10 infinite-horizon lower boundary must solve `0.4g(S)=80.87`; with the printed fit its smaller root is **1091.84 kt**, not Table 1's **1074.8** (and cannot lie below the flat-120-kt lower boundary ~1082.1 when effective cost is ~202.2 versus 200.9 kt). The `φ=0.75` **nonemptiness** margin is `(1−φ)g_max−80.87≈−6.84 kt`, whereas **−37.75 kt** is the LRP *safe-set* margin; do not swap them. Prop. 2.2's `[b∞,∞)` is false outside the declared cap for a logistic map with `F(S)→−∞`; use a cap-bounded invariant kernel when demonstrated, not a global upper-unbounded half-line. At K below `2K*=1769.2`, `F′(K*)<1` (current Table 4 contains such fits); the profile set includes them. Table 4's purported **refit-specific** q10 floor conflicts with a constructive-bound column equal to `g(K*)−80.87` (registered floor); determine which operation produced those rows before interpreting them.
