## 3. New analyses and new figures (all from archived files; none changes an archived number)

1. **Paired moving-block bootstrap re-run** with an explicitly shared index draw: reproduces every archived interval to the printed digit (Table S3) — the archived script's pairing by identical seeding is thereby confirmed. **Block-length grid 2–8** (Table S4): one classification changes with the block (Spec. B, M3, h = 1 includes zero for blocks ≥ 4); this is now stated in §4.1 and §5.6 rather than left implicit.
2. **Period decomposition** (§4.3, Table 6, Fig. 4; SI S4 Tables S5–S7): the 1990–1995 collapse band carries 93.6% of the pooled one-step persistence SSE on Specification A; post-1995 (n = 19) M3 is 41% below persistence at one step (16.7 vs 28.5 kt; paired MBB p = 0.19), M2 −32%, M4 −22%; at five years M2/M4 −30%; on Specification B every post-1995 margin is positive. Leave-one-origin-out check on the Specification B collapse band.
3. **Floor-forecast census** (SI S6): M1 at the numerical floor at 15/25 (h = 1) and 19/21 (h = 5) Specification-A origins, 22/59 and 36/55 on B; mechanism = training-mean catch (177 → 75 kt on A, 354 → 201 kt on B) exceeding the production of a depleted stock. This replaces v59's log-RMSE discussion, and the log column is dropped from the main tables.
4. **Rolling parameter record** (SI S5, Tables S9–S10): M1b's depensation threshold sits at 585–734 kt at origins 1992–2010 and collapses to ≈5 kt from 2011; M2–M4's K at the 5000-kt bound at 23/25 origins; Specification B ranges per band. **Allee profile** (Fig. S1): the one-step objective is monotone in 𝔰 and changes by 6.7% (coarse) / 2.9% (annual) across the range below the smallest observed state — the threshold is unidentified, its least-squares value is the boundary.
5. **Apparent-production figure** (Fig. 5) and the mechanism argument of §4.5 (Proposition 1, proof in Appendix A): the 1991–1993 transitions are declines larger than the recorded catch (apparent net production −113, −161, −66 kt under the coarse regime; −181, −240, −59 kt under annual landings), which no parameter value of the compensatory branch can reproduce; the lower equilibrium of the catch-driven Schaefer map is generated by the removals (S₋ = 144 kt under 240 kt of catch at the collapse fit, → 0 as C → 0; maximum production rK/4 = 500 kt), so the 1990 state of 862 kt cannot decline under the fitted map at all; the depensation threshold is unidentified and absorbs these transitions by default. Remark in SI S5: the depensatory module does not nest the Schaefer module as 𝔰 → 0 (the limit is the pure logistic without the Allee factor), so M1b vs M1 is a non-nested comparison.
6. **Delayed-persistence decomposition of M4** (SI S9): a one-year-delayed persistence forecast scores 184.4 / 329.8 kt on A and 158.0 / 337.4 on B; the delay accounts for 86.4 of M4's 97.5-kt one-step deficit on A and 73.6 of 121.3 on B.
7. **Secondary scores** (SI S7): Brier at B_lim with the convention disclosed, direction-of-change, and the D (sign-agreement) statistic with both conventions (v59's 56% is the "≤" convention, 33/59; "<" gives 54%).
8. **Simulation reporting** (§4.6, Fig. 6, Table 8; SI S8 Tables S13–S15): power decomposition (where the power is lost: the H2 hurdle alone vs the comparator), chance baseline (true module ranks first in 3.8% of D3 replicates — worse than chance), D6/D7 false-retained-module counts (M1 in 134/151 and 195/185 of 200), failed self-diagnostics (score dispersion ρ = 0.52; margin correlates ρ = 0.88 but cannot gate the decision).
9. **Catch-timing convention** (SI S14, Table S17; one sentence each in §3.3, §4.1, §5.6): the archived forecast path applies C_{t+1} to the step t → t+1 while the estimator pairs the increment with C_t. Noticed during re-execution; re-run of M2–M4 with the estimation convention lowers the one-step deficits (M3: 7% above persistence on A, 12% on B — still outside the band) and changes no five-year sign; consistent-convention paired MBB p-values reported. Verdict unchanged; archived numbers remain the ones in the tables.
10. **Vintages figure** (Fig. S2) for SI S11; **annual-landings fixed-window paths** (Fig. S3) for SI S14.
11. **Reproducibility** (SI S13): third-environment re-execution (Python 3.13.16 / numpy 2.3.5 / scipy 1.17.1) reproduces every result file including the M1b rows; the ±17 kt environment sensitivity of the Specification-B M1b optimum is retained as a disclosed finding.
