\section*{Contents of the supplement}
S1 proofs omitted from the main text (the slack game, the finite-horizon corollary in its sharp form, the feedback recursion); S2 the four-cube record and the pair tables for $m=4,5,6$; S3 the five-cube record; S4 the six-cube record (clique orbits, maximal survivable orbits with representative cells and certificates, census, obstructions, boundary sets); S5 observation tables (probe ladders, feedback game, deadline horizons); S6 alpha-set tables and values at probed beliefs; S7 floating-point study and runtimes; S8 verification record (check list, Lean inventory, figure provenance); S9 symbols and conventions. Main-text theorem and equation numbers are quoted as ``main Theorem~\mref{thm:criterion}'' etc.; they are read from the compiled main text, and the verification script confirms the correspondence (check TXT.2). All tables are generated from \texttt{results.json} by \texttt{make\_si\_tables.py}.
