\section{Symbols and conventions}\label{S:symbols}
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
$m$; $\Theta_m=\{\pm1\}^m$ & number of uncertain signs; cells\\
$U_m=\{\pm1\}^m\cup\{\circ\}$ & actions (sign vectors and the hold, identified with $0\in\R^m$)\\
$\dH$ & Hamming distance; $h$ = number of mismatched signs\\
$d(u,\theta)=-\tfr12+\tfr15\langle u,\theta\rangle$ & drift in stock units; floor $z\ge1$\\
$D(u,\theta)=2\langle u,\theta\rangle-5=2m-5-4h$ & drift in tenths; $D(\circ,\theta)=-5$\\
$L=10(z_0-1)$; $s_\theta(t)$ & initial slack in tenths; slack of cell $\theta$ at time $t$\\
$\calS_m(L)$; $\calM_m(L)$ & blind-survivable sets at level $L$; their maximal members\\
$\Lstar(S)$; $f(S)$ & threshold; first-step bound $\min_u\max_\theta(-D)^+$\\
$V(S)=\max_y\min_\theta\langle\theta,y\rangle$ & value of the linear criterion; survivable iff $V\ge\tfr52$\\
$q\in\Delta(S)$; $\sum_\theta q_\theta\theta$ & certificate of non-survivability; pooled cell\\
$r^\ast(m)=\lfloor(2m-5)/4\rfloor$ & radius of the floor balls\\
$\delta_m=\lfloor m-\tfr52\rfloor$; $K(m)$ & pair bound on the distance; Kleitman's bound\\
$G_m$ & graph on cells with edges at distance $\le\delta_m$ (admissible pairs)\\
$B_m$ & hyperoctahedral group, $|B_m|=2^mm!$\\
$\calW(S)$, $\calW_k(S)$, $\calW^{c}(S)$ & winning regions: indefinite, $k$-step, saturated at cap $c$\\
$\mathcal V_L(b)$; $\mathcal V^{(k)}_L(b)$ & blind value of a belief; $k$-step value\\
$\calA_k(L)$ & alpha-set of $k$-step outcome masks\\
$F(T,L)$; $\Fstar(T)$ & feedback survivability; feedback threshold\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Sign conventions: slacks and drifts are integers in tenths; ``survives'' means $s_\theta(t)\ge0$ for all $t$; a ``false collapse'' is a floating-point stock below $1$ at a time when the exact stock is $\ge1$. The cells of a representative are written as sign strings (\texttt{+} for $+1$, \texttt{-} for $-1$).
