\begin{abstract}
This Supplementary Information records material that supports the main text without carrying its argument. Part A (Northern cod): pass order and protocol status (S1); origin sets (S2); Diebold--Mariano and paired block-bootstrap grids with block-length sensitivity (S3); period decomposition, fixed windows and variant passes (S4); fitted parameters (S5); floor-forecast census (S6); secondary scores (S7); simulation design and deterministic primary-rule rerun, decomposition and carried-forward alternative-rule summaries (S8); lagged-start deficit (S9); outcome years (S10); vintage and threshold record (S11); 2022--2023 observation suspension (S12); runner reproducibility and verification (S13); and catch-timing sensitivity (S14). Part B (Edwards J-17): score-rule chronology and separate function-class dispositions (S15); origins and data processing (S16); fixed windows (S17); uncertainty implementations (S18); post-2007 threshold-indicator record (S19); Comal service series (S20); pumpage counterfactuals (S21); and climate-pass fixed windows with the Uvalde note (S22). Part C: baseline horizon profiles, full-sample autocorrelations and the exact rolling-origin J-17 decomposition (S23), plus this edition's verification record (S24). Main-text section, table and figure numbers are cited as such; SI sections are S1--S24. No archived forecast score was changed; the new checks and computations are the deterministic cod simulation rerun and the cross-system material in S23.
\end{abstract}
\end{frontmatter}
