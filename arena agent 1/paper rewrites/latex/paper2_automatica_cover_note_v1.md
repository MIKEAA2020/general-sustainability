## Summary of the submission

The paper develops an obstruction calculus for viability under incomplete observation: sound certificates of nonviability (common-action, timing, tube, recourse) that are finite, independently checkable, and exact — integer and rational arithmetic throughout, every headline number regenerated by shipped scripts. The Supplementary Material carries the complete proofs of all main-text statements, the expanded sufficiency review (cited, not reproduced), the auxiliary constructions, and the extended discussions and worked instances delegated from the main text to respect journal length.

## Material changes since the working series (disclosed proactively)

1. **Recourse certificate display corrected (main, Section 3.7; supp S1).** The certificate is now stated in the support-function form Γ_h(λ) = inf_d{Σλ_ℓβ^d_ℓ + ∫₀^τ h_U(Σλ_ℓk^d) + Σ_ℓ∫_τ^T h_U(λ_ℓk^d)} with firing condition Γ_h(λ) < 0. The earlier minimum-form (φ_U) display was attainment-conditional: its chaining step holds only when the policy's realized pairings attain the pointwise minima, which an arbitrary admissible signal need not do. The corrected form is proved in full in the Supplementary Material; on the three-branch instance the two forms share the exact critical delay τ* = 7/50 (the pooled window kernel vanishes identically, so every signal attains the bound there), and the instance's evaluation Γ_h(τ) = 7/50 − τ is tight (hold-then-brake is safe exactly to τ*).
2. **Proof architecture.** Brief sketches and short proofs appear in place in the main text; every complete proof, the extended discussions, and the worked instances are collected in the Supplementary Material (S1 blocks, "Complete proof / Complete discussion of … (main text, Section N)"), so the main text fits journal length without losing any verification content. Three previously condensed statements — the selector principle, the sparse common-action witness (with its convexity hypothesis made explicit), and the recourse soundness — now carry complete proofs (S1).
3. **Register.** The text has been swept to formal journal register: no internal-process language; abstracts fully self-contained; references in author-year form.
