\begin{abstract}
One object organizes the programme's certificates: the recursively viable
selector $\Gamma_N(B)$, whose emptiness at any factor is the obstruction,
whose dual witnesses are the certificates, and whose monitoring-adequacy
converse is the design reading. This paper consolidates the principle once,
with the exactness class stated on the theorem (finite-horizon finite
models and static observation are complete; elsewhere the duals are sound
exclusion tests and the residual dynamic gap remains Open Problem 1), the
causes kept distinct, and a concordance of every audited count --- which
integer is which kernel on which system, with each number cited to its
source paper and machine-re-verified here in exact arithmetic (8 check
families). Two corrections ship with the concordance: the delay rule on
the integer grid is the \emph{non-strict} identity --- viability at
deadline $T_{\mathrm{obs}}$ iff $T_{\mathrm{obs}} \le \sigma^{*}(z_0) =
z_0 - 1$ (the boundary cell $z_0 = 1 + T_{\mathrm{obs}}$ is viable; the
strict form fails there) --- and the coarsest adequate monitoring is
\emph{not} ``the'' partition into maximal common-action sets (on the
audited target the two maximal sets overlap, so no such partition exists;
two minimal adequate partitions exist instead). No completed item is
modified; the concordance cites.
\end{abstract}

\section{Purpose}

The programme's papers state one principle many times: viability under
incomplete observation is governed by a recursively defined selector, its
emptiness is the obstruction, each certificate is a checkable witness of
emptiness at one factor, and the design question inverts the test. The
principle is latent in the calculus paper's Theorems 1--7 and
Propositions 1--8, in the viable selector's meta-theorem, and in the
catalogue notes' audited instances. This concordance states it once with
its scope, tables the dual witnesses against their sources, states the
design converse with two corrections (the delay identity's boundary case;
the coarsest-partition existence), and lists every audited count with its
meaning and source --- so that each integer is cited once, from one
place. Nothing here modifies a completed item.

\section{The selector principle (primal)}

Fix a policy class $\Pi$, observation structure $\mathcal{I}$, review
length $\Delta$, horizon $N$. For an information state $B$ the recursively
viable selector is (Abaee 2026b, Definition 2; Abaee 2026a, Theorem 1)
\begin{gather*}
\Gamma_{N}(B) = \{\alpha:\ \mathrm{Reach}_{[0,\Delta]}(B,\alpha)
\subseteq \mathcal{V}\\
\text{and every successor}\ B' \in \mathscr{W}_{N-1}\},
\end{gather*}
with $\mathscr{W}_0 = \{B \subseteq \mathcal{V}\}$ and
$\mathscr{W}_N = \{B : \Gamma_N(B) \neq \varnothing\}$; the one-step
factors nest (Abaee 2026b, Proposition 1),
$\mathcal{A}_N(B) \subseteq \mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{tube}}(B,\Delta)
\subseteq \mathcal{R}^B_{\mathcal{V}}(B) \subseteq U^B(B)$.

\begin{theorem}[exactness class]\label{thm:A}
In a finite-horizon finite model, $B$ admits an $N$-step
observation-based policy in $\Pi$ keeping every compatible trajectory in
$\mathcal{V}$ if and only if $\Gamma_N(B) \neq \varnothing$; under static
observation the identity reduces to the existence of an open-loop robust
control (Abaee 2026a, Theorems 1 and 6; Abaee 2026b, Theorem 2, with the
finite qualifier in the theorem box). Beyond the class, exact
completeness requires a non-anticipative measurable selection
(Bertsekas -- Shreve) and is not claimed.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[soundness elsewhere]\label{thm:B}
Under the standing $C^1$/closed-graph/comparison hypotheses, emptiness of
any ladder factor, or of the blind-window predecessor at the observation
horizon, implies $B \notin \mathrm{ERViab}_{\mathcal{I}}(\mathcal{V})$
(Abaee 2026a, Theorems 2--5). The converse in continuous time --- a
measurable selector from pointwise-nonempty $\Gamma$ --- is Open
Problem 1, open.
\end{theorem}

\section{The design converse}

\begin{corollary}[one-step monitoring adequacy]\label{cor:C}
For finite states and a target $K \subseteq \mathcal{V}$, an observation
structure admits a one-step viable observation-based policy on $K$ iff
every fibre meeting $K$ satisfies
$\bigcap_{x \in F \cap K} \mathcal{R}_{\mathcal{V}}(x) \neq \varnothing$;
pairwise intersection is necessary and, off convexity, not sufficient
(the three two-point codices; Abaee 2026e, E1). Multi-step adequacy does
not compose; necessity-only remains the general claim.
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[coarsest adequate monitoring]\label{cor:coarsest}
Adequate partitions form an upper set in the partition lattice, so
minimal-cost adequate partitions exist --- but they need not be unique,
and they are \emph{not} ``the'' partition into maximal common-action
sets: on the audited four-state target the maximal common-action sets
$\{(1,2),(2,2)\}$ and $\{(2,1),(2,2),(3,1)\}$ \emph{overlap}, so no such
partition exists, and two distinct two-cell adequate partitions are
minimal (Abaee 2026e, E3, E7; verified here, check C2).
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[four design rules, exact]\label{cor:D}
(1) \emph{Timing:} on the delayed hidden-regime hold class, viability at
deadline $T_{\mathrm{obs}}$ iff $T_{\mathrm{obs}} \le \sigma^{*}(z_0) =
z_0 - 1$, equivalently $z_0 \ge 1 + T_{\mathrm{obs}}$ --- the
\emph{non-strict} identity; the boundary cell $z_0 = 1 + T_{\mathrm{obs}}$
is viable (the review lands exactly at exit), so the strict form of the
earlier editions is false on the grid (check C1). (2) \emph{Coarseness:}
a fibre merging states with disjoint safe-action sets obstructs at that
information state even when every branch is viable; remedy: split the
fibre. (3) \emph{Aggregation/certification:} an index whose fibres cross
a floor cannot support an exact safe/unsafe verdict; report the
certainly-safe set or fall silent --- label selection, not control.
(4) \emph{Bias:} the uncorrected certainty-equivalence law can empty the
selector of a system whose corrected-law selector is nonempty; the
$\tfrac{21}{100}$ figure is the \emph{uncorrected} canonical law's drift,
the corrected law's drift being identically zero (check C6).
\end{corollary}

\section{Restorative moves}

For a nonviable $B$ in the exactness class, the minimal restorative is
one of: a fibre split restoring a nonempty common safe action; an
instrument added to $U^B(B)$; a shortened $T_{\mathrm{obs}}$; a bias
correction. On a two-point belief the only nontrivial split is into the
two singletons --- the audited distances are immediate corollaries of
Theorem~\ref{thm:A} and Corollary~\ref{cor:C} (Abaee 2026f; the move
families, not the arithmetic, are the diagnostic).
