\begin{abstract}
Exact viability is fragile: indicators carry measurement error, thresholds
carry estimation error, and numerical methods need margins. This paper,
the D12 item of the generalization catalogue, develops buffered viability:
floors with a margin $\mathcal V_\delta=\{q_j\ge\delta\ \forall j\}$, and
the \emph{obstruction margin} $\mu(B)$ --- the largest margin at which a
belief remains obstructed --- as the regulator's measure of safety already
lost to information constraints. On the two-patch working system the
margin is computed exactly for the audited beliefs (e.g.\
$\mu(\{(1,2),(2,1)\})=2$ under both blind and full readings, maximality
certified on the margin grid); viability is nested in the margin
(viable at $\delta$ implies viable below), and the viable pair family
erodes strictly under buffering ($28\to3\to0$ as $\delta=0,1,2$). The
timing layer's buffered exit is exact twice: constant drift's last safe
step $\lfloor10(z_0-1-\delta)\rfloor$, and the contraction's
$\max\{k: q_0\ge(\tfrac{10}9)^k(1+\delta)\}$ --- the successor's
exit-time value with a multiplicative margin shift, verified at
boundaries. The measurement reading is recorded: threshold
mis-estimation up to $\mu(B)$ is covered exactly (6/6 check families).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and relation to the programme}

The catalogue gives three reasons for buffering --- measurement error,
threshold uncertainty, numerical margins --- and proposes the obstruction
margin. The programme's completed items supply the exact machinery whose
buffered re-reading this paper performs: the structure verdicts of the
successor, the exit-time values, the audited grids. No mechanism is
changed; the floors acquire margins and everything is recomputed exactly.

\section{The obstruction margin}

For margin $\delta\ge0$ let $\mathcal V_\delta$ be the buffered safe set
and $K_\delta$ the observation-based kernel. The obstruction margin of a
belief is
\begin{equation*}
\mu(B)=\sup\{\delta:\ B\notin K_\delta\},
\end{equation*}
read as: how much safety margin the information constraint has already
consumed.

\begin{proposition}[Nesting and exactness]
\label{prop:nest}
$\mathcal V_{\delta'}\subseteq\mathcal V_\delta$ for $\delta'\ge\delta$,
hence viability at $\delta$ implies viability below; on the finite margin
grid the supremum is attained, and maximality is certified by the next
grid point. For the audited belief $\{(1,2),(2,1)\}$: $\mu=2$ under both
the blind and full readings --- the belief is obstructed even at the
largest nontrivial margin, while singleton beliefs at full information
retain positive margin.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The inclusion is definitional; obstruction transfers upward along the
nested sets. Attainment on the grid is the maximum; the script certifies
obstruction at $\mu$ and viability at the next margin with nonempty
buffered set, for every reported value.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Buffered exit identities]
With constant per-step decline $\tfrac1{10}$, the last safe step for the
band $\{z\ge1+\delta\}$ equals $\lfloor10(z_0-1-\delta)\rfloor$ exactly.
With contraction $\alpha(q)=q/10$, it equals
$\max\{k:\ q_0\ge(\tfrac{10}9)^k(1+\delta)\}$: the successor's exit-time
value shifts multiplicatively in the margin.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Arithmetic-progression and geometric-progression identities, exact in
Fractions; the boundary equalities are part of the verified record.
\end{proof}

\section{Verified record (6/6 exact check families)}

E1 the nesting chain over all $36$ pairs and both audited structures;
E2 the margins $\mu(\{(1,2),(2,1)\})=\mu=2$ (blind and full),
$\mu(\{(2,2)\})=2$ (full), each maximality certified; E3 the
constant-drift identity on the $48$-cell grid $\times$ margins
$\{0,\tfrac1{10},\tfrac2{10}\}$; E4 the contraction identity at
boundaries and $\tfrac1{100}$-neighbors ($K\le3$); E5 the strict erosion
$28\to3\to0$; E6 the measurement reading: $V_2\subseteq V_1\subseteq
V_0$ and obstruction at margin $\delta$ covers every true threshold at
or below $1+\delta$.

\section{What is not claimed}

No robust or chance-constrained viability theory is developed beyond the
deterministic margin: the stochastic selector's probabilities and the
catalogue's chance-constrained extension (its own ``separate paper''
warning is adopted) are not touched. The margin is computed on the finite
grid, not estimated for continuous systems; no soft-constrained control
design, no penalty methods. The erosion counts are instance facts, not a
sensitivity theory. The obstruction margin measures information-caused
safety loss on this system; generalizing the reading to institutional
margins belongs to the decentralized companion.
