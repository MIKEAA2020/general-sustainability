\begin{abstract}

The obstruction programme has produced a calculus of certificates for
viability under incomplete observation, a continuous-to-finite bridge, a
selector unification, and an exact stochastic theory. This paper assembles
them into the architecture their successor requires: five layers of
obstruction, ordered by the resource whose absence each detects ---
dynamics (full-information exit), instantaneous belief-action compatibility,
timing, certification, and policy class --- each layer carrying the
certificate families of the companion papers as its witnesses. Two
generalizations are absorbed and carried to exact instances. General
information structures: the selector framework is formulated on arbitrary
observation partitions with read-then-act semantics, viability is monotone
under refinement, and a two-patch instance shows the lattice matters ---
two incomparable structures receive opposite verdicts on the same belief,
and refinement strictly erodes the certificates (twenty-eight versus
twenty-six viable pairs) precisely as the bridge's erosion identity
predicts. Exit-time value functions: the guaranteed survival time is the
quantity the first and third layers measure, its level sets are exactly the
viability kernels, the timing certificate is the comparison of that value
against the observation deadline, and on the audited classes the level-set
identities and the bridge's linear-programming thresholds coincide cell by
cell. Every structural claim is verified in exact rational arithmetic on
the companions' worked systems, with one instance at each layer boundary in
which the layer's obstruction fires while every coarser layer is silent.
The architecture is a presentation discipline, not a new mechanism; the
strongest claims remain the companions', each at the precision its
development supports.

\end{abstract}

\subsection{1. Introduction}\label{introduction}

Four papers form the obstruction programme: the calculus of obstruction
certificates (Abaee, 2026a), the continuous-to-finite bridge for
recourse-aware nonviability (Abaee, 2026b), the viable-selector unification
(Abaee, 2026c), and the stochastic selector (Abaee, 2026d). This paper is
the successor architecture they were building toward: the five-layer
organization of obstruction by the resource whose absence each layer
detects, with the two deferred generalizations --- general information
structures and exit-time value functions --- absorbed and carried to exact,
verified instances on the programme's worked systems.

The layers, top to bottom:
\begin{itemize}\tightlist
\item \textbf{Layer 1 (dynamics).} Full-information exit: a drift or
comparison condition under which the disturbance forces exit in bounded
time against every control. No information-theoretic content; the base
obstruction of Abaee 2026a.
\item \textbf{Layer 2 (belief action).} One-step incompatibility across an
information state: empty common admissible, safe, or tube-safe response
sets; the epistemic-emptiness construction; the common-action certificate
and its Farkas and Helly witnesses.
\item \textbf{Layer 3 (timing).} The blind window: information that arrives
after the guaranteed survival time has expired; the delayed-information
bound, the guaranteed survival time \(\sigma^{*}\), and the bridge's finite
linear programs with Farkas duals.
\item \textbf{Layer 4 (certification).} The fibre criterion: no exact
observation-only certifier when a fibre crosses the safe-set boundary; the
certainly-safe relaxation as the maximal sound labelling.
\item \textbf{Layer 5 (policy class).} Obstructions created by restricting
the controller class --- the certainty-equivalence trap --- which can
operate under injective observation with every coarser layer silent.
\end{itemize}

The architecture earns its keep at the boundaries, and Section~\ref{sep}
verifies one exact instance per boundary in which the layer's obstruction
fires while every coarser layer is silent. The two absorbed
generalizations supply the connective theory: general information
structures (Section~\ref{gis}) give Layers 2--5 their common semantics and
their ordering --- viability is monotone under refinement, certificates
erode as structures refine, and incomparable structures can disagree in
either direction; exit-time values (Section~\ref{etv}) give Layers 1 and 3
their common currency --- the guaranteed survival time, whose level sets
are the viability kernels and whose comparison against a deadline is the
timing certificate.

The discipline of Abaee 2026c, Section~7, is kept: the architecture
unifies \emph{form}, not \emph{cause}; each layer's obstruction has a
different epistemic status; and the strongest claims remain the
companions', each at the precision its development supports
(Section~\ref{discipline}).

\subsection{2. The five layers}\label{layers}

Fix the framework of Abaee 2026c: augmented states, information states,
response correspondences, and the recursive viable selector
\(\Gamma_{N}(B)\). Each layer is a region of that framework: a family of
correspondences, the certificate families that witness their emptiness,
and the companions' results that establish the witnesses.

\textbf{Layer 1: dynamics.} At a single branch the intersection is trivial,
and the correspondence empties on dynamics alone: the finite-time exit
certificate under the Isaacs-type drift condition, the comparison-function
refinement, the explicit bound \(t_{\mathrm{exit}} \le q(x_{0})/\varepsilon
\le a/\varepsilon\) (Abaee 2026a). Verified base instance
(Section~\ref{verified}, E5): every revealed branch of the hidden-regime
system is indefinitely viable under full information --- Layer 1 obstructs
only when the drift condition genuinely holds, never merely from
information loss.

\textbf{Layer 2: belief action.} The static intersection object
\(\Gamma^{B}(B) = \bigcap_{z \in B} \Gamma(z)\) at the admissible, safe,
and tube-safe choices of response: the common-action certificate with
Farkas and \(m{+}1\) witnesses, the admissibility register, the
minimal epistemic-emptiness construction, the tube obstruction and its
ladder (Abaee 2026a). Verified boundary instance: the two-floor belief has
both branches individually safe with an empty common safe set.

\textbf{Layer 3: timing.} The blind-window predecessor correspondence and
the guaranteed survival time \(\sigma^{*}\): the delayed-information bound,
the load-bearing class declaration, and Abaee 2026b's finite programs,
whose infeasibility certifies failure of every measurable
information-adapted controller with post-observation recourse priced in.
Verified boundary instance: the timing cells \(z_{0} \ge 2\) of the audited
grid are one-step-safe (Layer 2 silent) yet timing-certified.

\textbf{Layer 4: certification.} The label-selection correspondence
\(\Lambda_{K}(O^{-1}(y))\): no exact observation-only certifier when a
fibre crosses the safe-set boundary; the certainly-safe set as the maximal
sound relaxation (Abaee 2026a, 2026c). A verdict layer, not a
policy-viability obstruction. Verified boundary instance: the two-floor
index, a static model in which Layers 1--3 have no content and the
certification obstruction fires.

\textbf{Layer 5: policy class.} The declared-class correspondence
\(\Gamma(B; \Pi', \cdot)\) for a restriction \(\Pi' \subset \Pi\): the
certainty-equivalence trap, which empties the kernel under an injective
observation with Layers 1--4 silent (Abaee 2026a, Corollary 1 of 2026c).
Verified boundary instance: the biased-index instance, where the corrected
law is a witness for the unrestricted class and every
certainty-equivalence law has drift at least \(21/100\) on the band.

\subsection{3. Layer 2--5 on general information structures (D2)}\label{gis}

The companions' exact theorems assume exogenous information --- history
partitions of the hidden mode, independent of state, control, and
disturbance. The selector framework needs less, and this section records
how much less, exactly.

\begin{definition}[information structure]\label{def:struct}
An information structure on the augmented state space is a map
\(f: Z \to \mathcal{L}\) to a label space: \(f\) partitions
\(Z\) into cells \(f^{-1}(\ell)\), and \(f\) is the observation the
regulator reads when acting. \(f\) \emph{refines} \(g\) if same
\(f\)-label implies same \(g\)-label. Semantics: read-then-act --- the
regulator observes the current cell and selects one action per cell
(states sharing a cell are indistinguishable and share a response; this is
the common-action condition within cells); successors re-partition and the
recursion continues.
\end{definition}

Blind is the one-cell structure; full information is the singleton
structure; the companions' delayed model is blind up to a deadline and full
after. Layer 2's common-action certificate is the blind case; every
certificate family of Sections 2--5 of Abaee 2026a is a statement about
cells of some structure.

\begin{proposition}[refinement monotonicity]\label{prop:mono}
Viability of a belief is monotone under refinement: if \(f\) refines
\(g\), then every \(f\)-viable belief is \(g\)-viable, and every
\(g\)-certificate (emptiness witness) remains valid at \(f\). Along any
refinement chain the verdict changes at most from nonviable to viable;
certificates erode precisely as the bridge's identity
\(\Gamma_{\mathrm{coarse}} \ge \Gamma_{\mathrm{fine}}\) (Abaee 2026b)
predicts on the viability side.
\end{proposition}

\emph{Proof sketch.} A per-cell selection for the finer structure
restricts to a per-cell selection for the coarser one by merging cells
(the merged cell acts by one of the fine cells' actions and is safe on the
union because the coarse cell is partitioned by the fine one); conversely
an emptiness witness for the coarser common-action problem applies verbatim
within any fine cell it saturates. \hfill\(\square\)

\begin{proposition}[the lattice matters]\label{prop:lattice}
Refinement monotonicity does not extend off the refinement order: two
incomparable structures can receive opposite verdicts on the same belief.
Verified instance (Section~\ref{verified}, E1): on the two-patch audit
system, the belief \(\{(1,2), (2,1)\}\) is nonviable under the aggregate
reading \(y = z_{1} + z_{2}\) and viable under the coordinate reading
\(z_{1}\) alone --- the coordinate structure distinguishes what the
aggregate merges, the aggregate saturates no coordinate cell, and neither
refines the other. On the same system the refinement chain
\(\mathrm{blind} < \mathrm{aggregate} < \mathrm{full}\) is verdict-monotone
with strict improvement exactly at the full refinement, and refinement
strictly erodes the certificates: over all safe pairs, 28 beliefs are
viable under full information against 26 under the aggregate reading
(E2) --- the erosion identity's exact viability-side count.
\end{proposition}

\emph{Scope.} Beyond exogenous structures --- control-dependent
observations, active sensing --- the read-then-act semantics itself is no
longer an exact representation of the policy class, and only sound
statements survive: certificates for the exogenous relaxation remain valid
upper envelopes of the true policy class, in the same soundness-only mode
as the companion bridge's oracle-recourse extension (Abaee 2026b) and the
measurable-selection qualification of Abaee 2026c. This is the same
delimitation the bridge states, kept verbatim at the architecture level.

\subsection{4. Exit-time value functions (D3)}\label{etv}

Layers 1 and 3 measure the same quantity from two sides.

\begin{definition}[exit-time values]\label{def:etv}
For an information state \(B\), a declared class \(\Pi_{B}\), and the
constraint function \(q\) with \(\mathcal{V} = \{q \ge 0\}\), the
guaranteed survival time is
\[
T^{*}(B) = \sup_{\pi \in \Pi_{B}}\ \inf_{z_{0} \in B,\ d(\cdot)}\
\sup \{ t : q(x(t)) \ge 0 \},
\]
with the full-information value at singletons written \(T^{*}_{\mathrm{full}}\)
and the blind-window value, for the delayed model, the companions'
\(\sigma^{*}\).
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[level sets are kernels]\label{prop:levels}
For every horizon \(T\),
\(\{ B : T^{*}(B) \ge T \} = \mathscr{W}_{T}\): the level sets of the
exit-time value are exactly the finite-horizon viability kernels, and
Layer 1's exit certificate is the statement \(T^{*}(x_{0}) < \infty\)
witnessed by a drift bound, while Layer 3's timing certificate is exactly
\(T^{*}(B_{0}) < T_{\mathrm{obs}}\) --- the value compared against the
observation deadline. On the audited classes the identities hold cell by
cell: on the hidden-regime grid, \(\sigma^{*}(z_{0}) = z_{0} - 1\) and
\(\{\sigma^{*} \ge T\} = \{z_{0} \ge 1 + T\}\) for every audited deadline
(48 cells); on the companion bridge's contraction instance,
\(\sigma^{*}(z_{0}) = \max \{k : z_{0} \ge (\tfrac{10}{9})^{k}\}\), and the
level sets coincide with the finite program's feasibility thresholds
\(\{z_{0} \ge (\tfrac{10}{9})^{K}\}\) --- the bridge's linear programs
compute, exactly, the level sets of the exit-time value.
\end{proposition}

\emph{Proof sketch.} \(T^{*}(B) \ge T\) says some policy keeps every
compatible trajectory in \(\mathcal{V}\) through \(T\), which is
\(\mathscr{W}_{T}\) membership by Theorem 1 of Abaee 2026c; the two
families of computed identities are re-derivations in exact arithmetic
(Section~\ref{verified}, E3--E4). The Hamilton--Jacobi--Isaacs reading of
\(T^{*}\) --- its viscosity characterization and comparison-function
bounds --- is available in the literature and is not redeveloped here; the
programme's contribution is the level-set identification with the
certificate layer. \hfill\(\square\)

\begin{remark}[stochastic reading]\label{rem:stoch}
Under genuinely stochastic kernels the exit-time value's expectation
counterpart is the belief-state safety value of Abaee 2026d, and the
deficit \(1 - V_{k}\) is the stochastic analogue of
\(T_{\mathrm{obs}} - T^{*}\): certificates read as deficit lower bounds
there, as deadline comparisons here. The two readings coincide at the
deterministic degeneration.
\end{remark}

\subsection{6. Verified record}\label{verified}

\texttt{successor\_five\_layer\_v1\_verify.py} (standard library; exact
rational and integer arithmetic; deterministic) carries seven check
families, all passing: \textbf{E1} the information lattice on the two-patch
system --- blind, aggregate, \(z_{1}\)-only, \(z_{2}\)-only, and full
verdicts (\(N, N, V, V, V\)) on both audited beliefs, the refinement chain
verdict-monotone with strict improvement at full, and aggregate versus
\(z_{1}\)-only incomparable with opposite verdicts; \textbf{E2} erosion ---
28 viable pairs under full against 26 under the aggregate reading;
\textbf{E3} the hidden-regime level-set identity on all 48 cells;
\textbf{E4} the contraction instance's exit-time value
\(\max\{k : z_{0} \ge (\tfrac{10}{9})^{k}\}\) at exact rational powers and
neighbors, with level sets equal to the bridge's feasibility thresholds;
\textbf{E5} full-information branch viability (\(T^{*} = \infty\) on every
revealed branch); \textbf{E6} the four boundary instances of
Table~\ref{tab:sep}. Two defects were found and fixed during development,
both in the verification harness itself: an initial draft gave every
information structure blind semantics (one common action per belief)
instead of read-then-act per-cell semantics --- the crash it caused is what
surfaced the error --- and a refinement predicate with an inverted
implication; the manuscript's statements were unchanged, the harness was
corrected before any record was archived.

\subsection{7. What the architecture does not claim}\label{discipline}

The layers are a presentation discipline over the companions' results, not
new mechanisms: every certificate cited here is proved in Abaee
2026a--d, and the new content of this paper is the two absorbed
generalizations at their verified instances (Propositions~\ref{prop:mono}
and~\ref{prop:lattice}, and the level-set identities of
Proposition~\ref{prop:levels}) together with the separation table. The
layers do not rank importance: Layer 1 is not epistemic, Layer 4 is a
certification limit rather than a policy-viability obstruction, and Layer
5 can operate under perfect observation. The strongest claim the
architecture supports is the one Abaee 2026c already states, now with its
two generalizations in place: observation-based viability requires a common
recursively safe selector from information states to admissible responses;
the certificates are checkable sufficient emptiness conditions, exact in
finite-horizon finite models, sound local exclusion tests elsewhere,
organized by the resource whose absence each detects.
