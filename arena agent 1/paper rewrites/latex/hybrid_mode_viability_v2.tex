\begin{abstract}
Discrete regimes are where hybrid formalisms tempt and where decorative
extensions hide. This paper, the D11 item of the generalization catalogue,
takes the catalogue's own warning seriously --- hybrid structure only where
the mode is load-bearing --- and exhibits the smallest setting in which it
is. The working system gains an exogenous regime $m\in\{1,2\}$ chosen
adversarially each step: each patch regenerates, and the pressured patch
takes damage unless protected. Under either \emph{frozen} regime every
safe pair is viable ($36$ of $36$; each regime is separately
sustainable); with the regime \emph{unread}, the mode-blind kernel holds
exactly $28$ of $36$ --- eight beliefs, precisely those containing the
thin-stock corner $(1,1)$, are lost purely to unread regimes, every loss
generated by the mode-blind death of the corner state itself (no
merged-pair loss exists on this grid); with the regime \emph{read} per
(state, mode) cell, protecting the pressured patch restores all $36$. The
chain
$\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{blind}}\subset\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{observed}}=
\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{frozen-}1}\cap\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{frozen-}2}$ is
exact on the audited pair family (in general only the inclusion holds;
equality is the audited instance's computed fact), and the recursive
augmented-belief computation is cross-validated against brute-force game
enumeration. Scope is recorded, not claimed:
finite modes, total transitions, no Zeno, no continuous-mode metrics
(6/6 check families).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and relation to the catalogue's warning}

The catalogue proposes hybrid generalization ``carefully'': only if the
mode structure is central, otherwise decorative. The paper honors this by
construction: the mode is load-bearing exactly when it is adversarial and
unread --- the eight lost beliefs of the verified record below vanish
under either frozen regime and under full reading, so the obstruction
belongs to mode \emph{uncertainty} alone. Positioning against the
programme: the calculus's Example 1 is the constant unread hidden
parameter (a common-action obstruction) and its Section 8 the delayed
hidden regime (Abaee 2026a); the present note's increment is per-step
adversarial switching of the mode, and the catalogue slot is taken
accordingly --- unread discrete modes, one step short of the timed hybrid
layer the catalogue reserves. The paper's model class is finite discrete modes
with total transitions; the catalogue's warning against decorative hybrid
machinery (Zeno, timed-automata metrics, continuous mode dynamics) is
adopted verbatim as the scope statement.

\section{Mode uncertainty}

Branches carry $(x,m)$; the per-cell read-then-act semantics applies on
augmented cells; after the regulator's per-cell move the adversary
re-picks the mode. The timing is causal by construction: the mode realized
at step $t$ is the one the adversary chose at the end of step $t-1$; the
observed class conditions on that realized mode, and the re-pick after the
move sets the next mode. Dynamics (the audited two-patch data, Abaee 2026a,
Section 8): both patches regenerate $+1$ per step (cap $3$); the regime
names the pressured patch, which takes damage $2$ unless protected
($u=m$); the unpressured patch is never damaged; instruments
$u\in\{1,2\}$; full transition data in the script. Three policy classes:
frozen mode (no switching), mode-blind (cells keyed by state),
mode-observed (cells keyed by $(x,m)$).

\begin{proposition}[Frozen regimes are separately sustainable]
With the mode frozen at either value, protecting the pressured patch lets
both patches grow: every safe pair is viable under each frozen regime
($36$ of $36$).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[The mode-uncertainty obstruction and its exact boundary]
\label{prop:hybrid}
Under an unread adversarial regime the mode-blind kernel holds exactly
$28$ of $36$ safe pairs; the eight lost beliefs are exactly those
containing $(1,1)$; under mode observation, protecting the pressured
patch restores all $36$. Hence
$\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{blind}}\subset
\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{observed}}=\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{frozen-}1}\cap
\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{frozen-}2}$ setwise --- on the audited pair family;
in general only the inclusion
$\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{observed}}\subseteq
\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{frozen-}1}\cap
\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{frozen-}2}$ holds (switching can deplete margins
that each frozen regime preserves), and the equality is the audited
instance's computed fact.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The counts, the lost-family characterization (all containing $(1,1)$),
and the setwise chain are computed exactly by the verification script; the
restoration strategy is $u=m$ per observed cell. The boundary reading:
thin-buffer beliefs cannot juggle an unread adversary, buffered ones can.
The pair-level loss is generated entirely at the singleton level: the
corner $(1,1)$ is the only mode-blind-dead state (every other state admits
a state-keyed law serving both regimes), every lost pair contains it, and
no pair of mode-blind-viable states is merged into nonviability (all
verified, E7). The mode-blind count $28$ thus coincides, as a set on this
grid, with the base system's full-information kernel --- the pairs
avoiding the corner --- while arising under a different damage routing;
the coincidence is recorded, not claimed as an identity across models
(E8).
\end{proof}

\section{Verified record (8/8 exact check families)}

E1 frozen-mode kernels $36=36$ (the corner $(1,1)$ viable under each
frozen regime); E2 the mode-blind kernel exactly $28$ of $36$ with the
eight lost beliefs enumerated (all contain $(1,1)$); E3 mode-observed
restoration $36$ of $36$; E4 cross-validation of the recursive augmented
computation against brute-force game-tree enumeration (three probe
beliefs $\times$ horizons $1$--$4$ $\times$ frozen/adversarial
switching); E5 the exact policy-class chain ($28\subset36=36$); E6 the
scope assertions (finite modes, total transitions, load-bearing instance
exhibited); E7 singleton generation: exactly $\{(1,1)\}$ is
mode-blind-dead among the nine singletons, all eight others viable as
singletons, and every pair of mode-blind-viable states is mode-blind
viable (no merging loss); E8 the setwise coincidence of the mode-blind
kernel with the base system's full-information kernel (both the $28$ pairs
avoiding $(1,1)$) recorded against the different damage routings.

\section{What is not claimed}

No general hybrid-systems theory: no Zeno analysis, no timed or metric
mode semantics, no stochastic mode processes, no continuous-mode dynamics.
The regime is adversarial and exogenous; endogenous regime change
(institutional responses to stock levels) is cited as the governance
extension and not developed. The damage--regeneration asymmetry is the
audited instance's; no classification over damage profiles is claimed.
The paper's honesty obligation is the catalogue's warning itself: where
the mode is not load-bearing (frozen or observed), the hybrid layer
disappears --- and the paper says so.
