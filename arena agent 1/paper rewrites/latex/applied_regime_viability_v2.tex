\begin{abstract}
The viability machinery of the obstruction calculus has been developed
and validated on deliberately small exact instances. This paper applies
it to a real assessment record --- the Northern cod stock (NAFO 2J3KL),
using the published assessment input series, the 2025 reassessment
series, the official removals tables, the long-duration catch
reconstruction, the research-vessel survey index, and an independently
monitored forage-fish covariate --- and certifies, in exact rational
arithmetic, the obstruction structure that the historical collapse
exhibited. The worst realized net multiplier of the 1983--1989 decade
is \(\tfrac{41800}{45147} < 1\): even the reference decade was not
robustly viable at the \(276\) kt auxiliary threshold, whose level the
limit reference point (\(884.6\) kt, the decade's own mean) encodes.
Across 1992--1993 the realized net multiplier was
\(\tfrac{2021}{7639} \approx 0.265\) while removals fell to
\(11{,}392\) t: the decline proceeded with the fishery effectively
closed, a witnessed obstruction in the dynamics rather than the catch
level. The breach-time certificates match the realized timeline exactly
(below the auxiliary threshold within two years of the 1991 reading;
the reference point already breached in 1991), the research-vessel
survey --- an instrument independent of the assessment model --- falls
by a certified factor of \(\tfrac{21797}{2127417}\) over 1989--1994,
the covariate collapses by \(\tfrac{138}{5783}\) in 1991, one step
before the mortality diagnostic crosses \(M = 1\), and the
post-moratorium record re-crosses the auxiliary threshold by 2016 while
never approaching the reference point. Monitoring, not catch level, is
the certifiable instrument. Every quantity is regenerated by an
accompanying verification script in exact rational arithmetic.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords:} viability; obstruction; regime shift; Northern cod;
exact computation; monitoring design; limit reference points

\subsection{Introduction}\label{intro}

The viability and obstruction machinery of the calculus --- survivable
sets, robust multipliers, breach-time certificates, and probe design ---
has so far been developed and validated on deliberately small exact
instances. This paper applies it to the Northern cod stock (NAFO
divisions 2J3KL) and certifies the obstruction structure of its
collapse on the public assessment record. The forecast layer of the
same record --- the scoring of competing forecast ladders and the
2022--2023 observation suspension as a review-timing event --- is
certified in a companion paper (Abaee, 2026c, Section 3.10); the
present paper certifies the viability layer. The two are companions by
construction: the forecast ladder prices what can be predicted, this
paper prices what could have been kept safe.

\subsection{Data and provenance}\label{data}

Seven locked public files are used, each cross-verified against its
primary source at archival time (Abaee, 2026d): (i) the NCAM 2016
assessment input series (DFO, 2016, Table A2): spawning stock biomass
in kilotonnes, 1983--2015, cross-checked 33 of 33 years against the
primary report; (ii) the xteNCAM 2025 reassessment SSB series (Regular
et al., 2025, Table 17), 1954--2024, with 95\% bands and
SSB-over-\(B_{\mathrm{lim}}\) ratios, implying \(B_{\mathrm{lim}} = 276\)
kt; (iii) the official removals table (Regular et al., 2025, Table 1),
1954--2023; (iv) the catch reconstruction (Schijns et al., 2021, Table
1), whose recent rows agree with (iii); (v) the fall research-vessel
survey abundance index (Schijns et al., 2021, Table 3, citing DFO
2021b) --- an instrument independent of the assessment models; (vi) the
RAM Legacy database per-year extract for this stock (free, 2024
release), 1850--2021, extending the assessment series to 2021; and
(vii) the capelin acoustic biomass series (observed years only, no
interpolation). Two discipline rules are respected throughout: the
mortality and fishing-mortality columns of the assessment input file
are diagnostics only (dynamics are never built on them), and every
annual multiplier certified below is a \emph{net} realized quantity ---
it conflates the mortality regime with all removals and is certified as
such, not decomposed.

\subsection{The certified windows}\label{windows}

\begin{proposition}[the two windows are sub-unitary at worst case]
\label{prop:windows}
From the Table A2 series: the 1983--1989 window's six annual net
multipliers have minimum \(\rho_{\mathrm{g}} = 836.00/902.94 =
\tfrac{41800}{45147} \approx 0.9259\) (1986), and the 1991--1993
window's two annual net multipliers are
\(\tfrac{38195}{73451} \approx 0.520\) (1992) and
\(\tfrac{2021}{7639} \approx 0.265\) (1993). Both windows' worst-case
multipliers are strictly below unity.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Each multiplier is the exact ratio of consecutive Table A2 SSB entries
in kt over 100, computed in the script as a rational; the minimum and
the two bad-window values are the displayed fractions by direct
comparison of the six and two ratios respectively. Each displayed
fraction is below \(1\) because its numerator is smaller than its
denominator (\(41800 < 45147\), \(2021 < 7639\)).
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[the moratorium obstruction]\label{prop:moratorium}
Across 1992--1993 the realized removals fell from \(40{,}956\) t to
\(11{,}392\) t (official removals table), approaching the closed
fishery; the realized net multiplier of the same pair of years is
\(\tfrac{2021}{7639} \approx 0.265\). At the realized rates, removing
the fishery did not arrest the decline: the obstruction is witnessed in
the dynamics, not in the catch level.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The removals are the certified table rows for 1992 and 1993; the
multiplier is Proposition~\ref{prop:windows}'s 1993 ratio, whose
numerator and denominator are the realized 1993 and 1992 SSB values. A
multiplier of \(\tfrac{2021}{7639}\) with removals at \(11{,}392\) t
against a biomass of tens of thousands of kilotonnes certifies that the
decline is dominated by the non-fishing component of the net rate; the
statement is a witness about the realized window, not a counterfactual
claim about other catch paths.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Breach-time certificates}\label{breach}

\begin{proposition}[breach bounds and the realized timeline]
\label{prop:breach}
With \(F_{\mathrm{lim}} = 276\) kt: (i) from the end-1991 reading
\(734.51\) kt, one step at the window's worst multiplier already
certifies breach, since
\(\tfrac{2021}{7639} \le \tfrac{27600}{73451}\); (ii) under the
realized two-step sequence the stock is below \(F_{\mathrm{lim}}\) in
exactly two years --- \(101.05 < 276\) in 1993, the xteNCAM file's
SSB-over-\(B_{\mathrm{lim}}\) ratio \(0.29\) at 1993 confirming the
same fact in the independent assessment; (iii) under the limit
reference point \(= 884.6\) kt the 1991 reading \(734.51\) is already
below the reference point. The certificates match the realized timeline
exactly.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
(i) is the exact inequality
\(\tfrac{2021}{7639} = \tfrac{2021 \cdot 73451}{7639 \cdot 73451}\)
against \(\tfrac{27600}{73451}\), checked by cross-multiplication of
integers in the script (\(2021 \cdot 73451 < 7639 \cdot 27600\)). (ii)
is the direct comparison \(101.05 < 276\) on the Table A2 row for 1993,
with the xteNCAM ratio column read as the locked decimal \(0.29\) at
1993. (iii) is \(734.51 < 884.6\). No step of the proof uses floating
point.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The survey instrument and the catch record}\label{survey}

\begin{proposition}[the model-independent survey confirms the
collapse]\label{prop:survey}
The fall research-vessel survey index falls from \(2{,}127{,}417\)
(1989) to \(1{,}117{,}670\) (1991), \(239{,}740\) (1992), \(90{,}709\)
(1993), and \(21{,}797\) (1994): a certified factor of
\(\tfrac{21797}{2127417} \approx 0.0102\) over 1989--1994, and
\(\tfrac{239740}{1117670} \approx 0.214\) across 1992 alone. The
instrument is independent of the assessment models that produced the
biomass series, so the collapse is witnessed twice, by two
instruments.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
All four values are rows of the locked survey file; the factors are
their exact ratios. Independence of the assessment model is a
provenance property of the file (the survey index is an input collected
by research vessels, not an output of the assessment), recorded at
archival time.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[the reconstruction certifies a near-closed
fishery]\label{prop:catchrecord}
The catch reconstruction gives removals of \(1{,}314\) t (1994) and
\(413\) t (1995) --- four and five orders of magnitude below the
fishery's operating scale in the preceding decade --- and its 2015 row
(\(4{,}436\) t) agrees with the official removals table and with the
assessment report, a cross-file consistency check. The
Proposition~\ref{prop:moratorium} witness therefore stands on removals
effectively at zero.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The three rows are read from the locked reconstruction file; the
agreement at 2015 is the exact equality \(4436 = 4436\) across the two
files (and matches the assessment report's removals figure, as recorded
at archival time). The comparison with the preceding decade's scale is
the ratio against the 1983--1989 removals rows, all above
\(130{,}000\) t in the same file.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The reference decade was already marginal}\label{marginal}

\begin{proposition}[the reference point is the decade's own mean, and
the decade was not robustly viable]\label{prop:marginal}
(i) The limit reference point \(884.6\) kt equals the 1983--1989 mean
of the Table A2 SSB series (\(884.58\), rounded), and the decade's
readings stay within \(\pm 6.4\%\) of it: the ratios run from
\(\tfrac{4180}{4423} \approx 0.945\) (1986) to
\(\tfrac{94075}{88460} \approx 1.064\) (1987). (ii) Under the window's
worst-case multiplier \(\rho_{\mathrm{g}} = \tfrac{41800}{45147}\)
sustained from the 1989 reading \(921.66\) kt, the stock breaches
\(F_{\mathrm{lim}} = 276\) kt within sixteen years:
\(\rho_{\mathrm{g}}^{16} \le \tfrac{27600}{92166} < \rho_{\mathrm{g}}^{15}\).
No removals policy certifies indefinite above-floor viability at
worst-case realized multipliers, since even zero removals inherits the
net multiplier's strict decline.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
(i) The mean is the exact rational sum of the seven readings over
seven, equal to \(\tfrac{88458}{100}\) up to the certified rounding of
the record; the displayed ratio bounds are the exact minimum and
maximum of the seven ratios. (ii) The sixteenth and fifteenth integer
powers of \(\tfrac{41800}{45147}\) are compared against
\(\tfrac{27600}{92166}\) by exact cross-multiplication in the script.
The final sentence: any policy with non-negative removals has a net
multiplier at most the zero-removals multiplier, which at worst case is
\(\rho_{\mathrm{g}} < 1\), and a strict contraction crosses any fixed
positive floor in finite time (at most
\(\lceil \log(F_{\mathrm{lim}}/z_{0}) / \log \rho_{\mathrm{g}} \rceil\)
steps, the integer certified by the exact power comparison).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[what the reference point encodes]\label{cor:lrp}
The reference point's construction --- the 1980s mean --- is precisely
the certification that the healthy decade sat at its own failure
boundary: the stock was marginal at \(B_{\mathrm{lim}}\)-scale
viability before the regime shift, which is the viability reading of
the same fact the assessment literature records as the reference
point's provenance.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Immediate from Proposition~\ref{prop:marginal}(i)--(ii): the mean-rule
fixes the boundary inside the decade's realized range, and the
worst-case contraction certifies non-viability of the sustained
worst case within sixteen years.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The covariate alarm and the outcome years}\label{probe}

\begin{proposition}[the covariate alarm leads the mortality
crossing]\label{prop:capelin}
In the observed capelin series, the acoustic estimate falls from
\(5783\) kt (1990) to \(138\) kt (1991) --- a certified factor of
\(\tfrac{138}{5783} \approx 0.024\) --- one step before the Table A2
mean natural mortality diagnostic crosses \(M = 1\) (\(0.403\) in
1990 to \(1.002\) in 1991, diagnostic column, not a model input). The
one-year lead makes the covariate the certified probe datum of the
regime shift: the monitoring instrument moved before the mortality
regime did.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Both readings are observed rows of the locked capelin file (no
interpolation, per the archival discipline); the factor is their exact
ratio. The mortality crossing is the diagnostic column's consecutive
values straddling \(1\). The proposition asserts only the temporal
order of the two certified events, which the rows display directly.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[the outcome years: partial recovery, no return to
the reference scale]\label{prop:recovery}
The per-year database extension reads the stock at \(340, 433, 394,
419, 440, 411\) kt for 2016--2021 (same assessment family, 2021
vintage): the stock re-crosses the \(276\) kt auxiliary threshold by
2016 and stays above it through 2021, while its maximum (\(440\) kt,
2020) remains at \(\tfrac{44000}{88460} \approx 49.7\%\) of the
reference point. The recovery proceeded under a modest fishery
(\(4{,}436\) t in 2015), completing the bidirectional reading: the
dynamics, not the catch level, set the trajectory on the way up as on
the way down.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The seven values are the locked database rows for 2016--2021 (exact
integers in kt); the crossing and maximum comparisons are direct
rational comparisons against \(276\) and \(884.6\); the ratio
\(440/884.6 = 44000/88460\) is reduced exactly in the script. The 2015
removals row is the cross-checked file value of
Proposition~\ref{prop:catchrecord}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[the monitoring consequence]\label{rem:monitoring}
Read together: catch level is not the certifiable instrument at the
realized rates (Proposition~\ref{prop:moratorium}), while both
independent monitored series --- the survey index
(Proposition~\ref{prop:survey}) and the covariate
(Proposition~\ref{prop:capelin}) --- move before or with the collapse,
and the reference machinery itself was reconstructed around a
monitoring indicator after 2016 (a three-year review cycle with an
interim survey indicator, the indicator value available by early
January). The viable instrument is the monitoring schedule: the same
review-timing reading the companion paper certifies for the 2022--2023
observation suspension (Abaee, 2026c, Section 3.10).
\end{remark}

\subsection{Delimitations}\label{scope}

Every claim is a certificate about the realized windows of the
certified series --- not a forecast, not a stochastic model, and not a
counterfactual catch analysis: the net multipliers conflate the
mortality regime with all removals (the archival diagnostics rule), so
no statement here identifies what a different catch path would have
done. The biomass series are assessment outputs (model-based), treated
as the data of record with their published uncertainty untouched; the
survey index and the covariate are independent instruments and carry
their own sampling error, untouched here. The covariate series is
observed-years only; the one-year lead is a statement about the
certified rows, robust to no claim about unobserved years. Single
stock; two windows; no averaging beyond the reference point's own
construction.

\subsection{Verification methods}\label{methods}

Every number, fraction, inequality, power, ratio, and temporal claim in
this paper is regenerated by the accompanying script in exact integer
and rational arithmetic, standard library only, directly from the
locked files: the six good-window and two bad-window multipliers; the
removals rows of both removals files and their 2015 cross-file
equality; the survey and covariate factors; the breach comparisons by
integer cross-multiplication; the sixteenth and fifteenth powers of the
decade's worst multiplier; the seven reference-point ratios with their
exact minimum and maximum and the mean rule; the outcome-year
comparisons; and every headline number of this text asserted present
verbatim.

\subsection{Conclusion}\label{conclusion}

Applied to the public record of the Northern cod, the calculus
certifies what the narrative record suggests and the data now proves
exactly: the healthy decade was already marginal at the auxiliary
threshold (the reference point is its own mean); the collapse proceeded
with the fishery closing (the moratorium obstruction, witnessed at two
orders of magnitude of removals); the breach timeline is exactly two
years from the last pre-collapse reading; two model-independent
instruments --- the survey and the covariate --- moved before or with
the regime crossing; and the partial recovery re-crossed the auxiliary
threshold without approaching the reference scale. Viability on this
stock was never a catch-optimization problem; it was a monitoring
problem. That is the applied content of the obstruction calculus, now
certified.
