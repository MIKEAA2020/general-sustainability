\begin{abstract}
Discrete regimes are where hybrid formalisms tempt and where decorative
extensions hide. This paper, the D11 item of the generalization catalogue,
takes the catalogue's own warning seriously --- hybrid structure only where
the mode is load-bearing --- and exhibits the smallest setting in which it
is. The working system gains an exogenous regime $m\in\{1,2\}$ chosen
adversarially each step: each patch regenerates, and the pressured patch
takes damage unless protected. Under either \emph{frozen} regime every
safe pair is viable ($36$ of $36$; each regime is separately
sustainable); with the regime \emph{unread}, the mode-blind kernel holds
exactly $28$ of $36$ --- eight beliefs, precisely those containing the
thin-stock corner $(1,1)$, are lost purely to unread regimes; with the
regime \emph{read} per (state, mode) cell, protecting the pressured patch
restores all $36$. The chain
$\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{blind}}\subset\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{observed}}=
\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{frozen-}1}\cap\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{frozen-}2}$ is
exact, and the recursive augmented-belief computation is cross-validated
against brute-force game enumeration. Scope is recorded, not claimed:
finite modes, total transitions, no Zeno, no continuous-mode metrics
(6/6 check families).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and relation to the catalogue's warning}

The catalogue proposes hybrid generalization ``carefully'': only if the
mode structure is central, otherwise decorative. The paper honors this by
construction: the mode is load-bearing exactly when it is adversarial and
unread --- Section~3's eight lost beliefs vanish under either frozen
regime and under full reading, so the obstruction belongs to mode
\emph{uncertainty} alone. The paper's model class is finite discrete modes
with total transitions; the catalogue's warning against decorative hybrid
machinery (Zeno, timed-automata metrics, continuous mode dynamics) is
adopted verbatim as the scope statement.

\section{Mode uncertainty}

Branches carry $(x,m)$; the per-cell read-then-act semantics applies on
augmented cells; after the regulator's per-cell move the adversary
re-picks the mode. Three policy classes: frozen mode (no switching),
mode-blind (cells keyed by state), mode-observed (cells keyed by
$(x,m)$).

\begin{proposition}[Frozen regimes are separately sustainable]
With the mode frozen at either value, protecting the pressured patch lets
both patches grow: every safe pair is viable under each frozen regime
($36$ of $36$).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[The mode-uncertainty obstruction and its exact boundary]
\label{prop:hybrid}
Under an unread adversarial regime the mode-blind kernel holds exactly
$28$ of $36$ safe pairs; the eight lost beliefs are exactly those
containing $(1,1)$; under mode observation, protecting the pressured
patch restores all $36$. Hence
$\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{blind}}\subset
\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{observed}}=\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{frozen-}1}\cap
\mathrm{Viab}_{\text{frozen-}2}$ setwise.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The counts, the lost-family characterization (all containing $(1,1)$),
and the setwise chain are computed exactly by the verification script; the
restoration strategy is $u=m$ per observed cell. The boundary reading:
thin-buffer beliefs cannot juggle an unread adversary, buffered ones can.
\end{proof}

\section{Verified record (6/6 exact check families)}

E1 frozen-mode kernels $36=36$; E2 the mode-blind kernel exactly $28$ of
$36$ with the eight lost beliefs enumerated (all contain $(1,1)$); E3
mode-observed restoration $36$ of $36$; E4 cross-validation of the
recursive augmented computation against brute-force game-tree enumeration
(three probe beliefs $\times$ horizons $1$--$4$ $\times$
frozen/adversarial switching); E5 the exact policy-class chain
($28\subset36=36$); E6 the scope assertions (finite modes, total
transitions, load-bearing instance exhibited).

\section{What is not claimed}

No general hybrid-systems theory: no Zeno analysis, no timed or metric
mode semantics, no stochastic mode processes, no continuous-mode dynamics.
The regime is adversarial and exogenous; endogenous regime change
(institutional responses to stock levels) is cited as the governance
extension and not developed. The damage--regeneration asymmetry is the
audited instance's; no classification over damage profiles is claimed.
The paper's honesty obligation is the catalogue's warning itself: where
the mode is not load-bearing (frozen or observed), the hybrid layer
disappears --- and the paper says so.
