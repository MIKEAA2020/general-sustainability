\begin{abstract}
This paper is the programme's worked-systems flagship: one audit frame under
which every machine-verified count, witness, and design rule of the catalogue
is stated once, with one notation and one verification chain. It consolidates
the selector-principle concordance (the shared audit system, the corrected
delay identity with its boundary case, the coarsest-partition refutation, the
four-cell and decentralized and mode-blind concordances, the
certainty-equivalence convention, and the continuous benchmark), the static
duality edition (the two-floor instance, the normalized Farkas pair, the
convexity gap, the Helly-tight triple, and the benchmark caps), the monitoring
design rules with the non-strict review-timing identity, and the four audited
instance lines (vector floors, the policy-class lattice, discrete regimes, and
decentralized observation). No new theorem is claimed: every statement is a
pointer to a source edition whose script verifies it, and one master script
chains all seven lineage scripts (57 checks) with its own checks on the text.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords:} viability kernels; obstruction certificates; exact
arithmetic; worked systems; monitoring design; verification discipline

\subsection{1. Introduction}\label{introduction}

The obstruction calculus (\emph{An Obstruction Calculus for Viability under
Incomplete Observation}, the programme's theory flagship) states its
certificates mechanism by mechanism and consolidates them once as dual
witnesses of the recursively viable selector. The programme's remaining
published output has been organized the other way around: one short work per
instance line, each with its own notation, its own script, and its own
addendum. That organization is verification-grade but scattered, and the
programme's consolidation decision (the follow-up roadmap, v17) assigns the
catalogue facts a single home. This paper is that home.

The claim type of this flagship is the exact audit. What is consolidated here
is not new theory but the audited numbers themselves: for each worked system,
the state space, the threshold convention, the exact count or identity, the
witness, and the script that regenerates it. Where a source edition corrected
an earlier claim, the corrected form is the one stated, and the correction is
part of the audited record --- the strict-form delay rule is stated only to
record where it fails; the ``partition into maximal common-action sets'' is
stated only as a refuted reading; the certainty-equivalence drift labels carry
their round-four corrections.

\subsection{2. The audit frame}\label{audit-frame}

Three conventions govern everything that follows.

\emph{Exact arithmetic.} Every count below is computed in integer or rational
arithmetic by a deterministic, standard-library script; no solver, no
floating-point tolerance, no simulation. The scripts are named per section and
chained by the master verification (Section 8).

\emph{The shared audit system.} A large fraction of the catalogue instantiates
one three-state-coordinate system (Abaee, 2026a, Section 8): states
\(x = (z_1, z_2)\in\{0,1,2,3\}^2\), safe set
\(\mathcal{V} = \{z_1 \ge 1,\ z_2 \ge 1\}\), instruments
\(u \in \{0, 1, 2\}\) with transition
\(z^{+} = \min(3, z + 1)\) on the acted coordinate's regeneration and
\(\max(0, z - 1)\) elsewhere (the unacted coordinate loses one; \(u = 0\)
executes no instrument and both coordinates lose one). The corner \((1,1)\)
is the physically nonviable state, and the nine pair states of
\(\mathcal{V}\) generate \(C(9,2) = 36\) audited belief pairs at review
horizon \(H = 12\). Observation structures (partitions, laws, regimes) are
variations on this system; the continuous benchmark of Section 4 is its
polyhedral cousin.

\emph{Threshold and drift conventions.} The review-timing threshold is
\(\sigma^{*}(z_0) = z_0 - 1\) on the hold class, and the monitoring identity
is non-strict (Section 5). The canonical \emph{uncorrected}
certainty-equivalence law is the one whose drift is bounded below by
\(21/100\) on \([1,2]\); the corrected law's drift is identically zero
(Section 3).

\subsection{3. The concordance (the shared system, audited)}\label{concordance}

Source edition: \emph{The Selector Principle and Its Dual Witnesses: A
Concordance for the Obstruction Programme} v1
(\url{paper2_selector_concordance_v1}, script 8/8). Its eight checks,
restated once:

\emph{C1 (the delay identity and its boundary).} On the 48-cell audit grid
\((z_0, T_{\mathrm{obs}})\), viability at the review deadline holds if and
only if \(T_{\mathrm{obs}} \le \sigma^{*}(z_0) = z_0 - 1\): zero mismatches.
The strict form \(T_{\mathrm{obs}} < \sigma^{*}(z_0)\) fails at exactly the
boundary cell \((2, 1)\), which is viable; the boundary-inclusive form is the
corrected rule.

\emph{C2 (adequacy and the refuted coarseness rule).} Over all 15 partitions
of the target, fibre-admissibility holds exactly when singleton admissibility
does; two minimal two-cell adequate partitions exist; and the two maximal
common-action sets \(\{(1,2),(2,2)\}\) and \(\{(2,1),(2,2),(3,1)\}\) overlap
in \((2,2)\), so a monitoring design ``partitioning into maximal sets'' does
not exist. The coarsest-adequate-monitoring rule of the earlier monitoring
edition is corrected accordingly.

\emph{C3 (the four-cell policy-class concordance).} With the no-repeat codex
threaded as a parameter through the winning recursion: institutional
(aggregate, codex) 24 of 36 pairs; aggregate per-cell 26; full-information
codex 25; full information 28. The meet of the two middle cells is the
institutional kernel setwise; the corner \((1,1)\) is nonviable as a
singleton, and the top kernel is exactly the \(C(8,2)\) pairs avoiding it.

\emph{C4 (decentralized observation).} Unanimity over the 256-law space
(\(2^4 \times 2^4\): four coordinate values, two votes per agency) sustains
12 of 36 pairs; exactly 16 coordinated-viable pairs are lost; 16 of the 256
law pairs sustain the audited belief --- a coincidence of counts with the
loss count, not an identity; and forbidding agency 2's vote collapses the
kernel from 12 to 0.

\emph{C5 (discrete regimes).} With frozen regimes the corner is viable under
each, and the frozen kernel is 36 = 36; the mode-blind kernel is exactly 28
and coincides setwise with the base full-information kernel under a different
damage routing --- recorded as a coincidence.

\emph{C6 (the certainty-equivalence convention).} The canonical uncorrected
law's drift is at least \(21/100\) on a 101-point grid of \([1,2]\); the
corrected law's drift is identically zero.

\emph{C7 (the threshold convention).} The hold-class discrete survival
supremum is \(\lfloor z_0 - 1 \rfloor\) on the grid, with level sets exactly
\(\{z_0 \ge 1 + k\}\).

\emph{C8 (the continuous benchmark).} The critical aggregate is
\(Y^{*} = 27/5\) (the cap sum equals 2 exactly there); the \(Y = 5\) fibre is
viable with witness \((6/5, 4/5)\); the \(Y = 6\) fibre is obstructed with
cap sum \(47/25 < 2\) and dual measure \((1/2, 1/2)\) on the two active-floor
atoms, margin \(3/50\).

\subsection{4. Static duality and sparse witnesses}\label{static-duality}

Source edition: \emph{Minimax Dual Certificates} v1
(\url{minimax_dual_certificates_v1}, script 8/8). The audited
static core: on the two-floor instance the max--min equals the
min-\(\lambda\) max at \(-1/10\); the multiplier \(\lambda = (1/2, 1/2)\) is
the normalized Farkas pair of the calculus's worked certificate; the
convexity gap instance \(U = \{0,1\}\) with rows \((-1,+1)\), \((+1,-1)\)
has an empty common set, max--min \(-1\) against min-\(\mu\) max \(0\); the
Helly-tight triple \(\{u_1 \ge 1,\ u_2 \ge 1,\ u_1 + u_2 \le 1\}\) exhibits
the \(m{+}1\)-point sparse witness; and the benchmark caps
\(\mathrm{cap}_1 = 3/2 - (Y-2)/10\), \(\mathrm{cap}_2 = 59/50 - (Y-2)/10\)
give viability exactly at \(Y \le 27/5\), with fibre width
\(\Delta x_1(Y) = Y - 4\). Weak duality throughout; strong duality exactly in
the finite and static classes.

\subsection{5. Monitoring design rules}\label{monitoring}

Source edition: \emph{Monitoring Design} v3
(\url{monitoring_design_v3}, script 9/9), the round-six repair
edition. The audited rules, as identities on the shared system:
\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item \emph{Timing (E4, E9).} Viability at the deadline holds exactly when
\(T_{\mathrm{obs}} \le \sigma^{*}(z_0)\), boundary included; the strict form
fails at exactly one grid cell (C1's cell), and the repair is pinned by
check E9.
\item \emph{Coarseness (E5).} A fibre merging states with disjoint safe
action sets obstructs at that information state while every branch remains
viable; the remedy is a fibre split, not a finer threshold on the same index.
\item \emph{Aggregation (E6, C3).} An index whose fibres cross a floor cannot
support an exact safe/unsafe verdict; the certainly-safe set is the honest
report.
\item \emph{Bias (E7, C6).} The uncorrected law empties a nonempty kernel
under an injective observation map; the remedy is structural correction of
the observation map, not a better sensor.
\end{itemize}
The scoping check E8 records that the design rules are stated on the audited
systems; E1--E3 pin the grid, the fibre intersections, and the codex.

\subsection{6. The instance catalogue}\label{catalogue}

Four instance lines, each consolidated from its second (audited) edition.

\emph{6.1 Vector floors}
(\url{vector_floor_certificates_v2}, script 8/8). Under multiple
sustainability floors the Farkas certificate obstructs jointly: the
multi-constraint obstruction is a single certificate over all floors, not
one constraint at a time (the first edition's abstract claimed otherwise and
was corrected). The minimal infeasible subsystem reading (Remark 1) carries
witnesses; E7 verifies the pair family with the corner \((1,1)\)'s dynamic
nonviability; E8 pins the corner fact. The line's first-breach timing bound
and corner-cone reading are recorded as successor-layer objects.

\emph{6.2 The policy-class lattice}
(\url{policy_class_lattice_v2}, script 8/8). The four audited policy
classes form the Boolean lattice \(\{0,1\}^2\) with incomparable middle
cells: the audit's missing fourth cell (full information under the no-repeat
codex) holds exactly 25 of 36 pairs (witnesses \(\{(1,3),(3,1)\}\) and
\(\{(1,3),(2,1)\}\)), against the aggregate's 26 (witnesses
\(\{(1,2),(3,1)\}\), \(\{(1,3),(2,1)\}\)). The kernel meet equals the
institutional kernel setwise and the kernel join is the full-information
kernel (28, C3's top). The corrected certainty-equivalence labelling
(21/100 uncorrected, zero corrected) is programme-wide from this edition.

\emph{6.3 Discrete regimes}
(\url{hybrid_mode_viability_v2}, script 8/8). Mode uncertainty loses
value only through singletons: the corner \((1,1)\) is the only mode-blind-dead
state, every lost pair contains it, and no merged-pair loss exists (E7
verifies all nine singleton verdicts). The causal timing is fixed (the mode
realized at step \(t\) is the adversary's end-of-step-\((t-1)\) choice), and
the mode-blind kernel's setwise coincidence with the base kernel (C5) is
audited as a coincidence under different damage routings, with the corner's
frozen-regime viability pinned.

\emph{6.4 Decentralized observation}
(\url{institutional_observation_v2}, script 8/8). Votes on a shared
instrument: the law space is \(256 = 2^4 \times 2^4\), the unanimity kernel
12 of 36, the loss instance \(\{(1,2),(3,1)\}\) pinned, the deadlock
transition (\(u = 0\): both coordinates lose one) declared, agency 2's vote
on its own previous coordinate is a delayed own reading (local memory, not
common knowledge), and forbidding that vote collapses the kernel 12 \(\to\)
0 (C4). Decentralizability is an existence certificate over the law space.

\subsection{7. Absorbed lines and the practitioner reading}\label{absorbed}

The remaining catalogue lines are absorbed by pointer with their editions
retained: finite-horizon completeness, certificate duality, comparison
drift--exit, buffered (approximate) viability, and hidden-parameter learning
--- each with its script in the chain below --- together with the viable-selector
and five-layer successor lineages (absorbed into the theory flagship's
Sections 7--8), the stochastic-selector line (the probabilistic flagship's
seed), and the applications note with its utility record, whose limitation
register (verification-grade software; no calibrated case; completeness
scope; single-system scope) this flagship's provenance tables now partly
discharge. For the practitioner: the catalogue's operational reading is the
four monitoring rules of Section 5 plus the threshold convention C7 --- the
review must land no later than worst-case exit, boundary included; a merged
fibre is diagnosed by a split, not a threshold; aggregate indices report the
certainly-safe set or fall silent; and a biased observation map is repaired
structurally.

\subsection{8. Verification discipline}\label{verification}

One master script,
\url{paper2_worked_systems_v1_verification.py} (standard library
only), does two things. First, it chains the seven lineage scripts listed in
Table~\ref{tab:registry} as subprocesses and asserts each exits green:
\(8 + 8 + 9 + 8 + 8 + 8 + 8 = 57\) checks. Second, it runs its own checks:
the headline counts of Sections 3--6 are recomputed from the shared system's
primitives (the pair family, the codex-threaded winning recursion with the
no-repeat rule threaded as a parameter, the law space, and the benchmark
caps), and every number printed in this text is asserted present. A claim in
this paper is admissible exactly when both layers pass.

\subsection{9. Conclusion}\label{conclusion}

The catalogue now has one home. The consolidation changes no verdict: the
corrected delay identity stands with its boundary case; the coarsest-partition
rule stands refuted; the four-cell lattice stands with its incomparable
middles; the institutional and mode-blind kernels stand with their audited
coincidences; and the design rules stand as identities on the audited
systems. What changes is the address: one audit frame, one notation, one
verification chain, and a registry that records every source edition as
retained. The theory flagship carries the principles; this flagship carries
the numbers; and the remaining flagships (computational, probabilistic) seed
from the lineages already verified here.
