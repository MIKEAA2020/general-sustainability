## Executive disposition

Paper11 v67 resolves most presentation and versioning objections in the v66 review and provides substantially better disclosure of the retrospective data, catch timing, model limits and protocol differences. The current v67 verifier reports **139/139 checks passing**. Several review claims are now stale or identify a deliberate modeling choice rather than a coding error.

Five findings remain material:

1. **The J-17 application of Proposition 2 is not valid as written.** The 15.70-ft prediction omits `−μ²` and uses only the flux term as though it were the whole M2 increment. The fitted `δH_t` term is not common to M2 and persistence; it therefore does not cancel in that comparison. The formula itself is sound under its stated assumptions, but its J-17 interpretation and numerical example need correction. Proposition 2(iii) should make its mean assumption explicit.
2. **The M2m class clause changes the final classification.** M2m passes the written h=1 score inequalities but is declined by a separate class rule. Three climate variants also pass the stated h=1 point inequalities against M1 and persistence. The paper’s “nil verdict effect” wording and the final “none retained” accounting therefore need a clear score-gate/class-disposition split.
3. **The realized-future-flux oracle is still called a “nowcast.”** It uses fluxes unavailable at the forecast origin. It is a realized-flux conditional diagnostic/oracle hindcast, not an operational nowcast or forecast-skill result.
4. **The recovery trajectory in SI S17 is inconsistent with the current code.** The runner clips each emitted forecast to [610,710] ft; SI S17 shows values below 610 ft. The reviewer correctly identifies a manuscript/source mismatch, but the claim that the current runner fails to clip its output is contradicted by code.
5. **The power simulation is not reproducible from its source as currently advertised.** It seeds with Python’s randomized built-in `hash()`, hard-codes a local path and silently skips failed replicates. A separate `PYTHONHASHSEED=0` rerun completed all D1–D5 cells and preserved the qualitative pattern, but the D2 high-noise retention rate moved from 13.0% to 5.5%.

**Versioning decision:** a focused **Paper11 v68 is warranted**. The Proposition 2 application and protocol-verdict wording are substantive scientific/interpretive corrections; the clipping trajectory and simulation reproducibility also need repair. No v68 manuscript was created or numbered in this audit. Preserve v67 and make the corrections below in a later edition after the normal all-branch/version-note check.

## Proposition 2: corrected reading of the J-17 calculation

The proposition’s general decomposition is algebraically correct under the assumptions stated in the paper. For a persisted driver, if the *full* mechanism sequence has mean μ, variance σ², lag-one correlation ρ, and the mechanism residual is orthogonal to the persisted mechanism, the relative-MSE expression is

`MSE(persisted-driver) − MSE(persistence) = σ²(1 − 2ρ) − μ²`.

For the flux-only series `q_t = βR_t + γP_t` used in SI S23, the quoted values imply:

- `84.6 × (1 − 2 × 0.078) = 71.4024 ft²`;
- `μ² = 3.52² = 12.3904 ft²`;
- the flux-only persisted-driver contrast, if q were the whole mechanism, is `59.0120 ft²`;
- `sqrt(13.23² + 59.0120) ≈ 15.30 ft`, not 15.70 ft.

That calculation is **not an exact prediction for the implemented, clipped M2**. M2 uses the full map `α + βR_t + γP_t + δH_t`; `δH_t` is not part of persistence, so it does not cancel in M2-versus-persistence. The v67 text’s assertion that the drain term is “common to the comparison” is therefore not sufficient to apply the flux-only formula to M2 versus persistence. The 71.4-ft² term can describe a stylized *flux substitution* contrast between persisted and climatological fluxes if the common state term and the proposition’s residual/mean assumptions are retained; it cannot also be used unqualified to predict the full M2-versus-persistence RMSE.

The full-sample fitted mechanism including `δH_t` has mean −0.3902, SD 10.4984 and lag-one correlation .029. Those inputs differ materially from the flux-only mechanism. The reported full-sample M2/persistence RMSEs are 12.991/12.347 ft (MSE gap +16.324 ft²); the rolling-origin M2/M2m/persistence RMSEs are 14.6978/12.2832/13.2301 ft (M2–persistence MSE gap ≈40.99 ft²; M2–M2m gap ≈65.15 ft²). These are distinct estimation/evaluation objects and include fitted parameters and output clipping. Proposition 2(iii) should explicitly state `E[m_t]=μ` for the *full stationary mechanism*; M2m’s flux inputs are climatological, but the implemented increment still contains the state term and is not constant.

## Research-level assessment

The strongest potential contribution is the separation of **mechanism error** from **driver-substitution error**, paired with a scored baseline comparison. That is a useful conceptual synthesis and more novel than the individual one-pool or surplus-production maps. In v67, however, the central J-17 illustration mixes the flux-only mechanism with the full fitted map; the class clause also makes the apparent “no structure retained” conclusion depend on a separate taxonomy decision. Correcting those points would make the paper’s scientific development more coherent: distinguish score, uncertainty, and class judgment; then state what the two case studies do—and do not—support. The paper should not claim a universal management rule or cross-basin policy prescription from two retrospective systems.

## Minimum correction set for a warranted v68

1. Replace every realized-future-flux “nowcast” with **realized-flux conditional diagnostic/oracle hindcast**; state that the fluxes are unavailable at the origin. Apply the same rule to the precipitation oracle.
2. Separate the written H1/H2 (E1/E2) point-score results from the M2m class clause. Report M2m and the three climate variants as h=1 point-gate passes, then explain each separate class/comparator decision. Reconcile the Aug. 25 score protocols, Aug. 27 specification document and v67’s post-score description. Remove the “nil verdict effect” sentence or define its limited meaning.
3. Correct the Proposition 2 application: include `−μ²` for the flux-only stylized contrast; do not use the flux-only statistic as the M2-versus-persistence prediction; retain `δH_t` in the full mechanism; state Proposition 2(iii)’s mean condition.
4. Correct SI S17’s recovery path/figure to the clipped runner result and state where clipping binds. Do not alter the verified runner to reproduce the stale un-clipped path.
5. Make the power experiment reproducible with stable per-replicate seeds, portable paths, and failures that stop the run; report per-DGP rates and Monte Carlo uncertainty, including the D2 high-noise sensitivity. Preserve the archived CSV rather than silently replacing it.
6. Label all cod and aquifer results as retrospective hindcasts; qualify the five-year J-17 mean-versus-persistence finding to this record and horizon; remove general policy advice.
7. State explicitly that the 5% band is a decision margin, not a multiple-testing adjustment; describe the differing DM/HAC and moving-block readings; call recharge weakly autocorrelated rather than proved white noise.
8. Keep the disclosed catch-timing sensitivity and its result that the margins change but the current pass/fail outcome does not.

## Overall conclusion

The reviewers correctly identified a real Proposition 2 application gap, an SI/code clipping mismatch, protocol-class accounting problems, and reproducibility weaknesses in the stochastic power simulation. They also raised several concerns that v67 has already resolved or that overstate the evidence: the current code clips outputs, the M1 catch convention is explicit, the bootstrap sign-tail p-values are consistent with their percentile intervals, and the omitted advanced models are optional extensions. A focused v68 correction is warranted; v67 should remain preserved and unaltered.
