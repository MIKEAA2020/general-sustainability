## 2. New scientific content

* **Proposition 2 (increment decomposition, §5.1, proof in Appendix A).** For any one-step structural forecaster, MSE(model) − MSE(persistence) = E[d²] − E[m²] − 2E[u·m(x̂)], where m is the fitted mechanism, u its residual and d the substitution error of the driver. Corollaries: (i) a supplied (oracle) driver wins iff the mechanism residual is smaller than the increment; (ii) a persisted driver that is weakly stationary with lag-one autocorrelation ρ contributes σ²(1 − 2ρ) − μ², which is positive — the module loses — whenever ρ < ½ − μ²/(2σ²); (iii) a climatological driver never loses and beats a persisted one by σ²(1 − 2ρ). On J-17 the stylised prediction for the M2m-over-M2 advantage (71.4 ft²) is within 10% of the archived one (65.1 ft²) and the predicted ordering M2m ≤ persist < M2 is the observed one.
* **Remark 2 (crossover horizon).** For a weakly stationary predictand, RMSE(persistence)/RMSE(training mean) = √(2(1 − ρ_h)), so climatology overtakes persistence at the first horizon at which ρ_h < ½. On J-17 the observed ratios (0.82, 1.07, 1.22, 1.27, 1.26) track the prediction (0.86, 1.14, 1.31, 1.37, 1.36) and cross at h = 2; on cod the ratios stay at 0.18–0.58 and the formula does not apply, because the series is not stationary. Full horizon profile in SI S23 and Fig. 8c.
* **The cross-system claim of v66 is withdrawn and replaced.** v66 attributed the cod failure to a "weakly autocorrelated surplus production" by analogy with near-white recharge. The apparent net production of the assessed cod series has lag-one autocorrelation 0.75 (coarse catch regime), 0.71 (annual landings) and 0.91 (Specification B) — the opposite of weak. The cod deficit is the misspecification term of Proposition 2 (the 1991–1995 increments lie outside the class at the recorded catch; Proposition 1), not the persisted-driver term; v67 says so in §5, §7 and the conclusions, and the verifier asserts both the retraction and the computed values.
* **Proposition 1 (repeller; cod, from v69)** and the Edwards class-reduction remark (M2m ≡ affine AR(1) under constant fluxes) are kept and now sit on the shared design section.
* Fig. 8 (new): recharge near-whiteness, the increment–recharge coupling, and the crossover curves of the three series.
