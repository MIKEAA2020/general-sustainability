\begin{abstract}

Every obstruction certificate in the calculus of viability under incomplete
observation proves the same structural fact: at some information state, no
single response acceptable for every compatible branch exists at the
required level of the recursion. This paper develops that fact into a
framework. The recursive viable-selector correspondence is defined, and the
finite-horizon identity is established: an information state is viable
exactly when the correspondence at it is nonempty, with the measurable-selection
qualification that confines exact completeness claims to finite or suitably
regular models. A meta-theorem organizes the certificate families of the
companion papers --- dynamic exit, admissibility, common action, tube safety,
timing, certification limits, and policy-class restrictions --- as emptiness
conditions on correspondences obtained from one another by nesting,
restriction, and label selection, with three bridging propositions making
the passages precise: the correspondence ladder, timing as blind-window
predecessor emptiness, and certification as label selection. The framework
unifies the form of the certificates, not their cause: dynamic exit is not
epistemic, the fibre criterion is a certification limit rather than a
policy-viability obstruction, and the certainty-equivalence trap is a
policy-class restriction that can operate under injective observation. All
structural identities are verified exactly on the worked systems of the
companion papers.

\end{abstract}

\subsection{1. Introduction}\label{introduction}

Two companion papers develop a calculus of obstruction certificates --- finite,
checkable witnesses that no observation-based policy is viable. The first
(Abaee, 2026a) establishes the certificate families for discrete and
continuous systems; the second (Abaee, 2026b) builds the
continuous-to-finite bridge for the recourse-aware mode. The mechanisms can
look like a taxonomy: an exit condition here, a timing bound there, a fibre
criterion, a policy-class trap. The observation of this paper is that they
share one emptiness form --- seven specializations of a single
correspondence, not seven independent theorems, and not one cause worn
seven ways either (Section~7 states the discipline). At an information
state \(B\), an observation-based regulator must choose \emph{one}
response that works for \emph{every} compatible branch; each certificate
is a proof that, at some recursion level, no such response exists.

The paper makes this precise and then measures how much it carries.

\begin{itemize}\tightlist
\item \textbf{The recursive viable selector} (Section~2): the correspondence
\(\Gamma_{N}(B)\) of response segments that are admissible for the declared
class, implementable under the observation structure, safe through the
current review interval, and recursively viable at every successor
information state. Theorem~1: in finite-horizon finite models,
\(B\) is viable exactly when \(\Gamma_{N}(B) \neq \varnothing\) --- and the
measurable-selection qualification states exactly where pointwise
nonemptiness must be upgraded to an implementable selector.
\item \textbf{The meta-theorem} (Section~3, Theorem~2): declaring a policy
class intersects the selector with the class's requirements; an obstruction
certificate \emph{is} a checkable condition implying the corresponding
emptiness; and soundness is monotone --- restricting the class or tightening
the requirements preserves every verdict. The certainty-equivalence trap is
the canonical instance: the unrestricted correspondence is nonempty while the
class-restricted one is empty.
\item \textbf{Three bridging propositions} (Section~4): the correspondence
ladder
\(\mathcal{A}_{N}(B) \subseteq \mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{tube}}(B,\Delta)
\subseteq \mathcal{R}^{B}_{\mathcal{V}}(B) \subseteq U^{B}(B)\), under
precise hypotheses, so that emptiness at any coarser link certifies
recursive nonviability; the identity of the guaranteed survival time
\(\sigma^{*}\) with the horizon at which the blind-window predecessor
correspondence empties, so the timing certificate \emph{is} predecessor
emptiness; and the label-selection form of the fibre criterion,
\(\Lambda_{K}(O^{-1}(y)) \neq \varnothing\) for every \(y\), with the
certainly-safe set as the maximal sound label relaxation.
\item \textbf{The specializations} (Section~5): the certificate families of
the companions derived as instances, in one table, with the correspondence
emptied and the certificate object named in each row.
\item \textbf{Verified re-derivations} (Section~6): the two-floor belief, the
two-patch audit, the hidden-regime grid, and the certainty-equivalence
instance of the companion papers, re-derived as framework identities in
exact arithmetic (twelve check families, all passing).
\item \textbf{The discipline} (Section~7): what the framework does
\emph{not} unify. The form is common; the cause is not. Dynamic exit defeats
full information and is not epistemic; the fibre criterion limits
certification, not policy viability; the certainty-equivalence trap is a
restriction of the policy class and can operate under injective observation.
\end{itemize}

The strongest claim the framework supports is stated at the end of
Section~7 and is deliberately modest: observation-based viability requires a
common recursively safe selector from information states to admissible
responses; the certificates are checkable sufficient emptiness conditions;
the backward recursion characterizes selector existence exactly in
finite-horizon finite models; elsewhere the certificates are sound local
exclusion tests.

\subsection{2. The recursive viable selector}\label{selector}

\subsubsection{2.1 States, information, responses}\label{model}

Let \(Z\) be the augmented state space: a point \(z \in Z\) may carry the
physical state, hidden parameters, latent regimes, or institutional modes
affecting admissibility. Let \(\mathcal{V} \subseteq Z\) be the closed safe
set. An \emph{information state} is a nonempty set
\(B \subseteq Z\) of augmented states compatible with the observation
record. At known times \(0 = \tau_0 < \tau_1 < \dots < \tau_R\), the
observation history identifies a cell of a refining partition of the
compatibility relation; within a review interval \(I\), a \emph{response
segment} \(\alpha\) is a held action, an open-loop segment, or an
observation-based feedback law, as the model admits. For a response
\(\alpha\) at \(B\) and a next observation \(y^{+}\),
\(\mathrm{Succ}(B, \alpha, y^{+})\) denotes the successor information state.
The exposition is written for the deterministic set-valued semantics of the
companions; the stochastic extension is the belief-state value recursion
previewed in Section~8.

\subsubsection{2.2 The correspondence}\label{gamma}

Fix a policy class \(\Pi\), an observation structure
\(\mathcal{I}\), a review length \(\Delta\), and a horizon \(N\).

\begin{definition}[recursive viable selector]\label{def:gamma}
\(\mathfrak{R}_{\Pi,\mathcal{I}}(B)\) is the set of response segments that
belong to the class \(\Pi\), are implementable under \(\mathcal{I}\), and
satisfy the nonanticipativity convention. Inductively, \(\mathscr{W}_0\) is
the collection of information states carried by \(\mathcal{V}\), and
\begin{align*}
\Gamma_{N}(B) = \bigl\{ \alpha &\in \mathfrak{R}_{\Pi,\mathcal{I}}(B): \\
&\text{every compatible trajectory remains in } \mathcal{V}\\
&\quad\text{through the interval, and}\\
&\quad \mathrm{Succ}(B, \alpha, y^{+}) \in \mathscr{W}_{N-1}\\
&\qquad\text{for every admissible } y^{+}\bigr\},\\
\mathscr{W}_{N} &= \{ B : \Gamma_{N}(B) \neq \varnothing \}.
\end{align*}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[finite-horizon exactness]\label{thm:exact}
In a finite-horizon finite model, \(B \in \mathscr{W}_{N}\) if and only if
\(\Gamma_{N}(B) \neq \varnothing\): an \(N\)-step viable policy exists from
\(B\) exactly when the recursively viable response correspondence at \(B\)
is nonempty.
\end{theorem}

\emph{Proof.} Backward induction on \(N\). For \(N = 0\), viability is
membership in \(\mathcal{V}\), which is the definition of
\(\mathscr{W}_0\). If \(B\) is viable for \(N\) steps, the first segment of
a witnessing policy is admissible, keeps every compatible trajectory in
\(\mathcal{V}\) through the interval, and every possible successor
information state is viable for \(N - 1\) steps, so the segment lies in
\(\Gamma_{N}(B)\); conversely, selecting recursively from nonempty
\(\Gamma_{k}\) along the realized observation path assembles a viable
policy. \hfill\(\square\)

\begin{remark}[the selector qualification]\label{rem:selection}
Pointwise nonemptiness of \(\Gamma_{N}(B)\) is enough for backward induction
in a finite model. In general measurable spaces a policy requires an
implementable \emph{selection} \(B \mapsto \alpha(B) \in \Gamma_{N}(B)\),
and the following are distinct: nonemptiness at one specified information
state; nonemptiness at every state of a collection; and the existence of a
measurable nonanticipative selector on that collection. Exact completeness
claims are therefore made only for finite or suitably regular models
(measurable selection under standard conditions; Bertsekas and Shreve,
1978); elsewhere the certificates remain sound exclusion tests, which
require no selection.
\end{remark}

An \emph{obstruction certificate} is any checkable condition implying
\(\Gamma_{N}(B) = \varnothing\). The certificates of the companions are all
of this form; the next two sections say precisely which correspondence each
empties and by which passage the emptiness is transferred to
Theorem~\ref{thm:exact}.

\subsection{3. The meta-theorem}\label{meta}

Declare a policy class by four requirement families: admissibility under the
class rules, implementability under the observation structure, tube safety
over the review interval at the declared length, and recursive viability at
the declared horizon. Write
\begin{align*}
\Gamma(B; \Pi, \mathcal{I}, \Delta, N)
&= \Gamma^{\mathrm{adm}}(B) \cap \Gamma^{\mathrm{imp}}(B)\\
&\quad \cap \Gamma^{\mathrm{tube}}(B; \Delta) \cap \Gamma^{\mathrm{rec}}(B; N),
\end{align*}
the intersection of the correspondences enforcing each family.

\begin{theorem}[meta-theorem]\label{thm:meta}
In a finite-horizon finite model (or a suitably regular model in the
sense of Remark~\ref{rem:selection}), \(B\) is viable within the declared
class if and only if
\(\Gamma(B; \Pi, \mathcal{I}, \Delta, N) \neq \varnothing\); beyond that
scope the identity holds in the selector-qualified sense of
Theorem~\ref{thm:exact} (the nonemptiness-to-viability direction with an
implementable selection). Consequently:
(i) a checkable condition implying the emptiness of any one factor is an
obstruction certificate for the whole class;
(ii) the certificate semantics of the companions --- a finite,
policy-independent list of adversarial tests whose violation is forced ---
are exactly the dual witnesses of such emptinesses;
(iii) soundness is monotone: if \(\Pi' \subseteq \Pi\), or if any factor
correspondence is tightened, then
\(\Gamma(B; \Pi', \mathcal{I}, \Delta, N) \subseteq \Gamma(B; \Pi,
\mathcal{I}, \Delta, N)\), so every emptiness verdict for the larger class
remains valid for the smaller, and new verdicts may appear.
\end{theorem}

\emph{Proof.} (i) and the identity are Theorem~\ref{thm:exact} with the
class requirements carried in \(\mathfrak{R}_{\Pi,\mathcal{I}}\); (ii) is
the certificate semantics of the companions restated (a Farkas vector, an
adversarial label list, or a timing bound is a finitely checkable witness of
the emptiness of the relevant factor); (iii) is immediate from the
intersection form. \hfill\(\square\)

\begin{remark}[the three selector objects and the nesting]\label{rem:objects}
Three objects share the notation and must be kept distinct.
\(\Gamma_{N}(B)\) of Definition~\ref{def:gamma} is the inductive
correspondence. The four-factor object
\(\Gamma(B; \Pi, \mathcal{I}, \Delta, N)\) is its \emph{one-step
unpacking}: the recursive factor \(\Gamma^{\mathrm{rec}}(B; N)\) is the
inductive one, requiring
\(\mathrm{Succ}(B, \alpha, y^{+}) \in \mathscr{W}_{N-1}\) --- so the
intersection is inductive, not a flat static product. \(\mathcal{A}_{N}(B)\)
of Proposition~\ref{prop:ladder} is the singleton-action reading of the
same object. Moreover, under the standing hypotheses the factors are
\emph{nested},
\(\Gamma^{\mathrm{rec}}(B;N) \subseteq \Gamma^{\mathrm{tube}}(B;\Delta)
\subseteq \Gamma^{\mathrm{safe}}(B) \subseteq \Gamma^{\mathrm{adm}}(B)\)
(Proposition~\ref{prop:ladder}), so the four-fold intersection coincides
with its finest factor: the factorization does not assert independent
constraints --- it \emph{locates} each certificate family at a factor and
carries the monotonicity of part (iii). Emptiness at any factor is thus
emptiness along the filtration, and the coarser the factor, the more
easily the emptiness is certified.
\end{remark}

\begin{corollary}[policy-class obstructions]\label{cor:ce}
If \(\Gamma(B; \Pi, \mathcal{I}, \Delta, N) \neq \varnothing\) but
\(\Gamma(B; \Pi', \mathcal{I}, \Delta, N) = \varnothing\) for a restriction
\(\Pi' \subset \Pi\), then \(B\) is viable under \(\Pi\) and nonviable under
\(\Pi'\). In the certainty-equivalence trap of the companion paper,
\(\Pi'\) is the declared class of laws that act on the uncorrected
reading \(\widehat{z}\) as if it were the state (the canonical such law:
hold the reading constant); the restriction removes the required selector
even under an injective observation. The scope alignment with the
companion paper is exact: its remark is policy-specific for its own
instance, and the class form here is carried by the verified per-law
drift bound of Section~6 (canonical-law form) --- the full-class
quantifier over every uncorrected law is the successor architecture's
layer-five reading and is cited, not re-claimed.
\end{corollary}

\subsection{4. Bridging propositions}\label{bridges}

\begin{proposition}[the correspondence ladder]\label{prop:ladder}
Let \(B\) be an information state and \(\Delta \le\) one review interval.
Under the standing hypotheses of the companions (Abaee 2026a, Section 4:
constraint functions of class \(C^1\); disturbance correspondences with
closed graph; dynamics admitting the comparison argument), the recursively
viable, tube-safe,
instantaneously safe, and admissible correspondences satisfy
\[
\mathcal{A}_{N}(B) \subseteq \mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{tube}}(B, \Delta)
\subseteq \mathcal{R}^{B}_{\mathcal{V}}(B) \subseteq U^{B}(B),
\]
where \(\mathcal{A}_{N}(B) = \Gamma_{N}(B)\) with the singleton action
reading. Each inclusion can be strict. Consequently every emptiness
certificate at a coarser link is a certificate of recursive nonviability at
the finer one.
\end{proposition}

\emph{Proof.} An action viable for \(N\) steps keeps every compatible
trajectory in \(\mathcal{V}\) over the first interval, hence is tube-safe at
\(\Delta\); a tube-safe action is, at every active boundary state of every
compatible branch, a direction of nonnegative constraint drift against
every admissible disturbance (otherwise a measurable adverse selection
exits within the interval), hence instantaneously safe; instantaneous safety
requires admissibility at every branch. Strictness is real: the two-patch
instance of Section 6 has
\(\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{tube}}(\cdot, 2)\) empty where
\(\mathcal{R}^{B}_{\mathcal{V}}\) is a singleton. \hfill\(\square\)

\begin{proposition}[timing as blind-window predecessor emptiness]\label{prop:timing}
For the delayed-information model (no information before \(T_{\mathrm{obs}}\),
revelation at \(T_{\mathrm{obs}}\)), let
\(\mathrm{Pre}_{\mathrm{blind}}(\mathscr{C})\) be the collection of
information states admitting a blind response --- admissible for every
branch, common across indistinguishable branches, within the declared
window class \(\Pi_{\mathrm{win}}\) --- that keeps every branch in
\(\mathcal{V}\) through the window with every revealed successor in
\(\mathscr{C}\). Write \(\mathrm{Pre}_{\mathrm{blind}}^{t}(\mathscr{C})\)
for the \(t\)-fold iteration of the predecessor map, and say that
\(B_0\) \emph{survives at horizon} \(t\) (within the class) when
\(B_0 \in \mathrm{Pre}_{\mathrm{blind}}^{t}(\mathscr{C}')\) for the
revealed-successor collections \(\mathscr{C}'\) along the way. Let
\(\sigma^{*}_{\Pi_{\mathrm{win}}}(B_0)\) be the guaranteed
blind-window survival time of the declared class --- the class index is
load-bearing, since different classes empty at different horizons. Then
\begin{align*}
\sigma^{*}_{\Pi}(B_0) &= \sup \{ t : B_0 \text{ survives at horizon } t
\text{ under } \mathrm{Pre}_{\mathrm{blind}}^{\Pi} \}, \\
\sigma^{*}_{\Pi}(B_0) &< T_{\mathrm{obs}} \ \Longrightarrow\ B_0 \notin
\mathrm{Pre}_{\mathrm{blind}}^{\Pi, T_{\mathrm{obs}}}(\mathscr{C}).
\end{align*}
The timing certificate of the companions is exactly this emptiness, evaluated
at the observation horizon; and the declaration of the window class is
load-bearing, with exact boundary values on the audited grid (Section 6,
hidden-regime grid): the declared hold class survives to horizon \(k\)
exactly when \(z_{0} \ge 1 + k\) (discrete survival sup
\(\lfloor z_{0} - 1 \rfloor\), matching the successor's continuous
\(\sigma^{*}(z_{0}) = z_{0} - 1\) level sets), while the unrestricted
sequential class survives exactly when \(z_{0} \ge 2\) for \(k \ge 2\).
\end{proposition}

\emph{Proof.} The sup-identity is the definition of \(\sigma^{*}\) read
through Theorem~\ref{thm:exact}: survival to horizon \(t\) is precisely
nonemptiness of the blind predecessor correspondence at \(t\). If
\(\sigma^{*}(B_0) < T_{\mathrm{obs}}\), no blind response keeps every branch
safe to the horizon, so the correspondence at \(T_{\mathrm{obs}}\) is empty
whatever post-reveal collection \(\mathscr{C}\) is offered. \hfill\(\square\)

\begin{proposition}[certification as label selection]\label{prop:label}
For a label space \(\{0,1\}\) and the safety label
\(\mathbf{1}_{K}\), define the exact-label correspondence on a set \(F\) of
augmented states by
\(\Lambda_{K}(F) = \{ \ell \in \{0,1\} : \ell = \mathbf{1}_{K}(z)\ \forall z
\in F \}\). An exact observation-only certifier exists if and only if
\(\Lambda_{K}(O^{-1}(y)) \neq \varnothing\) for every observation \(y\); the
certainly-safe set is \(\{ y : \Lambda_{K}(O^{-1}(y)) = \{1\} \}\), the
maximal sound label relaxation. The fibre criterion is the label-selection
analogue of common-action selection at the zeroth level of the recursion;
it is a limit on certification, not a policy-viability obstruction
(Section 7).
\end{proposition}

\emph{Proof.} A certifier is a map \(\ell: O(Z) \to \{0,1\}\) with
\(\ell(y) = \mathbf{1}_{K}(z)\) for all \(z \in O^{-1}(y)\); such a value
exists exactly when \(\mathbf{1}_{K}\) is constant on the fibre, which is
nonemptiness of \(\Lambda_{K}(O^{-1}(y))\). The certainly-safe set collects
the fibres on which the only constant label is the safe one.
\hfill\(\square\)

\subsection{5. The specializations}\label{specializations}

The four one-step mechanisms of the companion paper are the static
intersection object at four choices of acceptable response, evaluated at
the information state --- the mechanism is the intersection across
compatible branches, \(\Gamma^{\mathrm{adm}}(B) = \bigcap_{z \in B} U(z)\)
for admissibility, \(\Gamma^{\mathrm{safe}}(B) = \bigcap_{z \in B}
\mathcal{R}_{\mathcal{V}}(z)\) for instantaneous safety,
\(\Gamma^{\mathrm{tube}}(B; \Delta) = \bigcap_{z \in B}
\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{tube}}(z, \Delta)\) for tube safety at a review
length (the held action reading) --- and the epistemic-emptiness
construction, which is the admissibility register evaluated on a merged
observation fibre --- one constant observation, two admissible-response
sets with empty intersection. The dynamic exit certificate is the
\(N = 0\), singleton-information-state base case of the same emptiness
form: at a single branch the intersection is trivial, and the drift
condition empties the boundary response set outright --- no
information-theoretic content, and no recursion needed. The delayed-information
obstruction is Proposition~\ref{prop:timing}: the same intersection,
demanded through the blind window and handed to the revealed successors.
The certification limit is Proposition~\ref{prop:label}. The
certainty-equivalence trap is Corollary~\ref{cor:ce}. One object --- a
response acceptable for every compatible branch at the required recursion
level --- seven emptinesses.

\subsection{6. Verified re-derivations}\label{verified}

The framework's identities are machine-checked in exact rational arithmetic
(\texttt{paper2\_viable\_selector\_v3\_verify.py}, standard library; twelve
check families, all passing) on the worked systems of the companions. \emph{Static intersection
(two-floor belief):} the common admissible correspondence is \([0,1]\) ---
nonempty --- while the common safe correspondence is empty: the
common-action certificate is exactly \(\Gamma^{B} = \varnothing\).
\emph{Label selection (two-floor index and its two-dimensional aggregate):}
the crossing fibre has \(\Lambda_{K} = \varnothing\), the safe fibre
\(\Lambda_{K} = \{1\}\), and the certainly-safe readings are exactly the
aggregate values \(I \ge \tfrac75\) (seven grid cells), matching the
companions' computation. \emph{Ladder (two-patch system of the calculus paper's protection
audit --- patches \(i \in \{1,2\}\) with stocks \(z_{i} \in
\{0,1,2,3\}\), recruitment \(+1\) per step cap 3, unprotected-patch
damage 2, instruments \(u \in \{1,2\}\) protecting one patch, floors
\(z_{i} \ge 1\); defined in Abaee 2026a, Section 8, and restated with
full data in the verification script):}
\(\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{tube}}(\cdot, 2) \subseteq
\mathcal{R}^{B}_{\mathcal{V}} \subseteq U^{B}\) holds at every state, is
strict at exactly five --- at \((1,2)\) the two-step held-action
correspondence is empty while the instantaneous one is \(\{1\}\) ---
and tight at exactly four (the counts are asserted exactly by the
record).
\emph{Recursive identity (two-patch audit):} the \(\Gamma_{N}\) correspondence
recursion and the belief-kernel recursion return identical verdicts on all
six audited beliefs (three viable, with witness selectors; three nonviable,
each certified by common-action emptiness), and singleton
\(\Gamma_{8}\) nonemptiness coincides with the full-information kernel.
\emph{Timing (hidden-regime grid, all 48 cells):} the blind-window
predecessor correspondence over the declared hold class is nonempty at
horizon \(k\) exactly when \(z_{0} \ge 1 + k\) --- the viability boundary ---
while the unrestricted sequential class is nonempty exactly when
\(z_{0} \ge 2\) for \(k \ge 2\): Proposition~\ref{prop:timing}'s
load-bearing declaration, verified. \emph{Policy class (the certainty-equivalence instance, canonical-law
form):} the corrected law has drift identically zero on \([1,2]\), so
the unrestricted correspondence is nonempty, while the canonical
certainty-equivalence law --- the one acting on the uncorrected reading
--- has drift at least \(\tfrac{21}{100} > 0\) there and exits the band
in finite time (direct iteration): the correspondence restricted to that
law is empty, Corollary~\ref{cor:ce} attained on the canonical
restriction. The quantifier over the whole uncorrected class is the
successor architecture's layer-five reading; this paper verifies and
claims the canonical-law instance and cites the rest. \emph{Factor
identity (audit addition):} on the six audited beliefs the four factor
correspondences nest exactly as Proposition~\ref{prop:ladder} states,
and the four-fold intersection equals its finest factor --- the
meta-theorem's factorization locates certificates along a filtration
(checks V7--V11).

\subsection{7. What is not unified}\label{discipline}

The framework unifies the \emph{form} of the certificates --- emptiness of a
response correspondence at an information state --- not their \emph{cause},
and conflating the two would misdescribe the companions. The dynamic exit
certificate is not epistemic: it defeats full-information control. The fibre
criterion is not a policy-viability obstruction: it concerns exact
certification, a verdict rather than a control. The certainty-equivalence
trap is not necessarily informational: it can occur under an injective
observation, because the restriction is on the policy class. Accordingly,
the strongest claim the framework supports is: observation-based viability
requires the existence of a common recursively safe selector from
information states to admissible responses; the obstruction certificates of
the companions are checkable sufficient conditions under which no such
selector exists; in finite-horizon finite systems the backward recursion
characterizes selector existence exactly; in continuous or infinite-horizon
settings the certificates are sound local exclusion tests. Within those
limits the selector framework is the spine on which the companion results,
and the extensions that follow, assemble.

\subsection{8. Outlook: the stochastic selector}\label{outlook}

The belief-state safety value of the companion paper's probabilistic
development is the stochastic extension of the recursive selector:
\(\Gamma_{N}\) replaced by the value recursion over distributional
information states, emptiness replaced by deficit from unity. Its exact
rational theory --- piecewise-linear values, support degeneration, and the
agreement between certificate boundaries and value boundaries on the audited
classes --- is developed in the sequel, whose second edition also
quantifies the class declaration used in Proposition~\ref{prop:timing}.
