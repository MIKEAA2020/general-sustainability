Please consider "SafeTransition: exact rational certification of transition safety for sustainability assessment" for publication in Environmental Modelling & Software as a Software Description.

Sustainability transitions are judged against separately binding floors — biomass limits, income thresholds, service levels — yet the scalarized indicators in common use certify such systems only in aggregate and only at audited snapshots. A weighted index can therefore declare a transition safe while a plan in the admitted mix violates a floor mid-interval, undetected. SafeTransition is a dependency-free Python 3 library (Python 3.9 or later, standard library only) that makes these assessments exact and independently checkable:

- five assessment operators spanning endpoint-only, aggregate (weak-sustainability), and trajectory (strong-sustainability) semantics, with proven one-sided tube verdicts: a passing tube certifies safety, a violating one is honest about its inconclusiveness;
- belief recursions for transitions under incomplete observation, with an explicit completeness (quotient) condition;
- Farkas weight-partition certificates for every aggregate acceptance, verified by an independent program that shares no code with the library;
- exact rational and dyadic margin arithmetic, retaining 323 denominator bits at the witness where binary floating point rounds the deciding gap away;
- a scaling study over six instance families with answers asserted at every size, a 59-test suite, and a 24-check exact benchmark on a regulated-fishery transition under ecological disturbance;
- an institutional dashboard for delivering verdicts and certificates to non-technical users.

Every deposited value re-derives from source by a single command. The verification deposit (https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.33764023) contains the complete package, the certificate protocol with negative tests, the figure pipeline, and preserved literature-search records. The work fits the journal's interest in rigorously verified environmental software and in reproducibility as a published artefact in its own right.

This manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and reports no competing interests. The author approves the submission in its entirety; an AI-use disclosure appears in the acknowledgements.
