\begin{abstract}
Many sustainability failures are caused not by noisy data but by structural
uncertainty resolved too late. This paper, the D10 item of the
generalization catalogue, formalizes that sentence inside the obstruction
calculus. The state augments with a hidden parameter
$\theta\in\Theta$; the belief lives on the product; the obstruction is the
emptiness of the joint floor-servable set over $(x,\theta)$ cells. On the
declining working instance ($\theta=\pm1$ gating the correctable component
of a $-\tfrac1{10}$ drift), the theory computes three exact thresholds:
with $\theta$ observed, every grid cell is viable; under one-probe
endogenous learning the probe reveals $\theta$ exactly (the branch
separation is $2$, so one observation splits the belief with no
statistics) but survival forces $z_0\ge\tfrac{21}{10}$; and with
exogenous revelation at horizon $T_{\mathrm{learn}}$ viability holds
exactly iff $z_0\ge\tfrac{21}{10}+\tfrac{T_{\mathrm{learn}}}{10}$ --- the
learning deadline acts as the timing obstruction's observation deadline,
and the kernel shrinks exactly linearly in it ($|K|=5,4,3,2$ on the grid
for $T_{\mathrm{learn}}=0,1,2,3$), antitone with strict steps. A two-patch
instance with unknown regeneration rate $g\in\{1,2\}$ shows the same
phenomenon on the programme's audited belief: unknown-regeneration
nonviable, $(x,g)$-observed viable (6/6 check families).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and relation to the programme}

Paper 2's hidden-mode example ($\dot z=\theta u$, $\theta=\pm1$) is the
seed; the catalogue generalizes it to augmented states
$(x,\theta)\in X\times\Theta$, beliefs on the product, and the learning
deadline $T_{\mathrm{learn}}$ in the role of the timing layer's
observation deadline, with endogenous learning as belief updating. The
stochastic selector supplies the belief-dynamics discipline; the successor
supplies the timing object; neither is modified. The list of affected
sustainability parameters --- regeneration rates, climate sensitivity,
institutional effectiveness --- motivates; the paper verifies on exact
instances.

\section{Hidden parameters in the calculus}

The augmented belief $B\subseteq X\times\Theta$ is read per cell under the
successor's read-then-act semantics; the obstruction is
\begin{equation*}
\bigcap_{(x,\theta)\in B}\mathcal R_{\mathcal V}(x,\theta)=\varnothing
\ \Longrightarrow\ B\notin\mathcal K_{\mathcal I}.
\end{equation*}
Learning enters as information: exogenous revelation at $T_{\mathrm{learn}}$
(the structure coarsens before, refines after) or endogenous probing
(actions chosen to split the belief).

\begin{proposition}[Learning deadline as timing obstruction]
\label{prop:deadline}
In the declining working instance with quantized control
$u\in\{-1,0,1\}$ and hold-then-probe-then-act schedules: viability holds
exactly iff $z_0\ge\tfrac{21}{10}+\tfrac{T_{\mathrm{learn}}}{10}$. The
kernel is antitone in $T_{\mathrm{learn}}$ with strict steps, and the
probe reveals $\theta$ exactly (the two post-probe branches differ by $2$,
so one observation splits the belief).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Before $T_{\mathrm{learn}}$ the drift is $-\tfrac1{10}$ (hold $u=0$), so
survival to the probe forces $z_0-\tfrac{T_{\mathrm{learn}}}{10}\ge$ the
probe threshold; the probe $u=+1$ moves $z$ by $-\tfrac1{10}+\theta$,
which must stay safe on both branches, giving the threshold
$\tfrac{21}{10}$ at $T_{\mathrm{learn}}=0$; after the probe the learned
law $u=\theta$ has drift $-\tfrac1{10}+\theta^2=\tfrac9{10}>0$, so
survival is indefinite. The branch positions $z_0-\tfrac1{10}T_{\mathrm{
learn}}-\tfrac1{10}\pm1$ differ by $2$, so the post-probe reading
determines $\theta$ uniquely. Antitonicity is the threshold identity;
strictness on the grid is the computed counts $5,4,3,2$.
\end{proof}

\section{Verified record (6/6 exact check families)}

E1 $\theta$-observed viability on all $16$ grid cells (drift
$\tfrac9{10}$); E2 the probe threshold $\tfrac{21}{10}$ as a branchwise
identity; E3 the deadline identity at $T_{\mathrm{learn}}=0,\dots,4$
(at $4$ exactly the top cell $\tfrac{25}{10}$ survives); E4 the exact
separation of the post-probe branches (injective revelation, no
statistics); E5 the antitone chain with exact counts
$|K|=5>4>3>2$; E6 the two-patch unknown-regeneration instance: the
audited belief with unknown $g\in\{1,2\}$ is nonviable under the
$g$-blind aggregate reading and viable under $(x,g)$-observed cells,
each fixed $g$ viability-preserving --- structural uncertainty, not noise,
is the obstructing object.

\section{What is not claimed}

No learning theory is claimed beyond the one-probe exact split:
Bayesian updating, repeated experiments, and exploration--exploitation
trade-offs are out of scope. The deadline result is for the audited
schedule family (hold, probe, learned act); other schedules are not
classified. The parameter $\theta$ enters additively; state-dependent
parameter dynamics and parameter drift belong to the hybrid companion's
setting. The sustainability parameter list motivates the model class and
carries no empirical content here.
