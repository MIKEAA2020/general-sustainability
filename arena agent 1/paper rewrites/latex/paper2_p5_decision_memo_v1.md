## 2. The options

**(a) Designate E1 v50 as P5.** Zero duplication: a 35-page, 47/47-verified,
provenance-locked applied paper exists. Its core method is forecast-ladder
scoring (Brier layer, RMSE ladder), with the programme's review-timing identity
framing the suspension analysis (§3.10) and the obstruction reading of the
2023 framework revision. Cost: the obstruction machinery appears in one
section, not as the paper's engine.

**(b) Build the obstruction-applied paper on the locked data.** A new paper:
hidden-regime viability on the NCAM/xteNCAM SSB series with the capelin
covariate against Blim = 276 kt and the LRP — the deadline law, the
hidden-parameter layer, and the monitoring identities as the engine, exactly
the methodology the flagships formalize. Cost: a full new build (the data
layer is reusable as-is; the model layer is new); E1 v50 continues to exist
regardless, so nothing is lost — only delayed.

## 3. Adjudication against the audit's default

The audit's default: "choose (a) unless there is a hard requirement that P5
must use the obstruction methodology." **No hard requirement exists** in the
charter record (L4 asks for calibration of programme *results*, which E1 v50
partially satisfies via §3.10 and the calibration record). The doubt the audit
allows ("if there is any doubt") is real but small: the programme's identity
is its machinery, and a flagship slot argued purely by designation weakens the
board's meaning.

**Recommendation: (a)** — designate E1 v50 as P5, with (b) recorded as a
candidate *future* applied-viability paper (it does not need the P5 slot to be
worth building, and its data layer is already locked). If the owner prefers
the full obstruction demonstration as the fifth flagship, (b) starts with a
one-page scoping memo per the audit; the data layer is ready either way.

**Either way:** the roadmap board and supersession map update at execution;
P1's owner venue decision remains the next non-technical blocker on the
programme's critical path.
