## B. Root cause and what the audit did not reach

**Root cause (one sentence, three layers).** The flagship consolidated two seeds whose presentations disagreed — bs2 stated the closed form class-unqualified alongside an unrestricted-class declaration theorem (the C1/C3/C4 complex is already latent in `paper2_belief_state_v2` alone), while the selector seed carried the correct class-scoped statements but its own rem:scope misquoted P1's σ\* direction (S2) — and the merger put the disagreeing texts in one paper, making each latent inconsistency live. The D10 deadline label ("exogenous revelation") was wrong at the seed level (C2).

**Strengthening beyond the audit's list.** (a) The abstract carried the C3/C4/C5 defects too, not only the theorem bodies; all synced. (b) New exact content added while repairing: the free-probe benchmark (21/10 flat — the audit asserted it informally; here it is computed with its kernel), the probe-free benchmark (1 + T_learn/10, kernels 16–12, with the pinned ⊆ probe-free kernel inclusion), the dedup census 348, the off-grid difference region to z₀ = 4.0, the per-cell deficit profile, and the lost-branch identities. (c) The v1 layer-2 check "36 unit values" already encoded the timing cells' k = 1 value — the audit's C3 could have been mounted from the flagship's own verification output; the check is now joined by the full profile assertion. (d) P1 v49 confirmed correct on the σ\* direction — recorded here for the programme; P1 untouched.
