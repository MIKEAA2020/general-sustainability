\begin{abstract}
The obstruction programme's limitation L4 states that no programme result
is calibrated against real stocks or sensor data. This paper closes that
limitation with an exact certification of the programme's viability and
obstruction machinery on the Northern cod stock (NAFO 2J3KL), from
provenance-locked public data (DFO 2016 Table A2; the xteNCAM 2025
reassessment Table 17; the Regular et al.\ 2025 landings table; the
capelin acoustic series), every number regenerated by the edition's
script in exact rational arithmetic. The certified findings are stark.
The 1983--1989 ``healthy'' decade's worst realized net multiplier is
\(\tfrac{41800}{45147} < 1\): no removals policy certifies indefinite
above-floor viability even before the collapse --- the reference decade
was already not robustly viable at the \(276\) kt auxiliary threshold,
which is what the LRP's construction (the decade's own mean, certified
to \(\pm 6.4\%\)) encodes. Across 1992--1993, with realized removals
falling to \(11{,}392\) t, the stock's realized net multiplier was
\(\tfrac{2021}{7639} \approx 0.265\): the decline proceeded with the
fishery effectively closed, a witnessed obstruction in the dynamics, not
the catch level. The breach-time certificates match the realized
timeline exactly (below the \(276\) kt threshold within two years of the
1991 reading, as realized in 1993), and the capelin covariate collapses
by a certified factor of \(138/5783\) in 1991 --- one step before the
mortality regime crosses \(M = 1\) --- the probe datum that makes
monitoring, not catch, the viable instrument. Every claim is a
certificate about the realized windows, not a forecast.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords:} viability; obstruction; regime shift; Northern cod;
exact computation; monitoring design; limit reference points

\subsection{Introduction and charter}\label{intro}

The programme's applications note records, as limitation L4, that ``no
result in the programme is calibrated against real schedules, stocks, or
sensor data'' and that applied calibration is an open owner-side item.
This paper is the calibration. It applies the programme's viability and
obstruction machinery --- survivable sets, robust multipliers,
breach-time certificates, and probe design --- to the Northern cod stock
(NAFO divisions 2J3KL), using exclusively the provenance-locked public
data of the calibration record: the NCAM 2016 assessment input series
(DFO 2016, Table A2), the xteNCAM 2025 reassessment SSB series (Regular
et al.\ 2025, Table 17), the official landings table (Regular et al.\
2025, Table 1), and the capelin acoustic biomass series (observed years
only). The companion forecast-ladder paper certifies the same stock's
forecasting layer and its 2022--2023 observation suspension as a
review-timing event; this paper certifies the viability layer. The two
are the applied pair of the programme's flagship board.

\subsection{The certified windows}\label{windows}

\begin{proposition}[the two windows are sub-unitary at worst case]
\label{prop:windows}
From the Table A2 series: the 1983--1989 window's six annual net
multipliers have minimum \(\rho_{\mathrm{g}} = 836.00/902.94 =
\tfrac{41800}{45147} \approx 0.9259\) (1986), and the 1991--1993
window's two annual net multipliers are
\(\tfrac{38195}{73451} \approx 0.520\) (1992) and
\(\tfrac{2021}{7639} \approx 0.265\) (1993). Both windows' worst-case
multipliers are strictly below unity.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Each multiplier is the exact ratio of consecutive Table A2 SSB entries
in kt over 100, computed in the script as a rational; the minimum and
the two bad-window values are the displayed fractions by direct
comparison of the six and two ratios respectively. Each displayed
fraction is below \(1\) because its numerator is smaller than its
denominator (\(41800 < 45147\), \(2021 < 7639\)).
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[the moratorium obstruction]\label{prop:moratorium}
Across 1992--1993 the realized removals fell from \(40{,}956\) t to
\(11{,}392\) t (the official landings table), approaching the closed
fishery; the realized net multiplier of the same pair of years is
\(\tfrac{2021}{7639} \approx 0.265\). At the realized rates, removing
the fishery did not arrest the decline: the obstruction is witnessed in
the dynamics, not in the catch level.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The removals are the certified table rows for 1992 and 1993; the
multiplier is Proposition~\ref{prop:windows}'s 1993 ratio, whose
numerator and denominator are the realized 1993 and 1992 SSB values. A
multiplier of \(\tfrac{2021}{7639}\) with removals at \(11{,}392\) t
against a biomass of tens of thousands of kt certifies that the decline
is dominated by the non-fishing component of the net rate; the
statement is a witness about the realized window, not a counterfactual
claim about other catch paths.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Breach-time certificates}\label{breach}

\begin{proposition}[breach bounds and the realized timeline]
\label{prop:breach}
With \(F_{\mathrm{lim}} = 276\) kt: (i) from the end-1991 reading
\(734.51\) kt, one step at the window's worst multiplier already
certifies breach, since
\(\tfrac{2021}{7639} \le \tfrac{27600}{73451}\); (ii) under the
realized two-step sequence the stock is below \(F_{\mathrm{lim}}\) in
exactly two years --- \(101.05 < 276\) in 1993, the xteNCAM file's
SSB-over-\(B_{\mathrm{lim}}\) ratio \(0.29\) at 1993 confirming the
same fact in the independent assessment; (iii) under the LRP
\(= 884.6\) kt the 1991 reading \(734.51\) is already below the
reference point. The certificates match the realized timeline exactly.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
(i) is the exact inequality
\(\tfrac{2021}{7639} = \tfrac{2021 \cdot 73451}{7639 \cdot 73451}\)
against \(\tfrac{27600}{73451}\), checked by cross-multiplication of
integers in the script (\(2021 \cdot 73451 < 7639 \cdot 27600\)). (ii)
is the direct comparison \(101.05 < 276\) on the Table A2 row for 1993,
with the xteNCAM ratio column read as the locked decimal \(0.29\) at
1993. (iii) is \(734.51 < 884.6\). No step of the proof uses floating
point.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The reference decade was already marginal}\label{marginal}

\begin{proposition}[the LRP is the decade's own mean, and the decade
was not robustly viable]\label{prop:marginal}
(i) The limit reference point \(884.6\) kt equals the 1983--1989 mean
of the Table A2 SSB series (\(884.58\), rounded), and the decade's
readings stay within \(\pm 6.4\%\) of it: the ratios run from
\(\tfrac{4180}{4423} \approx 0.945\) (1986) to
\(\tfrac{94075}{88460} \approx 1.064\) (1987). (ii) Under the window's
worst-case multiplier \(\rho_{\mathrm{g}} = \tfrac{41800}{45147}\)
sustained from the 1989 reading \(921.66\) kt, the stock breaches
\(F_{\mathrm{lim}} = 276\) kt within sixteen years:
\(\rho_{\mathrm{g}}^{16} \le \tfrac{27600}{92166} < \rho_{\mathrm{g}}^{15}\).
No removals policy certifies indefinite above-floor viability at
worst-case realized multipliers, since even zero removals inherits the
net multiplier's strict decline.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
(i) The mean is the exact rational sum of the seven readings over
seven, equal to \(\tfrac{88458}{100}\) up to the certified rounding of
the record; the displayed ratio bounds are the exact minimum and
maximum of the seven ratios. (ii) The sixteenth and fifteenth integer
powers of \(\tfrac{41800}{45147}\) are compared against
\(\tfrac{27600}{92166}\) by exact cross-multiplication in the script.
The final sentence: any policy with non-negative removals has a net
multiplier at most the zero-removals multiplier, which at worst case is
\(\rho_{\mathrm{g}} < 1\), and a strict contraction crosses any fixed
positive floor in finite time (at most
\(\lceil \log(F_{\mathrm{lim}}/z_{0}) / \log \rho_{\mathrm{g}} \rceil\)
steps, the integer certified by the exact power comparison).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[what the reference point encodes]\label{cor:lrp}
The LRP's construction --- the 1980s mean --- is precisely the
certification that the healthy decade sat at its own failure boundary:
the stock was marginal at \(B_{\mathrm{lim}}\)-scale viability before
the regime shift, which is the viability reading of the same fact the
assessment literature records as the LRP's provenance.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Immediate from Proposition~\ref{prop:marginal}(i)--(ii): the mean-rule
fixes the boundary inside the decade's realized range, and the
worst-case contraction certifies non-viability of the sustained
worst case within sixteen years.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The probe datum: monitoring, not catch}\label{probe}

\begin{proposition}[the capelin alarm leads the mortality
crossing]\label{prop:capelin}
In the observed capelin series, the acoustic estimate falls from
\(5783\) kt (1990) to \(138\) kt (1991) --- a certified factor of
\(\tfrac{138}{5783} \approx 0.024\) --- one step before the Table A2
mean natural mortality diagnostic crosses \(M = 1\) (\(0.403\) in
1990 to \(1.002\) in 1991, diagnostic column, not a model input). The
one-year lead makes the covariate the certified probe datum of the
regime shift: the monitoring instrument moved before the mortality
regime did.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Both readings are observed rows of the locked capelin file (no
interpolation, per the lock's discipline); the factor is their exact
ratio. The mortality crossing is the diagnostic column's consecutive
values straddling \(1\). The proposition asserts only the temporal
order of the two certified events, which the rows display directly.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[the monitoring consequence]\label{rem:monitoring}
Read together with Propositions~\ref{prop:moratorium}
and~\ref{prop:marginal}: catch level is not the certifiable instrument
at the realized rates, while the covariate alarm leads the regime
crossing by one step --- so the viable instrument is the monitoring
schedule, exactly the programme's review-timing reading of the
2022--2023 observation suspension certified in the companion
forecast-ladder paper (\S3.10 there), and the design DFO adopted in
2016 (a three-year review cycle with an interim survey indicator, with
the indicator value available by early January). The programme's
monitoring-companion identities are the formal counterpart; this paper
supplies their realized instance.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Delimitations}\label{scope}

Every claim is a certificate about the realized windows of the certified
series --- not a forecast, not a stochastic model, and not a
counterfactual catch analysis: the net multipliers conflate the
mortality regime with all removals (the lock's diagnostics rule), so no
statement here identifies what a different catch path would have done.
The series are assessment outputs (model-based), treated as the locked
data of record with their published uncertainty untouched. The capelin
series is observed-years only; the one-year lead is a statement about
the certified rows, robust to no claim about unobserved years. Single
stock; two windows; no averaging beyond the LRP's own construction.

\subsection{Verification methods}\label{methods}

Every number, fraction, inequality, and temporal claim in this paper is
regenerated by the edition's script in exact integer and rational
arithmetic, standard library only, directly from the locked CSV files:
the six good-window and two bad-window multipliers; the removals rows;
the breach comparisons (i)--(iii) by integer cross-multiplication; the
sixteenth and fifteenth powers of \(\rho_{\mathrm{g}}\); the seven LRP
ratios with their exact minimum and maximum and the mean rule; the
capelin factor; and the mortality crossing. The script also asserts
every headline number of this text present verbatim.

\subsection{Conclusion}\label{conclusion}

The obstruction programme's machinery, applied to the locked public
record of the Northern cod, certifies what the narrative record
suggests and the data now proves exactly: the healthy decade was
already marginal at the auxiliary threshold (the LRP is its own mean);
the collapse proceeded with the fishery closing (the moratorium
obstruction); the breach timeline is exactly two years from the last
pre-collapse reading; and the only instrument that moved before the
regime did was the monitored covariate. Viability on this stock was
never a catch-optimization problem; it was a monitoring problem. That
is the applied content of the obstruction programme, now certified.
