**To the Editors, *Mathematics of Operations Research***

Dear Editors,

I submit the manuscript *Probabilistic sufficiency under partial observation: belief-state safety values, survivable-set antichains, and the exact price of late information* for consideration as a research article.

**Problem.** Set-valued viability theory under incomplete observation decides whether an information state is viable; it does not say how much safety survives when it is not. The quantitative object is the belief-state safety value of a finite-horizon safety POMDP — the maximal probability, over a declared policy class, of staying in the safe set for k steps from a distribution over states. The POMDP literature characterises the qualitative (probability-one) problem on supports and treats the quantitative problem numerically; viability theory gives sufficient output-feedback conditions. What has been missing is an exact account of how the two theories meet: when does the probabilistic value recover the set-valued verdict, what structure does the value carry below one, and how do information timing, sensor noise and distributional ambiguity price out.

**Contribution.** The manuscript gives that account in exact rational arithmetic. (i) On finite models with deterministic kernels and observation maps the posterior support follows the set-valued post-state exactly, so the value is one if and only if the support is feedback-viable, and otherwise the deficit is at least the smallest prior mass — necessary as well as sufficient. (ii) The α-vectors of the value are the indicators of the maximal jointly survivable subsets of the support: a Sperner-bounded antichain whose bound is attained; the value of observation over blind control is at most 1 − max b, attained for every support size. (iii) On a delayed hidden-regime class the hold-class, open-loop and fully observed values are closed-form in all parameters; the class lattice orders them and the two gaps — the value of flexibility and the value of observation — are each min(w, 1−w) on disjoint, explicitly located cells, which shows the classical timing obstruction to be a statement about the declared hold class. (iv) Information becomes a design variable: a probe pinned to a deadline moves the viability threshold linearly between a free-probe and a probe-free benchmark, and a general hold–probe–learn schedule has an exact threshold that reduces to an additive law exactly under aligned worst losses, with a misaligned instance where the additive law fails. (v) Under noisy sensing the deficit is the prior mass weighted by fatal-reading probabilities, repeated readings trade floor margin for exponentially smaller wrong-majority probability, and a contamination operator interpolates exactly between expectation and worst case at one stage but not across stages.

**Fit.** The results are structural theorems about finite-horizon POMDPs — support identities, antichain representations of α-vectors, parametric closed forms, information-timing laws, robust operators — of the kind the journal publishes, with an unusual standard of evidence: every value, witness, count and threshold is regenerated by a 62-check exact-arithmetic script distributed with the submission, and three statements are machine-checked in Lean 4 with their scope declared. The related-work section states what is new relative to the qualitative POMDP theory, the viability and stochastic-viability literature, robust and distributionally robust POMDPs, and shielding.

**Reproducibility.** The exact module, the verification script with its archived output, the figure script and the table script accompany the manuscript; the Supplementary Material (S1–S8) carries the complete certified tables, derivations, the verification record and a symbol table.
