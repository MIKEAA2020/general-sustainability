# Highlights — paper02 v16

* **Exact sufficiency, both directions.** On finite models with deterministic kernels and observation maps the Bayesian posterior support follows the set-valued post-state exactly, so the belief-state safety value is one **if and only if** the support is feedback-viable, and otherwise the deficit is at least the smallest prior mass (Theorem 3.1) — a necessary-and-sufficient counterpart to the sufficient-only output-feedback criteria of viability theory.
* **α-vectors are an antichain.** The α-vectors of the value are the indicators of the maximal jointly survivable subsets of the support; they form a Sperner-bounded antichain, the bound is attained, and the deficit is the smallest mass left outside a maximal survivable set (Theorem 3.3). The value of observation over blind control is at most 1 − max b, attained for every support size (Proposition 3.2).
* **Closed forms and a class lattice.** On the delayed hidden-regime class the hold, open-loop and fully observed values are closed-form in all parameters; the value of flexibility and the value of observation are each min(w, 1−w) on disjoint, explicitly located cells, so the classical timing obstruction is a statement about the declared hold class (Theorems 5.1–5.2, Corollary 5.3).
* **The exact price of late information.** A probe pinned to deadline T moves the viability threshold linearly, z₀ ≥ 21/10 + T/10, between a flat free-probe benchmark and a probe-free one; a general hold–probe–learn schedule has an exact threshold that collapses to an additive law exactly under aligned worst losses, with a misaligned instance where the additive law overstates by one unit (Proposition 6.1, Theorem 6.2).
* **Noise and ambiguity, exactly.** Under noisy sensing the deficit is the prior mass weighted by fatal-reading probabilities (a two-state sensor attains 1/20 against a minimal mass of 1/2); n repeated readings bring the wrong-majority probability below (4ε(1−ε))^{n/2} at one unit of floor margin per reading; a contamination operator interpolates exactly at one stage but not across stages (1/16 against 1/8) (Sections 7–8).
* **Reproducible to the last digit.** Every value, witness, count and threshold is regenerated in exact rational arithmetic by a 62-check script; three statements are additionally machine-checked in Lean 4 with their scope declared.
