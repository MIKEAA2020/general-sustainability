# Addendum — *Minimax Dual Certificates for the Common-Action Obstruction*, Edition 1

**Lineage.** New standalone paper (born from the fifth joint-audit round; not a version of any existing paper).
**Source of the proposal.** `uploads/audits2.txt`, block 3 ("Universal Minimax Epistemic Duality Theorem"; block 4 is a byte-identical duplicate mislabeled as grok's — adjudicated once, as gemini's).
**What was audited before adoption.** Every step of the proposed theorem was checked: the Sion minimax core and the emptiness iff are correct under the stated hypotheses (verified exactly on the polyhedral instance, both directions); the sparsity step's proposed proof conflated Carathéodory's theorem (k+2) with Helly's theorem (k+1) — repaired with a finite-reduction + LP-duality + basic-solution route, and tightness certified directly; the singleton corollary's dual is an adversarial *distribution* over disturbances, not a single worst disturbance (V6 pins the distinction); the fibre-classification and stochastic-bridge "corollaries" are not proven by the duality and are not claimed; the claimed resolution of Open Problem 1 (dynamic envelope via an "Epistemic HJBI operator" and a measure-curve certificate) is rejected — the operator is nonlocal and not a well-posed PDE, no viscosity theory or selection argument is supplied — and Open Problem 1 remains open (the paper records the envelope as a conjecture with the gaps named).
**The benchmark.** The audit's worked two-stock model changed control sets between its two evaluations (viable at Y = 5 under a capacity constraint, obstructed at Y = 4.1 under a demand floor that contradicts that same capacity). The paper replaces it with one fixed control set (demand floor 2) and solves it in closed form: critical aggregate Y* = 27/5, viable witness (6/5, 4/5) at Y = 5, two-point dual certificate with margin 3/50 at Y = 6, fibre-width formula Δx₁(Y) = Y − 4.
**Adopted from the same audit round.** The state-versus-epistemic separation axiom (implemented as the three-class obstruction taxonomy on the audited grid: physical / epistemic / institutional, V7); the "Minimum Viable Monitoring" direction in its verified form (the critical-aggregate and fibre-width results are exact instances); the convexity-boundary finding (a two-action instance with a strict minimax gap, V3) as the honest scope statement separating the continuous convex layer from the audited finite codex systems.
**Verification.** `minimax_dual_certificates_v1_verify.py`: eight check families (V1 polyhedral iff both directions; V2 normalized-Farkas recovery λ = (1/2,1/2), ε = 1/10, matching the calculus paper's §3.4 worked certificate; V3 the non-convex gap; V4 Helly-tight k+1 sparsity; V5 the corrected benchmark with the closed form; V6 the singleton reduction with the load-bearing distribution; V7 the three-class taxonomy by full enumeration; V8 the fibre-width formula), exact rational arithmetic, 8/8 passing.
