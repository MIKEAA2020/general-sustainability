# Does a valid v3 exist?

Short answer: **A valid v3 is possible in principle, but not for the generalization you are currently imagining.** The Uvalde record has narrowed the space of valid v3s to a small, specific region—and it has also disqualified the most natural candidate. Below I separate *what v3 needs*, *what the Uvalde record has ruled out*, and *whether what remains is worth calling a v3*.

---

## 1. What a v3 minimally needs

Restating your six requirements, but sharpened:

1. **A named transferable quantity.** Not "the same aquifer." A specific scalar or relation that is asserted to be pool-invariant.
2. **A pre-specified decision rule with power.** Given D1–D3, this must be a conjunction (DM ∧ bootstrap CI ∧ sub-period sign ∧ driver-dependence), not a point-RMSE comparison.
3. **Pre-registered porting parameters.** Clip, driver mapping, pumpage driver, annual rule, domain.
4. **Explicit multiplicity control** across pools × thresholds × horizons × floors × drivers.
5. **A scope condition** that excludes objects to which the claim does not apply.
6. **Falsification conditions written before scores.**

These are necessary. They are not sufficient. The Uvalde record shows why.

---

## 2. What the Uvalde record has ruled out

The record eliminates three candidate v3s:

**Ruled out: "The E3 verdict generalizes."**
The point-rule flip on J-27 is within noise (D1–D3), driver-independent (D5), and reverses at h=5. There is no robust E3-verdict replication to pre-register.

**Ruled out: "The E4 constructive claim generalizes."**
Uvalde has no physical-threshold analog. E4's constructive leg is not definable on Uvalde. No v3 can pre-register a test that does not exist.

**Ruled out: "Pools of the same aquifer are exchangeable."**
The record's §5.0 audit shows the transferable quantity is *not* pool identity. Whatever is invariant, it is not "same aquifer."

What survives:

**Survives: E4's institutional *negative* result.**
"The institutional threshold is protected by wet years, not the pumping family" replicates on Uvalde, more strongly than on J-17. But it is a negative result and it is already the qualitative content of E4.

**Survives (tentatively): recharge whiteness as a pool-invariant.**
AC(1) 0.182 vs 0.168 across two independent pools. This is the strongest candidate invariant in the record. But it is a *mechanism input*, not a verdict.

---

## 3. The core problem: Uvalde is no longer naive

This is the deepest issue and it is not stated in the record.

A v3 is only confirmatory if its predictions are made **before the data are seen**. Uvalde's results are now known. Any v3 that pre-registers "on Uvalde, we predict X" is:

- **post-hoc** if X matches what was observed, or
- **falsified already** if X does not.

Either way, Uvalde cannot serve as the confirmatory second pool for any v3 written *after* 10 Sep 2026. It can only serve as:

- the **motivating** case for a v3, or
- one **exploratory** point in a multi-pool design whose confirmatory weight falls on pools not yet analyzed.

This is the same logic that made the frozen spec frozen. The record correctly says "exploratory" for its own results; the v3 discussion has not yet absorbed the implication that **the exploratory pool is now spent as a confirmatory object**.

---

## 4. Does a valid v3 exist?

Conditionally, yes. Three distinct v3s are possible. They differ in scope and in what they would actually confirm.

### v3-A: The moderator v3 (E3, re-specified)

**Claim:** Recharge whiteness is pool-invariant. Head persistence is the moderator that determines whether the frozen retention rule selects M1 (low persistence) or M2 (high persistence). The E3 "verdict" is not a pool-level constant; it is a function of head AC(1).

**What it needs:** A pre-specified functional form (e.g., retention switches at some AC(1) threshold), a pre-registered decision rule, and **a third pool** with head AC(1) in the untested range.

**Does it exist?** Only if a third pool is available whose data has not been examined. On Uvalde alone, no.

**Is it a generalization of E3?** No. It is a *replacement* of E3's verdict claim with a moderator claim. That is a stronger paper, but it is not the paper you have.

### v3-B: The institutional-negative v3 (E4, scoped)

**Claim:** On any pool with an institutional threshold and a drought-of-record floor, the robust kernel is empty under the declared pumping family.

**What it needs:** A pre-registered list of pools, thresholds, and floors, plus a decision rule for "empty." Uvalde confirms it; **a third pool must test it.**

**Does it exist?** Only if a third pool with an institutional threshold is available and unanalyzed.

**Is it worth doing?** It strengthens E4's negative result from "observed on J-17" to "invariant across N pools." That is a real contribution, but it is a *negative* generalization.

### v3-C: The gap v3 (E4, positive, scoped)

**Claim:** The constructive intervention result depends on the attractor-to-threshold gap being positive and large enough that the reactive family can hold the threshold. Pools with gap ≈ 0 are out of scope.

**What it needs:** A formal definition of the gap, a pre-registered threshold for "large enough," and **a second pool with a physical threshold and a positive gap.**

**Does it exist?** Only if such a pool exists and is unanalyzed. Uvalde is out of scope by construction, so Uvalde cannot be the test.

**Is it a generalization of E4?** It is a *conditional* generalization: "E4 holds where the gap is positive." That is honest, but it is narrower than "E4 generalizes."

---

## 5. So what do you actually need to find v3?

You need **one of the following**, in order of strength:

1. **A third pool** with the relevant structural feature (physical threshold for v3-C; head AC(1) outside the J-17/J-27 range for v3-A; institutional threshold for v3-B), with data not yet analyzed. Without this, no v3 is confirmatory.
2. **A re-specification of the papers** so that the claim is about a *moderator* (v3-A) rather than a verdict. This is a theoretical move, not a data move, and it is the most defensible path if no third pool exists.
3. **Acceptance that v3 is a scoped, conditional generalization** (v3-B or v3-C) rather than a broad one. This is weaker but honest.

What you do **not** need—and cannot use—is a v3 that pre-registers predictions on Uvalde. Uvalde is spent.

---

## 6. Does a valid v3 exist? — Direct answer

**Yes, but only under one of these conditions:**

- **v3-A** exists if you are willing to replace E3's verdict claim with a moderator claim, and if a third pool exists to test it. Without a third pool, v3-A is a *theoretical proposal*, not a specification.
- **v3-B** exists if a third pool with an institutional threshold exists and is unanalyzed. It generalizes a negative result.
- **v3-C** exists if a second pool with a physical threshold and positive gap exists and is unanalyzed. It generalizes a positive result conditionally.

**No valid v3 exists** for the claim "the two papers generalize" as stated, because:

- E3's verdict does not replicate robustly even on the second pool (D1–D5).
- E4's constructive leg is structurally undefined on the second pool (no physical threshold).
- The second pool is no longer naive, so it cannot carry confirmatory weight for any v3 written now.

The Uvalde record's own recommendation—*"do NOT promote a v3 confirmatory generalization on this evidence"*—is correct, and the deeper reason is the one it does not quite state: **the exploratory pool is spent, and no third pool is named.** A v3 without a third pool is a proposal, not a specification.

---

## 7. What to do next

1. **Decide which v3 you are actually pursuing** (A, B, or C). They require different pools and different pre-registrations.
2. **Identify a third pool** with the required structural feature, or accept that the generalization is theoretical.
3. **If no third pool exists**, write the v3 as a *moderator specification* (v3-A) and treat Uvalde as the motivating case, not the test.
4. **Do not write a v3 whose confirmatory test is Uvalde.** That would be a category error: it would look confirmatory while being post-hoc.

Until one of these is in place, the correct state of the program is what the record already says: **exploratory.**
