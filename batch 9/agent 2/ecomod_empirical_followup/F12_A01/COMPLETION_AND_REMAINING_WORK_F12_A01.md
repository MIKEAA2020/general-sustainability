## Quantitative remaining requirements

- **Brazil formations net sign:** a symmetric endpoint *absolute-area* error budget must be below 5.4314 million ha per endpoint to guarantee the mapped negative difference under that assumption (0.6385% of its reported footprint per endpoint). No such measured error budget has been supplied.
- **Indonesia combined forest net sign:** the corresponding threshold is 351,281 ha per endpoint (0.1858% of its reported footprint). The budget is not calibrated by the earlier pooled collection accuracy.
- **Indonesia forest-to-oil-palm endpoint cohort:** the mapped lower bound is about 111,612 ha. A *class-set symmetric-difference* error budget for the initial forest and final oil-palm classes must sum to less than that amount to retain a positive reference-defined lower bound. Equal allocations would be about 55,806 ha/endpoint (0.0295% of footprint). Small net area error does not imply small class-set error.
- **Brazil references:** the acquired collection-2.1 archive has 85,152 points but only 4,053 nonblank 2015/2023 pairs; 4,041 remain after non-observed/training filtering and 3,042 are also nonedge at both endpoints. Current C11 documentation uses reference collection 3 through 2024. The sample counts, reference vintage, filtering/weights and geographic pattern prevent treating the acquired pairs as a compatible national change-calibration sample.
- **Indonesia references:** the current C4.1 ATBD describes independent reference years 2000–2022; its summary provides no C4.1 global accuracy estimate. The current public accuracy page describes Collection 2.0. Compatible 2023 reference/design information or an independently supported endpoint error bound is still needed. This does not prove no newer evidence exists.
- **Finnish cycle volume:** positive approximate sampling inference survives unknown cross-cycle dependence. The additional unmeasured differential-bias budget would have to be below about 17.3 million m³ to preserve its positive lower bound. With only the second-moment sensitivity, the sign is not resolved. Exact primary endpoint AGB remains a distinct target.

## What has not been done — do not call these completed

1. No original national 2015–2023 ESA CCI AGB stock aggregation or calibrated change has been completed for any of the eight candidates. The provider extent tables do not substitute for native biomass, forest support and missing/converted-cell treatment.
2. Exact national-account component territories have not been certified equal to every provider physical footprint. The Finnish inventory's inclusion of Åland is established; that is not proof of every account component's convention. Canadian north-of-domain forest content remains unquantified.
3. No conversion association or causal effect has been estimated. The new LPs concern endpoint-cohort feasibility conditional on classifications, not independence of land-use causes or ecological condition.
4. No reporting false-negative rate or policy-accuracy improvement against independently calibrated ecological outcomes has been estimated. A nominal discordance in one of two exploratory map cases is not such an evaluation.
5. COD, CMR, CAN, FRA and AUS have no completed physical national analysis in this increment. Their account changes were computed. Their physical work is not marked permanently unavailable or irreparable.
