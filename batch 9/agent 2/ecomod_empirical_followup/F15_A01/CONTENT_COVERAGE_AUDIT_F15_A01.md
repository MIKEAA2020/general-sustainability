# F15 content-coverage audit

## Scope and rule

This audit covers the scientific content inherited from F12–F14. F15 is an additive editorial restoration, not a new empirical analysis. The main manuscript remains self-contained with six figures, two tables and five Results subsections. Specialist verification belongs in the SI; exhaustive machine-readable outputs remain in the workbook and immutable numerical deposits. No scientific result is treated as absent merely because every source cell is not typeset.

## Deliberate specialist compression

- Detailed local reference-record counts, coordinate/weight discrepancies, native zero/SD counts and probe-specific metadata remain in the SI rather than fragmenting the main narrative. They are not omitted.
- The standalone Finnish uncertainty figure is not duplicated in the SI because both constructions are visible in main Figure 6a. The original F12 figure remains unchanged.
- The account-availability graphic remains in the SI; its eligibility result and interpretation remain in the main. This is a placement decision, not loss of evidence.
- The SI does not reproduce a separate future-work essay or a second Results/Discussion. The main contains the scientific limitations and completion distinction; unfinished empirical tasks are tracked separately.
- Not every one of the 183 identifiers or every workbook cell is printed. Complete values, all LP witnesses and all inherited workbook sheets remain machine-readable. This is exhaustive-data preservation, not silent selective reporting.
- Provider raw workbooks, PDF bodies, rasters, archives and native density arrays are not redistributed. Source URLs, hashes, factual extractions, derived outputs and processing code are retained; this is a rights/storage decision, not scientific deletion.

Those are empirical tasks, not missing redistribution steps. `ACCESS_AND_EVIDENCE_WORKAROUNDS_F15_A01.md` records which newly identified sources can help and which gaps they do not close.
