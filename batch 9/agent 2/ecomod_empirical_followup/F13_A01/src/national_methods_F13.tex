\subsection{National account trajectories and mapped-cover series}\label{sec:nationalmethods}
For every eligible identifier we calculate actual endpoint account changes, the contributions of all five components to the initial total, and sensitivities excluding fishing, built-up land, or both. Per-person values remain separate quantities. The inherited secondary interval, 2010--2020, is evaluated for 182 members of the primary cohort with admissible endpoints. For forest biocapacity expressed as area times an accounting factor, the symmetric product decomposition distinguishes the area contribution from the factor contribution; the latter is not interpreted as measured forest condition.

Complete official provider statistics supply national mapped-cover series rather than extrapolations from local windows. Brazil uses MapBiomas Collection 11, with 27 states and six biomes; Indonesia uses Collection 4.1, with 38 provinces and seven island groups; the Democratic Republic of the Congo uses Collection 1, with annual country statistics for 2000--2025 \citep{MapBiomas2026,MapBiomasIDN2026,MapBiomasCOD2026}. Source-specific groups are fixed explicitly: Brazilian formations comprise classes 3, 5, 6 and 49; the separate formations-plus-savanna group additionally includes 4 and 7. Indonesian forest comprises 3, 5 and 76. Congolese source forest comprises dense forest (3), woodland (4) and mangrove (5), with plantations (9) separate. The same numeric code need not have the same ecological meaning across initiatives. These are operational mapped groups, not a harmonized international forest definition.

Leaf classes are summed once. Extent and consecutive annual transition margins are reconciled at the finest published geography for Brazil and Indonesia. Congo's country series is used directly: province and biome exports have different summed footprints and are not presumed to partition its country support. Constant exported label area does not certify direct observations everywhere, absence of imputation, or equivalence to the account's territorial domain. The Indonesian Collection 3 comparison is a collection-and-support sensitivity because the reported footprints differ, not a clean collection effect.

The national analyses are exploratory. Source acquisition continued after account directions, earlier empirical results and public trend headlines were known. All eight candidates are retained, including concordant, discordant and unresolved results; neither the country set nor reference cases constitute an outcome-naive probability sample.

\subsection{Endpoint cohorts constrained by annual transitions}\label{sec:flowmethods}
Annual mapped outflows count events and can count the same hectare repeatedly. To bound unique endpoint cohorts, let $T_{tij}$ be the observed area moving from class $i$ to $j$ between consecutive years. A marked flow $x_{tij}$ follows land mapped as the initial forest group in 2015. Impose $0\le x_{tij}\le T_{tij}$, set the first-year marked flow to $T_{tij}$ for forest-origin edges and zero otherwise, and require marked-flow conservation at each interior class--year node. Minimizing and maximizing the final marked inflow into a specified 2023 destination yields its feasible endpoint-cohort range.

For exactly balanced continuous-area capacities these bounds are sharp: any compatible land history induces a feasible marked flow, and feasible marked and complementary unmarked flows admit path decompositions on the acyclic network. No actual pixel history is reconstructed. We solve both national aggregate networks and networks restricted to the 49 reported Brazilian state--biome units or 38 Indonesian provinces. Restricting geographical rearrangement cannot weaken the raw bounds. Seven group--destination cases, two extrema, and national plus fine-unit networks require 634 linear programmes. Saved primal and dual witnesses are checked independently from the capacities; outward allowances of 0.02 ha per independently solved unit cover recorded numerical and source-conservation residuals. These allowances are not classification-error intervals. The cohort may pass through intermediate classes, so forest in 2015 and oil palm in 2023 does not necessarily mean a direct forest-to-oil-palm clearing event.

If each endpoint net-area estimate has absolute error at most $e$, its difference has error at most $2e$. Thus $|\Delta\widehat A|/2$ is a break-even endpoint error requirement, not an estimated confidence limit. For a mapped endpoint intersection lower bound $L$, the relevant error instead comprises class-set symmetric differences: if the initial forest and final destination sets have combined symmetric-difference area at most $E$, the reference-defined intersection is at least $\max(0,L-E)$. Small net-area error does not imply small class-set error.

\subsection{Inventory-cycle quantities, sampling uncertainty and bias}\label{sec:inventorymethods}
Official inventories provide a complementary route that does not require known cross-endpoint covariance or an exact paired census. Finnish NFI12 (2014--2018) and NFI13 (2019--2023) report living-tree volume and biomass components on productive and poorly productive forest including \AA land; original supplementary tables supply volume standard errors \citep{Korhonen2021,Korhonen2024}. French results report living \emph{bois fort tige} on production forest in metropolitan France including Corsica, with bark and a minimum tree diameter of 7.5 cm. We retain four French campaign windows, 2013--2017, 2018--2022, 2019--2023 and 2021--2025 \citep{IGN2018,IGN2026}. They are pooled inventory windows, not exact 2015 or 2023 observations, even where their middle years match those dates. Stem volume is not whole above-ground biomass.

For two approximately normal marginal inventory estimators with standard errors $s_0,s_1$, endpoint Bonferroni intervals give the sampling-error difference band
\begin{equation}
\Delta\widehat V\ \pm\ z_{0.9875}(s_0+s_1),\qquad z_{0.9875}=2.241403,\label{eq:invbonf}
\end{equation}
with at least approximately 95\% coverage, without cross-inventory independence. Finnish source rounding is included outward. A separate second-moment sensitivity uses $\sqrt{20}(s_0+s_1)$, conditional on unbiased errors and reported standard errors representing true error standard deviations; it does not assert exact finite-sample coverage for estimated variances.

For French published 95\% endpoint halfwidths $h_0,h_1$, subtraction gives $\Delta\widehat V\pm(h_0+h_1)$ with at least 90\%, not 95\%, coverage under the reported marginal coverage statements. We add a full published unit for each rounded estimate and halfwidth. Rescaling halfwidths by $z_{0.9875}/z_{0.975}$ supplies a separate approximate 95\% band under a marginal-normal interpretation. A four-endpoint Bonferroni sensitivity uses $z_{0.99375}/z_{0.975}$ to cover all three retained contrasts against the latest cycle simultaneously. Sampling intervals do not bound differential protocol, cubage, projection or other systematic bias. A differential-bias budget $B$ widens either side by $B$; no independently measured French bound on $B$ is supplied. The 2018 protocol documents unusable-wood deduction and survey changes, so exact retrospective method harmonization is not certified merely by matching volume labels.

Canadian NFI tables report above-ground biomass stock and annualized change from combined remeasured 2007--2017 and projected 2006--2017 observations, with filtering of strong artificial-change indicators \citep{CanadaNFI2025}. They are neither a pure paired field census nor the primary interval. We preserve the directly reported change interval rather than reconstructing change precision from stock intervals. The confidence level and implementation of the current change export are not independently established here; its separate relative-standard-error table does not reconcile with the interval under the usual definition. Consequently no new standard error or calibrated sign is inferred from that table. The inventory omits 262.190 million ha of stated northern regions; omitted biomass is not assumed zero. Supplementary Information gives source cells, units, coverage and consistency diagnostics.
