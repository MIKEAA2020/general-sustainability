## Quantities and claims deliberately not substituted

- Cover extent is not biomass, volume is not AGB, a GHG flow is not a stock, and a pooled campaign midpoint is not an exact endpoint.
- Annual hectare-events are not unique endpoint conversion hectares.
- Conditional mapped conversion bounds are not calibrated ecological conversion, biomass loss, association or causality.
- The Canadian small labelled RSE is not selected over its incompatible reported interval. Its current change confidence level is not invented.
- French method/cubage/unusable-wood differences are not silently assumed absent. Sampling intervals retain an explicit unbounded differential-bias caveat.
- Exact paired field censuses and known covariance are not asserted as universal prerequisites; compatible inventory designs, marginal intervals, direct change estimates and defensible bounds are valid alternatives.
