## Interpretation and remaining work

The candidate territorial database and local support probes are frozen, but the analytical forest sample is not finalized. All eight candidates remain on hold; none is permanently excluded. Independent endpoint forest observations, definitive territory correspondence, full-support coverage/missingness treatment and independent change-validation requirements remain. Local endpoint zeros are not forest classification, and successful access is not national coverage or ecological accuracy.

Raw pixel arrays were necessarily read for support counts. Their zero/missing distributions and single-year 2015 account forest-area/population fields were inspected. No claim of pristine blinding or untouched outcome information is made. The original F03 and F01 protocols remain archived; the known stale hash in the historical F01 provenance is preserved with the inherited explanatory record.
