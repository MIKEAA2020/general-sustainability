Do not extract arbitrary paths or execute archive contents. Source PDFs/archives will not be redistributed.

This is a descriptive coverage check, not a local area-weighted sample or a representative validation design. If no points occur, report zero without enlarging or moving the footprint. Do not inspect or compute local class confusion, mapped change agreement, transition accuracy, biomass changes, or national prevalence in this audit. Header/schema and code completeness are not ground-truth validation. Do not choose whichever archive gives favourable agreement.

Assess exact payload equivalence across the two archives where IDs/coordinates permit; attribute any comparison only to verified common fields/records. If training exclusion, inclusion probabilities, interpretation dates, class crosswalks or collection identities cannot be certified from these artifacts and the already reviewed documentation, retain them unresolved. A field name suggesting a weight is not proof of its estimand or correct design use. Distinct analyst agreement is not independence from mapping training; identical public datasets are not independent reference replications.
