### 2.4 What additional allocation information would help — newly derived and executed

A sensitivity family imposes a reciprocal density-contrast bound `R` between the mapped group and its complement in each mixed cell at both endpoints. Sharp cell bounds follow from the mixture equation. The homogeneous endpoint `R=1` is an **assumption**, not an empirical stock estimate.

The lower group-change bound reaches zero at:

- **R = 1.4555078889**, formations;
- **R = 1.9495126065**, formations plus savanna.

Strict positive-sign identification within this particular family requires a common bound below the relevant value. No such bound has been independently established. The unrestricted model remains primary. This analysis exposes exactly what an auxiliary restriction would have to supply; it does not fill missing references or covariance by fiat. The two finite-R families are evaluated separately, not asserted as a jointly calibrated ecological model.
