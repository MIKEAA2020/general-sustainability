# C16: external revision record

The abstract is reproduced verbatim from the 38-page C14 version. It begins with the definition of biocapacity as an aggregate annual account combining land area and productivity, then asks what information the total retains. It does not cast constituent yield differences as a contradiction of that definition.

The orchard and elevator analogies, research questions, reporting-cut illustration, concluding orchard synthesis, author acknowledgments and original author AI declaration remain unchanged. The corrected total-area bound and reference-refinement values remain unchanged.
