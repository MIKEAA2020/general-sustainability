# C17: external revision record

This revision implements the six prose decisions authorized by the author after the C14–C16 comparison. It is a targeted integration, not a rollback to C14 or a new model experiment.

## Changes

1. **Introduction opening:** restore C14’s declarative sentence about an aggregate production-based measure increasing despite a constituent land class losing area.
2. **NFA explanation:** restore the sentence explaining that distinguishing mechanisms requires constituent areas and per-hectare contributions, replacing the repeated rhetorical question.
3. **Literature transition:** restore C14’s sentence motivating analysis of the information retained by aggregate biocapacity.
4. **Provision/assets bridge:** insert only C14’s first sentence distinguishing annual biological provision from the ecological assets supporting it. Retain both analogies in full rather than substituting C14’s shorter paragraph for them.
5. **Concluding synthesis:** integrate C14’s complementary-information point with the reporting recommendation: “Component accounts and independent physical observations provide complementary information and should be reported alongside the aggregate.” Keep the subsequent orchard callback verbatim, including its final sentence.
6. **Terminology:** replace “productivity sentinel” with “productivity indicator” consistently in the article and SI. For the area/yield series jointly, use “inputs to an illustrative proxy” rather than the awkward “indicator proxy.” Preserve the cereals-only, account-boundary and non-stock qualifications.

## Preservation and deliverables

The abstract remains verbatim C14. The analogies, explicit research questions, acknowledgments, original author AI declaration, scientific equations, identification illustration, numerical values, scientific corrections and policy-control distinctions remain unchanged from C16. Numerical assets, figures and the simulation implementation are unchanged. Follow-up F03 is unchanged.

The main and SI have unique C17 basenames: `ecomod_C17_main` and `ecomod_C17_SI`, for both PDF and LaTeX. The repository-availability link and build/verification filename references point to C17. Earlier revisions are neither renamed nor overwritten. Exact source differences are included in `metadata/source_comparisons/`.

Compilation and source-integrity checks are reported separately in the package metadata. This revision makes no new claim of empirical validation or new physical-stock results. Revision history remains outside the journal article.
