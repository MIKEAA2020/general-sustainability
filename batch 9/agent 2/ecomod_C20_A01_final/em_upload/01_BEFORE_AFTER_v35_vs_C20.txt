## 2. Substantive additions since the reviewed draft

1. **Sharp static identification bounds.** Under independent observation of area or of per-hectare contribution, the compatible allocation set reduces to computable intervals, proved sharp, with feasible endpoint witnesses. The readout `Υ(q) = 0.05 + 0.064q(1−q)` has image exactly [0.05, 0.066] gha ha⁻¹ and is non-monotone, so `q = 0.1` and `q = 0.9` give the same per-hectare contribution: quality is not identifiable from yield alone.
2. **Integrated contribution budgets.** Conversion, maintenance/retirement, restoration, quality and fast-yield terms are integrated separately; closure error falls monotonically with timestep refinement in all four reported scenarios.
3. **NFA Edition 2026 empirical analysis.** Endpoints, per-capita series, E_prod/B crossover (1973), component attribution, Divisia sensitivity to the base-year share, level fallback and a cropland-area/cereal-yield proxy with an explicit empirical residual.
4. **Operational falsification map.** Five conditional predictions with independent data requirements and pre-specified rejection outcomes, plus sampling, uncertainty and prospective-rejection rules.
5. **Controlled technology-wave experiment.** Matched on/off runs at two initial populations with step-halving refinement and a conservation check.

## 3. Corrections that superseded earlier claims or artefacts

1. **Solver corrections.** Pre-run history now initialised across the maximum active delay; the time-zero delayed-capacity sample computed rather than inserted; the technology increment propagated consistently into current `B`, the returned series and the delayed history; state arrays forced to floating point.
2. **Withdrawn artefact.** The technology-wave recovery outcome that drove capital area to about 2.44 ha, reserve below its 0.10 ha floor and `B` back to about 0.15 gha was a bookkeeping error. Corrected values: `max A_c = 1.731 ha`, `min A_r = 0.321 ha`, final `B = 0.899 gha`, conservation residual 4.44e-16 ha. The superseded values are not reported as model results.
3. **Units.** The earlier `gha·yr⁻¹` convention was withdrawn in favour of annual account amounts in gha (see point b of the response).
4. **Claim scope.** "No fold / no critical slowing" is now restricted to the reduced fixed-demand relation; floor-proximity thresholds are labelled proximity measures (2.4101 / 2.7005 / 2.9779 / 3.1407 cap at `A_c0` = 0.40 / 0.70 / 1.00 / 1.18 ha); the 20/40-year equality is an imposed-cap consistency test.
5. **The "1961–2022 consistent with" sentence was removed** and replaced by the measured decomposition with an explicit account-versus-stock boundary.

## 4. What is not claimed

The manuscript does not claim a measured decline in physical ecological capital, does not identify its parameters from field data, does not validate the coupled dynamics against observations, and does not forecast. NFA account values are treated throughout as annual accounting quantities, not as physical-stock measurements.
