## What is deposited, and what is not

**Deposited:** the two-land and one-stock model implementations; the identification, contribution-integral and timestep-refinement calculations; the NFA Edition 2026 and OWID input snapshots with hashes and attribution; the 1961–2023 annual joined series; endpoint, component, Divisia and proxy transformations; the drawdown, gate-sign and comparator experiment outputs; figure-generating code and the resulting vector figure files.

**Not deposited:** no primary field observations were collected for this study, so none exist to deposit. The manuscript's empirical content is a decomposition of publicly available National Footprint Accounts and OWID/FAOSTAT series, plus conditional model simulations whose parameters are representative rather than fitted.
