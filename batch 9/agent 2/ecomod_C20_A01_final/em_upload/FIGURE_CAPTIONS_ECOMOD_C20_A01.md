## Main text

### Figure 1 — `Figure_1.pdf`

*Baseline model results: the composition mask at fixed demand, the balance relation with its minimum-area boundary, and drawdown depth across initial demand.*

```latex
Baseline model results (Table~\ref{tab:params}). Account amounts $B$ and $E$ are in gha, land areas are in ha, and population is in cap. (a) Fixed demand ($E=0.825$\,gha), restoration off, and initial conditions $A_{f0}=0.6$\,ha, $A_{c0}=1.5$\,ha, and $q_0=0.9$. $B$ rises from $0.594$ to $0.825$\,gha as $A_c$ falls from $1.500$ to $0.808$\,ha and $A_f$ rises from $0.600$ to $0.923$\,ha. Shading marks the interval with $\dot B>0$ and $\dot A_c<0$ (about $149$\,yr; only the first $60$\,yr are shown). Within $25$\,yr, $98\%$ of the rise in $B$ and $85\%$ of the fall in $A_c$ occur. (b) Fixed-demand balance relation $B_{eq}(A_c)$; the listed balance areas are $A_c^*(E)=1.110$, $0.626$, $0.385$, $0.143$, and $0.070$\,ha at $E=0.70$, $0.90$, $1.00$, $1.10$, and $1.13$\,gha. The curve meets the minimum $A_c=0.05$\,ha at $\Eceil\approx1.138$\,gha; no balance point is admissible at higher demand. (c) Endogenous-demand runs with $\tau_g=20$\,yr, $\tau_p=25$\,yr, and $\chi_r=0.10$\,gha$^{-1}$\,yr$^{-1}$. In the tested runs, the sampled minimum area varies monotonically with $P_0$ for each initial area $A_{c0}$ ($E_0=eP_0$); the finite grid does not establish a general continuity theorem. The floor is approached asymptotically. Triangles mark the proximity values in Table~\ref{tab:model}, defined as within $10^{-6}$\,ha of the floor; no discontinuity is observed.
```

```latex
Aggregate and per-capita movements in NFA Edition 2026 (world, 1961--2023; complete observations). $B$ is total biocapacity, $E_{\mathrm{prod}}$ is the workbook's Global Ecological Footprint of Production, and $P$ is the single UN PopStat population series; per-capita values are calculated from totals. ### Figure S1 — `Figure_S1.pdf`
```

*Full delayed one-stock comparator trajectory with a logistic technology increment.*

```latex
Full delayed one-stock comparator with a logistic technology increment. Parameters are $A_0=1.0$ stock unit, $P_0=0.45$\,cap, $b_0=0.5$, $b_G=0.6$, $\rho=0.08$\,yr$^{-1}$, $A_{\max}=1.2$ stock units, $e=0.55$\,gha\,cap$^{-1}$, $r=0.02$\,yr$^{-1}$, $\eta=0.03$\,yr$^{-1}$, $\alpha=0.2$\,gha$^{-1}$\,yr$^{-1}$, $\tau_g=15$\,yr, $\tau_p=25$\,yr, $A_{\mathrm{ext}}=0.02$ stock units, $K_{\min}=10^{-4}$\,cap, $\Delta b=0.5$\,gha per stock unit, $\kappa=0.04$\,yr$^{-1}$, and $t_w=120$\,yr; $\sigma=1$, $\omega_{Tb}=1$, and $D_0=0$. This is a conditional model illustration, not an observation or a two-land result.
```
