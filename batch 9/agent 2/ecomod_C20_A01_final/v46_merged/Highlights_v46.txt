Manuscript: Rising biocapacity, falling ecological capital: how aggregation conceals composition
Author: Amin Abaee (ORCID 0000-0002-0019-1842)
Manuscript number: ECOMOD-26-1191
Version: ECOMOD v46 (merged revision of C20 A01), 13 November 2026

- Aggregate biocapacity can rise while ecological-capital land is drawn down.
- The aggregate alone cannot identify which land class is growing or shrinking.
- Fixed demand drives capital land to a floor: no fold, no critical slowing.
- Contribution budgets separate conversion, retirement, restoration and quality.
- NFA 1961-2023: biocapacity +24.86% while biocapacity per person falls 52.71%.
