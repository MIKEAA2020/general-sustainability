**Subject:** Request for the numerical workbook behind Forests of Australia, and clarification of the observation date

Dear colleagues,

I am an independent researcher comparing national forest area and aboveground biomass evidence against
biological-supply accounts, with Australia one of eight national cases. The Forests of Australia dataset
is the most promising Australian source for my purpose; the geospatial layer, metadata, lineage and
lookup tables are retrievable, but I have not been able to complete the numerical module.

**Access record, stated plainly.** A direct request to the source page timed out (90 seconds) in my
environment, and a separate request for the official-source workbook did not complete. Neither is a
finding that the file is unavailable; it is an access limitation on my side, and I am asking for the
file or a working direct link.

**What I am requesting.**

1. The **numerical workbook behind the published release** — area and biomass/volume tables by forest
   type, tenure and jurisdiction, at the release years — rather than the map layer alone.
2. **Observation dates** for each table: the observation year (or window) that each figure actually
   reflects, as distinct from the **publication year in the release title**. A release labelled 2023
   need not represent a 2023 observation, and for a boundary-matched comparison the difference
   determines whether the Australian case is usable at all.
3. **External-territory coverage**: which external territories and offshore jurisdictions are inside the
   national totals, and which are excluded.
4. **Consistency of the forest definition and minimum mapping unit** across the release years used, so
   that a 2015-to-latest contrast is like-for-like.
5. If biomass is not a direct stock measurement in the release, a **statement of what the biomass or
   carbon figures are derived from** (carbon model versus direct stock), so that it is not mistaken for
   an independent physical observation.

**Why the date question matters.** My comparison pairs a physical observation with an account value at
the same endpoint. Reported publication labels cannot be allowed to stand in for observation dates, and
I will not resolve external-territory coverage by assumption; I would rather record the Australian case
with its limitations explicit.

I will cite DCCEEW and the dataset as specified under CC BY 4.0, and will publish only derived results.
