# R4 — MapBiomas Indonesia: Collection 4.1 2023 reference labels and design information

**Route:** email to the MapBiomas Indonesia team (landy.mapbiomas.id contact).
**Unblocks:** F13 item 5 (current map reference calibration).

---

**Subject:** Request for C4.1 2023 reference labels, design weights and class-transition confusion information

Dear MapBiomas Indonesia team,

I am an independent researcher comparing national land-change mappings against biological-supply
accounts, with Indonesia as one of eight national cases. I have the Collection 4.1 ATBD and method
documentation, and the public statistics pages. The reference/design side is what I am missing.

**What I understand from the acquired documentation.** C4.1 describes independent annual references
through 2022. The public accuracy-assessment page concerns an older collection, so it does not supply
the reference error for the current collection.

**What I am requesting** for **Collection 4.1**, at the **2015 and 2023** endpoints:

1. **Reference labels** at both endpoints with point identifiers that permit pairing.
2. **Design weights / inclusion probabilities**, sample design and stratification, and the estimator used
   for area and change.
3. **Training versus validation role** for each reference point, with support metadata, so
   training-overlap can be handled explicitly.
4. **Class and class-transition confusion information** — specifically **initial-forest to
   final-destination** class sets, not only net-area error.
5. **Support and definition metadata**: the forest definition used, minimum mapping unit, how
   plantations, mangrove, shrub and oil-palm classes are treated, and any boundary/coverage rules for
   provinces or transmigration areas.

**Why.** My use requires a class-set symmetric-difference budget to convert mapped change into a
bounded physical-change statement at a matched boundary. Net-area error cannot be converted into that
budget. A design-based marginal endpoint bound, or an all-population/class-set upper bound, is fully
sufficient; I am not asking for an exact paired census or a known covariance. If the 2023 reference is
not yet finalized, a statement of its status and expected availability would also be useful, because I
would rather record the Indonesian case as pending than substitute a different collection's error.

I will cite MapBiomas Indonesia as specified, will not redistribute reference records, and will publish
only derived aggregates and bounds under your stated terms.
