**Subject:** Request for the underlying CEO database behind the national forest cover report (academic use)

Dear colleagues,

I am an independent researcher comparing national forest-change evidence against biological-supply
accounts, with Cameroon one of eight national cases. Your published national report is the only
Cameroonian source I have found with an explicit point-based design description, and it is exactly the
kind of evidence my comparison requires. What I am missing is the numerical layer behind it.

**Status of what I have.** The report itself is available as a PDF through an indexed route; the country
page does not expose a separate workbook download, and an automated request returned an HTML anti-bot
challenge rather than the document — which I record as an access limitation, not as evidence the data is
withheld.

**What I am requesting.** The **workbook or CEO export** behind the report — or the specific sheets
listed below, if the full file cannot be shared:

- `DA_CEO database_Etape 1` — the 29,409 collected points;
- `DA_plot Count_Etape 2` and `DA_Terres_Etape 3` — annual point counts and area matrices;
- `DA_Erreur Aléatoire` and `DA_Terres_Etape3 Incertitudes` — activity-data uncertainty;
- `FE_Terres` and `FE_Terres Incertitudes` — biomass/emission factors and their uncertainty;
- `Résultats_MC` and `Résumé_MC` — the Monte Carlo outputs.

**Accompanying questions, if convenient.** (i) Which biomass/emission factors are nationally derived
versus taken from default or foreign sources, and how are the latter typed in the workbook? (ii) Do the
Monte Carlo results treat factor and activity-data uncertainty jointly, and with what assumed
dependence? (iii) What are the observation years and the forest definition behind the annual point
counts? These affect how I can describe the Cameroonian case, and I would rather record a limitation
precisely than infer one.

**Use and terms.** Non-commercial academic research, published in a peer-reviewed journal with Cameroon's
institutions credited. Point records will not be redistributed; only derived aggregates and bounds will
be published, under whatever conditions you specify. If the appropriate route is an official data
request rather than this email, I would be grateful to be directed to it.
