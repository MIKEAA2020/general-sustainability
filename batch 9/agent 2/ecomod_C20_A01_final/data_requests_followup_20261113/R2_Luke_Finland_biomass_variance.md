**Subject:** Request for design-based variance information for NFI biomass estimates (academic use)

Dear Luke Natural Resources Institute Finland,

I am an independent researcher carrying out a boundary-matched comparison of national biological-supply
accounts against independently measured forest biomass, using Finland as one of eight national cases.
The Finnish national inventory series has been the most usable of the eight on level terms, and I am
writing to ask for the one input I have not been able to obtain from the published tables.

**What we already have.** Biomass by component, species and region from the NFI12 and NFI13 supplementary
workbooks (Silva Fennica article 10662 supplement S2, sheet 27; article 24045 supplement S2, Table 60),
together with the volume tables and their standard errors, and official national area figures including
Åland.

**What is missing.** Those biomass tables carry **no biomass standard-error or confidence-interval
columns**, and the volume standard errors cannot be transferred to biomass — the two quantities have
different estimators and different variance structures. Without a defensible precision for the biomass
totals, the Finnish comparison can only be reported as a point contrast, which is weaker than what the
other cases in the study now support.

**Request.** Any of the following would resolve it, in descending order of usefulness:

1. **Design-based standard errors, confidence intervals, or coefficients of variation for the published
   biomass totals** (total and by component), for the cycles/years in the NFI12 and NFI13 tables.
2. **The underlying design information** needed to compute them ourselves: plot selection design, panel
   and cluster structure, inclusion probabilities or expansion factors, and the estimator used for
   biomass totals — together with any microdata or design outputs you are able to release.
3. If neither is releasable, a **statement of what the published biomass totals' precision is
   understood to be**, and confirmation that no variance is currently published, so that we can
   register the limitation explicitly rather than imply a precision we do not have.

**Two definitional points** that would let us match boundaries and periods honestly:

- The **measurement years and target definitions** for each cycle behind the biomass tables — we compare
  same-period contrasts and do not want to treat a cycle label as a calendar year.
- The **Åland treatment**: whether Åland is inside or outside the national totals used in the biomass
  tables, and how its inclusion changes the total, since our comparison unit must match the account's
  territorial coverage.

We will cite Luke and the NFI as specified, will not redistribute any microdata, and will report the
precision basis we actually used. A reply stating that only items 1–2 are unavailable is just as useful
as the data itself.

With thanks for the published tables, which are unusually transparent.
