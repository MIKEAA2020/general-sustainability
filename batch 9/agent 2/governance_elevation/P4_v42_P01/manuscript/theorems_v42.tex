\subsubsection{7.2 Cadence-independent protective stability}

\textbf{Theorem 7.2 (Input--output margin for protective review).}
\emph{Let $a=-A_N>0$, $d>0$, $c=-C_E>0$, $k=-C_Z>0$, and assume the even-pairs identity. Define}
\[
G(s)=\frac{B_Es}{(s+a)(s+d)},\qquad
\gamma=\frac{2B_E}{d}\left(\frac{a}{d}\right)^{a/(d-a)},\qquad \mu=\gamma\frac{k}{c},
\]
\emph{with $\gamma=2B_E/(ae)$ when $a=d$. If $\mu<1$, the protective equilibrium is locally exponentially stable for every fixed finite deployment delay, for every fixed positive review interval under the native signal hold, and for every positive interval under the exact frozen-effort review. The native conclusion also allows any fixed finite additional measurement latency. For the $\theta$-review of the linear effort equation, every interval is stable whenever $1/2<\theta\le1$ and $\mu<2\theta-1$.}

\emph{Proof.} The ecological transfer from effort perturbation to memory is $G$. Its impulse response is
\[
g(t)=\frac{B_E}{d-a}\left(de^{-dt}-ae^{-at}\right).
\]
It changes sign once, at $t_0=\log(d/a)/(d-a)$, has zero integral, and satisfies $\|g\|_1=\gamma$. The effort filter $-k/(s+c)$ has induced $L^\infty$ gain $k/c$. A pure delay and a sampling/hold operator each have unweighted $L^\infty$ gain one. Their feedback interconnection therefore has strict gain $\mu<1$. For any fixed hold interval $T_r$ and latency $\tau_p$, introduce an exponential weight $e^{\epsilon t}$: the hold/latency gain is at most $e^{\epsilon(T_r+\tau_p)}$, while the two stable filter gains depend continuously on $\epsilon$ near zero. A sufficiently small $\epsilon>0$ preserves strict small gain and proves exponential decay, with a rate that need not be uniform in $T_r$ or $\tau_p$.

For frozen effort, the sampled ecological impulse-response coefficients are integrals of $g$ over consecutive hold intervals, so their absolute sum is at most $\gamma$. The exact effort recursion has coefficients $\alpha=e^{-cT_r}$ and $\beta=-k(1-\alpha)/c$, hence discrete $\ell^\infty$ gain $|\beta|/(1-|\alpha|)=k/c$. Discrete small gain gives Schur stability. For the $\theta$ recursion,
\[
\alpha_\theta=\frac{1-(1-\theta)cT_r}{1+\theta cT_r},\qquad
\beta_\theta=\frac{-kT_r}{1+\theta cT_r},
\]
its gain is at most $k/[c(2\theta-1)]$. The stated condition again gives strict small gain. \ensuremath{\square}

At Candidate A, outward interval evaluation gives $\gamma<0.070632$ and $\mu<0.138027$. Thus $\theta\ge0.57$ is a certified sufficient range. Because $k/c=E^*/(Z_{\rm ref}+\delta)$ for the calibrated cap, the conclusion holds for \emph{every} $\eta_p>0$ at the same ecological equilibrium, not only $\eta_p=0.914$. More generally $\gamma\le2B_E/d=qN^*$. The sufficient bound $qKE^*/(Z_{\rm ref}+\delta)<1$ certifies a parameter family without frequency scanning. For example, $r\in[0.018,0.022]$, $K\in[90,110]$, $q\in[0.0009,0.0011]$, $E^*\in[1.8,2.4]$, $\delta\in[0.06,0.08]$, $Z_{\rm ref}=1$, arbitrary $\tau_m>0$, $\eta_p>0$ and $E_{\max}>E^*$ give $a>0$ and an upper bound below $0.274$. Exact/native review and $\theta\ge0.64$ review satisfy the sufficient margin throughout this box. This is a mathematical robustness box, not an empirical confidence region.

\subsubsection{7.3 A class of review schemes with eventual stability}

\textbf{Theorem 7.3 (Contractive review class).}
\emph{Consider the frozen-effort ecological flow $F(T_r)=e^{A_{\rm hold}T_r}$ and an update with third row $(0,\beta(T_r),\alpha(T_r))$. Suppose $\alpha,\beta$ are continuous, $\beta$ is bounded for large $T_r$, and $\alpha(T_r)\to\alpha_\infty$ with $|\alpha_\infty|<1$. Then the reviewed equilibrium is stable for all sufficiently large $T_r$. If the scheme is first-order consistent and its undelayed Jacobian is unstable, it is unstable for all sufficiently small positive $T_r$ and has at least one stability transition.}

\emph{Proof.} The stable ecological block and the cancellation give
\[
F(T_r)\longrightarrow u\mathbf e_3^\top,\quad
u=\begin{pmatrix}-A_E/A_N\\0\\1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Multiplying by the review factor yields a limiting third column $(-A_E/A_N,0,\alpha_\infty)^\top$ and no other nonzero columns; its eigenvalues are $0,0,\alpha_\infty$. Bounded $\beta$ controls the vanishing remainder, so spectral-radius continuity proves eventual stability. Consistency gives $M(T_r)=I+T_rJ_{\rm cont}+o(T_r)$; a positive-real-part eigenvalue of $J_{\rm cont}$ gives a multiplier outside the unit disc for small $T_r$. Continuity completes the argument. \ensuremath{\square}

The exact exponential update and every $\theta$-review with $\theta>1/2$ belong to this class; explicit Euler does not. For the native hold, $A_Z$ is Hurwitz and the limiting map is $-A_Z^{-1}C_Z\mathbf e_3\mathbf e_2^\top$. Its only potentially nonzero eigenvalue vanishes because $\mathbf e_2^\top A_Z^{-1}\mathbf e_3=0$. This independently proves eventual stability for the native operator. The conclusions concern these specified classes, not consistency alone and not an implementation-invariant crossing location.

\subsubsection{7.4 Nonlinear stock-floor protection}

\textbf{Theorem 7.4 (A cadence-independent viable stock region).}
\emph{Suppose the protective cap satisfies $0\le C(Z)\le\overline E<E_{\max}$ on $Z\ge0$, $q\overline E<r$, and the initial memory history is nonnegative. For $0\le E(0)\le\overline E$ and $N(0)\ge N_b:=K(1-q\overline E/r)$, the region $E\in[0,\overline E]$, $N\in[N_b,K]$, $Z\ge0$ is forward invariant, provided $N(0)\le K$. This holds for continuous protective feedback with any fixed deployment delay, for native held measurements at any cadence and latency, and for the exact nonlinear frozen-effort quota review. It is a nonlinear stock-floor guarantee, not just an equilibrium spectrum.}

\emph{Proof.} The memory equation preserves nonnegativity. At $E=0$ the effort derivative is nonnegative, and at $E=\overline E$ it is nonpositive for every admissible current or held cap. At $N=N_b$ the stock derivative is nonnegative because $E\le\overline E$; at $N=K$ it is nonpositive. These inward-pointing conditions prove invariance for continuous and native held feedback, including intervals between assessments.

At a frozen-effort review, integrate the scalar quota relaxation with the sampled cap $C$ fixed. Set
\[
y=\frac{E-C}{E_{\max}-E},\qquad
\widehat y=y\exp\{-\eta_p(1-C/E_{\max})T_r\},\qquad
E^+=\frac{C+E_{\max}\widehat y}{1+\widehat y}.
\]
This update stays between $E$ and $C$ and therefore preserves $[0,\overline E]$; the held stock flow preserves $[N_b,K]$. Its linearisation is precisely the exact review factor of Proposition 7.1. \ensuremath{\square}

For the hyperbolic quota, choose $\overline E=E_0=E_A^*(1+\delta)$ at $Z_{\rm ref}=1$. The calibrated values give $E_0\approx2.2345$ and $N_b\approx88.8277$. The theorem applies on the declared operating region for \emph{all} review intervals, rather than only at the annual scale. Across the mathematical parameter box above, with $E_{\max}\in[26,34]$, the same argument guarantees the conservative common floor $N\ge75.74$, with the corresponding cap and admissible initial effort. This does not identify a physical fishery floor, cover arbitrary initial effort near $E_{\max}$, guarantee a provision objective, or transfer to the mobilising/mixed laws. For a mobilising law, local spectral restabilisation alone still supplies no such invariant operating region.
