**How I will use the data (two sentences).**

> I use the ground-plot records as an independent physical observation of forest aboveground biomass, to test whether reported changes in national biological-capacity accounts — the National Footprint and Biocapacity Accounts, expressed in global hectares — are accompanied by measured biomass change at matched boundaries and periods; Canada is one of eight national cases in that comparison. I aggregate the tree records to **national and terrestrial ecozone level** and publish only derived aggregates, so I need (a) the **remeasured** stock change with its design-based uncertainty and (b) enough coverage information to state which parts of the national total are ground-sampled, photo-plot only, or projected.

Nothing plot-level, no coordinates, and no raw records would appear in any publication.

**NFI ground plots — please proceed now.** Rounded coordinates (±10 km), all jurisdictions, measurement years through the 2022 release, as in the form.

**MAGPlot — please hold the MAGPlot portion to v1.3.** Your description settles it: v1.2 has tree data only, without compiled volume, biomass, age or the ecological plot layers, so using it for this purpose would mean reimplementing biomass compilation across twelve contributing jurisdictions. v1.3, compiled with an augmented version of the same compiler the ground-plot program uses for those attributes, is both less work and more comparable to the ground plots — and comparability is the whole point of a boundary-matched comparison. I would rather wait and use one compiler consistently than mix two. When v1.3 is released, please re-open the MAGPlot part of the request; **v1.2 is not needed.**
