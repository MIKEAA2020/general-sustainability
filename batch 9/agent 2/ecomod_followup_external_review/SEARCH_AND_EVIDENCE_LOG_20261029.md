## 2. Selection logic

Sources were retained when they directly constrained a proposed contribution, supplied a close empirical comparator, clarified an accounting/measurement distinction, or changed data feasibility as of the assessment date. Central sources and official documentation were prioritized over generic citation accumulation. Related sources were followed purposefully; no formal citation-network census was performed.

No database-wide inclusion/exclusion counts, language restriction protocol, duplicate-resolution record, risk-of-bias instrument or PRISMA flow was implemented. No claim is made that Google Scholar, Web of Science, Scopus, preprint servers or regional/non-English literatures were exhaustively searched. Searches failing to produce an exact match do not support priority claims.

## 3. Evidence-depth conventions

- **Selected primary full text:** passages named in the matrix were read; this does not mean every method, appendix, figure, supplement or cited premise was audited.
- **Primary abstract/summary:** only claims in that material support the review. Full methods or numerical reproducibility cannot be inferred.
- **Official documentation/metadata:** supports provider-stated coverage, version and cautions; not independent validation of the dataset.
- **Search-only lead:** sufficient to identify a comparator to investigate, not to assert detailed methodological equivalence.
