# External prior-art review: empirical follow-up and enhancements
**29 October 2026 — whole scope confirmed by the user**

Start with [the review and recommendations](EXTERNAL_PRIOR_ART_REVIEW_20261029.md).

## Bottom line

The broad account–asset masking argument, spatial compensation, conversion pathways, trajectories and forest demography already have important precedents. Proceed with a narrower empirical question: **independently measured account–asset agreement/disagreement on matched boundaries, validated conversion evidence, and tested incremental diagnostic information.** Do not presume global forest-biomass decline or claim conceptual priority.

Particularly important precedents are Mancini's stock–flow agenda, Xu's natural/planted forest compensation, Maes's heterogeneous forest-condition trends, Karim's field biomass/biocapacity linkage, and established cropland/trajectory/accuracy methods. Latest official documentation supports a provisional **2015–2023** full-account comparison window; CCI v7 and HU-SoilCarbonGrids improve candidate data availability but do not remove validation requirements.

## Read the limits with the findings

This is purposeful screening, **not a systematic or exhaustive review**. Forty records do not mean forty full-text articles. Selected full text, primary abstracts, official documentation and search-only leads are explicitly distinguished. The first 33 exact queries were not retained and are recorded only as reconstructed themes; the last five are retained verbatim. The official SEEA PDF was exposed only through its first 30 pages. Some comparator methods/supplements remain required reading before submission.

No raw-data analysis, empirical calibration, manuscript/plan/figure edit, branch merge, novelty certificate or publication-ethics clearance is included. Submitted sources and existing reports remain unchanged. No copyrighted full texts, credentials or unrelated workspace files are redistributed.
