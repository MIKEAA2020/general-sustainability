## 3. Finland — Luke NFI and biomass-model teams

**Subject:** Existing Appendix A simulation outputs and same-domain NFI12/NFI13 AGB endpoint covariance

I retrieved Report 6/2026 and the corrected second edition, Report 12/2026. Appendix A is text-identical between editions. It updates mineral-soil litter-input uncertainty through 2023 with Repola et al. (2025) models and states that full sampling-covariance matrices, including below-ground components, were estimated for each VMI8–VMI13 cycle and sampling region on each Monte Carlo iteration. Steps 5–7 simulate biomass and produce annual regional/national time series. A2–A5 print examples only, and Jukuri has no supplementary matrix/simulation files.

Please provide, from this **existing** workflow where estimand-compatible:

1. The full current-model uncertainty inputs underlying A2–A4 for all species/models/components: fixed-effect parameter covariance, stand/tree random components and residual covariance, or the cited Repola et al. (2025) manuscript and machine-readable equivalents.
2. The full A5-type sampling-covariance matrices for every VMI cycle, sampling region, species/component and Monte Carlo iteration, including below-ground components; or, preferably if smaller, the resulting machine-readable step-7 living-biomass simulations with unrounded values, units, domain, component definitions, iteration count and seed/version metadata.
3. Same-domain national **NFI12 (2014–2018) and NFI13 (2019–2023) above-ground living-tree biomass endpoint estimates**, their design variances/SEs and the **cross-inventory covariance**, for South Finland, North Finland and the whole country where available. Please distinguish all Forest Land from Forest Land Remaining Forest Land and dry matter from carbon.
4. The design variance of NFI13−NFI12, including shared permanent-panel and temporary-panel contributions. Two independent endpoint SEs are not sufficient.
5. If Appendix A’s mineral-soil litter-input domain or interpolation is not the target AGB endpoint estimand, please provide the corresponding provider-computed target covariance or explain the exact domain/estimand transformation. Exact plot locations and Voronoi geometry are unnecessary if aggregate design contributions are supplied.
6. Please also clarify how these current-model calculations relate to the NID’s old-model annualized Table 6.4-13 and its printed-unit inconsistency; the two should not be mixed or counted as independent evidence.

**Minimum sufficient response:** same-domain NFI12/NFI13 national AGB endpoint variances and cross-inventory covariance, plus current Repola et al. (2025) prediction/model covariance or an existing provider-computed equivalent from the Appendix A pipeline.

Please provide:

1. The fixed-annual-panel Change Estimation Procedures v1.08 or its current superseding implementation, including permanent/temporary-panel and imputation covariance rules.

Please provide the correct change variance/covariance interpretation rather than an inferred export-bug explanation.

**Minimum sufficient response:** the fixed-panel change procedure/current implementation and version-matched full-precision `var_biom`/`sep_biom`/interval outputs with formulas and covariance rules sufficient to reproduce national biomass-change uncertainty without mixing static and dynamic versions.
