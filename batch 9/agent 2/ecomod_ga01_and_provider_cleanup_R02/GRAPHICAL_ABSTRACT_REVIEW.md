The NFA values are read from C19’s primary 2023 endpoint/component CSV fields. Estimated 2024–2025 rows are excluded. Model output and NFA account decomposition are presented as separate evidence streams; neither is used to calibrate the other in this graphic.

## Scope retained in GA01

- Conversion can add to the annual account at the margin while `A_c` declines; this does not identify a physical stock change.
- `B` alone cannot identify the allocation; independent observations are needed to sharpen compatible-area bounds.
- The minimum-area floor and no-fold/no-critical-slowing statement are restricted to the reduced fixed-demand branch.
- Recovery requires an explicit gated restoration flow; lower demand alone does not restore `A_c` in the stated run.
- The selected trajectory is a representative conditional model illustration, **not a forecast or fitted historical reconstruction**.

## Suggested caption

> **Composition can mask ecological-capital land drawdown.** Converting ecological-capital area to fast-provisioning area can raise the annual biocapacity account while ecological-capital area declines. In the selected fixed-demand delayed-quality illustration (`E = 0.825` gha, restoration off), B rises about 39% while `A_c` and its annual contribution fall 46.16% and 51.72%, respectively; this conditional run is not a forecast. Identification and reduced-branch recovery limits are stated separately from NFA Edition 2026 global account changes for 1961–2023, which are annual account values rather than physical-stock observations.
