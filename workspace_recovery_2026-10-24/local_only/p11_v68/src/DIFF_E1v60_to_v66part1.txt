+ \paragraph{Guarding against selection after the fact.} Why a protocol must be fixed beforehand, and why stating it as a commitment rather than a preference matters, is the data-snooping problem: when many candidate forecasters are scored on the same realisations, the best observed score is upward-biased as an estimate of skill, and the usual per-comparison tests do not restore validity.
