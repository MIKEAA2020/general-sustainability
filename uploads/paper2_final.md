## Abstract

Paper 2 proposed a cross-domain synthesis (fisheries, petroleum, institutions) with a conjunction formula. This paper was rejected for: conjunction formula broken, EROI threshold trivializes, Tainter model undefined, Argentina misclassified, hallucinated citations.

## 2. The Cod Case Is Not a Valid Test

### 2.1 What We Thought

The cod case was implicitly used as a test of the conjunction formula. The implication was: the cod collapsed because institutional dynamics failed.

### 2.2 What the Exact Data Shows

From CSAS SAR 2016/026, Table A2:

| Year | SSB (kt) | M | Interpretation |
|------|----------|---|----------------|
| 1992 | 382 | **2.214** | Moratorium enacted |
| 1993 | 101 | **2.575** | Collapse |

**The collapse was driven by high natural mortality (M = 2.2–2.6), not by institutional failure.**

### 2.3 Institutional Response Was Fast

- Δτ_gov = -1 year (moratorium enacted before SSB crossed 300 kt)
- M_act = +14.9 kt/yr (surplus exceeded catch on average)
- The institutional response was FAST and ADEQUATE
