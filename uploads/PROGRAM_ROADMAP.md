# Research-program roadmap and reading order

The program has one accounting framework and several deliberately named analytical objects. Results transfer only when the registry and operator assumptions below match.

## Reading order

1. **Paper I — baseline ledger and named cores**
   - Establishes the vector accounting framework, diagnostics, and the gated scalar inner and working four-state models.
   - The working four-state equilibrium is a frozen-donor/derived-target object, not a closed finite-donor rest.

2. **Paper II — finite-donor correction**
   - Replaces the derived target by primitive donor-limited transfers.
   - Shows that the finite-donor primitive system has no interior positive-effort rest and cannot sustain a positive constant working flux indefinitely.

3. **Paper III — institutional sign and operator**
   - Separates scarcity-mobilising and protective feedback.
   - Distinguishes continuous delay from sampled review operators.

4. **Paper IV — yield-gap reduction**
   - Gives a conditional local attracting-invariant-graph result under a uniform yield gap and slack-fibre spectral hypothesis.
   - At yield parity the vector system remains unreduced.

5. **Paper V — stage structure**
   - Changes the growth law, observer, and maturation representation.
   - Its stage spectra are not recalculations of the scalar logistic core.

6. **Paper VI — spatial operators**
   - Spatializes selected named cores, primarily mean-field frozen-A and local three-state fields.
   - Mean-field nonzero modes decay in the proved blocks; local delayed spatial Hopf remains open.

7. **Paper VII — empirical first-passage proxies**
   - Interprets groundwater, fisheries, and phosphate table proxies under statistical surrogate processes.
   - Does not compute Paper I's model-generated `Tdep`.

8. **Paper VIII — certification protocol**
   - Defines the Moore--Spence/Krawczyk and eventual radii-polynomial route for the inner three-state fold.
   - Reports no completed fold certificate.

## Transfer rule

A result transfers only if all of the following agree:

- state vector and target closure;
- institutional topology;
- continuous-delay versus sampled-review operator;
- local versus mean-field spatial topology;
- theorem, numerical-result, interval-certificate, or conjecture status.

## Standard status vocabulary

- **Theorem:** proved under stated hypotheses.
- **Conditional theorem:** depends on a yield gap, modal-Hurwitz condition, or closure hypothesis.
- **Numerical result:** computed for named parameters/operator.
- **Interval certificate:** validated inclusion with outward-rounded arithmetic.
- **Conjecture/open:** not yet proved or validated.
