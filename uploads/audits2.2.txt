I cannot ship a solver, a fishery calibration, or a GitHub library from here. Those remain the usefulness bottleneck. ### Primal

**Definition (recursively viable selector).** Fix a policy class \(\Pi\), observation structure \(\mathcal{I}\), review length \(\Delta\), horizon \(N\). For an information state \(B\),

\[
\Gamma_N(B)
=\bigl\{\alpha\in\mathfrak{R}_{\Pi,\mathcal{I}}(B):
\operatorname{Reach}_{[0,\Delta]}(B,\alpha)\subseteq\mathcal{V}
\text{ and every successor }B'\in\mathscr{W}_{N-1}\bigr\},
\]

with \(\mathscr{W}_0=\{B\subseteq\mathcal{V}\}\) and \(\mathscr{W}_N=\{B:\Gamma_N(B)\neq\emptyset\}\). Nested one-step correspondences (original Prop. 3 / Viable Selector Prop. 1):

\[
\mathcal{A}_N(B)
\subseteq\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{tube}}(B,\Delta)
\subseteq\mathcal{R}_\mathcal{V}^B(B)
\subseteq U^B(B).
\]

A pointwise nonempty \(\Gamma_N(B)\) is not a measurable selector; exact completeness requires a non-anticipative selection on the collection of information states (Bertsekas–Shreve). That qualification is load-bearing.

**Theorem A (exactness class).** If the state, action and observation spaces are finite, then \(B\) admits an \(N\)-step observation-based policy in \(\Pi\) that keeps every compatible trajectory in \(\mathcal{V}\) if and only if \(\Gamma_N(B)\neq\emptyset\). Proof: backward induction (original Theorem 1). If the observation is static, the same identity reduces to existence of an open-loop robust control (original Theorem 6).

**Theorem B (soundness elsewhere).** Under the standing \(C^1\) / closed-graph / comparison hypotheses, emptiness of any link in the ladder, or of the blind-window predecessor \(\operatorname{Pre}_{\mathrm{blind}}\) at horizon \(T_{\mathrm{obs}}\), implies \(B\notin\operatorname{ERViab}_\mathcal{I}(\mathcal{V})\). The converse — existence of a measurable selector from nonempty pointwise \(\Gamma\) — is not claimed in continuous time. That is Open Problem 1, not a theorem.

### Dual

Every obstruction certificate of the original paper is a checkable witness that one factor of \(\Gamma\) is empty. In the polyhedral case the witness is literally Farkas; elsewhere it is a Farkas-type object (empty intersection, timing bound, fibre-crossing, Isaacs drift, class restriction).

| Certificate | Factor emptied | Dual witness | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admissibility | \(U^B(B)=\emptyset\) | no common instrument | original Prop. 5 |
| Common action | \(\mathcal{R}_\mathcal{V}^B(B)=\emptyset\) | Farkas pair on stacked safe-action inequalities; Helly \(m+1\) if convex | original Thm. 2, Prop. 4 |
| Tube | \(\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{tube}}(B,\Delta)=\emptyset\) | uniform-margin exit in every review | original Prop. 2 |
| Timing | \(\operatorname{Pre}_{\mathrm{blind}}(\mathscr{C})\) empty at \(T_{\mathrm{obs}}\) | \(\sigma^*(B_0)<T_{\mathrm{obs}}\), or \(\inf q/\varepsilon<T_{\mathrm{obs}}\) | original Thm. 3; **integer grid: viability iff \(T_{\mathrm{obs}}\le\sigma^*=z_0-1\)** |
| Finite-time exit | \(\Gamma\) empty already at singletons | Isaacs/Dini outward drift on a strip | original Thm. 4 |
| Fibre (certification) | no exact label \(\ell\circ O=\mathbf{1}_K\) | a fibre meets both \(K\) and \(K^c\); certainly-safe set \(\mathcal{Y}_{\mathrm{safe}}=\{y:O^{-1}(y)\subseteq K\}\) | original Prop. 7, Cor. 1 |
| CE trap | \(\Gamma_{\Pi_{\mathrm{CE}}}=\emptyset\) while \(\Gamma_{\Pi_{\mathrm{perf}}}\neq\emptyset\) | uncorrected law has strictly positive drift; bias-corrected law does not | original Remark 4 |

**Causes are not unified.** Dynamic exit empties \(\Gamma\) under full information (not epistemic). The fibre criterion is a certification limit, not a policy-viability obstruction. The CE trap is a policy-class restriction and can fire under injective observation. Unifying form (emptiness of a selector) while keeping causes distinct is the Viable Selector §7 claim, and it is the strongest claim the framework supports.

**Weak duality (always).** A dual witness implies \(\Gamma=\emptyset\), hence nonviability in the declared class. **Strong duality (finite-horizon finite, or static observation).** \(\Gamma=\emptyset\) if and only if a dual witness exists (the obstruction tree of original Theorem 1; Farkas for polyhedral common action; the hold-class vs sequential partition of the 48-cell grid).

**Stochastic reading (deficit dual).** Replace set-valued \(\Gamma_N(B)\) by the belief-state safety value \(V_k(b)\). Then \(V_k(b)=1\) is nonempty selector at the deterministic degeneration; \(\Delta_k(b)=1-V_k(b)\) is the dual deficit; chance-constrained common action and min-mass bounds are dual feasible solutions, not surrogates (stochastic-selector Prop. 3). Exact rational alphas on rational data. Robust certificates of the original paper appear only at that degeneration; do not conflate expected survival with worst-case viability.

### Design dual (the converse governance actually needs)

Obstruction answers *why not*. Design needs *what to measure*. In the exactness class this is a corollary, not a new theory.

**Corollary C (one-step monitoring adequacy).** For finite \(X\) and a target \(K\subseteq\mathcal{V}\), an observation structure \(\mathcal{I}\) admits a one-step viable observation-based policy on \(K\) if and only if every fibre \(F\) of \(\mathcal{I}\) that meets \(K\) satisfies

\[
\bigcap_{x\in F\cap K}\mathcal{R}_\mathcal{V}(x)\neq\emptyset.
\]

The coarsest adequate \(\mathcal{I}\) is the partition of \(K\) into maximal sets with nonempty common safe action. Pairwise nonempty intersection is necessary and, without convexity of the instrument sets, not sufficient (Helly fails; the three two-point codices of the monitoring note are the witness). Dynamic (multi-step) fibre-admissibility does not compose; necessity-only remains the general claim (original ladder / residual gap).

**Corollary D (four checkable design rules, now identities).**  
1. **Timing.** On the delayed hidden-regime hold class, viability iff \(T_{\mathrm{obs}}\le\sigma^*(z_0)=z_0-1\) (equivalently \(z_0\ge 1+T_{\mathrm{obs}}\)). The strict inequality \(T_{\mathrm{obs}}<\sigma^*\) in the monitoring note is false on the integer grid. Remedy: review faster than worst-case exit, or a faster indicator of the tightest floor.  
2. **Coarseness.** A fibre that merges states with disjoint \(\mathcal{R}_\mathcal{V}\) is an obstruction at that information state, even if every singleton is viable (original Thm. 2, Example 1). Remedy: split the fibre, not a finer threshold on the same index.  
3. **Aggregation / certification.** An index whose fibres cross a floor cannot support an exact safe/unsafe verdict. Report \(\mathcal{Y}_{\mathrm{safe}}\) or fall silent (original Cor. 1). This is label selection, not control.  
4. **Bias.** Uncorrected certainty-equivalence can empty \(\Gamma\) of a system with nonempty perfect-information kernel. Remedy: use the observation map’s structure. The 21/100 figure is the *uncorrected* drift; “corrected-class drift” in the policy-class note is a reversed label.

**Distance to viability (finite, enumerated).** For a nonviable \(B\), the minimal restorative is one of: a fibre split that restores nonempty \(\mathcal{R}_\mathcal{V}^B\), an instrument added to \(U^B(B)\), a shorter \(T_{\mathrm{obs}}\), or a bias correction. On a two-point belief there is exactly one nontrivial split. That is a corollary of Theorem A plus Corollary C, not a lattice paper.

### What this does to the programme

- **Original paper (Track A).** Insert Theorems A–B and Corollaries C–D as one section titled “Selector, dual certificates, and monitoring adequacy.” State the unifying idea in the introduction in one paragraph. Completeness claimed only for finite-horizon finite and static observation. Residual dynamic gap remains Open Problem 1. Estimation-tube relation: common-action emptiness is emptiness of the belief-state predecessor; the calculus is that programme’s local certificate layer.  
- **Catalogue notes (D4, D7, D8, D11, D13, vector floors, policy lattice, monitoring, decentralized, discrete regimes).** Withdraw as papers. Keep only non-recycled facts in a “worked systems” supplement: Helly failure without convexity, 48-cell hold-class vs sequential partition, matching \(u=m\) under switching, anti-symmetric voting on \(B^*\) if the two-patch system is *defined* once. Every 28-of-36 count is the same 8-viable-state pair family; cite it once.  
- **Viable Selector / Stochastic Selector notes.** Absorb: selector definition and exactness into Theorem A; deficit dual into the stochastic paragraph; seven-family and 384-alpha campaigns into the supplement *after* the scripts are archived with named models. Fix empty section refs, the unrestricted boxed iff, “in-window adaptivity,” and CE terminology before any of that text is reused.  
- **Track B (LP companion).** Still the computational lever; this principle tells it what to compute (emptiness of \(\Gamma\), or a dual feasible \(\lambda\), or \(\sigma^*\)). It is not replaced.  
- **Usefulness.** The four rules in Corollary D, with the integer delay identity corrected, are the two-page note an indicator working group can read. The library MVP implements Theorem A on finite models and Corollaries C–D as reports (Farkas pair, fibre-crossing, certainly-safe set, \(T_{\mathrm{obs}}\) vs \(\sigma^*\)). No further D-item is required for that.

### What this does not claim

No continuous-time completeness; no general hybrid or \(n\)-agency theory; no sensor-cost mechanism design; no identification of obstruction with barrier certificates (they are dual in spirit, not the same object); no assertion that the 9-state toy is a general computational calculus. Strong duality is finite-horizon finite or static. Elsewhere the dual is a sound exclusion test.

That is the upgrade: one primal, dual witnesses, design converse, exactness class written on the theorem box, causes kept distinct. It is already latent in original Theorems 1–7, Propositions 1–8 and §6.4. Writing it once, modestly, and deleting the rest is the largest rigorous increment available without new mathematics and without a solver.
