**Boundary 6 as a tagged norm, component-wise floors, and the population this program already named**

**Final version incorporating all surviving findings from the complete research program**

## Abstract

The parent program's sixth boundary is a value premise, not a physical law and not a control theorem. It says that a persistent socio-ecological arrangement is inadmissible if the adaptive capacity of the worst-off relevant population — including future persons, as a modelled constituency — is below a stated minimum or is declining.

This paper does three things and no fourth.

First, it splits the informal B6 sentence into typed claims so that a floor, a non-decline clause, a non-aggregation rule, and a colonial-pattern forbid cannot travel under one name.

Second, it states what is presently measurable without invention. Household purchasing power on a locked World Bank PIP query is one axis. Multidimensional deprivation already has an instrument (Alkire–Foster / global MPI). Neither instrument is B6. Both are world-hooks for *deprivation*. The residue of B6 — who pays, who is excluded, who may use remaining variety against whom — is not filled with a 0–1 "agency" score.

Third, it names the population this research program already owes: inshore communities in Newfoundland and Labrador after the 1992 Northern-cod moratorium. A recovered or recovering biomass series that coincides with a destroyed recruitment-of-people loop is exactly the pattern B6 was written to catch. The paper does not invent that human series. It specifies the lock (agency, table, window) and the discriminants. Until those numbers exist, B6 is not operationalized.

## 3. Why a Single Safety Function Is the Wrong Object

The discarded draft encoded $\bigcap_k\{a_k \ge c_k\}$ as a smooth barrier function. The iff is a definition plus a standard limit. At finite $\rho$, compensation is reintroduced. At $\rho \to \infty$, the function is unnecessary.

**What replaces it:** For each *measured* axis $k$:
$$m_k(G,t) = I_k(G,t) - c_k$$

Report the vector $m(G,t)$. If any measured $m_k < 0$, the arrangement fails N1 on that axis for that $G$.
