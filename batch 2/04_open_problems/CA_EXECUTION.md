# C-a Execution: Full Decidability at Fixed Data

## C-a.Thm2 (PROVEN)

On the finite class (compact X, finite reviews N, compact U/D, Hausdorff-continuous successors), every sentence of the judgment language — including negations and Boolean combinations — is decidable for each fixed instantiation, in O(N·|grid|) time, by backward predecessor iteration.

## C-a.Thm3 (PROVEN)

The zero-one law for monotone claims does not extend to non-monotone claims. The correct extension is instance-level decidability (Thm2), not axiom-level determination.

## Scope

The theorem is for the abstract finite class. Application to a specific calibrated model requires verifying the Lem4 hypotheses on that model. Logical completeness remains OPEN.
