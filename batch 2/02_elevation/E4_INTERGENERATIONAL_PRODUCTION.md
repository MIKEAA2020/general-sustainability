# E4 — Intergenerational Production

## E4.Lem1 — Jump-margin transfer — PROVEN

Depth co-Lipschitz margin `(ℓ, b)`: `R_g(K_{g,−r}) ⊆ K_{g+1,−(ℓr−b)}`. The margin is declared data, not derivable from Lipschitz + boundary margins (the over-derivation attempt is recorded and refuted).

## E4.Thm2 — Eroded generation transfer — PROVEN

Under (1) within-generation erosion conditions, (2) jump-margin transfer, (3) non-Zeno data: the eroded generation path is strongly invariant.

## E4.Thm3 — Production assembly — PROVEN (exact instantiation)

Per-generation certificate gfps exist (E2.B1); measurable intergenerational policies exist (E2.B2(a)); accumulate/cap/forgive are declared τ^h semantics.
