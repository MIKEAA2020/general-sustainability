## 1. The shared diagnosis and its standing

Both audits converge on one diagnosis: the science is strong, the empirical work is genuinely valuable, the formal contributions are real, and **the presentation is wrapped in internal scaffolding** that domain referees cannot be expected to decode ("Wave E," "R04-admitted," "Ω_2016," "the General Theory's §15," "negative certificate"). Both conclude the corpus must be re-wrappered for external scientific communities rather than for the internal knowledge base.

**This diagnosis is confirmed — and it is already the adjudicated and partially executed position of this programme.** The fourth-session records (2026-08-29) adjudicated the same diagnosis from the external style review and answered it structurally: the two-layer edition architecture (internal editions as the auditable record; journal-facing editions as the submission versions, Paper 1's `manuscript_v3.md` as the executed template), the per-paper venue-pass register, and the paper-types-and-venues decision memo. The audits' independent arrival at the same diagnosis is recorded as convergent external validation of that architecture. The genuinely new content of these audits is the publication-strategy layer (venues, sequencing, the monograph question), evaluated in §§3–4 below.
