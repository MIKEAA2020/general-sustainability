with the **original submission** (`uploads/ECOMOD-26-1191.pdf`, full text in
`/home/user/fulltext.txt`). It makes explicit what changed and why. Section numbers on
the *after* side refer to v34; the *before* side is the original single-stock
"productivity illusion" model as submitted.

- **Before:** *Overcoming the productivity illusion with delayed feedbacks and emergent carrying capacity* — a two-delay single-stock (single-capital `A`) DDE model, threshold at a *combined* delay ≈80 yr.
- **After:** *Emergent Carrying Capacity and the Composition Illusion: Two-Land Conversion and the Identifiability of Collapse* — a two-land model (fast provisioning `A_f` + ecological capital `A_c`) with a separate per-hectare quality state `q`, a *finite-rate* conversion, no interior fold, no critical slowing, and an explicit identifiability result.

## 2. Scientific-content changes (the substantive ones)

### 2.1 One stock → two books, with a separate capacity state
- **Before:** single capital `A`, `B = bA + b_G G(A)`, an interior MSY fold in the capital-dominated regime, and a two-delay interaction threshold.
- **After:** biocapacity sums two books, `B = b_f A_f + [b_c + b_G G_c(q)] A_c`, where the per-hectare capacity `q` is a *distinct state* (regenerated by `G_c(q)`, never a land-area transfer). Area is conserved one-to-one; conversion is a finite rate `u_c = min(S/b_f, A_c − A_c,min)/τ_conv`, not instantaneous.

### 2.2 Fold → no fold; fold replaced by a typed-floor collision
- **Before:** a saddle-node (fold) at the MSY, realised in the capital-dominated regime; critical-slowing / early-warning framing.
- **After:** `B_eq(A_c)` is **monotone** in `A_c`, so for each `E < E_ceil` a *unique* interior equilibrium exists and there is **no interior saddle-node**. Above `E_ceil≈1.138` the capital book is driven to its typed floor and debt accrues — a *floor collision*, not a fold. The leading eigenvalue is constant ≈−0.0537 yr⁻¹ and does **not** tend to zero, so there is **no critical slowing down** (a *negative*, falsifiable prediction 3).
- This also corrects a one-stock-specific result the original reported as generic (`+0.62`, `5.4 yr` mask, `τ_g≈18–20 yr` cliff): those are now explicitly re-labelled as one-stock **comparator** features, not two-land properties.

### 2.3 The composition diagnosis and the identifiability result (new)
- **After:** the composition diagnostic `Δb_conv = b_f − (b_c + b_G G_c(q))` determines whether conversion *raises* current accounting capacity; it is **structural** (robustly `+0.78` at baseline) not contingent on an interior capacity maximum.
- The composition step is **not identifiable from the aggregate alone** (gha embeds yield and equivalence factors), so the empirical programme is a flux reconstruction needing independent `A_c` (land cover) and `b_f` (yield) proxies.

### 2.4 The aggregation face (real data) — new, data-grounded
The paper now applies the exact identity `d ln B = d ln(B/P) + d ln P` to the National Footprint & Biocapacity Accounts (world, 1961–2022): population `+0.959` (`+464%`), per-capita `−0.752` (`−364%`), overshoot onset in 1971. This is the **aggregation** face of the masking result, explicitly *distinguished from* the two-land composition illusion, and the public World accounts' lack of a land-type split is used to demonstrate the identifiability limit directly.

### 2.5 Recovery asymmetry (new)
Recovery is **not** reversible by reversing the forcing. It requires an explicit restoration flow (`R_rc`, surplus-gated), and the **gate sign matters**: a surplus-gated form is sign-correct rewilding but is inert during a shortfall; a deficit-gated form acts during the shortfall but can deepen it. Reported via the recovery metric `\mathcal{R}_c(T)` (SI §S5.4).

### 2.6 Empirical calibration (new) — regeneration timescale and provisioning-book yield
- **Regeneration timescale (SI §S5.3a).** Instead of relying only on published summary statistics, the paper now fits a lagged saturating recovery model directly to **raw recovery curves** in two ecosystem classes: the forest leg (2ndFOR, Poorter et al. 2016; 1,334 plots, 41 chronosequences; 35 good fits, median R² ≈ 0.68) gives `t₅₀ ≈ 30 yr` and `τ_r ≈ 38 yr`; the fishery leg (RAM Legacy B/BMSY; 33 recovery episodes, median R² ≈ 0.92) gives `t₅₀ ≈ 5.6 yr` and `τ_r ≈ 3.4 yr`, with 13 of 33 trajectories right-censored. Soil is retained as a cited timescale (Poeplau et al. 2011, ≈17–23 yr) because the available SOC data are intervention-comparison, not a natural recovery curve. This is explicit about what it is and is not: it estimates the regeneration *rate* `ρ` (a recovery timescale), not the *lag* `τ_g`.
- **Provisioning-book yield and area (SI §S5.3b).** The fast-book yield `b_f` is anchored directly on the **measured** NFA world cropland biocapacity: `b_f(1961) = CroplandBiocapacity(1961)/A_f(1961) = 0.93 gha·ha⁻¹·yr⁻¹` (NFA land-type value, `Record = BiocapTotGHA`, `Cropland` column), removing the earlier dependence on the unidentified share `α`. Over 1961–2022, `A_f` rose 1.17× while `b_f` rose 2.43× (measured, gha·ha⁻¹·yr⁻¹; 3.09× for the physical cereal sentinel), so `d ln b_f` dominates `d ln A_f` — the provisioning-side expression of the composition premium.
- **Aggregate attribution.** The cropland (fast) book grew 2.85× while the non-cropland book is flat (≈0.99–1.01×), so the cropland book accounts for essentially all of the net 1961–2022 aggregate biocapacity growth — ≈103% against the +23.0% aggregate series and ≈97% against the newer +24.5% land-type total (both reported; see the SI §S5.3b vintage note). This is a measured, vintage-robust expression of the composition mechanism.

## 4. Rigour / honesty changes

- **Effective-parameter discipline** (Limitations, Model scope): the capital land is an *effective class*, not one real land type; `b_c, b_G, ρ_c` are not individually identifiable from a single cross-section; only `b_Gρ_c` and `b_c,eff` are identifiable combinations.
- **Illustrative, not calibrated**: representative parameters, `τ_g`/`ρ_c` banded (≈25–33 yr, rep ≈29 yr) and labelled illustrative; the SI §S5.3 derives the band from sourced recovery timescales and flags the two readings that were not faithful to the sources.
- **Scope statements kept**: most governing parameters are representative rather than fitted, the empirically
  anchored quantities being the capital-land regeneration timescale (§S5.3a) and the provisioning-book yield and
  area (§S5.3b); "the model is not a forecast"; "fit-defect disclosure"; "interval discipline".
