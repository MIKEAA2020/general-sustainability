The figures below marked **(one-stock comparator)** depict the retained one-stock single-capital `A` limit whose features (`+0.62`/`+0.625`, `5.4 yr` mask, `τ_g≈18–20 yr` cliff) are **one-stock, not two-land** properties; they are included as the comparator the manuscript keeps as a limit.
