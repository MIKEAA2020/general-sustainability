# Highlights

**Manuscript:** How Aggregation Can Conceal Composition: Aggregate Biocapacity and the Identifiability of Modelled Ecological-Capital Drawdown

Five highlights (Elsevier format; each ≤ 85 characters):

- Global biocapacity rose ~23% (1961-2022), almost all from cropland (2.85x).
- Per-capita biocapacity fell by half (-53%) while the aggregate rose.
- Converting ecological capital to provisioning land raises total, capital falls.
- The capital drawdown is demand-driven, hits a typed floor (not a fold).
- An aggregate biocapacity cannot identify its own composition (two-land).

---

## Key contributions (expanded)

1. **The aggregate conceals its composition, and the concealment is structural.** Total biocapacity is a
   value-weighted sum of heterogeneous land accounts (cropland, grazing, forest products, fishing grounds,
   built-up land). Raw accounts show global biocapacity rose ≈23% over 1961–2022, yet the rise came almost
   entirely from cropland (2.85×), while the non-cropland book was essentially flat at the endpoint (≈1.01×).
   Per-capita biocapacity fell by half (−53%) over the same window, hidden behind the rising aggregate.

2. **A minimal two-land model makes the masking mechanism precise.** Conversion from ecological-capital land to
   fast-provisioning land can raise the measured total while the model's capital book falls — a *composition*
   illusion, distinct from (and complementary to) the productivity illusion.

3. **The capital drawdown is demand-driven and floor-colliding, not a fold.** The capital book is drawn to its
   typed floor once demand exceeds a ceiling (≈1.138); the leading eigenvalue is constant along the branch, so
   there is **no** critical-slowing-down precursor, and the observable warning is the floor being reached rather
   than a vanishing eigenvalue.

4. **Recovery requires a gated restoration flow.** Reduction of demand or conversion does not reverse prior
   conversion; restoration is protective only when surplus-gated (a deficit-gated form can deepen the
   short-run deficit). The stabilising institutional sign is the protective one.

5. **The identifiability result drives the policy prescription.** Because biocapacity is expressed in
   value-weighted global hectares, the aggregate does not identify its own composition; component reporting is
   required at every level at which an aggregate is used to summarise ecological change, and distinguishing
   composition requires independent land-cover and yield data.

6. **Not a forecast, and no measured capital decline.** The finding is a measured composition pattern plus an
   identifiability property; it does not show a measured decline in the model's ecological-capital book, and it
   is robust to the NFA vintage (the core +23.0% aggregate series vs. the +24.5% land-type re-based total).
