### 1. The Degeneracy of the Viability Formalism

*   **The Flaw:** The paper leverages the heavy mathematical machinery of viability theory (robust viability kernels) to evaluate the policies. 
*   **The Bedrock Issue:** Viability theory was invented to solve problems where a controller dynamically selects an action from a set of permitted controls ($u \in U(x)$) at each time step to navigate a state space. By stripping the controller of dynamic choice and hardcoding the policy, the system collapses into a simple, autonomous 1D difference equation.

State: *"Because the evaluated governance rules are strict deterministic mappings rather than set-valued control spaces, the viability formalism mathematically degenerates into classical 1D fixed-point stability analysis.
