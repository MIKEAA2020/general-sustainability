### Flaws / internal inconsistencies (real)

**1. Abstract vs Theorem 5(4): \(I\) is not open.**  
The abstract calls \(\mathrm{FP}_{\mathrm{agg}}=I\) “a nonempty open set.”  
Theorem 5(4) correctly splits \(I=\{x<1,\,s_1<2,\,s_2<2,\,s_1+s_2\ge 2\}\) from its open interior \(\{0<x<1,\,0<s_1<2,\,0<s_2<2,\,s_1+s_2>2\}\). The face \(s_1+s_2=2\) is in \(I\) and prevents openness. Same abstract-over-body pattern as the fisheries obstruction sentence. The claim in §1.3 (“an open region of strict separation”) is the accurate one.

**2. Abstract vs Theorem 8: “only by resource augmentation.”**  
The abstract says states of the gap “can be bridged into the noncompensatory assessment *only* by resource augmentation at a defined cost,” then immediately cites the blend theorem that closes the same gap by convexifying the menu, with no change in \(x\).  
Within the declared four-action menu, reweighting cannot and STAGED-augmentation can — that is Theorem 5(7)+§5.5. Blend is a different menu. The word “only” is false unless restricted to the original menu; Theorem 8 is the paper’s own counterexample to the unrestricted reading. §5.3 already says the impossibility certificate is menu-relative. The abstract should match that.

**3. “Three implications” vs four.**  
§5.4 opens with “three implications, each stated at its actual strength,” then labels **First** through **Fourth**. Counting error.

**4. Notation clash: Theorem 4 writes \(W\subseteq C\).**  
§2.2 states that the weight cone is \(W\), command architecture is \(C\), action set is \(\mathcal{A}\), “the three objects never share a symbol.” Theorem 4 then takes a weight family \(W\subseteq C\). \(C\) here is being used as a cone. The proof also needs \(W\subseteq W_+\) for \(\mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{typ}}\subseteq\mathcal{V}_W\). Local repair: write \(W\subseteq W_+\).

**5. Blend successor notation.**  
The action table gives FAST and SLOW the *same* successor \((1,x,s+e)\). The proof of Theorem 8 writes \(\delta e+(1-\delta)e'\) as if the resets differed. Harmless (the mixture still lies in convex \(G\)), but it suggests a datum that is not the one in §4.5.

None of these breaks a theorem. They are status and specification failures.
