S1      Symbols and model extensions
A global hectare (gha) is a hectare normalized to world-average biological productivity for the rel-
evant accounting year. Annual Ecological Footprint and biocapacity account flows are expressed
in gha, with the annual basis implicit in the account year (Global Footprint Network, 2009, 2019).
In the model, B and E are annual account-equivalent quantities; in the empirical analysis, B and
Eprod are observed NFA account values. The model contributions Yf and Yc and shortfall S are
expressed in gha, but are not separately reported NFA series. The leaky debt state D is the
time-integral of the model shortfall in gha yr; Ḋ and ηD are in gha.

S2.3    Characteristic structure of the full delay system
With state z = (Af , Ac , q, P, D) the linearized system about any point of a given active set is
ż(t) = J0 z(t) + Jg z(t − τg ) + Jp z(t − τp ) with characteristic equation det ∆(λ) = 0, ∆(λ) =
λI − J0 − Jg e−λτg − Jp e−λτp . J0 carries the conversion and debt feedbacks on the current state
(the 2 × 2 block above, plus the D row ∂ Ḋ/∂z = (−bf , −Υc , −bG,c G′c Ac , e, −η) on the deficit side
and the ∂bf /∂D = −αbf couplings); Jg has the single entry G′c (q ∗ ) in the q row; Jp has the row
(rP ∗2 /K ∗2 ) e−1 ∂B/∂z in the P position (from ∂ Ṗ /∂K = rP 2 /K 2 and K = B/e). Two scalar
delayed modes decouple at the baseline. The quality mode linearized at qmax is ξ˙ = −ρc ξ(t − τg )
(rate −0.08 yr−1 without the delay); its characteristic root satisfies λ = −ρc e−λτg , and since
ρc τg = 1.6 > π/2 the linear delayed mode would be oscillatory. In the nonlinear model this
cannot occur because q̇ = Gc (q(t − τg )) ≥ 0 for q(t − τg ) ∈ [0, qmax ] and q is clamped at qmax , so
quality is monotone non-decreasing and converges to qmax ; the linear oscillation is an artifact of
linearizing a one-sided law at its boundary. The population mode on the no-conversion face is the
delayed logistic law with rτp = 0.5 < π/2, below the Hutchinson threshold, so it is non-oscillatory
at the baseline.

S2.5    Boundedness asymmetry and the crossover shortfall
From Eq. (12), ln bf = ln(bf 0 + Tb ) − αD. The first term lies in [ln bf 0 , ln(bf 0 + ∆b)], so
the technology channel contributes at most ln(1 + ∆b/bf 0 ) = ln(1.85/0.85) = 0.778 to ln bf
at the baseline. From Eq. (13), Ḋ ≤ S̄ − ηD with S̄ the running supremum of the shortfall,
so D(t) ≤ max{D(0), S̄/η} and the degradation channel contributes at least −αS̄/η. The two
are equal when S̄ = S † = (η/α) ln(1 + ∆b/bf 0 ) = 1.296 gha at the baseline. The fast-land
contribution Yf = bf Af shares this structure; the capital-land contribution Υc Ac is unaffected by
either channel. Under the additive law bf = bf 0 + Tb − αD, both channels are bounded quantities
of the same kind (∆b against αD∗ ) and the logarithmic asymmetry disappears, the bound above
therefore applies to the multiplicative specification.

S2.6    Macro-ratio identities
Define RB = E/B, RA = E/Yf and the fast-land share ψf = Yf /B; then RA = RB /ψf is
an identity. The direct-yield share within the capital-land contribution, ψc = bc Ac /Yc , is a
different quantity and RA = RB /ψc is false. RB = 1 is aggregate balance. With baseline shares
σf = σc = 1, RA > 1 (demand exceeds the fast-land account yield) is necessary but not sufficient
for conversion, because the shortfall requires E > B = Yf + Yc , not merely E > Yf ; with
reserved flows the threshold changes. The capital-land area declines when uc > Rrc , with uc
given by Eq. (5). Under the baseline shares, active conversion therefore requires RB > 1; in
the stated reservation extension σf , σc < 1, conversion and declining capital-land area can occur
while RB ≤ 1, so aggregate overshoot is not required in that extension. At the regeneration
maximum q ∗ = qmax /2 the direct-yield share of capital value is bc /(bc + bG,c ρc qmax /4) = 0.758 at
the baseline; this is a property of that point, not a structural constant.


S3     Numerical methods and sensitivity analyses
Numerical method. The delay equations are integrated by the method of steps with an explicit
first-order Euler scheme. The positive-part operator is exact; the state bounds are Ac ≥ Amin    c ,
Af ≥ 0, q ∈ [0, qmax ], P ≥ 0, D ≥ 0, and K ≥ Kmin . The reserve is calculated from Ar = Atot −
Af − Ac . Pre-run histories of B and q are constant at their initial values over [− max(τg , τp ), 0).
In the technology-wave experiment, both runs use the common no-wave prehistory; the active
Tb (0) changes current B(0), and subsequent wave-adjusted B values enter the delayed history
used for K(t − τp ). The demonstration run and bisection thresholds use ∆t = 0.05 yr; drawdown-
depth sweeps use ∆t = 0.1 yr, with selected thresholds evaluated at 0.05 yr. For Ac0 = 1.0 ha, the
10−6 ha floor-proximity threshold changes from 2.955 to 2.98 cap across these step sizes; threshold
values are therefore reported as tolerance- and resolution-dependent bands.

Delayed versus current quality in the regeneration term. The reported trajectories use
the delayed regeneration law Gc (q(t−τg )), as specified in Eq. (10). A current-quality specification,
Gc (q(t)), is evaluated as a sensitivity. Because q is monotone and the regenerative contribution
is small (bG,c Gc ≤ 0.016 gha ha−1 , compared with bc = 0.05 gha ha−1 ), the differences in the
reported summaries are below 1%. The current-quality specification extends the demonstration

Well-posedness. The admissible state space Ω = {(Af , Ac , Ar , q, P, D) : Ai ≥ 0, q ∈

each admissible history. Stability at S = 0 is evaluated from the deficit side.

S5.4    Observation-operator hierarchy
  NFA accounting observations. Total and component biocapacity values in gha support state-
   ments about the composition and movement of the NFA accounts under the stated edition
   and conventions. They do not directly observe biomass, soil condition, wetland extent, fish
   biomass, or the model state Ac .

  Fast-provisioning area. The external world cropland-area series is arable plus permanent-crop
   area, 1961–2023 (ha) (Our World in Data, 2026a). It is used as an independently sourced
   proxy input, not treated as identical to the NFA cropland-area series.

  Crop-productivity sentinel. The OWID/FAOSTAT cereal-yield series, 1961–2024, is measured
   in tonnes per hectare (Our World in Data, 2026b). It is not an all-crops index and is not
   converted into a physical stock. The base-anchored b̂f is only the index-scaled term in the
   illustrative account proxy.

  Ecological-capital components. Forest extent, growing stock or biomass, soil carbon and condi-
   tion, wetland extent and condition, and species- or stock-specific fish biomass could inform sep-
   arate physical observation operators. They would require harmonized spatial boundaries, ref-
   erence years, definitions, uncertainty, and explicit treatment of overlap. No common-boundary
   global physical-stock series for the relevant components was assembled here. Existing NFA
   forest biocapacity and the residual C are not substitutes for those measurements.

  Model mapping. In the model, Yc = Υc (q)Ac is a model-defined capital contribution. Em-
   pirically, neither Ac nor q is identified by B or by an NFA component; the proxy residual
   C = B − X is not assumed to equal Yc . A defensible observation operator would need inde-
   pendent physical measures and a declared mapping before a model-to-data comparison.

   Table S10 summarizes this mapping without equating an NFA category to a model state.

S5.5    Regeneration anchors
The capital-land regeneration timescale is a lumped recruitment-response timescale. Plot-level
age–above-ground-biomass records from Neotropical secondary forests (2ndFOR; 1,334 plots, 41
chronosequences, 35 good fits) yield median t50 ≈ 30 yr and τr ≈ 38 yr under a lagged saturating
recovery model (Poorter et al., 2016). Deplete-then-rebuild trajectories for 33 collapsed fish
stocks in RAM Legacy (B/BM SY ) yield t50 ≈ 5.6 yr and τr ≈ 3.4 yr (Hutchings and Reynolds,
2004; Neubauer et al., 2013); soil organic carbon re-equilibrates over approximately 17–23 yr
following land-use change (Poeplau et al., 2011). Forest biomass reaches about half of old-growth
values by 20 yr and 90% at a median of about 66 yr, while approximately 29% of collapsed fish
stocks recover to 50% within 5–15 yr. These records span distinct systems and are not pooled
into a single empirical rate. Forest and soil observations provide decadal-scale context for the
representative effective response scale of about 29 yr; the 10–40 yr interval is a modeling range
for the lumped capital-land class, not a pooled empirical estimate. The faster fish-stock episodes
are not treated as a direct lower bound on that class. Neither ρc nor the delay τg is calibrated
from these observations.

S5.6    Recursive identifiability in the land-type accounts
NFA Edition 2026 shows why the aggregate should be read together with its account composi-
tion, without translating account movement into physical-stock movement. From 1961 to 2023,
cropland contributes +24.0577 percentage points of the +24.8568% change in total B; built-up
land contributes +7.4416 points, forest biocapacity −5.9525, grazing land −0.2688, and fishing
grounds −0.4212. The non-cropland account sum, which includes built-up land, changes by only
+0.7990 points (1.0092×) because those unlike account movements net against one another. In
the same-edition 1961–2022 sensitivity, the corresponding contributions are cropland +23.8183,
built-up land +7.2854, forest biocapacity −5.8137, grazing land −0.2890, and fishing grounds
−0.4559 points; the non-cropland sum changes by +0.7267 points (1.0083×). These are account-
ing attributions, not evidence that forest biomass, soil condition, wetland extent, fish biomass, or
modeled Ac declined. Built-up land is an NFA account class, not ecological capital; its inclusion
in the non-cropland sum is arithmetic, not ecological aggregation.

S5.7    Physical-stock scoreboard: specification, not inventory
No common-boundary global physical-stock series for the relevant components was assembled,
and no physical-stock composite or global decline finding is presented. This absence does not
establish that such a series could never be assembled. Table S12 is an unpopulated specification
of candidate measures and comparability requirements; it contains no empirical endpoint values
and is not a model calibration.
    Heterogeneous physical stocks cannot be integrated by simply summing their native units:
forest biomass or soil carbon (mass), wetland or cropland extent (area), fish biomass by species
or assessed stock, freshwater storage (volume), and condition indicators (often dimensionless or
categorical) are neither dimensionally commensurate nor necessarily spatially disjoint. Annual
flows such as harvest, catch, increment, recharge, or emissions must also remain distinct from
stocks and extents. Even after normalization into a common score, overlap, baselines, uncer-
tainty, ecological thresholds, valuation, and permitted substitution determine the interpretation.
Component measures therefore form a nonadditive vector unless common spatial and temporal
boundaries, normalization or valuation, and substitution rules are declared. A scalar composite
is not neutral and cannot be inferred from the NFA gha accounts. This distinction is consis-
tent with ecosystem-extent and ecosystem-condition accounts being reported separately from
ecosystem-service and monetary presentations in SEEA Ecosystem Accounting (United Nations
et al., 2021).
    In the two-land model, Ac is an effective area state (ha) and q is a separate capacity-quality
index. Neither is directly observed in the NFA aggregate. A physical-stock assessment could
inform a model observation operator only after its boundaries and mapping to those effective
states are specified. The proxy residual C = B − X = B − b̂f Af is not ecological capital, a
measured non-cropland stock, or an estimate of Υc (q)Ac ; the NFA components remain account
results rather than physical-stock observations.

Reserved biocapacity share. With a common reservation share σf = σc = σ < 1, human-
available supply is σB and the shortfall is S = [E − σB]+ . The reservation lowers the demand
at which shortfall-driven conversion begins; conversion is inactive while E ≤ σB. With distinct
shares, the corresponding condition is E ≤ σf Yf + σc Yc , which need not equal σB. These are con-
ditional results for the specified parameterization and do not establish robustness to disturbances
or parameter uncertainty.

Institutional signature. Related work distinguishes protective from mobilizing institutional

whereas deficit-gated restoration acts during it and can reduce provision further (Section S4.4).

Aggregation and typed feasibility. In related assessment work, B is the linear, perfectly
substitutable member of an aggregator family (Lemma A of Abaee, 2026b). Aggregate feasibility
can therefore coexist with violation of a typed floor on Ac ; no positive-elasticity aggregator is
uniformly safe (Theorem S2(ii) in Abaee (2026b)). Under pathwise feasibility, a decline below a
typed floor during the transition remains a violation even if the endpoint later recovers. These
results distinguish an aggregate account criterion from constraints on individual components.

S8     Controlled technology-wave experiment
The matched experiment compares endogenous-demand runs with Tb active or inactive, using
P0 = 1.0 or 1.2 cap. Both runs in each pair share Atot = 2.5 ha, Af 0 = 0.6 ha, Ac0 = 0.9 ha,
q0 = 0.9, and D0 = 0.0 gha yr. Parameter values and numerical methods match; only ωT b differs.
The common pre-run histories are constant at q0 = 0.9 and the no-wave initial account value
B = 0.560184 gha in both runs. Shared parameters are bf 0 = 0.85 gha ha−1 , bc = 0.05 gha ha−1 ,
bG,c = 0.8 gha yr ha−1 , ρc = 0.08 yr−1 , α = 0.03 gha−1 yr−1 , η = 0.05 yr−1 , ηf = 0.05 yr−1 ,
µ = 0.06 yr−1 , e = 0.55 gha cap−1 , r = 0.02 yr−1 , τg = 20 yr, τp = 25 yr, τconv = 1 yr, and
χr = 0.10 gha−1 yr−1 . The active-wave parameters are ∆b = 1.0 gha ha−1 , κw = 0.1 yr−1 , and
twave = 40 yr; the integration horizon is 250 yr. The logistic increment is nonzero at t = 0
(Tb (0) = 0.017986 gha ha−1 ), giving B(0) = 0.560184 gha without the wave and 0.570976 gha
with it. The difference at t = 0 arises from the logistic tail; the land, quality, population, and
debt states are otherwise matched, and the pre-run B history remains common. The wave reaches
half its increment at twave = 40 yr.
