S1      Symbol table and stated extensions
Following GFN/NFA practice, each annual footprint or biocapacity account value is reported in
gha, not gha yr−1 (Global Footprint Network, 2009, 2019). In the model, B and E are annual
NFA account values; Yf and Yc are derived annual contributions to B; and S is an account-
valued model shortfall, not a separate NFA series. These quantities have units gha and their time
derivatives have units gha yr−1 . The leaky debt state D is the model-internal time-integral of
the shortfall and has units gha yr, so Ḋ and ηD are gha. Neither S nor D is an NFA-reported
account item.

S2.3     Characteristic structure of the full delay system
With state z = (Af , Ac , q, P, D) the linearized system about any point of a given active set is
ż(t) = J0 z(t) + Jg z(t − τg ) + Jp z(t − τp ) with characteristic equation det ∆(λ) = 0, ∆(λ) =
λI − J0 − Jg e−λτg − Jp e−λτp . J0 carries the conversion and debt feedbacks on the current state
(the 2 × 2 block above, plus the D row ∂ Ḋ/∂z = (−bf , −Υc , −bG,c G′c Ac , e, −η) on the deficit side
and the ∂bf /∂D = −αbf couplings); Jg has the single entry G′c (q ∗ ) in the q row; Jp has the row
(rP ∗2 /K ∗2 ) e−1 ∂B/∂z in the P position (from ∂ Ṗ /∂K = rP 2 /K 2 and K = B/e). Two scalar
delayed modes decouple at the baseline. The quality mode linearized at qmax is ξ˙ = −ρc ξ(t − τg )
(rate −0.08 yr−1 without the delay); its characteristic root satisfies λ = −ρc e−λτg , and since
ρc τg = 1.6 > π/2 the linear delayed mode would be oscillatory. In the nonlinear model this cannot
occur because q̇ = Gc (q(t − τg )) ≥ 0 for q(t − τg ) ∈ [0, qmax ] and q is clamped at qmax , so quality is
monotone non-decreasing and converges to qmax ; the linear oscillation is an artifact of linearizing
a one-sided law at its boundary. The population mode on the no-conversion face is the delayed
logistic law with rτp = 0.5 < π/2, below the Hutchinson threshold, so it is non-oscillatory at the
baseline. No entry, determinant sign or eigenvalue is inherited from the one-stock characteristic

S2.5    Boundedness asymmetry and the crossover shortfall
From Eq. (12), ln bf = ln(bf 0 + Tb ) − αD. The first term lies in [ln bf 0 , ln(bf 0 + ∆b)], so
the technology channel contributes at most ln(1 + ∆b/bf 0 ) = ln(1.85/0.85) = 0.778 to ln bf at
the baseline. From Eq. (13), Ḋ ≤ S̄ − ηD with S̄ the running supremum of the shortfall, so
D(t) ≤ max{D(0), S̄/η} and the degradation channel contributes at least −αS̄/η. The two are
equal when S̄ = S † = (η/α) ln(1 + ∆b/bf 0 ) = 1.296 gha at the baseline. For the fast-book yield
itself, Yf = bf Af shares this structure; the capital term Υc Ac is unaffected by either channel.
Under the additive law bf = bf 0 + Tb − αD, both channels are bounded quantities of the same
kind (∆b against αD∗ ) and the logarithmic asymmetry disappears, which is why the paper states
the result for the multiplicative form.

S2.6    Macro-ratio identities
Define RB = E/B, RA = E/Yf and the fast-land share ψf = Yf /B; then RA = RB /ψf is an
identity. The direct-yield share inside the capital book, ψc = bc Ac /Yc , is a different quantity
and RA = RB /ψc is false. RB = 1 is aggregate balance. With baseline shares σf = σc = 1,
RA > 1 (demand exceeds the fast-book account yield) is necessary but not sufficient for conversion,
because the shortfall requires E > B = Yf + Yc , not merely E > Yf ; with reserved flows the
threshold changes. The capital book declines iff uc > Rrc , with uc given by Eq. (5). Under the
baseline shares, active conversion therefore requires RB > 1; in the stated reservation extension
σf , σc < 1, conversion and a declining capital book can occur while RB ≤ 1, so aggregate overshoot
is not required in that extension. “RB = 1 is the fold” belongs to the one-stock frozen-demand
reducer (Section S7), not to the two-land model. At the regeneration maximum q ∗ = qmax /2
the direct-yield share of capital value is bc /(bc + bG,c ρc qmax /4) = 0.758 at the baseline; this is a
property of that point, not a structural constant.

S5.4    Observation-operator hierarchy
  NFA accounting observations. Total and component biocapacity values in gha support state-
   ments about the composition and movement of the NFA accounts under the stated edition
   and conventions. They do not directly observe biomass, soil condition, wetland extent, fish
   biomass, or the model state Ac .
  Fast-provisioning area. The external world cropland-area series is arable plus permanent-crop
   area, 1961–2023 (ha) (Our World in Data, 2026a). It is used as an independently sourced
   proxy input, not treated as identical to the NFA cropland-area series.
  Crop-productivity sentinel. The OWID/FAOSTAT cereal-yield series, 1961–2024, is measured
   in tonnes per hectare (Our World in Data, 2026b). It is not an all-crops index and is not
   converted into a physical stock. The base-anchored b̂f is only the index-scaled term in the
   illustrative account proxy.
  Ecological-capital components. Forest extent, growing stock or biomass, soil carbon and con-
   dition, wetland extent and condition, and species- or stock-specific fish biomass could inform
   separate physical observation operators. They would require harmonized spatial boundaries,
   reference years, definitions, uncertainty, and explicit treatment of overlap. No comparable
   global physical-stock inventory for the relevant components is assembled here. Existing NFA
   forest biocapacity and the residual C are not substitutes for those measurements.
  Model mapping. In the model, Yc = Υc (q)Ac is a model-defined capital contribution. Em-
   pirically, neither Ac nor q is identified by B or by an NFA component; the proxy residual
   C = B − X is not assumed to equal Yc . A defensible observation operator would need inde-
   pendent physical measures and a declared mapping before a model-to-data comparison.

   Table S10 summarizes this mapping without equating an NFA category to a model state.

S5.5    Regeneration anchors
The capital-land regeneration timescale is a lumped recruitment-response timescale; the anchors
bound a band and do not calibrate ρc . A lagged saturating recovery model fitted to plot-level
age–above-ground-biomass records of Neotropical secondary forests (2ndFOR; Poorter et al.,
2016, 1,334 plots, 41 chronosequences, 35 good fits) gives a median t50 ≈ 30 yr and a recovery
timescale τr ≈ 38 yr; the same model fitted to deplete-then-rebuild trajectories of collapsed fish
stocks (RAM Legacy B/BM SY , 33 episodes) gives t50 ≈ 5.6 yr and τr ≈ 3.4 yr (cf. Hutchings
and Reynolds, 2004; Neubauer et al., 2013); soil organic carbon re-equilibrates after ≈ 17–23 yr
following land-use change (Poeplau et al., 2011). Forest biomass recovers to about half of old-
growth values by ≈ 20 yr and to 90% at a median ≈ 66 yr (Poorter et al., 2016) (the 66–95 yr
tail lies beyond the core band); only ≈ 29% of collapsed fish stocks recovered to 50% within 5–
15 yr (Hutchings and Reynolds, 2004). The two fitted legs bracket the effective timescale (forest
slow, fishery fast) with soil between them; the band is ≈ 25–33 yr (representative ≈ 29 yr; order
10–40 yr). These are statements about the regeneration rate, not the delay τg . In the one-stock
comparator the forest value lies at or above that model’s ≈ 18–20 yr cliff; in the two-land model
the drawdown boundary is a demand threshold and the anchors bound a band rather than locate
a cliff.

NFA Edition 2026 shows why the aggregate should be read together with its account composi-

modeled Ac declined. Built-up land is an NFA account class, not ecological capital; its inclusion
in the non-cropland sum is arithmetic, not ecological aggregation.

S5.7    Physical-stock scoreboard: specification, not inventory
No common-boundary global physical-stock inventory for the relevant components has been as-
sembled in this paper, and no physical-stock decline finding or composite is presented. This
limitation does not imply that the required source data could never be assembled. The score-
board in Table S12 is a specification for a future, separately sourced assessment only; it contains
no empirical endpoint values and is not a calibration of the model.
    Heterogeneous physical stocks cannot be integrated by simply summing their native units:
forest biomass or soil carbon (mass), wetland or cropland extent (area), fish biomass (stock by
species or stock), freshwater storage (volume), and condition indicators (often dimensionless or
categorical) are neither dimensionally commensurate nor necessarily spatially disjoint. Annual
flows such as harvest, catch, increment, recharge, or emissions must also remain distinct from
stocks and extents. Even after normalization into a common score, overlap, baselines, uncer-
tainty, ecological thresholds, valuation, and permitted substitution determine the interpretation.
Component measures therefore form a nonadditive vector unless common spatial and temporal
boundaries, normalization or valuation, and substitution rules are declared. A scalar composite
is not neutral and cannot be inferred from the NFA gha accounts. This distinction is consis-
tent with ecosystem-extent and ecosystem-condition accounts being reported separately from
ecosystem-service and monetary presentations in SEEA Ecosystem Accounting (United Nations
et al., 2021).
    In the two-land model, Ac is an effective area state (ha) and q is a separate capacity-quality
index. Neither is directly observed in the NFA aggregate. A physical-stock assessment could
inform a model observation operator only after its boundaries and mapping to those effective
states are specified. The exploratory residual C = B − X = B − b̂f Af is not ecological capital, a
measured non-cropland stock, or an estimate of Υc (q)Ac ; the NFA components remain account
results rather than physical-stock observations.

S6        Policy extensions
Half-Earth and reservation. The Half-Earth proposal (Wilson, 2016) caps human-usable
annual biocapacity account value (gha) at half of the total. In the two-land setting this is a
reservation E ≤ σB with dimensionless σ the human-available share, enforced either as a cap on
the fast book or on the carrying capacity; with σf , σc < 1 in Eq. (4) the shortfall is computed
against the reserved flows, so conversion begins earlier for a given demand and the ceiling of
Proposition 2 is lowered to σ-weighted values. On the nominal path a reservation that keeps
E ≤ σB succeeds with no liquidation. This is a nominal-path consequence until it is certified
against a declared disturbance class (persistent productivity, bf or ρc shocks) under a preregistered
retention rule (a governance module is retained only if it improves a declared protection-and-
supply score), with the model’s declared fit defects converted into an erosion margin on the closed
loop’s contraction rate; until then it is a property of the nominal calibration, not a robustness
claim.

Institutional signature. In the mobilizing/protective taxonomy of the delay companion

shortfall (Section S4.4).

Weak and strong sustainability reading. In the material-cycle reading of the ledger com-
panion (Abaee, 2026c), weak sustainability is the idealized regime in which substitution and
regeneration jointly redistribute matter so that it is used as it arises – substitution admissible


                                                    15
only through an identified physical pathway, never merely because a weighted aggregate permits
it – and strong sustainability is “the regime in which that closure fails – either because no iden-
tified physical pathway re-routes the extracted matter, or because depletion outruns the rate at
which such a pathway could be deployed”, so that a separately binding floor on a critical stock
binds. The composite annual account value B (gha) is the weak-sustainability index: the linear,
perfectly substitutable member of the aggregator family of the assessment companion (Lemma A
of Abaee, 2026b); it can satisfy its aggregate floor while the typed floor on Ac is violated by
exactly the amount that the fast-land yield gains against the capital loss, and in that family no
positive-elasticity aggregator is uniformly safe (its Theorem S2(ii)). Under exact-tube semantics
the intermediate passage during which Ac falls is itself a violation even if the endpoint recovers.

Reporting rule. A growth headline for total biocapacity carries no information about whether
cropland growth is compensating for stagnation or offsetting changes in the other components.
The informative assessment states, at every level at which an aggregate is used: component
endpoint ratios, log contributions, attribution shares, data vintage and endpoint sensitivity.

  MSY and fold. B(A) has an interior maximum (the MSY of Schaefer, 1954) only in the

  The +0.6 eigenvalue and the two-gain Hopf classification. The comparator’s deficit-region

S8     Controlled technology-wave experiment
This controlled extension tests whether an active Tb wave can produce a larger leaky-debt peak
than an otherwise matched no-wave run. The pair uses endogenous demand E = eP (so the
surplus-gated Rrc is active only when B > E), Atot = 2.5 ha, Af 0 = 0.6 ha, Ac0 = 0.9 ha,
q0 = 0.9, D0 = 0.0 gha yr, and P0 = 1.0 or 1.2 cap. Shared parameters are bf 0 = 0.85 gha ha−1 ,
bc = 0.05 gha ha−1 , bG,c = 0.8 gha yr ha−1 , ρc = 0.08 yr−1 , α = 0.03 gha−1 yr−1 , η = 0.05 yr−1 ,
ηf = 0.05 yr−1 , µ = 0.06 yr−1 , e = 0.55 gha cap−1 , r = 0.02 yr−1 , τg = 20 yr, τp = 25 yr, τconv =
1 yr, and χr = 0.10 gha−1 yr−1 . The wave parameters are ∆b = 1.0 gha ha−1 , κw = 0.1 yr−1 ,
and twave = 40 yr; the horizon is T = 250 yr. Within each pair, the physical initial state,
parameters, wave-free pre-run history, and solver are identical; only the Tb switch differs. The
logistic is already nonzero at t = 0 (Tb (0) = 0.017986 gha ha−1 ), so the derived initial account is
B(0) = 0.560184 gha without the wave and 0.570976 gha with it. This small initial difference is
part of the declared forcing, not a change to Af , Ac , q, P , or D; Tb reaches half its increment at
the marked midpoint twave = 40 yr.

with the wave, while the maximum over t ≥ twave is higher. Table S13 keeps these all-time
and post-midpoint definitions separate. We call t ≥ twave the post-midpoint window; it is not
an assertion that a logistic wave has ended at twave . The full metrics and trajectories are de-
