# Article 008 Formal-Content and Data Inventory

**Source:** `uploads/paper4_rev3.md`

## Proposed quantities

\[
\Omega_c
=
\frac{E_{\rm governance}}
     {E_{\rm system}},
\qquad
\alpha=\Omega_c\tau_d.
\]

As used in the table, both \(E\) quantities are USD/year rather than exergy. \(\alpha\) has units of time, not dimensionless units.

## Proposed threshold

\[
\alpha\ge1\text{ year}.
\]

Status: heuristic and unsupported; used inconsistently as necessary and sufficient.

## Proposed Tainter linkage

\[
\partial_{C_{inst}}U
=
\beta_{benefit}-\beta_{cost}.
\]

Status: stylized model; not verified as a formal Tainter result or dual to \(\alpha\).

## Proposed regulatory-capture index

\[
\mathrm{RCI}
=
\frac{\text{enforcement expenditure}}
     {\text{capture-attempt expenditure}+\epsilon}.
\]

Status: not operationally validated.
