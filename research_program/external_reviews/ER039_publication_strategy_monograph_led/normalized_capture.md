# ER039 Normalized Capture — Monograph-Led Publication Strategy Audit 1

## Receipt status

Complete substantive capture of the first of several announced publication-strategy audits. ER039 recommends a canonical flagship article, four-to-five problem-led specialist papers, a definitive monograph, and a versioned technical/reproducibility compendium.
