# ER039 Point Inventory — Preliminary, Pending Remaining Publication Audits

## Status

First of several publication-strategy audits. No final architecture implementation until the announced batch is jointly assessed with the internal recommendation.

## Strong points

1. Correctly distinguishes journal priority/claim validation from definitive full-system synthesis.
2. Correctly rejects both one oversized paper and one paper per source module.
3. Correctly treats the monograph as a designed primary product, not a leftovers repository.
4. Correctly requires a durable versioned compendium with concordance and machine artifacts.
5. Correctly frames specialist papers by mathematical/inferential problem rather than source provenance.
6. Correct article-independence test: distinct question, independent result, substantial specialized machinery/evidence, and distinct readership.
7. Correct publication sequence: preprint/concordance, flagship, specialist papers, compendium, then monograph.
8. Broadly consistent with the corrected five-assured-plus-A021 architecture.

## Points for joint scrutiny

1. The premise that every module instantiates one literal canonical equation is not yet established. The programme may require a typed canonical system or theorem schema encompassing flows, RFDEs, sampled maps, hybrid systems, PDE extensions, stochastic/information states, and normative constraints—not one undifferentiated differential equation.
2. “Monograph-led” should mean architecture-led, not necessarily monograph-published-first; ER039 itself correctly recommends journal priority first.
3. Conservation/viability/noncompensation may fit the formal-foundations paper, but final partition should be tested against length and audience after the canonical theorem map is complete.
4. The monograph and compendium count as substantial publication products; they must not be used to conceal unfinished theorem or reproducibility gates.
5. Domain/application chapters must remain fully preserved even when they do not yet merit standalone papers.

## Provisional disposition

Strongly retain the flagship + problem-led papers + monograph + compendium architecture. The decisive prerequisite is a canonical typed equation/schema and complete module concordance. Await remaining audits before final publication-plan implementation.
