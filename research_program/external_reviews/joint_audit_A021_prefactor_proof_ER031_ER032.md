# Joint Audit — ER031 and ER032 Product-Bunching Proof Roadmaps

## Decision

ER031 and ER032 are jointly retained as a correct roadmap, not as a proof. They agree that finite-discrete product bunching at 35–40 periods is strong evidence and that continuum operator error, validated Floquet data, projections/prefactors, concrete coupling, localization, and theorem matching remain required.

## Accepted

- direct operator-product bunching formulation;
- 30 periods marginal, 35 periods numerically closed, 40 periods preferred for validation margin;
- time-map graph transform without ambient inversion;
- separate base-map, derivative-transform, sampled-to-semiflow, and vertical-projection steps;
- in-tube rather than global attraction;
- no theorem promotion.

## Corrections

- orbit passport and floor margin are complete;
- an internal slack operator is selected and numerically characterized, though not source-derived A021 `G`;
- `M_c` is numerically estimated, not absent;
- a concrete numerical scaffold exists, but no proved NAIM;
- the numerical manuscript remark is already implemented;
- exact continuum validation remains open.

## Implementation consequence

No new manuscript change. The validated-numerics specification and Fourier seed prepared after ER031 are the controlling next-stage deliverables. Further formal graph-transform prose is deferred until the operator certificates exist.
