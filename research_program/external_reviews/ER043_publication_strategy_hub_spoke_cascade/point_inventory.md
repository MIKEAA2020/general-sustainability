# ER043 Point Inventory — Publication Strategy Audit 5

## Strong points

1. The Minimal Working Realization principle is a useful anti-duplication rule.
2. Audience segmentation and autonomous research questions are necessary anti-salami-slicing safeguards.
3. Separate computational artifacts for Papers 4 and 5 are required for independent reproducibility.
4. Staged submission is preferable to indiscriminate simultaneous journal submission.
5. Gate-dependent treatment of Papers 6–7 agrees with the controlling proof/validation hierarchy.
6. A later integrated monograph is the correct home for the uncompressed canonical narrative.

## Qualifications and rejected elements

1. “Hub” must describe navigation, not proof dependence. Papers 3–5 cannot outsource assumptions, existence, uniqueness, or model-specific validity needed for review to unpublished Papers 1–2.
2. A Minimal Working Realization must include local definitions, assumptions, interface proposition, status, and artifact pointer; it cannot be only an abbreviated equation plus citations.
3. Uploading all Papers 1–5 simultaneously is unsafe before each passes its own closure and reproducibility gate. A repository identifier is not automatically a DOI, and stable availability does not create theorem validity.
4. Waiting specifically until Paper 2 reaches minor revision is too rigid and outside author control. Release/submission waves should be triggered by internal closure and stable citable versions, not editorial stage alone.
5. A standard cross-reference footnote should be adapted to each paper and must not imply that foundational proofs can be omitted when they are required locally.
6. Candidate venues are provisional and need current scope/policy/length verification.
7. The suggested Paper 7 content remains conditional; it must not be presented as assured.

## Disposition

Retain MWR, autonomous-question, artifact-independence, staged-release, and monograph principles. Replace proof-dependent hub-and-spoke with a citation-closed federated series and readiness-triggered waves.
