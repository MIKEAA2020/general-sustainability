## Verdict

The yield-gap derivative estimate and finite-time small-coupling idea are useful. The claimed RFDE invariant graph is not established.

## Critical gaps

- No normal-versus-tangent domination condition.
- History domains are not automatically compact.
- The cited finite-dimensional Fenichel theorem is not matched to the RFDE semiflow.
- Graph argument notation is inconsistent and the transient estimate is poorly stated.

Demote invariant-graph and Hopf-persistence theorems until an exact RFDE persistence theorem and hypotheses are supplied. Supplement only; no separate paper.
