# Self-Contained Computation Closure Packet

## Purpose

Finish the programme's unresolved computational and computer-assisted proof obligations without changing claim status prematurely. Unlike the mathematical-architecture packet, this packet intentionally includes numerical seeds, coefficient exports, scripts, certificates, model/version records, and computational-review history.

## Priority order

1. A021 continuum periodic-orbit validation.
2. Continuum monodromy/Floquet/projection enclosure.
3. Product bunching, localization, and concrete coupling.
4. A025 fold validation and continuous-DDE transfer.
5. A022/A023 stage/spatial version reconciliation and independent result.
6. Reproducibility archives for A011/A012/A018/A020/A022–A025.

## Status discipline

Numerical convergence, finite interval arithmetic, source-attested truth, and a continuum proof are separate statuses. No theorem, fold, NAIM, or causal claim may be promoted until its exact gate closes.

Start with `10_MASTER_COMPUTATION_PROMPT.md`, use `11_COMPUTATION_DOCKET.md`, and return results in `12_REQUIRED_OUTPUT_SCHEMA.md`.
