# Archival Note: Rejected Multi-Domain Collapse Conjunction

## Status

This document preserves a rejected research branch. It is not a scientific-paper candidate and does not supply evidence for a universal collapse law.

## 1. Rejected claim

The abandoned branch attempted to combine physical stock limits, energetic thresholds, and institutional response timing into one cross-domain conjunction for irreversible collapse. It is rejected because the variables were not consistently typed, some component models were undefined, and the formula did not establish a causal bridge among domains.

## 2. Decisive reasons

1. Fast observation or control cannot regenerate a nonrenewable stock or reverse a physical impossibility.
2. Combining a stock-depletion timer with a controller condition does not by itself produce a nontrivial explanatory law.
3. The submitted EROI specialization collapsed to a trivial boundary under its own parameter choice.
4. The Tainter-inspired component contained undefined quantities and invalid dimensional operations.
5. A currency crisis was classified as civilizational collapse without a stable case definition.
6. Citation problems documented in the earlier audit prevent reliance on the submitted bibliography.

These points reject the original construction. Any future circulation would need the exact formula, self-contained counterexamples, and citation-level documentation.

## 3. Northern cod does not rescue the conjunction

Northern cod is not confirmation of the rejected formula. DFO’s M-shift formulation estimates high M during the collapse, but M is a model-conditional unobserved-death allocation and may absorb unreported fishing deaths. Alternative assessments allocate mortality differently. The date of the 1992 moratorium and annual biomass estimates also do not establish that governance was causally adequate.

The valid lesson is narrower: collapse and delayed recovery should be separated, and a fixed scalar autonomous model cannot exactly reproduce the full non-monotonic assessment path. Neither result supplies the rejected universal conjunction.

## 4. Material retained elsewhere

- The physical-obstruction counterexample belongs in the flagship antitheorem catalogue.
- The corrected scalar-autonomous cod result belongs in the A014 case record.
- Citation and model-definition failures remain in the audit archive.

## 5. Disposition

The multi-domain conjunction remains rejected. No separate publication is assigned. This note exists to prevent accidental revival or citation of the abandoned formula as an established result.
