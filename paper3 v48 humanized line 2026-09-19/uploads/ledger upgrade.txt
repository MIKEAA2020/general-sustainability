astra:
## The decisive upgrade: certify an actionable safety claim, not merely a better depletion indicator

Your strongest contribution can become a **proof-carrying sustainability assurance framework**:

> Given a declared boundary, evidence base, model, uncertainty class, ecological constraints, and essential-service requirements, determine whether a **common, implementable policy** can preserve all requirements—and identify the intervention windows, responsibilities, and measurements needed to keep that claim valid.

The ledger becomes the evidence substrate. The maintainability kernel becomes the decision logic. The public product becomes a **time-limited, auditable action contract**.

The most important missing step is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Componentwise adequacy}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\text{jointly feasible, evidence-supported, institutionally implementable safety}
}
\]

Below are nine upgrades that make this transition substantive rather than rhetorical.

---

## 1. Upgrade the Claim Passport into a proof-carrying assurance contract

A passport classifies a statement. A contract specifies **exactly what follows from which evidence, under which assumptions, and who must do what to preserve it**.

### Add six fields beyond the existing passport

| Additional field | What it prevents |
|---|---|
| **Exact proposition and quantifiers** | Confusing “a safe scenario exists” with “this policy is safe against all declared disturbances” |
| **Common policy or intervention-portfolio ID** | Certifying different components under mutually incompatible policies |
| **Evidence sufficiency and identification status** | Treating a precisely estimated surrogate as an identified physical stock |
| **Approximation direction and numerical assurance** | Treating an optimistic relaxation or sampled simulation as a safety proof |
| **Assumption dependencies and invalidation triggers** | Reusing a certificate after its data, policy, or operating conditions have changed |
| **Decision authority, implementation obligation, and review mechanism** | Producing recommendations that nobody is authorized, funded, or accountable to implement |

### Make status multidimensional

Your proposed status list mixes different concepts. “Record-relative,” “conditional,” “certified,” and “quarantined” are not mutually exclusive alternatives.

Use separate fields:

- **Semantic kind:** turnover ratio, frozen-rate ratio, record-relative index, scenario hitting time.
- **Verification result:** established within scope, refuted within scope, undetermined, not applicable.
- **Evidence basis:** measurement, accounting reconciliation, model calculation, formal verification, empirical validation.
- **Operational applicability:** active, expired, superseded, withdrawn, outside declared conditions.

A record-relative index can be correctly computed and verified while remaining entirely non-predictive.

### Make semantic upgrades explicit, not automatic

For example:

| Inputs | Legitimate output type | Missing evidence for a stronger claim |
|---|---|---|
| Anomaly scale and anomaly trend | Record-relative persistence diagnostic | Absolute anchor or other identifying information |
| Absolute stock, fixed barrier, negative current net trend | Frozen-rate barrier ratio | Future dynamics and policy scenario |
| Dynamic model, initial-state information, barrier, scenario | Scenario-conditioned hitting-time output | Model validation and uncertainty characterization |

A useful compiler error would be:

> **Cannot construct an exhaustion forecast from an anomaly-persistence index. Missing: identified absolute state or a validated alternative state-to-barrier model.**

This is more powerful than attaching a disclaimer after publication: it prevents an unsupported semantic conversion.

### Retain distinct proof obligations

Keep at least four named obligations:

1. Accounting consistency.
2. Moiety conservation where applicable.
3. Positivity and donor feasibility.
4. Satisfaction of declared safety requirements.

Do not force every ledger variable into the same conservation ontology:

- Physical inventories may obey material balances.
- Quality and age structure require additional dynamics.
- Biodiversity attributes are not generally conserved quantities.
- Services are production-and-access readouts.
- Financial values follow accounting and valuation rules, not material conservation.

Also encode **containment and disjointness**, not just units. Total biomass and spawning biomass may share units while overlapping. Reserves may be a classified subset of resources. Adding either pair can double-count without any dimensional error.

**Human payoff:** a reader can distinguish a correct calculation, a supported forecast, an actionable guarantee, and a legally authorized decision.

## 2. Strengthen noncompensation into **joint, noncompensatory assurance**

Two mathematical corrections substantially improve defensibility.

### A. The principle should be anti-compensation, not anti-scalar

The statement “no scalar can certify componentwise adequacy” is too strong.

Let \(g_i\) be dimensionless adequacy margins, with safety requiring \(g_i\ge0\). A positive weighted criterion

\[
\sum_i w_i g_i\ge0
\]

does not generally establish all requirements. With two independently variable margins, choose

\[
g_1=-\varepsilon,
\qquad
g_2=\frac{w_1}{w_2}\varepsilon.
\]

The score passes while the first requirement fails.

But

\[
\min_i g_i\ge0
\]

is exactly equivalent to componentwise adequacy. A Boolean “all requirements passed” can also be legitimate.

The rigorous principle is:

> **Independent adequacy requirements cannot generally be certified through compensating weighted surpluses. They must be checked individually or through a logically equivalent noncompensatory test.**

Special restricted domains can admit conservative weighted sufficient conditions, so a universal impossibility claim needs explicit domain assumptions.

This distinction also permits legitimate cost optimization **after** safety feasibility has been established.

### B. Componentwise feasibility is not joint feasibility

The deeper missing theorem is the quantifier distinction:

\[
\forall i\;\exists \pi_i:\text{requirement }i\text{ is satisfied}
\]

does not imply

\[
\exists \pi\;\forall i:\text{all requirements are satisfied}.
\]

Suppose one service requirement needs normalized operating effort \(u\ge0.7\), while an ecological requirement needs \(u\le0.3\). Both are individually feasible. Their intersection is empty.

A dashboard can therefore show individually defensible green rows while **no common policy supports the entire dashboard**.

Require every joint assurance claim to reference:

- The same policy.
- The same scenario and disturbance assumptions.
- The same shared capacities and budgets.
- Compatible boundary transfers.
- The same time horizon.

### C. Joint risk needs its own treatment

“Each component has 95% probability of remaining safe” does not establish 95% probability that **all components remain safe throughout the horizon**.

The relevant event is:

\[
\Pr\!\left(
g_i(z_t,u_t,t)\ge0
\;\text{for every }i,\ t\in[0,H]
\mid D,M,\sigma
\right).
\]

Report this joint pathwise probability, or an explicit allocation of component failure-risk bounds whose sum meets the adopted joint risk limit.

**Human payoff:** the framework prevents both surplus-based greenwashing and incompatible-policy greenwashing.

## 3. Make maintainability institutional, information-aware, and action-timed

The maintainability kernel should not operate on physical stocks alone.

Use an augmented state containing:

- Physical inventories.
- Quality, spatial distribution, and functional condition.
- Infrastructure and production capacity.
- Projects under construction and their remaining lead times.
- Institutional permissions and operational constraints.
- Information available to the decision-maker.

Let \(\mathcal I_0\) represent the declared set of state–parameter combinations consistent with available evidence. A finite-horizon operational kernel is:

\[
\mathfrak K_H=
\left\{
\mathcal I_0:
\exists\pi\in\Pi_{\mathrm{causal,implementable}}
\ \forall(z_0,\theta)\in\mathcal I_0
\ \forall w\in\mathcal W,
\quad
g_j(z_t^\pi,u_t^\pi,t)\ge0
\quad\forall j,\ t\in[0,H]
\right\}.
\]

Here:

- The policy uses only information available at the time.
- The disturbance class is declared, not “all conceivable futures.”
- Implementability includes capacity, authority, financing, ramp rates, and deployment delays.
- Requirements include ecological constraints and essential-service access.

This exposes two important governance errors.

**First:** a kernel that permits instant termination of essential water, food, or energy services overstates governability.

**Second:** a kernel that treats all market demand as an inviolable service floor understates feasible transitions and can encode existing inequality.

Use explicitly justified **essential-service floors**, not unrestricted willingness-to-pay demand.

### Separate technical and institutional feasibility

With otherwise identical assumptions,

\[
\Pi_{\mathrm{implementable}}
\subseteq
\Pi_{\mathrm{technical}}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\mathfrak K_{\mathrm{implementable}}
\subseteq
\mathfrak K_{\mathrm{technical}}.
\]

The difference identifies a **governance gap**: a technically possible safe future that current authority, funding, coordination, or response speed cannot deliver.

### Do not infer indefinite maintainability from a finite simulation

Remaining safe through \(H\) proves a finite-horizon claim.

An indefinite claim additionally requires, for example, reaching a terminal set with a verified continuation that remains safe under the declared future conditions. Monte Carlo survival over a finite horizon is not an infinite-horizon viability certificate.

### Replace “distance to the kernel” with feasible intervention windows

Euclidean distance between unlike state variables is coordinate-dependent and often unhelpful. More useful outputs are:

- Feasible intervention start-time windows.
- Latest safe commitment time.
- Maximum admissible review delay.
- Required extraction reduction.
- Minimum replacement capacity.
- Conditions under which the safe option disappears.

Report a single deadline only when earlier initiation is demonstrably no worse; otherwise report the feasible start-time intervals.

### A worked example

Assume, purely illustratively:

- Stored water: \(100\ \mathrm{Mm^3}\).
- Required floor: \(60\ \mathrm{Mm^3}\).
- Constant net drawdown without intervention: \(5\ \mathrm{Mm^3/yr}\).
- A verified alternative supply takes three years to commission.
- After commissioning, it maintains essential services and eliminates net drawdown.
- All other constraints are satisfied.

| Quantity | Value | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Frozen net-rate ratio to zero | 20 years | A zero-stock arithmetic ratio |
| Frozen net-rate ratio to the declared floor | 8 years | Also a hitting time under this particular constant-rate, no-action model |
| Latest safe initiation of the alternative supply | 5 years | Eight-year buffer minus three-year deployment lag |

At a stock of \(75\ \mathrm{Mm^3}\), the system remains physically above its floor, but further delay removes the safe intervention option.

**Human payoff:** the central message changes from “eight years remaining” to “this intervention must begin within five years, under these assumptions.”

## 4. Turn infeasibility into **transition-necessity certificates**

A red dashboard is not enough. When no admissible policy satisfies the declared requirements, the engine should explain **what must change**.

Useful outputs include:

- An irreducible conflict set of requirements and assumptions.
- A lower bound on required new capacity.
- A necessary reduction in nonessential demand.
- A minimum efficiency improvement.
- A necessary shortening of deployment or review delays.
- A missing substitution pathway.

### A simple necessity theorem

Consider a finite, nonrenewing support stock \(S\), with floor \(B\).

Suppose:

- \(d(t)\) is required service.
- \(c(t)\) is the maximum service deliverable through the declared nondepleting pathways.
- At least \(\rho\) units of the finite support stock are required per remaining service unit.

A necessary condition for feasibility through \(H\) is:

\[
\rho\int_0^H[d(t)-c(t)]_+\,dt
\le
S(0)-B.
\]

This does not establish sufficiency: timing, infrastructure, other support pools, and quality constraints can make the problem harder.

But failure of this inequality produces a constructive statement:

> Under the declared pathways and intensity bound, the required service cannot be maintained without exceeding the permitted drawdown. At least this much replacement capacity, efficiency improvement, or reduction in nonessential demand is necessary.

If the residual requirement stays strictly positive indefinitely, an infinite-horizon kernel is empty under these assumptions.

This also clarifies why “no interior rest” and extraction-integrability results need explicit nonrenewal and boundary-flow hypotheses. They are not automatic consequences of ledger typing.

### Do not hide infeasibility with penalties

An optimizer must not silently trade away an ecological requirement or an essential-service floor because its numerical penalty is cheaper.

Where requirements conflict, return the conflict and the feasible repair alternatives. Any authorized change to requirements must remain visible as a **change of requirements**, not an improvement in system performance.

**Human payoff:** “unsafe” becomes an actionable statement about necessary transition conditions rather than an unexplained verdict.

## 5. Certify what cannot be inferred—and distinguish loss of safety from loss of assurance

The observation layer should produce **identifiability certificates**, not just posterior intervals.

### A groundwater non-identifiability result

Consider an anomaly-only observation model:

\[
y_t=S_t-c+\varepsilon_t,
\]

where \(c\) is an unknown absolute reference.

The transformation

\[
S_t'=S_t+a,
\qquad
c'=c+a
\]

leaves the observation model unchanged.

Absent an independent absolute anchor or additional validated dynamics that break this symmetry, the observations cannot identify the absolute stock-to-barrier distance.

More precise anomaly measurements or a longer anomaly record do not, by themselves, solve this problem.

Thus, if “9.5 years” was obtained from an anomaly-scale/trend ratio, it cannot become an absolute exhaustion forecast through relabeling. A narrow posterior could instead reflect a restrictive prior that supplied the missing absolute information.

### Avoid requiring the wrong quantity

Absolute basin storage is not always necessary for a useful decision.

If the operational failure is:

- A well-head threshold.
- Salinization.
- Compaction.
- Loss of affordable pumping.
- Failure of a community water service.

Then a validated model of that observable-to-barrier relationship may support a useful intervention decision without identifying total aquifer volume.

This is an important human-usefulness upgrade:

> **Measure the state needed for the decision, not the state demanded by an inherited depletion metaphor.**

### Do not manufacture closure during assimilation

Hard conservation does not mean hard observations.

Allow noisy measurements, bias, and unresolved typed flows. Report the residual or missing route and its admissible bounds. Do not insert an unconstrained “other flow” merely to make the books balance and then treat it as observed reality.

Likewise, probabilities require declared observation and process laws. Conservation equations alone do not identify a stochastic model.

### Distinguish three events

1. **A requirement is physically violated.**
2. **No policy guarantees compliance across the declared uncertainty set.**
3. **Available evidence no longer supports certification.**

These are not equivalent.

Leaving a robust assurance set means a guarantee has been lost; it does not necessarily mean every future is doomed.

### Make monitoring decision-relevant

Choose measurements by their ability to change a decision:

- Distinguish policies requiring incompatible actions.
- Resolve an unidentified barrier distance.
- Test an assumption close to invalidating the certificate.
- Separate recoverable from irreversible damage.
- Detect a missing material route.

Include measurement delay, cost, and data rights. Reducing uncertainty is not valuable if waiting for the measurement destroys the remaining safe option.

**Human payoff:** uncertainty becomes an actionable diagnosis, not a vague confidence score or a reason for paralysis.

## 6. Replace simple yield shares with **critical-support feasibility and non-displacement accounting**

The proposed decomposition

\[
Y=Y_{\rm regenerative}+Y_{\rm drawdown}
+Y_{\rm substitution}+Y_{\rm import}
\]

is generally not a valid partition.

Imports may be regenerative or drawdown-supported. A substitute may be imported. A recycled input may still depend on depleting energy, water, or infrastructure-support stocks.

These describe overlapping axes.

### Use orthogonal provenance and support records

For homogeneous outputs where attribution is defensible, distinguish:

- **Origin:** domestic, imported, transit origin.
- **Material pathway:** virgin, reused, recycled, transformed.
- **Critical-support condition:** renewal-supported, drawdown-dependent, unresolved.
- **Functional quality:** usable quality, contamination, accessibility.
- **Time basis:** operating period, seasonal cycle, transition horizon.

A crop can simultaneously depend on:

- Groundwater drawdown.
- Renewable solar energy.
- Imported phosphorus.
- Soil deterioration.
- Uncertain pollination support.

Those dependencies are not additive fractions of the crop.

Also, temporary seasonal storage drawdown is not necessarily liquidation. The classification depends on recovery feasibility, timing, and barrier compliance over the relevant cycle.

### Add an all-support production question

Instead of claiming a universal “78% regenerative yield,” ask:

> What quantity of this specified, quality-qualified output can be supported through the declared horizon while every critical input, sink, and service requirement remains feasible under a common policy?

This is a **supportable-output envelope**, not an attribution percentage and not an unconditional sustainability guarantee.

### Make substitution a joint pathway certificate

An admissible substitution must demonstrate:

- A real production and delivery pathway.
- Compatible inputs and outputs.
- Shared energy and material capacity.
- Construction and replacement requirements.
- Deployment timing.
- Quality and service equivalence.
- Compliance with donor-region, sink, and distributional constraints.

The rule should not be “no stock may ever decline.” Managed transitional drawdown can be compatible with declared floors and a verified safe continuation.

The stronger, more useful rule is:

> **No intervention may claim success by transferring an unreported violation to another stock, sink, place, period, or population.**

### Keep conservation and functionality distinct

Mass conservation does not establish:

- Energy feasibility.
- Thermodynamic admissibility.
- Exergy preservation.
- Biodiversity persistence.
- Service accessibility.

Exergy is not a conserved material quantity; its accounting requires a declared environmental reference and dissipation treatment.

Reserve reclassification is another important case: price or regulatory changes can alter economic reserve designations without creating or destroying geological material.

### Add an additionality gate for improvement claims

A larger recycling flow does not, by itself, establish reduced primary extraction. Demand, rebound, quality, displacement, and operating decisions matter.

An “avoided depletion” claim needs:

- A declared counterfactual.
- Matching system boundaries.
- Treatment of leakage and rebound.
- Appropriate causal evidence—or an explicit label saying it is only a modelled scenario contrast.

Similarly, do not mix domestic **extraction** with imported embodied **net depletion** and call the result an accounting identity. Responsibility accounts must use consistently defined burdens. Production and consumption responsibility are alternative attribution views, not additional physical stocks.

**Human payoff:** circularity and substitution become credible transition pathways rather than mechanisms for moving depletion out of view.

## 7. Build a proof-producing compiler, not a universal optimizer

The reference architecture should be:

\[
\text{Typed evidence}
\rightarrow
\text{reconciled ledger}
\rightarrow
\text{admissible scenarios}
\rightarrow
\text{common-policy synthesis}
\rightarrow
\text{independent verification}
\rightarrow
\text{action contract}.
\]

### The engine needs several distinct output types

1. **Verified conditional bound.**
2. **Validated possibility-of-violation witness.**
3. **Policy-feasibility or infeasibility result.**
4. **Missing-information or non-identifiability diagnosis.**
5. **Necessary-transition or repair certificate.**
6. **Outside-scope or unsupported-approximation result.**

A solver returning a number is not itself a certification outcome.

### Be precise about LP and convex certificates

For a declared policy and a valid trajectory polytope \(P\), an affine requirement can be bounded using

\[
\underline g_{j,k}
=
\min_{\xi\in P}g_j(\xi_k).
\]

A verified dual lower bound of at least zero can certify the corresponding requirement over that modelled set.

But the scope must be explicit:

- A one-period flux envelope is not a whole-trajectory proof.
- Grid-point safety is not continuous-time safety without inter-step bounds.
- Nonlinear dynamics do not become an LP merely by writing conservation equations.
- Scenario-dependent controls require **nonanticipativity constraints**: the policy cannot use future information.
- A violating path in an outer relaxation may be spurious and requires validation against the declared dynamics.
- A negative lower bound does not establish inevitable failure.
- Numerical tolerances and rounding must be included in the verification argument.

Use an independent checker for certificates where practical. The optimizer proposes; the checker verifies the supported proposition.

### Make certificates composable—but not circular

A multi-region or supply-chain module can publish an interface contract containing:

- Typed boundary transfers.
- Capacity and quality envelopes.
- Timing commitments.
- Assumptions about upstream supply.
- Downstream guarantees.

Composition requires matched interfaces and jointly satisfiable assumptions.

A water plant cannot assume unlimited renewable electricity while the electricity supplier assumes unlimited cooling water from that same plant, without checking the coupled system.

Recycling loops are legitimate. **Circular assurance without verified initial inventories, losses, capacities, and joint feasibility is not.**

### Add certificate dependency management

Every active certificate should depend on versioned:

- Data.
- Model.
- Barrier declarations.
- Policy.
- Disturbance assumptions.
- Upstream contracts.

Changes trigger targeted re-verification or an applicability flag. Historical certificates remain in the audit trail; their current applicability may expire.

Cryptographic signatures establish provenance, not truth. Formal proofs establish consequences of assumptions, not the empirical truth or ethical legitimacy of those assumptions.

**Human payoff:** the system can explain why a claim was valid, why it ceased to apply, and what must be repaired.

---

## 8. Make the governance object a public, contestable action contract

Separate four responsibilities:

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Data and model steward | Evidence quality, model applicability, unresolved uncertainty |
| Barrier and service-floor authority | Legal or normative basis, affected groups, review and appeal |
| Policy implementer | Authority, funding, delivery, monitoring, response obligations |
| Independent verifier | Reproducibility, inference validity, certificate scope, audit trail |

Scientific failure-threshold estimates and adopted policy limits should have separate records.

A policy authority can revise a legal limit. It cannot change a physical failure mechanism merely by relabeling it. Conversely, scientific modelling alone cannot settle distributional legitimacy.

### Replace the enormous dashboard as the primary interface

Keep the detailed dashboard for experts. Give managers and the public a one-page decision card:

1. **What is established—and within what scope?**
2. **Which common policy supports the claim?**
3. **What are the feasible intervention windows?**
4. **Which assumptions or measurements are decision-critical?**
5. **Whose essential services and burdens are affected?**
6. **Who must act, with what authority and resources?**
7. **What triggers review, withdrawal, or appeal?**

“Unknown” must not automatically become “safe,” “unsafe,” or grounds for punitive treatment of a poorly monitored community.

Also maintain a **scope-gap register**. A certificate covering five assessed support pools does not establish the absence of an unmodelled sixth dependency.

**Human payoff:** the framework supports accountability without laundering political choices into technical certainty.

## 9. Establish novelty through decision performance, not breadth or “first” claims

The individual ingredients have substantial antecedents in material-flow accounting, industrial ecology, environmental-economic accounting, viability theory, formal methods, and metrology.

The defensible novelty is their **explicit, executable connection**:

> A quantifier-aware interface from typed sustainability evidence to jointly implementable policy assurance, intervention windows, constructive necessity results, and revisable public accountability.

Avoid “first” until a systematic prior-art comparison supports it.

### Use the three pilots to test different failure modes

| Pilot | Distinctive test | Decision-ready output |
|---|---|---|
| **Groundwater** | Anomalies versus identified states; local head, quality, compaction, and service constraints | Safe pumping and project-initiation windows; critical next measurement |
| **Phosphorus** | Physical material versus reserve designation; recovery quality; circularity versus additionality | Feasible recovery portfolio; primary-input requirements; sink constraints |
| **Fisheries** | Pressure indices versus demographic trajectories; incompatible harvest rules; assessment delays | Jointly feasible harvest policy; review timing; scenario-conditioned biological and service risks |

Start operationally with one bounded groundwater pilot. Use phosphorus and fisheries to test portability, not as simultaneous commitments to three full operational systems.

### Build an adversarial conformance benchmark

Include cases where:

- Mass balances but a donor becomes negative.
- Mass is conserved but function becomes inaccessible.
- An absolute stock is unidentified despite precise anomaly data.
- A total and its subset are counted twice.
- Every component is individually feasible but no common policy exists.
- Every marginal risk looks acceptable but joint pathwise risk is not.
- A deployment lag destroys the safe option before a physical floor is reached.
- Recycling or imports transfer an unreported shortfall.
- National service averages conceal an essential-service deficit.

A credible engine should distinguish these cases, including when the correct result is **undetermined**.

### Evaluate human and operational outcomes

Compare against current sector best practice, not only obviously misused stock/flow ratios.

Measure:

- False reassurance.
- Unnecessary restrictions.
- Correct understanding of time-like indicators.
- Identification of feasible interventions.
- Decision and implementation lead time gained.
- Audit time and data burden.
- Calibration of probabilistic outputs, including censoring.
- Distribution of service shortfalls and transition burdens.

Use historical information as it was available at the decision date; otherwise retrospective tests can quietly benefit from hindsight.

### Narrow the first release

Prioritize:

1. Claim and quantifier grammar.
2. Joint, implementable finite-horizon viability.
3. Intervention windows and necessity certificates.
4. Independent verification and constructive failure diagnosis.
5. One operational pilot with decision-performance evaluation.

Treat broad financial valuation, general safe RL, a global dashboard, and multiple proof-assistant implementations as later extensions.

For interoperability, reuse established unit and provenance conventions rather than inventing another complete data universe.

---

## Revised central thesis

> **Sustainability is not established by a depletion number, a balanced account, or a collection of individually favourable indicators. Within an explicitly declared scope, it requires typed evidence and a common implementable policy capable of preserving physical, functional, and essential-service requirements under declared uncertainty. The central outputs are auditable intervention windows, necessary transition conditions, and transparent limits to knowledge—not a compensatory score or an unconditional exhaustion date.**

A strong title would be:

### **From Depletion Numbers to Actionable Sustainability Assurance: Typed Evidence, Joint Viability, and Safe Intervention Windows**

The resulting human-facing promise is clear:

**What can still be protected, by which feasible actions, started when, for whose benefit, on what evidence, and under whose responsibility?**

# Typed Depletion Ledgers v2 — an upgrade memo

## 0. Where v1 stops

v1 is right about the four things that matter: predicates must be separated, scalars cannot certify, "time to depletion" is several quantities, and safety is about staying governable, not about hitting zero. It stops short in eight places, and each stopping point is where a profound upgrade lives:

1. **"Typed" still means "units."** Dimensional analysis already exists. The ledger needs a *resource* type theory in which mass is linear (used exactly once) and money is not.
2. **Noncompensation is a purely negative result.** It says what no scalar can do, not what the one legitimate scalar is, nor when compensation *is* admissible.
3. **Time-like quantities are separated by label, not by theorems.** Nothing yet says which of them are provable bounds, which are extrapolations, and which are comparable.
4. **One kernel, where governance needs three horizons** — barrier crossing, loss of governability, loss of recoverability — plus a resilience radius and a ratio against institutional delay.
5. **Barriers are fixed boxes.** Real barriers are uncertain, contested, coupled, and revised; certificates never expire.
6. **The assimilation layer has no identifiability gate**, which is exactly how a GRACE anomaly gets laundered into a "9.5 years" forecast.
7. **The standard is not composable or game-resistant.** No interface reconciliation, no closed-moiety rule, no ratchet, no provenance monotonicity.
8. **Thirty-four modules, no generating core, no adoption hook.** Impact requires that the ledger plug into SEEA, disclosure regimes, and reporting guidelines rather than compete with them.

The rest of this memo fixes these in order of depth.

## 2. The upgrades

### A. Mass is linear, money is not (P1)

**Types.** `Stock[μ; c; q; j; t]` — moiety μ (P atoms, N, C, H₂O, individuals), compartment c, quality/accessibility class q, jurisdiction j, time t. `Flow[μ; c→c'; …]`. `Anomaly[μ; c; baseline]`. `Service[s; group g]`. `Claim[currency]` (money). `Capital[…]` (wells, pumps, ships). `Index[…]`.

**Linear discipline.** For conserved moieties, every `Flow` term is consumed *exactly once* in the balance system. Then:

- **Contraction** (a flow used twice) *is* double counting / duplicated recovery credit / phantom mass.
- **Weakening** (a flow dropped) *is* a ghost sink / missing yield fraction / omitted boundary flow.

The Phantom Mass Audit stops being a checklist and becomes a type checker with a soundness theorem: *a well-typed ledger has zero residual in every natural-block mass identity.*

**Coercions are morphisms with obligations**, never free:

- `Anomaly[μ;c;baseline] → Stock[μ;c]` requires a `Datum` certificate (saturated volume × storage coefficient, for groundwater). Without it, the "9.5 years" number is *typed out* of hitting-time status automatically — no human judgment needed.
- `Reserve → Resource` requires an economic-classification declaration (price, technology, cutoff grade). Reserve-life ratios inherit that type.
- `Stock[q] → Stock[q']` (ore grade, recyclate class, aquifer depth) requires a quality morphism with energy/material accounting; "100% recycled" claims must show class preservation or declare downcycling.
- `Biomass → Carbon` conserves C, not biomass (metabolism is a typed sink).
- `Capital` never coerces to `Stock`: a well is not water.
- `Claim` has no conservation coercion at all (credit creation).

**Predicate applicability matrix** (new artifact, one page, more useful than most of the 34 modules):

| Object kind | Balance | Conservation | Positivity | Barrier | Functional | Kernel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Element moiety (P, N, H₂O) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Biomass / population | ✓ | ✗ (C only) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Biodiversity, species | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ (extinction = 0) | ✓ | as support quality | ✓ |
| Service balance sᵢ−dᵢ | ✓ | ✗ | floor | ✓ | — | ✓ |
| Money | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ | readout only | — | ✗ |
| Anomaly / index | — | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ |

A claim that applies a ✗ predicate is a *type error*, publicly reportable as such.

### B. Time-like quantities as dependent types with a comparability theorem (P3)

Write every time-like quantity as `T[pred; A; B; σ]`: predicate, assumption set A, barrier B, scenario σ. **Two such quantities are comparable only if pred and B coincide and A is nested (or σ identical).** This is the formal content of "numbers sharing a label answer different questions."

The three tiers become three *epistemic kinds*, not three levels of a hierarchy of sophistication:

1. **Bounds** — certifiable from sign/monotonicity assumptions alone, no model needed. Gross turnover S/Y is a *provable lower bound* on exhaustion time (B = 0) under frozen extraction and nonnegative regeneration. The fisheries pressure scale Θ = ln(SSB/B_lim)/F is a *provable lower bound* on time to B_lim under constant F provided surplus production is nonnegative on [B_lim, SSB]. Data-poor users can certify these today.
2. **Extrapolations** — frozen-net-rate ratios (S−B)/[−Ṡ]₊. Bounded in neither direction. Status: descriptive/conditional, never certified.
3. **Scenario distributions** — conditional on declared σ and priors; may be *defective* (positive mass at "never"). **"Median years left" is undefined when P(never within horizon) > ½**; report P(hit by H) and the censored fraction, not medians.

**Ordering results worth stating explicitly** (they are short, and the punchline is the point):

- With B = 0 and R ≥ 0: T_gross ≤ T_frozen. **With B > 0 no ordering exists** — which is precisely why the two must not share a label.
- **Flux-polytope bracketing**: for any scenario whose fluxes are admissible, T_scn ∈ [T_earliest, T_latest], both computed by LP over the admissible envelope (linear relaxations of nonlinear regeneration give *sound* brackets). Models produce points; the LP produces certified brackets around them.

**Record-relative type.** A GRACE trend divided into a GRACE anomaly is `Index[anomaly-persistence; baseline 2004–09]`. It has time units and is not a hitting time. Type, not judgment.

### C. Certification Aggregation Theorem and admissible compensation (P2)

**Theorem.** f: ℝ^M → ℝ certifies box safety at level c (f(x) ≥ c ⇒ x ≥ B) iff the superlevel set {f ≥ c} ⊆ B + ℝ^M₊. For M ≥ 2 no half-space is contained in a shifted orthant, so **no weighted sum with any weights certifies**. The canonical certifier is the **Liebig scalar** ℓ(x) = min_m (x_m − B_m)/s_m, reported *with the name of the binding component*.

**Corollary (alarm/certificate asymmetry).** With positive weights and threshold equal to the weighted barrier, an aggregate *deficit* does imply at least one component deficit. Weighted aggregates are therefore **valid alarms and invalid certificates**. This rehabilitates footprint-type indices for one purpose and forbids them for the other — a sharper and more persuasive position than "no aggregates."

**Compensation is admissible exactly along certified substitution morphisms** (item 7). Weak vs strong sustainability stops being a philosophical stance and becomes a property of the substitution hypergraph: a moiety with no admissible substitution edge is *forced strong*. Phosphorus is the canonical example — there is no edge, so the ledger forces strong sustainability without anyone having to argue for it.

Dashboards therefore get exactly one certified scalar (ℓ and its binding component); everything else is non-certifying communication.

### D. Three horizons, a radius, and a ratio (P5)

Let C be the declared safe set, K = Viab(C) the maintainability kernel, and R_H = Capt_H(K) the set from which K can be re-entered within horizon H under maximal admissible effort. K ⊆ C and K ⊆ R_H.

- **T_C**: barrier crossing (physical/functional).
- **T_K**: loss of governability — still safe, but no admissible policy keeps it so.
- **T_R**: **irreversibility horizon** — leaving R_H. Aquifer compaction, salinization, depensatory recruitment collapse, soil carbon loss below the aggregate-stability threshold all move x out of R_H *without* crossing C. This is where "recoverable vs non-recoverable drawdown" gets a formal home.

**Resilience radius** ρ_K(x) = inf{‖w‖ : disturbance w ejects x from K} — computed by robust viability; a distance in disturbance space, dual to "required effort reduction" in control space.

**Governability ratio** G_m = T_K,m / (τ + h), with τ the institutional response delay and h the review interval. **If G < 1, no review-based governance can succeed even with perfect decisions; automatic triggers are mandatory.** Report P(G < 1) from the posterior. As a linearized sanity check for a deviation dynamics with instability rate a, proportional feedback under total dead time τ_tot is stabilizable only if a·τ_tot < 1 (PD: < 2); sampling adds ≈ h/2 to dead time. Illustratively, a = 0.2 yr⁻¹, τ = 1.5 yr, h = 5 yr gives a(τ + h/2) = 0.8 — a fishery governed at the edge. The operational test is viability with the delay augmented into the state, but the rule of thumb is what a minister will remember.

### E. Identifiability gate and typed noise (P6)

Add to every passport an **identifiability status** for each parameter behind the claim: structurally identified / practically identified from declared data streams / jointly identified only / unidentified. Non-identified rates cannot support certified ratios. GRACE alone identifies ΔS, never S; a water balance identifies recharge and pumping only as a difference until pumping records or a recharge model enter. This one field prevents most groundwater mislabeling mechanically.

**Typed noise.** Process noise on conserved moieties must be *transfer noise* (zero-sum across pools or an explicit boundary term); observation noise is unrestricted. A diffusion term that creates mass is a type error. This is the precise meaning of "observation surrogate, not stochastic completion."

### F. Barriers and certificates as first-class, uncertain, expiring, ratcheted objects (P4)

**Barrier passport**: type (exhaustion, functional, resilience, accessibility floor, service minimum, sink ceiling, equity floor); basis — *empirical, model-derived, or normative* (normative barriers are legitimate but must be labeled as decisions with a responsible institution, not as discoveries); distribution and the precaution quantile in use; review interval; revision conditions; **ratchet rule**: barriers may tighten at any review, loosen only with new evidence plus public comment; change log.

**Certificate object**: the dual of the certification program (linear Farkas certificate for linear envelopes, sum-of-squares barrier certificate for nonlinear regeneration), with validity domain, assumptions, and **expiry** — the next review or the first trigger. A violation witness is a concrete admissible trajectory and doubles as the dashboard's stress scenario.

**Safe sets need not be boxes** (N:P ratios, size-structure thresholds, coupled sink ceilings). Allow declared semialgebraic safe sets, and declare the trade-off: boxes are maximally auditable, coupled constraints are more realistic; the choice is a passport field.

**Corridor emptiness witness.** When physical barriers and service/equity floors cannot be jointly satisfied, the program is infeasible and the Farkas witness names *which floors conflict with which barriers*. Political trade-offs become explicit, auditable conflicts instead of hidden weights. This is arguably the single most valuable governance output of the whole system.

### G. Composability and anti-gaming (P6, P7)

- **Interface reconciliation predicate**: ledger A's outflow to B equals B's inflow from A in type and amount. Basins glue to regions to nations; mismatches expose phantom trade.
- **Closed-moiety declaration**: conservation is certifiable only if all pools of the moiety within the boundary are enumerated; otherwise the ledger is "open" and conservation is quarantined. Prevents shifting depletion into uncertified pools.
- **Boundary freezing** with change log (against gerrymandering wet neighbors into a basin); scenario registry (against scenario shopping); signed, append-only audit trail (tamper-evident; no blockchain required).
- **Provenance meet**: the certification level of a derived claim is the meet of its inputs' levels. A Level-0 anomaly passed through a Level-3 model yields a Level-0 output. Only new certified data raises a level — which is exactly what the value-of-information module computes.
- **Refusal with upgrade path**: the engine's most useful output is often a refusal that lists the minimal certificates or measurements that would unlock each currently quarantined claim.

### H. Functional accessibility formalized (item 6)

Pools carry a joint mass–quality state (m, q). Accessibility a(q, E) = 1[E_recover(q, depth, grade) ≤ E_budget]; functional stock S_f = ∫ a dm. **Theorem**: if q declines monotonically with cumulative extraction and E_recover(q) ↑ ∞ as q → q_min, then S_f hits zero at finite cumulative extraction while mass is exactly conserved. Conservation certified ≠ functional safety certified. Applies to ore grade, pump lift, soil organic matter, fish size/age structure, recyclate downcycling. An EROI-like *energy floor* becomes a barrier type in its own right.

### I. Deficit decomposition identity and controllable fraction (items 27, 29)

ΔS = R − H − L + I − X ± Q + ε, with ε flagged as phantom. Map each term to the governance controls that can move it; **controllable deficit fraction** κ = |controllable terms| / |deficit|. Climate-driven recharge loss is a deficit the manager cannot govern; over-abstraction is one they can. Reporting κ alongside the deficit is the honest heart of "diagnostics to governance instruments."

**Governance debt** gets a definition: avoidable drawdown under a feasible alternative rule, computed by *ledger replay* (the ledger is executable, so history can be re-run under shorter review intervals or automatic triggers). Debt vocabulary becomes computable, not rhetorical.

### J. Finance derived from duals, not parallel (item 14)

The shadow price of barrier safety is the dual variable of the barrier constraint — a ledger-derived quantity, not an estimate. Depletion liability = minimum-cost admissible restoration/substitution path back into K over the hypergraph. Stranded support risk = asset value × (1−α) × P(T_K < asset life). Money remains a claim; nothing in this module touches conservation.

### K. Trade responsibility identity (item 8)

Production-based and consumption-based attributions are two partitions of the same conserved extraction: Σ_r Prod_r = Σ_r Cons_r = global extraction, when the attribution is complete. **Conservation constrains the sums, not the shares.** MRIO's monetary-proportional allocation is an *allocation rule* and must be labeled as a choice. Imported depletion exposure is typed by the source basin's passport and inherits its level — a nation cannot import Level-3 certainty from a Level-0 exporter.

### L. Equity as per-group positivity; intergenerational equity as kernel nonemptiness (item 16)

Three water pools with typed morphisms and losses: physically available → institutionally allocated → actually delivered. Equity floors are per-group barriers on delivered service; a distributional barrier constrains the p-th percentile, not the mean. **Intergenerational equity is K ≠ ∅**: the future's option set is nonempty. The barrier declaration protocol supplies procedural justice (who declared, on what basis, with what revision rights); the emptiness witness supplies transparency about trade-offs.

### M. A fourth pilot — "the ledger that refuses" — and a retro-audit corpus (item 30)

**Pilot 4: antibiotic effectiveness** (or pollinator function). A depletable common with *no* conservation law. The engine must decline conservation and positivity-of-mass predicates and certify only barrier/functional/pressure claims. A standard that visibly refuses is more trustworthy than one that always produces a number.

**Retro-audit corpus.** Run 100–200 headline depletion numbers ("60 harvests left," "fishless oceans by 2048," "peak phosphorus 2030," Earth Overshoot Day, basin "years left") through the passport. Publish the distribution of label misuse using the **false-claim taxonomy** with formal signatures: turnover-as-exhaustion, anomaly-as-stock, reserve-as-resource, gross-for-net, flux-derived barrier, scalar-as-certificate, quality-blind recycling, infrastructure-as-stock, import-blind neutrality, record-relative extrapolation. This is an empirical paper, a media event, and a teaching corpus in one.

### N. Adoption architecture (items 23, 32)

## 2. New mathematics

### T1 — Depletion charts and the Baseline Covariance Lemma
Every time‑like depletion number is $\tau = N[S]/R[S,\cdot]$ with a stock functional $N \in \{S,\ S-b,\ S-S_{\text{base}},\ S_{\text{recoverable}}\}$ and a rate functional $R \in \{\text{gross outflow},\ -\dot S,\ \text{model trajectory}\}$, plus flags (frozen / evolving, absolute / record‑relative).

**Non‑comparability:** two depletion numbers are comparable only if they occupy the same chart up to positive affine reparametrisation.

**Lemma (baseline covariance).** For record‑relative indices, under $S_{\text{base}} \to S_{\text{base}}+\delta$,
$$\tau \to \tau - \delta/|\dot S|.$$
A record‑relative "time to depletion" is therefore **not a physical quantity**: it moves by $\delta/|\dot S|$ under an analyst's choice of baseline window. One line, checkable by any referee, and it disposes of an entire error class.

### T2 — Frozen‑Rate Bias Theorem (the curvature number)
Define the dimensionless **curvature number** on the barrier distance $u = S-b$:
$$\kappa \;=\; \frac{u\,\ddot u}{\dot u^{2}}.$$
For the power‑law depletion family $\dot u = -c\,u^{p}$ (which nests constant extraction $p=0$, stock‑proportional harvest $p=1$, and stress‑accelerated decline $p<0$), one has $\kappa = p$ and **exactly**
$$T^{*} \;=\; \frac{T_{\text{frozen}}}{1-\kappa}, \qquad T^{*}<\infty \iff \kappa<1 .$$
Consequences:
- The frozen‑rate ratio is **biased by a knowable factor**, not merely "a different question."
- **A single dimensionless number computed from $(u,\dot u,\ddot u)$ decides whether the stock reaches the barrier in finite time at all.**
- Stock‑proportional harvest ($\kappa=1$) — the commonest real case — makes the frozen‑rate horizon *constant forever* and the zero‑hitting time *infinite*, while the **barrier**‑hitting time stays finite and exceeds the frozen estimate by $\ln(S_0/b)/(1-b/S_0)$ (≈1.4× at $b/S_0=0.5$, ≈2.6× at $0.1$).
- **Reporting rule:** no frozen‑rate horizon may be published without $\kappa$ and its uncertainty. If $\kappa$ is unestimable or $\kappa \ge 1-\epsilon$, the figure is quarantined and replaced by a scenario ensemble.

### T3 — Concealment Capacity: auditing every composite index ever built
Normalise each component to $y_m\in[0,1]$ (0 = total failure, 1 = at target); adequacy = $y_m=1\ \forall m$. For a weighted‑sum index $A=\sum w_m y_m$ passing at threshold $\theta$:
$$\boxed{\ \Gamma_m \;=\; \min\!\Big(1,\ \tfrac{1-\theta}{w_m}\Big)\ }$$
is the **largest fraction of component $m$'s range that a passing score can hide.** Corollary: *any component with weight $w_m \le 1-\theta$ can be driven to complete failure while the index passes.* At $\theta=0.8$, every component weighted under 20% is fully concealable.

For power means $M_p$, $\Gamma$ decreases monotonically as $p\downarrow$; geometric mean gives $\Gamma_m=1-\theta^{1/w_m}$ (89% concealable at $\theta{=}0.8$, $w{=}0.1$); and
$$\Gamma(\min)=0 \quad \text{uniquely.}$$
**Representation theorem.** A scalar aggregator certifies componentwise adequacy iff it is a min‑type (bottleneck) functional of monotone component transforms. This turns your "noncompensation principle" from a negative slogan into (i) a **constructive design rule** — if you must aggregate, use the worst normalised margin and publish $\Gamma$ — and (ii) an **instant audit** of existing indices from their weights and thresholds alone, no data required. Apply it publicly to a handful of headline indices and the framework acquires immediate teeth.

### T4 — Duality criticality: substitutability as a finite dual price
Let $V(x)=\{v\ge 0: \dot x = S^{\!\top}v + b,\ \text{routing/donor constraints}\}$ be the flux polytope. Barrier safety is an LP; let $\lambda_m$ be the dual on barrier $m$.
$$\lambda_m<\infty \iff \text{an admissible substitution pathway exists in the current technology set} \iff \text{P(AdmissibleSubstitution) holds}.$$
Infeasibility / $\lambda_m=\infty$ ⟺ **critical natural capital**. Hence:
> Cost–benefit analysis is well‑posed only on the sub‑ledger with finite duals. On the critical sub‑ledger the correct output is a **constraint, not a price** — derived, not asserted.

This dissolves the weak/strong sustainability debate into a per‑moiety, technology‑indexed, revisable **Criticality Register**, and it makes your "financial shadow ledger" a *dual object* of the physical ledger rather than a parallel invention.

### T5 — The Nyquist Condition for Environmental Governance
Let $K^{\tau}$ be the maintainability kernel when controls are frozen for a review interval $\tau$, and $L=\sup\|f\|$ the maximal drift under admissible controls. Then $K^{\tau}\subseteq K^{0}$, monotone in $\tau$, with $K^{\tau}\supseteq K^{0}\ominus \tau L$ (Hausdorff erosion), so
$$\mathrm{dist}(x,\partial K^{\tau}) \ \ge\ \mathrm{dist}(x,\partial K^{0}) - \tau L
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\boxed{\ \tau \ \le\ \mathrm{dist}(x,\partial K^{0})\,/\,L\ }$$
**An assessment interval longer than the kernel margin divided by the maximal drift forfeits safe operating space by construction.** This is a governance design rule with the same status as a sampling theorem: institutional latency is now a *measured consumer of safety*, and "time to loss of governability" $T_{\text{gov}}\le T_{\text{barrier}}$ becomes the lead time governance actually possesses.

### T6 — Predicate identifiability and the Minimum Identifying Set
With observations $y=Hx+\varepsilon$ and constraints $Ax=c$, predicate $P$ is **certifiable from data** only if its truth value is constant on the fiber $\{x: Hx=y,\ Ax=c\}$. Otherwise: quarantine.

Canonical application: total‑column storage anomaly observes a *sum*; the partition into elastic (recoverable) and inelastic (compaction) storage change lies in $\ker H$. **Therefore any recoverability claim from storage anomalies alone is unidentifiable, not merely uncertain.** The **Minimum Identifying Set** is the minimal set of additional observation rows restoring rank — e.g. InSAR subsidence + storage coefficients + nested piezometers. This makes value‑of‑information **two‑tier**: structural identifiability first (rank), variance reduction second (Bayesian). Your Level‑0→4 ladder becomes a *derived* consequence of rank conditions rather than a convention.

### T7 — Independence of the predicate set
Constructive counterexamples (2–3 compartments each): balanced‑but‑negative ledgers (P1∧¬P3); conserved and positive but barrier‑violating (P2∧P3∧¬P4); barrier‑safe but irreversible (compaction, salinisation, cohort collapse: P4∧¬P5); conserved but functionally dead (phosphorus to deep‑ocean sediment, metal to dilute tailings: P2∧¬P6); currently safe with empty continuation set (P4∧¬P7). Formalise **this** lattice in Lean/Isabelle — not the assimilation stack. It is the load‑bearing claim and it is provable in weeks, not years.

### T8 — Boundary invariance and Typed Double Entry
A claim functional is **manipulation‑proof under boundary redrawing** iff it depends only on fluxes across a closed surface enclosing the moiety's physical support, plus typed stocks within. Territorial accounts fail; consumption‑based accounts pass.

Then the accounting innovation that kills offset fraud:
> **Counterparty Closure.** No credit may be recognised without a matched, signed, typed **counter‑entry in a named counterparty ledger**, same moiety, same passport ID. Registry‑level invariant: $\sum$credits $=\sum$debits per moiety type, globally.

This is double‑entry bookkeeping extended across institutional boundaries and material types — checkable by a registry query, and immediately applicable to water‑neutrality, biodiversity net gain, and carbon offset claims.

### T9 — Non‑Detection Fallacy Bound (the silent liquidation window)
Detecting a trend $\beta$ in yield with residual sd $\sigma$ and AR(1) $\rho$ requires
$$n \;\gtrsim\; \Big(\tfrac{z\,\sigma_{\text{eff}}\sqrt{12}}{|\beta|}\Big)^{2/3}, \qquad \sigma_{\text{eff}}=\sigma\sqrt{\tfrac{1+\rho}{1-\rho}} .$$
Illustrative: $\sigma=10\%$ of yield, $\rho=0.5$, $\beta=-1\%/\text{yr}$, 95% level ⟹ **≈24 years**. Therefore "no significant decline over 15 years" is *uninformative*, not reassuring. Coupled to your yield‑composition identity $Y=Y_{\text{reg}}+Y_{\text{draw}}+\dots$, this quantifies **how long a liquidating system is statistically indistinguishable from a sustainable one** — and flips the burden of proof: *a precautionary barrier is mandatory whenever $T_{\text{obs}} < T_{\text{det}}$.*

### T10 — Paper‑Claim Overhang (hydrological / quota insolvency)
Let $E_{\text{safe}}$ be the barrier‑safe extraction envelope from the flux polytope and $e_i$ the legal entitlements. Then
$$\text{Overhang} \;=\; \textstyle\sum_i e_i - E_{\text{safe}}$$
reported **per moiety and per reliability class**, with a priority waterfall as in insolvency. Colorado, Murray–Darling, Indus, and most quota systems are insolvent on paper. This is an auditable, legally actionable balance‑sheet statement that no existing water or fisheries accounting produces.

## 3. Instruments that make it adoptable and non‑gameable

**(a) Quarantine Asymmetry Principle — essential safeguard.** A framework that issues "unproven" verdicts will be captured by whoever benefits from delay. Fix it structurally: *absence of a safety certificate is not a certificate of absence of risk.* Quarantine constrains **reassurance** claims at least as strongly as alarm claims; when identifiability fails (T6) or $T_{\text{obs}}<T_{\text{det}}$ (T9), the default is the precautionary barrier, with the burden allocated by reversibility class (P5). Without this clause, your standard is a denialist instrument. With it, it is a precautionary one with rigorous triggers.

**(b) Retro‑certification corpus (the empirical validation you currently lack).** Passport ~300 historical depletion claims — 1970s "30 years of oil," Hubbert peaks, Limits to Growth runs, peak phosphorus, Ogallala forecasts, cod and anchoveta collapses, lithium/cobalt exhaustion claims, remaining carbon budget statements. For each: predicate actually established vs. claimed; chart (T1); $\kappa$ if recoverable; whether the machinery would have quarantined it *with the data available at the time*. This is simultaneously the framework's validation, its most citable artifact, and a **gold‑standard benchmark**.

**(c) Auto‑passporting.** With (b) as training/eval data, build an LLM‑assisted claim‑typing service: ingest a paper, report, or press release → draft passport with predicate, chart, missing fields, non‑claims. Human‑verified. Without automation the standard is unenforceable at the scale of the literature; with it, journals and newsrooms can adopt it at marginal cost.

**(d) The Three‑Line Depletion Disclosure.** Adoption requires a version that fits a figure caption:
> **1. Numerator / denominator:** what stock functional over what rate, with baseline declared.
> **2. Predicate:** which of P1–P7 this establishes.
> **3. Non‑claim:** what it does not establish.
> *A number that cannot fill three lines is quarantined.*

**(e) Standards crosswalk with predicate audit.** Map to SEEA, SNA, GHG Protocol, ISSB/IFRS S2, CSRD/ESRS, TNFD, EU Taxonomy, ISO 14046, JORC/CRIRSCO/NI 43‑101, MSC, SDG indicators. Findings write themselves: JORC "reserves" is an **economic‑accessibility classification**, so "300 years of phosphate reserves" mislabels its predicate; market‑based Scope 2 is a **claim, not a mass**, and fails Counterparty Closure. Position PCSA as the *predicate layer over SEEA*, not a rival system — that is the difference between a cited paper and an adopted standard.

**(f) Sink‑side symmetry — your largest impact multiplier.** Depletion of a source and saturation of a sink are formally dual. Apply T1–T2 verbatim to the **remaining carbon budget**: "X years left at current emissions" is a frozen‑rate ratio on a sink with $\kappa\neq 0$, an undeclared baseline, and a temperature‑threshold barrier whose declaration protocol is implicit. The world's most quoted sustainability number is a textbook instance of the error class you are formalising. Say so, with the arithmetic.

**(g) Preregistered behavioural trial.** The framework's biggest practical risk is that componentwise dashboards degrade decisions relative to scalar scores. Test it: randomise policymakers, journalists, and asset managers across (i) scalar index, (ii) componentwise dashboard, (iii) dashboard + passports + non‑claims; outcomes = detection of a concealed component failure, calibration of hitting‑time beliefs, resistance to a greenwashed vignette, decision latency. If (iii) wins, you have evidence no rival index possesses. If it loses, T3's min‑representation gives you the principled fallback. Either way the framework becomes falsifiable — which almost no sustainability accounting proposal is.

# Profound Upgrades: From Typed Architecture to Self-Verifying Public Infrastructure

The 34 items define a complete *architecture*. The remaining weaknesses are not architectural — they are grammatical (predicates are listed, not generated), adversarial (nothing stops the certifier from being gamed or captured), reflexive (the standard never reports its own track record), cognitive (the human reader ends at a dashboard), and kinetic (adoption is wished for, not designed). The following upgrades (continuing your numbering) close those five gaps.

---

## I. Formal core: from predicate list to generative grammar

**35. The distance–rate grammar ("periodic table of depletion numbers").** Every time-labeled depletion number has the form **T = D / R**, where D is a *typed distance* and R is a *typed rate*. The predicate established is the triple (D, R, dynamics-assumption class):

| Distance ↓ / Rate basis → | Gross turnover intensity | Frozen net drift | Scenario-conditional drift | Stochastic posterior |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mass: S − B | stock-turnover time | **frozen-rate ratio** (FR class: counterfactual normalization) | scenario hitting time | first-passage distribution |
| Log: ln(S/B) | **pressure scale Θ** (e.g., fisheries) | log-decline horizon | demographic hitting time | posterior barrier-crossing CDF |
| Quality: Q − Q_min | grade-throughput time | functional-access horizon | scenario accessibility time | accessibility-risk distribution |
| Service: s − d | pressure on floor | service-deficit horizon | scenario deficit time | deficit-risk distribution |

The confusion dies at the root: the word "time" attaches to the *units*, never the semantics. Groundwater's 9.5 y is a cell in this table (record-relative, net-drift column), not a countdown. The passport's predicate field becomes *generated*, not chosen — closing the classification loophole structurally rather than by vigilance.

**36. Admissibility certificate for frozen ratios.** Under dS = −r dt + σ dW, the first-passage time is inverse-Gaussian with mean exactly (S − B)/r. So the frozen ratio is *the exact mean of a frozen world* — and nothing else: no tail information, invalidated by drift nonstationarity or regime switching. Issue **class certificates**: FR-1 (mean under frozen conditions; admissible as normalization), FR-2 (admissible with stated drift-variability bounds), FR-X (quarantined: nonstationary drift). This is the upgrade that lets journalists keep simple numbers *when defensible* — enormously widening the coalition for the standard.

**37. Dynamic margin calculus.** Item 9 declares precautionary margins rhetorically; make them *computed*. Certified barrier = declared floor + margin:

> B_eff = B_declared + m, with m = max over disturbance class of [ drift·(δ_detect + δ_act) + z_α·σ·√δ + irreversibility surcharge + ambiguity surcharge ].

This is safety-stock/reorder-point theory generalized to nonlinear barriers — and it fuses items 3, 9, and 15: **slow institutions must hold larger margins, by formula, not by virtue**. The review-interval stability margin becomes a computable distance. Governance quality enters the physics of the certificate.

**38. Minimal contradiction objects as the atomic unit of audit.** When the flux polytope is infeasible, the Farkas certificate proves failure, but the *auditor-facing* product is the **minimal infeasible subsystem**: "Irrigation withdrawal (3.2), recharge (1.1), ΔStorage (0.4) — these three entries are jointly inconsistent by ≥ 1.7 km³/yr; all else checks." Pair with the cheapest-repair LP (the data-reconciliation lineage of Crowe; Narasimhan & Jordache — acknowledge it; the novelty is typing + barriers + certificates, not reconciliation). Audit reports become lists of minimal contradictions with remediation arcs, not pass/fail verdicts.

**39. Shadow-price corridor theorem.** Make item 14 theorem-driven: the dual multipliers on barrier constraints of the flux program *are* defensible scarcity prices — the marginal value of one more unit of stock, given the ledger and scenario. Under degeneracy the dual solution is a face → report the **price corridor**, never a point. Non-claim: λ is ledger- and scenario-relative, not the global value of nature. This gives "shadow price of barrier safety" its first rigorous definition.

**40. Impossibility theorem, sharpened into a measurable metric.** For n ≥ 2, *no* continuous strictly monotone scalarization can certify componentwise adequacy: strict monotonicity implies f(B − εeᵢ + M·eⱼ) > f(B) for large M, i.e., every aggregate certifies some barrier-violating state. Then go beyond impossibility: define the **false-safe volume** μ{x : x violates some barrier ∧ f(x) ≥ f(B)} and *publish it for every popular index* (ecological footprint, adjusted net savings, HDI-style composites). "Your index has a 23% false-safe region" converts a philosophical objection into an operational audit.

**41. Conservation as a typeclass, not an axiom.** The kernel should implement algebraic structure *per type*: **Conserved** (mass, energy: strict balance); **Storable-with-deletion** (species, genomes: births/deaths as transitions; extinction is an *annihilation event with no sink pool* — mass balance does not apply); **Claim** (money: double-entry conservation in its own domain, no morphism to mass except declared readouts); **Information** (genetic diversity: copyable, erasable, unrecoverable). This generalizes "don't sum unlike things" into "know which algebraic structure your stock carries" — and prevents the specific greenwash of pointing at conserved mass while unrecoverable information is deleted. Extinction debt = committed future deletion events (lag-typed).

**42. Quality-typed circularity ceiling.** Item 11 needs its thermodynamic spine. With per-cycle retention η (per *quality class*, since downcycling is a type change, not retention), steady-state primary requirement is (1 − η)·T for service throughput T — displacement factor 1/(1 − η). Certificates must state η per class, the exergy cost of achieving it, and the resulting ceiling; η → 1 is excluded by dissipation, so "circularity ends depletion" fails as a *type error*, not an opinion. Compute the ceiling for P, Cu, Li, plastics as flagship results.

## II. Compositionality and scale

**43. Compositional certificates (assume–guarantee).** Ledgers nest: basin ⊂ nation ⊂ planet. Prove the composition theorem: if ledgers A and B each pass and their interface fluxes are type-balanced, the union passes — certificates compose. This is what makes planetary coverage computable without a planetary computer, and what lets a firm-level passport plug into a national ledger.

**44. Aggregation functors with loss certificates.** Aggregation is legitimate as a *structure-preserving map with an explicit residue*: every rolled-up figure must ship its **information-loss certificate** — the componentwise facts it hides (which pools, which deficits, which tails). Aggregates become legal for communication, illegal for certification, and *self-documenting about what they conceal*.

**45. Temporal and hysteresis typing.** Add time to the type system: instant-stocks vs. interval-averaged stocks, rates-at-instants vs. flows-over-intervals; recoverable vs. non-recoverable drawdown as a *type-level* flag (compaction, salinization), not a footnote. Temporal mislabeling (annual flow read as stock; record-relative trend read as state) is the most common laundering channel — make it unrepresentable.

**46. Default ledgers for the planet.** Auto-generate Level-0/1 ledgers for every major aquifer (GRACE/GLDAS), every FAO fishing area, every country×material (IRP), every country×energy (IEA) — pre-typed, status = *descriptive*, with a documented upgrade path for local authorities. This reverses adoption physics: nobody adopts the standard; **everyone already has one and is invited to improve it.**

## III. Trust under adversaries: the certification game

**47. Gaming-surface maps and cross-ledger tripwires.** For each certificate class, enumerate what the type system *cannot* see (unreported extraction is invisible to a ledger built on reported flows — types can't detect missing moieties). Publish the blind spots; close them with independent observation and **cross-ledger physics triangulation**: crop-ledger water demand must match water-ledger withdrawals must match energy-ledger pumping kWh (kWh ≈ ρg·lift·V/η). Fraud in one ledger violates conservation in another. Physics becomes the tripwire; the gaming-surface map becomes a standing section of every certificate.

**48. Proof-carrying claims.** Restructure the engine (item 2) after proof-carrying code: a **tiny, formally verified checker** (the trusted base — verify it in Lean/Coq, reimplement it independently) that validates type correctness, conservation balances, barrier arithmetic, and signatures; the large engine merely *emits witnesses* (Farkas vectors, bounded simulation traces, hashed posterior samples) that the checker re-verifies in milliseconds. The kernel is **sound by construction, incomplete by design** — "indeterminate: insufficient data" is a first-class output, never a failure. Trust no longer depends on the certifier's codebase.

**49. Optimistic certification with challenge windows.** Certificates are provisionally accepted; anyone may post a counter-witness during a challenge window; bonds back both certificates and challenges (slashing for false certificates *and* frivolous challenges). This is rollup-style fraud proving implementable in a plain registry — it converts auditing from a scarce profession into an open adversarial market.

**50. Ledger Transparency log.** Append-only public log (Certificate Transparency analogue) of every certificate, retraction, barrier revision, and ontology version — no silent redefinition, no double issuance, no quiet deletion of yesterday's floor. Certificates carry **TTL tied to drift rate × monitoring cadence**; expired certificates auto-downgrade to record-relative. Stale safety is worse than declared ignorance.

**51. Ledger Vulnerability Registry (CVE-style).** Named, numbered laundering patterns — phantom mass, recovery-credit duplication, quality-neutral recycling, infrastructure-as-stock — each with an ID, severity, and detector status; engines and auditors ship detectors; an annual top-10 report names the year's dominant fraud geometries. Item 24's phantom audit becomes a living, citable vulnerability database.

**52. Calibration auditing: the standard grades itself.** Every certified probabilistic statement (P(crossing by date) = p) is scored against realized outcomes; publish reliability diagrams and Brier-type scores *per predicate class*. Weather services did this for a century; sustainability claims never have. A certificate then carries the engine's track record, and "trust us" is replaced by "check our calibration curve." This is the single largest trust upgrade available.

**53. Standard stress-test battery.** Environmental analogue of bank stress tests: a standardized disturbance library (drought decade, demand surge, price shock, trade embargo, compound events) applied to every certified ledger; result = a **stress passport** (barrier margins under each standardized disturbance, kernel membership retained/lost). Regulators already know how to consume stress tests; give them a familiar object.
