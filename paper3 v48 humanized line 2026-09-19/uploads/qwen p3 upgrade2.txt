corrections:
1. **No unsupported prevalence claims.**

3. **Preservation of the paper’s scope discipline.**

# Upgrade Program: From Typed Diagnostic Grammar to Auditable Decision Infrastructure

## Strategic objective

The paper already constructs a typed stock–flow accounting layer in which conservation, positivity, barrier safety, and depletion semantics are separated. The next step is to make that layer operational without weakening its discipline.

The upgraded framework should move from:

> “This indicator can be read beyond its predicate.”

to:

> “Here is the componentwise certificate, the declared assumptions, the scope of the claim, and the next audit or monitoring step.”

The guiding principle remains:

> Scalar summaries may rank and communicate. Certification requires the vector.

## 2. Executable Typed Ledger Certification Engine

2. moiety conservation;

4. barrier compliance;

6. ghost sinks;
7. phantom mass;

The five double-counting rules from the paper should become automated audit rules.

### Mathematical upgrade

The current flux-bounding envelope theorem uses declared flux boxes. A stronger certification engine would use the actual admissible flux polytope:

\[
\dot{x} = S_T v + b,
\qquad

subject to:

\[

subject to the ledger constraints.

The dual programme can produce either:

### Why this matters

## 4. Support-Provenance and Yield-Composition Accounting

Operationalize the productivity illusion using the paper’s service-readout and support-provenance machinery.

The latest rewrite defines:

\[

=
\sup\{\alpha \in [0,1] : \alpha \bar{s} \in \Gamma_{\text{reg}}(x,t)\}.
\]

The upgrade turns this into a practical diagnostic.

For any measured yield \(Y\), where data permit, report:

\[
Y =
Y_{\text{regenerative}}
+
Y_{\text{drawdown}}
+
Y_{\text{substitution}}
+
Y_{\text{import}},
\]

with strict typing.

### Required declarations

Each component must be declared:

- regenerative yield: supported by renewal at the rate of use;
- drawdown yield: supported by reduction of a declared support pool;
- substitution yield: supported by an identified typed pathway;
- imported yield: displaced to another system.

If the decomposition is not identified, the report should say so.

### New indicators

1. Regenerative support fraction:

\[
\alpha_{\text{reg}} = \frac{Y_{\text{regenerative}}}{Y}.
\]

2. Liquidation fraction:

\[
1 - \alpha_{\text{reg}}.
\]

3. Support-pool coverage ratio:

\[
\frac{\text{support-pool regeneration}}{\text{support-pool drawdown}}.
\]

4. Recoverability status:

```text
recoverable / uncertain / likely non-recoverable / not assessed
```

5. Permanence risk:

```text
compaction, salinization, soil loss, biodiversity loss, institutional lock-in
```

### Why this is aligned

The paper’s productivity-illusion discussion says that measured yield can remain high while the support pool is drawn down. This upgrade makes that diagnostic explicit without claiming a two-pool model where one has not been established.

## 6. Functional Accessibility and Quality Module

Examples:

- phosphorus mass remains, but becomes dispersed;
- groundwater mass remains, but becomes too deep, saline, or costly;
- fish biomass remains, but loses reproductive structure;
- soil minerals remain, but lose biological function.

### Proposed module

Track declared quality variables:

- ore grade;
- aquifer depth and pump lift;
- soil organic matter;
- nutrient bioavailability;
- contaminant concentration;
- fish age structure;
- biomass energy density;
- recovery energy requirements.

### Proposed predicate

Add a separate declared predicate:

> functional accessibility safety.

It should not be confused with:

- accounting consistency;
- conservation consistency;
- barrier safety;
- thermodynamic admissibility.

### Why this is aligned

The latest rewrite states that depletion is loss of access, not loss of matter. This module gives that statement a practical accounting form.

## 7. Substitution and Circularity Certificates

Formalize substitution as a ledger-native predicate.

The paper already refuses to admit substitution merely because an aggregate production function allows it. The upgrade makes substitution auditable.

A substitute is admissible only if:

1. the physical pathway is identified;
2. the pathway is typed in the ledger;
3. deployment rates are feasible;
4. energy and material requirements are accounted for;
5. it does not draw down another critical stock;
6. it does not overfill a sink compartment;
7. quality degradation is accounted for;
8. timing matches the rate of depletion.

### Proposed object

Represent technological options as a pathway hypergraph:

```text
inputs
outputs
yields
recovery fractions
deployment constraints
energy requirements
capital requirements
time lags
```

Then define:

\[
\text{AdmissibleSubstitution}_{i \to j}
\]

as a predicate, not a scalar elasticity.

### Why this is aligned

The latest rewrite treats weak and strong sustainability as two regimes of one ledger, distinguished by whether the material cycle closes at the rate of use. The substitution certificate makes that regime distinction operational.

# What the Upgraded Framework Should Not Do

To remain aligned with the latest rewrite and your clarifications, the upgraded framework should not:

1. claim that indicators are false simply because they are not physical hitting times;
2. claim that conflation is routine without evidence;
3. promote scalar aggregates to certificates;
4. derive barriers from flux data;
5. treat stochastic surrogates as stochastic completions of the ledger;
6. reduce the closed physical ledger to the open institutional system;
7. treat infrastructure as resource stock;
8. treat anomaly indices as absolute stocks;
9. treat reserve-life ratios as physical exhaustion forecasts;
10. treat removals-only pressure scales as demographic forecasts;
11. claim established two-pool models where they remain registered gaps;
12. use diagnostic labels to determine material routing.

---

# Revised Novelty Narrative

The upgraded novelty narrative should be confident but bounded:

> This work constructs a typed stock–flow accounting layer in which conservation follows from incidence structure, positivity follows from donor limitation, services are readouts rather than conserved mass, and depletion claims are separated by predicate. It provides a formal grammar for componentwise sustainability accounting: each indicator carries the question it answers, the assumptions it requires, and the certificate it can support. Scalar summaries may rank and communicate; certification requires the vector.

If the upgrades are implemented, the narrative becomes:

> This work extends typed sustainability accounting from diagnostic grammar to auditable decision infrastructure: claim passports, certification engines, maintainability horizons, support-provenance accounting, scenario grammars, and componentwise governance dashboards. It does not replace physical models or declare thresholds. It makes the predicates explicit, so that each claim can be audited at its actual strength.

---

# Final Recommendation

The most important upgrade is not adding more indicators. It is to make the framework into a **constitutional grammar for sustainability claims**:

- typed objects;
- separated predicates;
- declared barriers;
- componentwise certificates;
- maintainability horizons;
- support-provenance accounting;
- machine-readable scope statements;
- public dashboards;
- auditable interventions.

In one sentence:

> The framework should evolve from showing that depletion indicators can be read beyond their predicates into providing the public infrastructure by which societies can see, audit, and govern componentwise ecological limits without false aggregation.
