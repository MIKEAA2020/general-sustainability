**Aim.** Numbers that share the label "time to depletion" answer different questions. A typed stock–flow ledger proving conservation, positivity and barrier safety as separate predicates lets each claim carry only what it establishes.

## §1 Problem and reframing
**Compensatory aggregation:** scalar indices summarize heterogeneous stocks whose cross-component trades are never declared as mathematics, so a severe deficit coexists with a positive aggregate; Earth Overshoot Day is the "public flagship object". **Classification drift:** reserve-life ratios, trend-persistence indices and removals-only pressure scales circulate as one quantity, producing "a number in units of years that is not a time to any physical event". The **productivity illusion** is arithmetic (deficit offset by surplus) or dynamical (yield sustained by liquidating a support pool — groundwater, soil carbon, bioavailable nutrients): pool size is a property of the resource, recoverability a property of the *rate*. Weak versus strong sustainability is reframed as **two regimes of one typed system, separated by whether the material cycle closes at the rate of use**, not by natural-capital substitutability (against Neumayer 2013 and Ekins et al. 2003; extending Daly 1990, whose deep-time clause is scoped to slow geological compartments). Waste is a relational status, not an intrinsic property; a substitute is either a recycled flux returned to the regenerating pool or a non-renewable drawdown on a second compartment. Seven contributions are claimed; **not claimed**: stochastic completion, thermodynamic admissibility, identification of the two-pool groundwater model, and any empirical finding about a named basin, aquifer or fishery.

## §2 The typed ledger
A **moiety** is the only object to which a conservation law attaches; species, compartment (location) and stock (amount + unit) are distinct — "carbon in the atmosphere" is a location-specific stock, not a moiety. Dynamics ẋ = Sᵀv(x,y,θ) + Bᵀu_∂ + dx, v ≥ 0: entries add within a row only when types and units agree, conversions enter only as explicit stoichiometric coefficients, and LᵀSᵀ = 0 gives d(Lᵀx)/dt = LᵀBᵀu_∂ + Lᵀdx — one law per moiety and boundary, which does not create a scalar sustainability mass. Forward invariance is a third obligation, requiring every primitive outflow to vanish or be limited when its donor is empty. The closed finite-donor block x_L = (N, A_act, A_geo, U) saturates recruitment and geological return by active-pool access s = A_act/(A_act+A₀) and donor availability σ = A_geo/(A_geo+A_g0): R = rN(1−N/K)s, T = κ_A N s, B = R+T, with primitives e_GA = ω_A A_eq,intrinsic σ, e_AG = ω_A A_act, C_A,lim = C_A σ, γ_U U. Three recharge laws are tabulated so none is substituted for another: donor-limited exchange; target-relaxation ω_A(A_eq−A), **banned** unless donor-limited because it runs backward at an empty donor and is admissible only with an effectively infinite external reservoir, which makes the system open; and the companion's derived target A_eq,W = A_eq,intrinsic + κ_A K/ω_A, not a closed-block law and the reason the two systems do not reduce. ## §3–§4 Certificates and guarantees

Prop 2: 3 does not follow from 1 — constant extraction above regeneration is exactly mass-balanced yet breaches any positive lower barrier — so "a single integrated assessment that reports only one of them declares less than it appears to". Lemma 3 reconstructs readouts by integrating observed fluxes: **barriers are declared, not computed**; Theorem 5 sandwiches each readout between envelope integrals over declared flux boxes. Prop 6 bounds hitting times under uniform negative drift, while Ṡ = −kS (τ₀ = ∞) shows positive extraction need not exhaust in finite time. Every finite hitting time thus belongs to a compartment or a readout barrier, and "exhaustion of the natural block" is transfer across the boundary into product, waste and inert compartments — depletion as loss of *access*, not of matter (Daly 1990). The closed ledger then proves: conservation for the natural block and the two larger systems (7–9); orthant invariance via Nagumo, donor limitation being the exact sufficiency requirement (10–11); **no interior rest at positive effort** (12), so the working point (N*, A_act*) = (89.526, 397.87) at E* ≈ 2.090 is not a rest point, there R* = qE*N* ≈ 0.187 > 0; the vanishing-extraction rest set (13); and integrable extraction qEN ∈ L¹ bounded by M(0), so no trajectory sustains 0.187 forever (14). ## §5–§6 Services, deficit, arithmetic
Services are readouts s = O(x,u,θ): the contemporaneous balance b_i = s_i − d_i ≥ 0 speaks only to that instant and implies nothing about the trajectory in either direction. Def. 1 declares the state-dependent balance domain B(x,t), against which any scalar certificate claim is checked; Def. 2 defines the directional regenerative-support fraction α_reg over Γ_reg ⊆ Γ_all (restrictions from a pathway or technology model, not the stock ledger), leaving a gap (1−α_reg)s̄ that never sums unlike units. The unreduced physical deficit Δ_phys = C − M̂ᵀS does not drive the physics; it equals −Ṅ only on the single-resource specialization (Rem. 16), not by definition. Depletion time separates into three quantities: gross turnover intensity J_A^gross = g/A and coverage (A−A_min)/g are dependency measures, neither a time to depletion since g > 0 ⇏ Ȧ < 0; the frozen net-depletion ratio H_A^loc = (A−A_min)/[−Ȧ]₊ (+∞ at no net decline) is "still not a trajectory forecast"; and the scenario-conditioned hitting time T_A(x₀;π,d) should be reported as a distribution or robust interval, "not a single universal date". Prop 17's bound H₀/(1±ε) under a declared rate bracket is the only regime in which the frozen ratio *is* a horizon. Exit times τ_m^± make horizon safety the condition τ_exit > T; "exhaustion" is reserved for S_m = 0, other failures are barrier violations, upper barriers bind symmetrically (CO₂ accumulating as fossil stocks decline), and a sustainability claim needs the terminal condition x(T) ∈ K_maint, the viability kernel of the barrier set.

## §10–§11 Refusals and conclusion
Weighted certification is **rejected, not discouraged**: on B(x,t), wᵀb ≥ 0 fails to imply b ≥ 0 whenever a compensating pair is admitted, so no non-negative weighting is a componentwise certificate unless that pattern is excluded. The universal-failure theorem — for m ≥ 2 and *every* w ≥ 0 some admissible pair has wᵀb ≥ 0 while a component is negative, as a two-compartment donor-limited transfer shows — puts "the burden of proof on the aggregation, not on the componentwise report". Five double-counting rules bind: one balance per moiety; explicit stoichiometry; yield routing of every remainder; no ghost sinks; classification labels kept out of the columns. Negative and boundary content is first-class, because a quantity that answers exactly one question, stated with that question, is the framework working. The limitations run **(i)–(viii)**: (i) the two-pool exact groundwater specialization remains open, only the one-pool affine approximation admitted; (ii) the phosphorus and groundwater rows are registered template obligations with no constitutive content behind their identification ladders; (iii) §7's propositions concern declared surrogates, not the ledger, so they neither compute its hitting time nor conserve its mass; (iv) the §6.5 records are classified diagnostics, none a calibrated early-warning system or forecast; (v) the non-reduction boundary is permanent mathematics, not a gap; (vi) Theorem 15's hybrid balance stays conditional, its jump-interpretation and yield-routing obligations open per application; (vii) support-saturation limits are local and finite-time; and (viii) nothing empirical is asserted about any named resource system beyond the classifications of public data products at their source status. Section 11 holds that both failure modes are representation errors and that the preventing representation is the content: conservation proved from the incidence structure, positivity from donor limitation, services as readouts and not mass, depletion time as three quantities and not one. Stating the applied numbers with the questions they answer makes them usable; collapsing them into one "time to depletion" makes them false. Weak and strong remain two readings of one typed ledger, the vector reading carrying the certificate and the scalar b·M check being the operational overshoot test.
