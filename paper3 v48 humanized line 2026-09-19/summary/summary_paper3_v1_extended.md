**One-sentence thesis.** Depletion numbers that share the label "time to depletion" answer different questions, and a typed stock–flow ledger in which conservation, positivity, and barrier safety are proved as three separate predicates makes each claim carry only the predicate it actually establishes.

## 1. The two failure modes (§1.1)

*Compensatory aggregation*: heterogeneous stocks and service flows are summarized by scalar indices whose cross-component trades are never declared as mathematics, so a severe deficit in one component coexists with a positive aggregate; the paper names Earth Overshoot Day as the "public flagship object" of this failure (Lin et al., 2018; Wackernagel and Beyers, 2019; Blomqvist et al., 2013) and Meadows et al. (1972) as its dynamic form. *Classification drift*: reserve-life ratios, trend-persistence indices and removals-only pressure scales circulate as one quantity. Concrete instances given: a reserve-life ratio divides reserves by production and calls the quotient a horizon; a groundwater anomaly index reports the fitted distance to the series' own minimum divided by the fitted rate, "a number in units of years that is not a time to any physical event"; a fisheries indicator divides a log biomass margin by a fishing mortality.

A third named failure, the **productivity illusion**, has two senses: arithmetic (deficit offset by surplus) and dynamical (yield inflation — measured yield exceeds true sustainable yield because it is maintained by liquidating a support pool: groundwater, soil carbon, bioavailable nutrients). Key formulation: every pool is regenerative on some timescale (a crop within a season, an aquifer within years to decades, a mineral deposit over geological time); "the size of the pool is a property of the resource. Whether a drawdown is recoverable is a property of the rate." The paper's mechanical picture is an elevator rated for ten that holds fourteen: the cable parts after accumulated wear it never shows, the wear is diffuse and invisible to everyone riding, the snap is sudden and total; the two-pool architecture exists "to make the wear measurable while the cable still holds, not to predict the snap."

On the data side, reserves are an economic classification moving with prices, technology, exploration and regulation, so the ratio is not an exhaustion forecast; the paper cites Illakwahhi, Vegi and Srivastava (2024) on "depletion within a century" claims resting on single-source USGS data, and Tilton (2003), Tilton and Lagos (2007) for reserves rising through a century of rising production. A fourth slide is identified: bookkeeping balance, stoichiometric conservation, thermodynamic admissibility and sustainability safety are four predicates that the literature conflates — "a mass-balanced ledger can be chemically impossible… a ledger satisfying all declared barriers can fail conservation."

Weak and strong sustainability are reframed as **two regimes of one system**, distinguished not by substitutability of natural capital (the received reading, Neumayer 2013; Ekins et al. 2003) but by *whether the material cycle closes at the rate of use*. Weak is the idealized closure in which substitution and regeneration redistribute matter as it arises (regeneration is included for physical completeness, "often on deep-time scales", citing Daly 1990) — scoped to slow geological compartments, with the paper explicitly noting that its applied record's regenerative compartments renew on human timescales, where regeneration is co-equal or dominant. Waste is then "a relational status, not an intrinsic property of any material": matter that accumulates because it cannot be put to immediate use for lack of knowledge, technology, or timely redistribution. Strong sustainability is failed closure — no identified physical pathway, or depletion outrunning deployment. Substitution is admissible only where a pathway exists that does not draw down a different critical stock; in ledger terms a substitute is either a recycled flux returned to the regenerating pool or a non-renewable drawdown on a second compartment. Two readings of one object: scalar B = b·M (aggregate regeneration vs consumption: does the cycle close in aggregate?) and the vector ledger (does it close only by depleting a critical compartment or over-filling waste?). Both required; neither demotes stocks; both instantaneous, hence the scenario-conditioned hitting time supplies the drift.

## 2. Contributions, and what is not claimed (§1.2)

Seven: (1) the typed primitive-flux ledger (compartments with material identity, boundary, unit; non-negative primitives through a signed incidence matrix; conversions only as explicit stoichiometric coefficients; every one-way transfer donor-limited — lineage: Brunner & Rechberger 2004, Fischer-Kowalski et al. 2011, Feinberg 2019); (2) three certification layers separated and proved (flux-reconstruction identity, conservation-law reduction, flux-bounding envelope with barrier-certificate corollary); (3) the closed finite-donor theorem set; (4) depletion arithmetic as three non-interchangeable quantities plus upper barriers, exit times, infinite-horizon maintainability, uniform-drift bounds, and a counterexample to "positive extraction implies finite exhaustion"; (5) application classifications at exact status; (6) first-passage semantics on declared surrogates with seven explicit non-claims; (7) the interface with institutional dynamics: exact shared object plus a non-reduction theorem.
**Not claimed:** no stochastic completion of the ledger; no thermodynamic admissibility; no identification of the two-pool groundwater hypothesis; no empirical finding about any basin, aquifer or fishery beyond the descriptive status of tabulated indicators.

## 3. The ledger (§2)

Four concepts are separated: a **moiety** is the only object a conservation law attaches to; a **species** is a chemical/biological form; a **compartment** is a location; a **stock** is a compartment's amount with a unit ("carbon in the atmosphere" is a location-specific stock, not a moiety). State dynamics: ẋ = Sᵀv(x,y,θ) + Bᵀu_∂(t) + dx, v ≥ 0 (eq. 1), with y boundary states, θ constitutive parameters, dx a stated disturbance class. Entries add within a row only when types and units agree; if LᵀSᵀ = 0 then d(Lᵀx)/dt = LᵀBᵀu_∂ + Lᵀdx — one law per conserved moiety and boundary, and "the identity does not create a scalar sustainability mass". Three clarifications: dx must itself be typed; Sᵀ may hold signed entries while v ≥ 0 (separate declarations); forward invariance is a separate requirement (each primitive outflow must vanish or be limited when the donor is empty; target-relaxation from a finite donor is admissible only after donor limitation is explicit).

The closed finite-donor block is x_L = (N, A_act, A_geo, U) with support factor s = A_act/(A_act+A₀) and donor fraction σ = A_geo/(A_geo+A_g0); R = rN(1−N/K)s, T = κ_A N s, B = R+T; geo-primitives e_GA = ω_A A_eq,intrinsic σ, e_AG = ω_A A_act, C_A,lim = C_A σ, γ_U U (detritus return). A three-row table keeps the recharge laws apart: donor-limited exchange (the closed block's law, rest at A_act = A_eq,intrinsicσ), target-relaxation ω_A(A_eq−A) (**banned** unless donor-limited — it runs backward at an empty donor; admissible only if the source is an effectively infinite external reservoir, which makes the system open), and the working derived target A_eq,W = A_eq,intrinsic + κ_A K/ω_A (the companion's completion, "not a closed-block law, and the reason the two systems do not reduce"). Under the institutional-failure specialization (µ = ν = ρ = 0, C_A = 0) the block is eq. (2): Ṅ = R − qEN; Ȧ_act = −B + e_GA − e_AG + γ_U U; Ȧ_geo = −e_GA + e_AG; U̇ = T − γ_U U, driven by a memory–effort pair (Z,E) responding to qEN − R and never to mining. Registered parameters: r = 0.02, K = 100, q = 0.001, κ_A = 0.05, ω_A = 10⁻³, A₀ = 1, A_eq,intrinsic = 50, γ_U = 0.2; A_g0 declared positive with σ ≈ 1 under the registered scale separation (A_geo ≫ A_g0); A_g0 = 0 is the discontinuous-perturbation limit, not the registered regime. The four-row incidence block is displayed; six internal columns sum to zero and harvest and mining carry −1, so Ṁ = −qEN − C_A,lim reads off the display. R is the difference of two non-negative primitives, so the signed entry stays inside the discipline. Uptake T never enters Ṅ — it is an A_act→U throughput with the living stock catalytic.

Further specializations: a six-compartment illustration (X, U, A, G, P, W; eight primitives g, m, h, d_U, e_GA, e_AG, c_G, r_P; split fractions α, ρ_P; 1ᵀS = 0), explicitly "a monomaterial projection" whose constant splits and absorbing-sink convention are declared choices, and the paper states that block (2) is **not** a specialization of it — two different timescale lumpings of the same physical story, with no incidence specialization mapping one to the other. A four-stock resource–sink–nutrient–product system (S,K,N,P) yields d(S+K+N+P)/dt = I_N − Q_P and, with a harvest floor H ≥ H_min > 0, two empty-kernel mechanisms: no assimilation (δ ≡ 0) or weak assimilation (δ(K_max) < w(H_min)) drive the sink past any finite ceiling in finite time, so the viability kernel of {S ≥ S_min, 0 ≤ K ≤ K_max} is empty — the obstruction requires θ_K > 0. This is "the precise sense in which a 'balanced' mass ledger can still be physically inadmissible."

Mechanism typing (§2.5): routing is never determined by diagnostic labels; where extraction is split between standing-stock removal and recruitment suppression (C_stock = ψqEN, C_recruit = (1−ψ)qEN) the assignment needs per-channel evidence, with illustrative calibrated pairs (soil zinc: ψ = 0.85 removal vs 0.25 impaired mineralisation; pollinators: 0.70 adult mortality vs 0.20 brood failure; trough depth varies by ~1.5 from mechanism alone) stated as illustrative, not constitutive. Prevented recruitment is an omitted inflow — routing it to product or waste "would create mass that was never in the stock"; damage to the capital stock (aquifer compaction, severe soil loss) is a capacity drift or an inert transfer, not a split channel.

§2.6, Theorem 1: under support saturation the stock equation reduces to the logistic with ‖X_κ − X₀‖ = O(κ) (r = µ−δ, K_log = (µ−δ)/c), and Remark 2 pins the scope: the identity is pointwise on the interior support region, non-uniform through the depleted-pool boundary (A/(K_A+A) = 0 at A = 0 for every K_A > 0), does not eliminate U or make A constant, and is "not a transfer principle for bifurcation thresholds" — so a logistic stock equation is a saturated support-pool readout, not a primitive law.

## 4. Three certificates (§3)

Layer 1 accounting consistency (the balance law holds a.e.), Layer 2 conservation consistency (ℓᵀSᵀ = 0 for each declared quantity), Layer 3 barrier safety (B_m(t) ≤ S_m(t) ≤ B̄_m(t) for every component). Proposition 1: Layer 2 ⇒ Layer 1 for conserved quantities, with invariance when b = 0. Proposition 2: Layer 3 does not follow from Layer 1 (constant extraction exceeding regeneration is exactly mass-balanced and drives the stock through any positive lower barrier in finite time; the extinction trajectory N ≡ 0 is mass-balanced yet violates barriers), and a barrier-satisfying trajectory may be generated by fluxes failing a stoichiometric constraint or dropping a moiety through the yield-routing violation; thermodynamic admissibility presupposes a mass balance but is not implied by it. Hence: "a single integrated assessment that reports only one of them declares less than it appears to." The typed safety set K(t) = {x ≥ 0 : B(t) ≤ Cx ≤ B(t)} and V_typed are conjunctive — no weighted aggregate is the decision criterion. Lemma 3 (flux reconstruction) integrates S = Cx to give the identity plus the equivalence of pointwise and integrated barrier inequalities, distinguishing the auditable case (prescribed/observed v, b) from the endogenous case, and stressing that **barriers are declared, not computed**: the flux data do not compute ecological thresholds, aquifer collapse thresholds, or concentration boundaries. Proposition 4 gives the reduction; conservation does not imply barrier safety (a moving barrier can become infeasible against a constant moiety), and barrier–conservation compatibility is "a linear feasibility programme, not a slogan". Theorem 5 (flux-bounding envelopes) sandwiches each moiety readout between integrals of the positive/negative parts of (CSᵀ) over declared flux boxes, yielding a corollary that every trajectory compatible with the bounds is barrier-safe — qualified: the bounds are conservative over box extremes that are not jointly realizable, state-dependent fluxes need a forward-invariant box, the envelope is an interval computation and "not a forecast", the tight certificate is the LP over the stoichiometric polytope with the box as its auditing relaxation. The worked envelope gives M(t) ∈ [M(0) − (qE_max K + C_A)t, M(0)]. Proposition 6 bounds the hitting time by (S(0)−B)/ε under uniform negative drift, and the accompanying counterexample (Ṡ = −kS, so S(t) = S(0)e^{−kt} > 0 and τ₀ = ∞; τ_B = k⁻¹log(S(0)/B) diverges as B→0) kills "positive extraction implies finite exhaustion". A named proposition fixes the boundary discipline: total moiety mass is invariant, so every finite hitting time belongs to a compartment or a barrier on a readout, and "exhaustion of the natural block" is transfer across the block boundary into product, waste and inert compartments — the Daly (1990) reading that depletion is loss of *access*, not loss of matter.

## 5. What the closed ledger guarantees (§4)

Theorem 7 (natural-block mass identity): Ṁ = −qEN − C_A,lim with M = N+A_act+A_geo+U, at the declared α = 0 routing; with a detritus-routed fraction α the export is (1−α)qEN. Theorem 8 (stoichiometric conservation) for the seven compartments (N,P,W,I,U,A_act,A_geo): each column of Sᵀ is a +1/−1 transfer, routing tensors are column-stochastic, so 1ᵀẊ = 0. The full seven-row incidence is displayed, and the retirement split (ρ_P, 1−ρ_P) and the inert-bound source are declared routing choices on which "no classification depends". Theorem 9 gives dM₆/dt = 0 for the six-compartment system term by term, with scope notes: the argument applies to an expanded typed incidence system when quality grades are split, not automatically to a quality-neutral loop, and in open systems Ṁ₆ = I_∂ − O_∂ must be written rather than preserving a nominal invariant by letting an unobserved donor go negative. Theorems 7–9 are three instances of one lemma, differing only in which compartments are declared. Theorem 10 (orthant invariance of the closed block) and Theorem 11 (the six-compartment cone) are proved face by face with Nagumo's tangent-cone criterion (Aubin 1991), the donor-limitation condition being "the exact sufficiency requirement — algebraic cancellation alone does not establish invariance", with the lineage in Jacquez & Simon (1993) and the target-relaxation law re-banned. Theorem 12: at constant effort E* > 0 a rest point satisfies R + C_A,lim = 0, hence R = 0 with no mining, so N = 0, N = K, or A_act = 0, and none is compatible with positive effort and positive stock — so the working point (N*, A_act*) = (89.526, 397.87) at E* ≈ 2.090 is **not** a rest point (Ṅ = 0 holds there by construction, R* = qE*N* ≈ 0.187 > 0, and the abiotic pair fails). Theorem 13: with E ≡ 0 the rest set is exactly the extinction family, the carrying-capacity family and the frozen-biomass face, with e_GA = e_AG pinning A_act = A_eq,intrinsicσ; adding basal mortality collapses the frozen-biomass face without touching Theorems 7–12 or 14. Theorem 14 (integrable extraction): M(t) = M(0) − ∫qEN ds ≥ 0 forces qEN ∈ L¹(0,∞), so no trajectory sustains the working extraction value ≈0.187 forever; a constant comparison flux c exhausts the budget by M(0)/c, while proportional extraction need not reach zero in finite time — "the integral bound is the whole statement". Theorem 15 is conditional (hybrid jumps between locally finite event times, with the jump term; internal-transformation jumps require Lᵀ(χ⁺−χ⁻) = 0 or a factorization; boundary-crossing jumps are boundary impulses), kept at its own status and not merged with Proposition 4. §4.9, "Cancellation is cheap": summing six material equations of a ten-state admissibility template gives an exact identity that proves neither forward invariance nor admissibility — the template's geological exchange is not donor-limited, and two further negative witnesses (a variance closure unrealizable by any non-negative spatial distribution, an output functional with no displayed state equation) are recorded in the supplementary as audited admissibility failures.

## 6. Services, deficit, depletion arithmetic (§5–6)

Services are readouts s = O(x,u,θ) (linear s = Q(θ)v when they are selected or converted fluxes, each row declaring the delivery boundary and unit conversion), the contemporaneous balance b_i = s_i − d_i, and the caution that b_i ≥ 0 does not imply a sustainable trajectory in either direction. Definition 1 makes the feasible balance domain B(x,t) the object against which any scalar certificate claim is checked. Definition 2 defines the directional regenerative-support fraction α_reg over Γ_reg ⊆ Γ_all (feasible after declared regenerative-flow, system-boundary, material-quality and exergy/capacity restrictions — application inputs, not constructed by the stock ledger), with the gap (1−α_reg)s̄ in service units, attainment requiring closedness, a non-interpretation discipline (the definition neither subtracts raw material from service nor proves a stock is declining), and the provenance partition (renewable flow, recycled material, imports, non-renewable drawdown) never adding unlike units. The unreduced physical deficit is Δ_phys(t) = C(t) − M̂ᵀS(t) — a diagnostic that does not drive the physics and equals −Ṅ only on the single-resource specialization (Remark 16: qEN − R = −Ṅ, so Λ = [qEN − R]₊ = [−Ṅ]₊), that collapse being "a property of the specialization, not a definition of liquidation". Decline pressure: since qEN − R = O(N) as N → 0, the incremental decline signal vanishes near extinction while a positive baseline in the effort law can still sustain commanded effort — a controller that must respond to low stock irrespective of its rate of change needs a separately registered level-dependent channel.

Depletion time is split into three named objects: gross turnover intensity J_A^gross = g/A and support coverage H_A^gross = (A−A_min)/g (neither is a time to depletion, because g > 0 ⇏ Ȧ < 0 — at an interior steady state turnover is balanced), the frozen net-depletion ratio H_A^loc = (A−A_min)/[−Ȧ]₊ with the +∞ convention at no net decline, and the scenario-conditioned hitting time T_A(x₀;π,d) = inf{t : A^{π,d}(t) ≤ A_min}, whose honest output under uncertainty is "a distribution or robust interval, not a single universal date". Proposition 17 brackets any horizon within H₀/(1±ε) under a declared rate bracket, and the paper notes the only regime in which the frozen ratio *is* a horizon (|H − H_A^loc| ≤ 2εH₀/(1−ε)) and that at a stationary state with g > 0, H_A^loc = +∞ while H_A^gross < ∞. Exit times τ_m^± and τ_exit = min{τ⁻,τ⁺} with "horizon safety on [0,T] is τ_exit > T" come with two disciplines: equality at hitting requires continuity of stock and barrier (jumps can cross without equality), and "exhaustion" is reserved for S_m = 0 while physical emptiness, functional failure, regime-shift thresholds and economically recoverable reserves are barrier violations. Upper barriers matter symmetrically (CO₂ accumulation violates an upper barrier while fossil stocks decline), and a claim of sustainability rather than finite-horizon admissibility needs the terminal condition x(T) ∈ K_maint, the viability kernel of the barrier set — the finite-horizon diagnostic is necessary, not sufficient. Robust semantics quantify barrier safety over θ ∈ Θ and d ∈ D.

## 8. First-passage semantics on declared surrogates (§7)

Two objects, not one: the ledger's T_A lives on trajectories of the mass-conserved ledger or a named reduced system; the public quantities are constructed proxies. Definition 6 sets A(t) = A₀ + µt + ςW_t with µ = â˙ < 0 and stops at the record-relative barrier A_min^win, and the definition *includes* the non-completion clause: the surrogate "is not a hydrological constitutive law, is not mass-conserving, and is not a perturbation or stochastic completion" of the ledger. Proposition 18 (a standard fact, stated for notation) gives T_GW ~ IG(ν,λ) with ν = d/|µ|, λ = d²/ς², E[T] = ν = H_GW^win and Var = ν³/λ = dς²/|µ|³, conditional on treating µ and the barrier as fixed; the equality of the surrogate's mean with the deterministic trend ratio is "the precise sense in which the tabled groundwater numbers are first-passage means of a declared surrogate". Corollary 19: as ς → 0⁺, T_GW → H_GW^win in probability, and for every finite ς the IG median lies strictly below the mean (right skew toward short passage times) — "the inequality must not be inverted"; these are conditional statements about the surrogate, not corrections to the tabled years, and they do not show water mass depleting faster. The record-relative barrier discipline has three clauses: already-at-minimum gives T = 0 deterministically for every ς with a degenerate IG limit ("zero cells report zero relative to the selected observational barrier — not zero physical uncertainty and no confirmation of collapse"); an independent threshold A♯ < A_min^win lengthens the surrogate's mean to (A₀−A♯)/|µ|, within the surrogate only; and the load-bearing content is the interpretation boundary itself. Proposition 20 runs the geometric-Brownian analogue dB = −hB dt + ςB dW, giving E[T_fish] = log(B₀/B_min)/(h+ς²/2), with the ς²/2 shortening identified as an Itô convention rather than a property of the physical process. The phosphate passage time under Ġ = −P is (G₀−G_min)/P, reserve-life being G_min = 0. §7.7 lists seven explicit non-claims (processes not completions and not mass conserving; no theorem relates â˙ to −Ȧ of the reduced systems, the finite-donor system, or the delay equations; T_A is not shown IG — it would be only if the residual were Brownian with constant drift, which (2) does not supply; the historical minimum is not an identified physical barrier; a shorter surrogate median or Itô mean is not evidence of faster physical loss; H_A^gross and the productivity-illusion reading are not first-passage results; the fisheries calculation is not a stage-structured model and the phosphate calculation not a geological-reserve model). §7.8 adds that â˙ is estimated from a finite, possibly autocorrelated record whose minimum supplies the barrier, that integrating measurement error, serial dependence, seasonality, aggregation, trend breaks or climatic drivers yields a predictive mixture rather than one IG law, and that a residual scale estimated from the same window does not identify process noise — "no calibrated predictive distribution is claimed". The practical message for industrial-ecology measurement: first-passage distributions on declared surrogates are usable as descriptive statistics, but their drift, barrier and noise inputs each carry their own identification story, and a calibrated forecast requires that story to be discharged.

## 9. Domain templates (§8) and the institutional interface (§9)

Templates enter at **registered status**: the phosphorus ladder (phosphate rock → fertilizer → soil pool → runoff, with mining c_G and recycling routes α, ρ_P) has its yield/loss functions, recovery fractions and price response "declared, not established", its competing-model ladder being an identification object and its falsification protocols (which observation rejects which routing assumption) recorded obligations rather than results. The groundwater template enters with an admitted object and a declared gap: the one-pool affine approximation behind the anomaly index is admitted, the two-pool active-plus-slow-donor model is not established, and the registered requirements for closing the gap are aquitard geometry, multi-depth piezometry, pumping tests, tracer/isotope/water-age evidence, recharge estimates, prior ranges for storage and fast–slow coupling, and the discipline that leakage terms may not absorb unexplained residuals. §8.3 turns the same discipline on extractor-side economics: Clark's open-access equilibrium S_OA = c/(pq) is infeasible as a management target under any floor S_min > S_OA; the modified golden rule g′(S_δ) = δ sets the optimal stock for the discount rate; a tax moves S_OA to c/((p−τ)q) but "does not move the physical floor"; instrument parameters and constraint thresholds are different objects, and g here is a declared readout for the remark only, not a primitive of the closed block.

§9 fixes the partition by contract.
