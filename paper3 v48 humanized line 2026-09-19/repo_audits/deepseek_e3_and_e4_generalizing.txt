## Generalization Protocol for E3 and E4  
### From Pool-Specific Ports to Transferable Claim Schemas, Scope Conditions, and Pre-Registration

The Uvalde record already supplies the empirical port. What it does not yet do is state the **generalization** of E3 and E4 in a form that survives across pools. The correct move is not to generalize the numerical results. It is to generalize the **claim schemas**, name the **transferable invariants**, and state the **scope conditions** under which each claim is definable.

E3 and E4 are not single universal claims. Each decomposes into two parts:

- **E3**: a *mechanism* claim and a *verdict* claim.
- **E4**: a *constructive* leg and a *negative institutional* leg.

The two parts have different portability. The Uvalde record shows exactly which part travels and which does not.

---

## 1. Objects and notation

Let a **pool** \(P\) be indexed by a well \(w\). No pooling across wells; each pool is its own object. For pool \(P\):

- \(H_t\): head series.
- \(R_t\): recharge driver.
- \(P_t\): pumpage driver.
- \(\rho_H = \mathrm{AC}(1)\) of \(H\): head persistence.
- \(\rho_R = \mathrm{AC}(1)\) of \(R\): recharge whiteness.
- \(\mathcal{M} = \{ \text{persist}, \text{mean}, \text{AR1}, \text{stock-flow}, \dots \}\): forecast ladder.
- \(K\): a declared threshold.
- \(F\): a declared recharge floor.
- \(H^*_0(P,F)\): zero-pumping fixpoint under floor \(F\).
- \(m(P,K,F) = K - H^*_0(P,F)\): **attractor-to-threshold margin**.

The decisive invariant is \(m\). The decisive scope condition is whether a **physical threshold** exists.

---

## 2. E3 generalization

### 2.1 E3 has two separable claims

**E3-mechanism.** When recharge is near-white and head is persistent, persistence is a strong baseline; added stock-flow structure does not robustly improve out-of-sample forecast.

**E3-verdict.** Under a point-RMSE retention rule, the stock-flow model is or is not retained.

The Uvalde record shows:

- **E3-mechanism replicates**: \(\rho_R \approx 0.168\), \(\rho_H \approx 0.844\).
- **E3-verdict flips nominally** (M2 retained at h=1), but the flip is not robust: DM insignificant, bootstrap CI covers zero, sub-period unstable, driver-independent, and the mean-reversion hypothesis is falsified.

Therefore the generalization is:

> **E3-mechanism is portable. E3-verdict is not a portable point statistic.**

### 2.2 Generalized E3 claim schema

For a pool \(P\) with recharge whiteness \(\rho_R\) and head persistence \(\rho_H\):

- If \(\rho_R\) is near-white and \(\rho_H\) is high, persistence is a strong baseline.
- A stock-flow model may show short-horizon artifacts from including any temporally structured exogenous regressor. This is not evidence that structure earns its keep.
- The only admissible E3 verdict is a **conjunction**:
  1. Diebold–Mariano significant at pre-declared level.
  2. Bootstrap CI on RMSE gap excludes zero.
  3. Sub-period sign stability.
  4. Driver-dependence test passes for mechanism claims.

If any conjunct fails, the verdict is **not met**, regardless of point-RMSE ordering.

### 2.3 Scope condition for E3

E3-mechanism generalizes to pools where:

- recharge is near-white, and
- head is persistent.

E3-verdict generalizes only under the conjunction rule above. A bare point-RMSE retention rule is underpowered and non-robust.

## 3. E4 generalization

### 3.1 E4 has two separable legs

**E4-constructive.** Reactive rules earn a nominal supply margin at the *physical* threshold; a non-empty robust kernel exists under the drought-of-record floor.

**E4-negative institutional.** No declared policy holds the *institutional* threshold under the drought floor.

The Uvalde record shows:

- E4-negative replicates **more strongly** than on J-17.
- E4-constructive **cannot be defined** on Uvalde because there is no physical threshold and the zero-pumping drought-floor attractor falls short of the institutional line.

Therefore the generalization is:

> **E4-constructive is scoped to pools with a physical threshold and negative attractor-to-threshold margin. E4-negative institutional is broader: it holds whenever the institutional threshold lies above the zero-pumping attractor under the declared floor.**

### 3.2 The decisive invariant: attractor-to-threshold margin

For pool \(P\), threshold \(K\), and recharge floor \(F\), define

\[
m(P,K,F) = K - H^*_0(P,F).
\]

- If \(m > 0\): zero pumping alone falls short of \(K\). No positive pumping rule can bridge the gap. The robust kernel at \(K\) is empty.
- If \(m < 0\): zero pumping clears \(K\) with margin. A constructive policy may exist; this requires policy-family analysis.

This is structural, not numerical. It depends on pool hydrology, threshold choice, and recharge floor.

### 3.3 Physical threshold existence as a scope condition

E4-constructive requires a **physical threshold** \(K_{\text{phys}}\) with an ecological or legal referent — e.g., spring cessation, ESA-listed species flow requirement.

If no such threshold exists, E4-constructive is **not definable**. The Uvalde Pool has no physical threshold: the EAHCP covers Comal and San Marcos Springs only; Uvalde’s 845/840/835 are purely institutional stage levels.

Therefore:

> **E4-constructive scope:** pools with a physical threshold and \(m(P,K_{\text{phys}},F) < 0\).  
> **E4-negative scope:** pools with an institutional threshold and \(m(P,K_{\text{inst}},F) > 0\).

### 3.4 Floor dependence

The margin is floor-dependent. Under a milder recharge floor \(F'\), \(H^*_0(P,F')\) rises, so \(m\) falls. This explains the narrow Uvalde result at 835 ft under q05/q10: under milder floors, the zero-pumping attractor rises above 835, so a constructive kernel becomes possible. Under the drought-of-record floor, it does not.

Thus a complete E4 generalization must state the recharge floor explicitly. No margin is floor-free.

## 4. Cross-pool comparability audit

The transferable object is not “same aquifer.” It is the **claim schema** plus its scope conditions.

| Porting choice | Pool-invariant? | Role in generalization |
|---|---|---|
| Head series construction | Yes | Low risk |
| Annual rule | Yes | Low risk |
| Domain/window | Yes, if matched | Low risk |
| Recharge driver mapping | Partly | Not load-bearing for E3 flip; unusable for v3 credit |
| Pumpage driver | Untested | Must be pre-registered |
| Head AC(1) | No — differs | Real structural difference |
| Clip bounds | No — free parameter | Must be pre-registered |
| **Attractor-to-threshold margin** | No — decisive | **Determines whether E4-constructive is definable** |
| **Physical threshold existence** | No — absent on Uvalde | **Scope condition for E4-constructive** |
| Institutional thresholds | Partly | Same qualitative role |

Read-out: the two pools are comparable for the forecast-ladder question and for the institutional-threshold question. They are **not** comparable for the constructive intervention question. The comparator that decides this is the attractor-to-threshold margin.

---

## 5. Generalized statements

### 5.1 E3*

**E3-mechanism*.** For a pool with near-white recharge and persistent head, persistence is a strong baseline; stock-flow additions may show short-horizon artifacts but do not robustly improve forecast.

**E3-verdict*.** A stock-flow model is retained only if the pre-declared conjunction of DM significance, bootstrap CI excluding zero, sub-period sign stability, and driver-dependence is satisfied. Point-RMSE ordering alone is not a verdict.

### 5.2 E4*

**E4-constructive*.** If a pool has a physical threshold \(K_{\text{phys}}\) and \(m(P,K_{\text{phys}},F) < 0\) under the drought-of-record floor \(F\), then a non-empty robust kernel may exist; policy-family analysis is required to construct it.

**E4-negative institutional*.** If a pool has an institutional threshold \(K_{\text{inst}}\) and \(m(P,K_{\text{inst}},F) > 0\) under floor \(F\), then no declared policy holds \(K_{\text{inst}}\). The kernel is empty by structure.

These two statements are the generalization of E4. They separate the constructive leg from the negative institutional leg and state the scope conditions for each.

---

## 6. Pre-registration requirements for a v3

A `SPECIFICATION_v3.md` should be written before any confirmatory score.

1. **Name the transferable invariant.** The candidate is the attractor-to-threshold margin \(m(P,K,F)\). Physical-threshold existence is a scope condition.
2. **Pre-specify the E3 decision rule.** Require the conjunction: DM significant, bootstrap CI excludes zero, sub-period sign stable, driver-dependence test passes.
3. **Pre-register free porting parameters.** Clip bounds, driver mapping, pumpage driver.
4. **Control multiplicity.** Across pools × thresholds × horizons × floors × driver definitions. State the correction explicitly.
5. **State scope limits up front.** E4-constructive generalizes only to pools with a physical threshold and negative margin. Uvalde is out of scope for that claim by construction.
6. **Adopt falsification conditions as acceptance tests.** Write them before scores are computed.

---

## 7. What the Uvalde record establishes for generalization

1. E3-mechanism replicates; E3-verdict does not.
2. E4-negative institutional replicates more strongly than on J-17.
3. E4-constructive does not transfer for a structural reason: no physical threshold, and \(m(840) = +6.47\) ft under the drought floor.
4. The narrow 835 ft kernel under milder floors is honest but not a replication.
5. The decisive blocker is structural, not numerical: the attractor-to-threshold gap and physical-threshold existence are the transferable scope conditions.

---

## 8. Bottom line

To generalize E3 and E4, separate each into its portable part and its non-portable part.

- **E3**: generalize the mechanism; reject the point-rule verdict. Use the conjunction rule.
- **E4**: generalize the negative institutional verdict broadly; generalize the constructive leg only to pools with a physical threshold and negative attractor-to-threshold margin. State the recharge floor explicitly.

The Uvalde Pool is comparable for the forecast-ladder and institutional-threshold questions. It is out of scope for E4-constructive by construction. That is the complete generalization.
