## 3. Weaknesses to strengthen

### 3.1 The "verdict flips" framing overstates the point result

Section 3 header says "the E3 *verdict* flips." It then correctly says the flip is within noise and driver-independent. The header should be softened to match: **"the E3 point-rule retention nominally flips."** The verdict does not flip; the point rule does, and the record itself shows the point rule is not a reliable verdict statistic on this sample.

### 3.2 The h1-vs-h5 divergence is under-interpreted

M2 wins at h=1 (7.25 vs 8.09) but loses badly at h=5 (21.70 vs 18.16). The record attributes the h1 win to the mean-reversion term tracking Uvalde's higher head persistence. But this sits awkwardly with the fact that **higher AC(1) should favor persistence, not the stock-flow model**. If head AC(1) = 0.844, naive_persist should be *stronger* on J-27, not weaker. The more likely reading is:

- M2's h1 advantage is a **short-horizon fitting artifact** (the δ·H term absorbing one-step noise), which is why it does not survive to h=5 and why it is driver-independent (D5).
- The record should say this explicitly: M2 is not capturing a persistent recharge–head relationship; it is capturing the *head* autocorrelation that naive_persist already captures more parsimoniously at h=5.

This strengthens the "neither replicated nor refuted" conclusion and makes the D5 result less surprising.

### 3.3 Driver-choice justification is thin

`Basin_1 + Basin_2` is asserted as the Uvalde-area analog to San Antonio `Total`. The record lists an alternative (`Uvalde-B1–4`) only inside D5. A v3-relevant exploratory record should:

- state the inclusion criterion for B1+B2 vs B1–B4,
- report the D5-style ladder for all candidate driver definitions (B1, B2, B1+2, B1–4, mismatched Total),
- flag this as one of the weakest comparability links.

### 3.4 Clip bounds are asserted, not justified

`[820, 890] ft` is called "rounded observed extremes." For J-17, `[610, 710]` presumably maps to physical bounds (spring elevation / aquifer base). For J-27, the analog is unclear. The record should either:

- name the physical/operational bounds that justify 820 and 890, or
- flag the clip as a **free comparability parameter** that a v3 would have to pre-register and sensitivity-test.

### 3.5 Kernel emptiness is conditional on the drought-of-record floor only

The headline negative result—every kernel empty at 835/840/845—is reported under the **drought-of-record floor (19.8)**. The record lists q05 (54.7) and q10 (71.6) but does not report kernel status under them. This matters: under q05 or q10, some policies might be non-empty. The record should report kernel status under **all three floors** (or state explicitly that only the drought-of-record floor was evaluated and why).

### 3.6 The "distance from attractor to threshold" is the key comparability variable and is not named

BAU fixpoint on J-27 is 828.21; institutional threshold 840 → gap ≈ 11.8 ft. On J-17, the analogous gap (BAU fixpoint to 660) is presumably much larger. That gap is *why* reactive rules "barely bite" on Uvalde. This is a structural pool characteristic that a v3 would need to formalize, and it is more explanatory than the raw fixpoint values. The record should compute and report both gaps side by side.

### 3.7 No effect-size / practical-significance context

The h1 RMSE gap is 8.09 − 7.25 = 0.84 ft. For water management, is 0.84 ft meaningful? The E4 fixpoint differences (828 vs 833) clearly are, because they cross thresholds. The E3 RMSE gap needs the same treatment: state whether 0.84 ft is within measurement/operational noise for Uvalde head.

## 4. Missing pieces to complete

### 4.1 D6 is referenced but not reported

Section 7 says "Diagnostics D1–D6," but Section 3 reports only D1–D5. Either:

- add the D6 definition and result, or
- correct the reference to "D1–D5."

This is a straightforward completeness fix, but it matters because a reader cannot tell whether D6 was run and suppressed or never existed.

### 4.2 Apply D5 logic to J-17 as a control

D5 shows the J-27 M2 win is driver-independent. The symmetric question is: **is the J-17 M2 *non-retention* also driver-independent?** If a mismatched driver on J-17 still fails to retain M2, that strengthens E3. If a mismatched driver on J-17 *does* retain M2, then E3's mechanism claim is weakened even on its home pool. This is the single most important missing diagnostic. It is a direct test of whether E3's mechanism is about recharge at all, or about something else that happens to correlate with recharge on J-17.

### 4.3 Matched-window control on J-17 (1941–2023)

J-17 uses 1934–2023; J-27 uses 1941–2023. The 1930s drought of record is in J-17's window but not J-27's. Re-run the frozen J-17 ladder on 1941–2023 and report whether the E3 retention verdict changes. If it does, the cross-pool comparison is confounded by window. If it does not, the comparability argument is stronger. Either way, this is cheap and decisive.

### 4.4 Explicit falsification conditions

The record has diagnostics but no stated **falsification conditions** for its own exploratory claims. Add a short section:

- What would have counted as a *robust* E3 replication on J-27? (e.g., DM significant at α, bootstrap CI excluding zero, sub-period sign stable, and D5 showing driver dependence.)
- What would have counted as a *robust* E4 replication? (e.g., non-empty kernel at ≥1 threshold under the drought floor.)
- State plainly that neither condition was met.

This makes the "neither replicated nor refuted" conclusion auditable rather than rhetorical.

### 4.5 Survey of Uvalde physical-threshold candidates

The record says Uvalde "has no spring-cessation analog." That is a strong claim and should be supported by a short survey: are there river baseflow thresholds, ecological minimums, water-quality thresholds, or operational minimums in the Uvalde Pool that could serve as the analog? If none exists, say so with citations. If one exists but was not used, explain why. This is the load-bearing comparability claim for E4's constructive leg, and it is currently asserted rather than demonstrated.

### 4.6 "Second-well" vs "second-pool" terminology

The title says "Second-Pool Record" but the body uses "second-well analysis" in places. J-27 is a well; the Uvalde Pool is the pool. Standardize: the *object* is the Uvalde Pool, indexed by well J-27. This matters because the no-pooling rule is about pools, not wells.

### 4.7 Multiplicity note on exploratory claims

The record correctly proposes multiplicity control for v3 but does not note that its own exploratory comparisons (multiple thresholds, horizons, driver definitions) are uncorrected. That is fine for exploratory work, but it should be stated so no reader mistakes the exploratory p-values for confirmatory ones.

## 5. Proposed additions (concrete)

I would add four short sections and modify two existing ones:

**New §3.0 — Falsification conditions (pre-stated for this record).**
State what would have counted as robust replication for E3 and E4, and that neither was met.

**New §3.8 — J-17 matched-window and mismatched-driver controls.**
Report (a) J-17 ladder on 1941–2023, (b) J-17 M2 retention under mismatched driver. These are the two controls that determine whether E3's mechanism survives D5-style scrutiny on its home pool.

**New §4.5 — Candidate physical thresholds for Uvalde.**
Survey and either identify an analog or document its absence.

**New §5.0 — Comparability audit table.**
One row per porting choice (head, annual rule, domain, recharge driver, pumpage driver, clip, thresholds), with columns: *pool-invariant?* / *evidence* / *residual risk*. This is the artifact a v3 would inherit.

**Modify §3 header** from "the E3 verdict flips" to "the E3 point-rule retention nominally flips."

**Modify §4** to report kernel status under all three floors, not just drought-of-record.

## 6. Bottom line

The record's core conclusion—**do not promote a v3 on this evidence**—is correct and well-supported. The main things that would make it *stronger as an exploratory artifact* are:

1. Reporting D6 (or fixing the reference).
2. Applying D5 logic to J-17, which is the missing control that tests E3's mechanism on its home pool.
3. Running J-17 on the matched 1941–2023 window.
4. Reporting kernel emptiness under q05/q10 as well as drought-of-record.
5. Naming the "attractor-to-threshold gap" as the key structural comparability variable.
6. Stating explicit falsification conditions for the exploratory claims.

None of these change the recommendation. They make the recommendation **auditable**, which is what a v3 would need in order to inherit this work legitimately.
