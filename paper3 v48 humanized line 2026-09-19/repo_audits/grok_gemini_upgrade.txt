## The upgrade: two books, two lands, conversion not salvage

Make the orchard literal. Fruit and trees were never one state.

**Flow land** `A_f` (cropland / intensive systems): high yield `b_f`, fast response, soil-debt on `b_f`.  
**Capital land** `A_c` (forest / soil / fishery): slow `ρ_c`, long `τ_g`, interior MSY, genuine fold.  
**Demand is met from flow first; the shortfall converts capital land into flow land.** That is the historical mechanism (agricultural expansion), not `/b_G` as “eat the trees.”

Measured biocapacity is the GFN object — a yield-weighted aggregate:

\[
B = b_f(D,t)\,A_f + b_c A_c + b_{G,c} G_c(A_c).
\]

If `b_f > b_c` (true in the National Footprint Accounts: cropland equivalence ≫ forest), **conversion raises `B` while `A_c` falls.** The productivity illusion is then generic, not a 5.4 yr window on a 1.6 yr crop. That is the original title result, restored as an accounting identity rather than a delicate transient.

### States and units (four states, two delays)

| Symbol | Meaning | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| `A_f` | flow land | ha |
| `A_c` | capital land | ha |
| `P` | population | cap |
| `D` | soil/productivity debt on flow land | gha·yr |
| `A_r` | reserved / unused (Half-Earth) | ha, algebraic)

World constraint: `A_f + A_c + A_r = A_tot` (fixed). `A_r` is the typed floor.

### Equations

```
G_c(A_c) = ρ_c A_c (1 − A_c / A_{c,max})                         (1)
```

**What each new symbol is**

- `κ` (gha·ha⁻¹): hectares of capital land that must be converted to meet 1 gha/yr of flow shortfall. This is the *conversion* coefficient, **not** ecological `V_eco`. For forest → cropland, `κ` is order the cropland yield (one ha converted yields `b_f` gha/yr), so `S/κ` is ha/yr of deforestation. Distinct from `b_{G,c}` (timber salvage of the slow stock, used only if you also harvest `A_c` as timber: `L_c`).
- `σ_f, σ_c`: harvestable shares (reservation). Half-Earth is `A_c ≥ A_{c,min}` or `A_r ≥ ½ A_tot`, a **typed floor on `A_c`**, not a cap on composite `B`. That is the informational result of old §9, now an equation.
- `L_c = [H_c − σ_c Y_c]₊ / b_{G,c}`: optional timber harvest on the capital stock (Schaefer / fold channel). Food shortfall does **not** go through `b_{G,c}`.
- Ecological turnover of forests lives in `ρ_c, τ_g, A_{c,max}` only. Set `V_eco ∼ 30–50 yr` there without touching the conversion gain.

Default closure: `A_r` absorbs the residual; conversion is one-way on the fast scale (abandonment can return `A_f → A_c` with its own delay `τ_g`, which is the recovery-blocking mechanism).

### Why this is well-posed and does not mix books

- `A_f, A_c` are physical hectares (identifiable decomposition; old §2.1 remark becomes true).
- `B, E` are flows (gha·yr⁻¹); `D` is gha·yr; `κ` converts a flow shortfall into a land-conversion rate.
- `γ = 1/b_{G,c}` is timber harvest only. `1/V_eco = ρ_c`-scale. **Never write `γ = 1/V`.**
- One-sided `[·]₊` remains: surplus does not create land. Donor limitation: `S/κ` cannot exceed available `A_c`.
- Extinction floors: `A_c ≥ A_{c,ext}`, `A_f ≥ 0`, `K ≥ K_min` (needed because (2) can send `b_f → 0`).
- Delays only where they were: recruitment of `A_c`, demography on `K`. `D` is not a delayed state.

Linearisation around a conversion equilibrium is 4-D with a possible zero mode if you keep a family; with a finite arable ceiling `A_{f,max}` the family collapses to isolated points and `det = 0` vs `det > 0` cannot be confused.

# Part 1. Root Cause Analysis: Why Did the Eigenvalue Collapse?

### Root Cause 1: The Harmonic Mean of Global Biocapacity Turnover
The manuscript sets out to model the Global Footprint Network (GFN) accounts. In GFN bookkeeping, what constitutes the Earth’s annual biocapacity?
- **Cropland & Pasture**: $\approx 65\text{--}70\%$ of utilized biocapacity (turnover time $V_{\text{ag}} \approx 1\text{--}2\text{ yr}$).
- **Marine & Inland Fisheries**: $\approx 5\text{--}10\%$ of utilized biocapacity (turnover time $V_{\text{fish}} \approx 3\text{--}7\text{ yr}$).
- **Forest Products & Carbon Sequestration**: $\approx 20\text{--}25\%$ of utilized biocapacity (turnover time $V_{\text{for}} \approx 30\text{--}60\text{ yr}$).

Turnover rates are **frequencies** ($1/V$, $\text{yr}^{-1}$), not times. When a multi-asset system is liquidated under an aggregate demand footprint $E$, the effective capital turnover time $V_{\text{eff}}$ of the composite stock is the **demand-weighted harmonic mean**, not the arithmetic maximum:
$$\frac{1}{V_{\text{eff}}} = \sum_{k} \frac{w_k}{V_k}$$
where $w_k$ is the share of demand placed on asset $k$ ($\sum w_k = 1$).

If 70% of human footprint demand is agricultural/pastoral ($V_{\text{ag}} \approx 2\text{ yr}$) and 30% is forest/carbon absorption ($V_{\text{for}} \approx 50\text{ yr}$):
$$\frac{1}{V_{\text{eff}}} = \frac{0.70}{2} + \frac{0.30}{50} = 0.350 + 0.006 = 0.356\text{ yr}^{-1} \implies \mathbf{V_{\text{eff}} \approx 2.8\text{ yr}!}$$

**The fast-turnover food sectors dominate the extraction dynamics by two orders of magnitude.** 
By setting $V = 50\text{ yr}$ to represent "global ecological scale," the memo accidentally modeled a planet populated exclusively by timber-eating humans who consume no crops, meat, or fish. 

At the true planetary footprint turnover $V_{\text{eff}} \approx 3\text{--}5\text{ yr}$, $b_G = b \cdot V_{\text{eff}} \approx 1.5\text{--}2.5$. The transverse root is:
$$\lambda \approx \frac{1}{V_{\text{eff}}} - r \approx 0.25 - 0.02 = \mathbf{+0.23\text{ yr}^{-1}} \quad (\text{timescale } \sim 4.3\text{ yr})$$
The delay-independent vicious cycle is physically present at the global biosphere level.

### Root Cause 2: The Subsystem Amputation Fallacy
Look at how the transverse eigenvalue was derived:
$$\lambda = G'(A^*) + \frac{1}{V} - r$$
This formula was derived on **subsystem S0**, where $\alpha = 0$ and $D \equiv 0$.

Why did the positive root vanish when $V = 50$ on S0?
Because if $V = 50$, one hectare of land contains 50 years of yield. If harvesters incur a 2% deficit, and you **forbid soil exhaustion, forbid climate feedback, and forbid erosion ($\alpha = 0$)**, it takes 50 years to cut through that capital. Population adjusts at rate $r = 0.02$ (doubling time 35 yr). Of course a linear, non-degrading 50-year woodpile buffers a 2% population growth!

The vicious cycle in slow-turnover ecosystems (forests, soils) does not occur because humans chop down every tree in 18 months. It occurs because **overshoot damages the flow yield of the surviving stock ($D \to b$)**. 

By turning off $D$ to compute stability, the analysis amputated the very organ designed to destabilize slow-turnover capital, leaving only linear area-scalping.

### Root Cause 3: The Timescale Boundary ($\Pi = 1$) is a Law, Not a Defect
The condition for the zero-delay transverse eigenvalue on S0 is:
$$\lambda = \frac{1}{V} + G'(A^*) - r > 0$$
At a sustainable boundary ($G'(A_{\max}) = -\rho$):
$$\frac{1}{V} > r + \rho$$
Or near MSY ($G'(A^*) \approx 0$):
$$\frac{1}{V} > r \iff \mathbf{V < \frac{1}{r}}$$

With $r = 0.02\text{ yr}^{-1}$, $1/r = \mathbf{50\text{ yr}}$.
$50\text{ years}$ is not a random number: **it is the demographic expansion timescale (two human generations).**

The equation states an exact ecological law:
> *In the absence of functional degradation, standing natural capital buffers demographic overshoot if and only if the capital turnover time exceeds the human demographic expansion timescale ($V > 1/r$). If capital turns over faster than population expands ($V < 1/r$), liquidation feedback overpowers demographic adjustment, triggering an immediate, monotonic vicious cycle.*

Presenting this threshold as a "weakening of the paper" misinterprets the result. It is a unifying ecological theorem.

## Pillar 2: The Two-Channel Vicious Cycle

### Mathematical Proof of Channel 2 (Destabilizing the Slow Forest)

$$\frac{dA}{dt} = G(A) - \frac{E - b(D)A}{b_G}$$
$$\frac{dD}{dt} = E - [b(D)A + b_G G(A)] - \eta D$$

$$J_{AD} = \begin{pmatrix} G'(A^*) + \frac{b_0}{b_G} & -\frac{\alpha b_0 A^*}{b_G} \\ -(b_0 + b_G G'(A^*)) & \alpha b_0 A^* - \eta \end{pmatrix}$$

The trace of this subsystem is:
$$\operatorname{Tr}(J_{AD}) = \underbrace{G'(A^*) + \frac{1}{V}}_{\text{Channel 1}} + \underbrace{(\alpha b_0 A^* - \eta)}_{\text{Channel 2}}$$

Even when $V \to \infty$ ($1/V \to 0$, infinite timber woodpile):
$$\operatorname{Tr}(J_{AD}) \to G'(A^*) + \alpha b_0 A^* - \eta$$
Whenever the degradation rate exceeds the debt repayment rate ($\alpha b_0 A^* > \eta + |G'|$), **the system has a positive real eigenvalue $\lambda > 0$ regardless of how large the standing stock is.**
